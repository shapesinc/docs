Your sidebar shows one list of chats, with the most recent activity first and pinned chats at the top. Chats you haven't used in a while move to [Archive](/archive) so the list stays focused on what you're working on now.

## Keep the shared work easy to return to

For a trip with friends, keep the main planning chat pinned while you work through dates, travel, and places to stay. People contribute in that shared chat, and a shape's replies and files stay with the conversation. Use [threads](/threads) for focused decisions without creating a separate chat for every question.

Each person organizes their own sidebar: pinning a chat does not pin it for everyone, and **Mark as unread** is a reminder for you to return. An owner can rename the shared chat to make its purpose clearer. When the trip is over, **Archive** clears it from your list while others can keep using it; a new message brings it back.

## Pin chats

Open the **⋯** menu next to a chat and choose **Pin**. Pinned chats stay at the top of the list. Choose **Unpin** to move a chat back.

## Filter the list

The filters above your chats include:

- **All**: every chat in your list.
- **Unread**: chats with new messages. It appears when you have unread chats.
- **DMs**, **Social**, and **Private**: appear once you have more than 50 chats, and only when they narrow the list. **DMs** are one-to-one chats with people, **Social** chats include other people, and **Private** chats are just you and shapes.

The **Categories** filter appears once a chat is in a category. To add one, ask a shape in an Agent chat to create a category and move a chat into it.

## Search your chats

Select **Search...** at the top of the sidebar, or press **Cmd+K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on other computers. Results are grouped under **All**, **Chats**, **Shapes**, and **Users**. Before you type, you can browse **Your Shapes**, **Followers**, **Following**, and **Friends**.

## The chat menu

Open the **⋯** menu next to a chat to see what you can do with it. On touch devices, swipe the chat to reveal **⋯**.

| Option | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Open in new tab** | Opens the chat in a new browser tab (desktop) |
| **Rename** | Renames a chat you own |
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Keeps the chat at the top of your list |
| **Mark as unread** | Marks the chat unread so you come back to it |
| **Archive** / **Unarchive** | Moves the chat to Archive, or brings it back |
| **Block User** / **Unblock User** | Blocks the other person in a DM |
| **Invite** | Opens **Invite friends** for a group chat |
| **Share** | Copies a link to a chat that's just you and shapes |
| **Leave Chat** | Leaves a group chat you don't own |
| **Delete** | Deletes a chat you own, or a DM. **Undo** appears for a few seconds |

### Chat names that follow the conversation

New chats get a name from their first exchange. In an Agent chat, that name updates when the conversation clearly moves on to something else, such as a menu question that turns into planning a whole flight. The new name shows in the sidebar and the chat header right away. A name someone typed is never changed, and chats set to **Chat** keep their first name.

## Archived chats

Choose **Archive** to clear a chat from your list without deleting it. **Undo** appears right after, and a new message brings the chat back. Archiving a shared chat only changes your own list.

Chats you haven't used for a while are archived automatically. To change this, open **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Chats & Shapes** and adjust **Archive when inactive** and **Days of inactivity** (14 by default).

To find an archived chat, open **Settings** → **Archive**, or select **Check Archive** at the bottom of your chat list when it appears. Select **Restore** to bring it back. See [Archive](/archive) for details.

## Workspaces

Workspaces give a team, family, or project its own chat list. Select the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to open the switcher and move between your personal account and your workspaces. On a computer, the logo beside the name takes you **Home**.

- On desktop, **Ctrl+Shift+1** opens your personal account and **Ctrl+Shift+2** to **Ctrl+Shift+9** open your workspaces in order. **Ctrl+Shift+[** and **Ctrl+Shift+]** move to the previous or next one.
- The switcher also has **Manage memories**, and inside a workspace, **Invite people**. Members can choose **Leave workspace**; owners have **Workspace settings** instead.
- To start one, choose **Create workspace** and enter a **Workspace name**. Adding an icon and inviting people are optional, and you can search for people to invite.

Workspaces have their own membership. Switching workspaces changes the chat list you see; it does not invite anyone or move existing chats. Leaving a workspace removes your access to its chats and workspace DMs, and you need another invitation to return.

Workspaces are separate from the retired related-chat groups. Existing chats were not moved into new workspaces. For a focused discussion inside one chat, use a [thread](/threads); the thread keeps that chat's audience. See [Features and limits](/features) for the other chat controls.

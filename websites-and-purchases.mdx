In Agent chats, shapes can use websites to research options, fill forms, and help you buy or book. Tell the shape what you want, including dates, location, preferences, and budget.

> Compare these three options for our trip. Show us the total and cancellation terms before booking.

The shape uses its cloud browser. Browser time uses your shared daily computer allowance, then eligible credits; see [computer and browser usage limits](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work). To work in apps and files on your own machine instead, [connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).

## Sign in to a website

When a site needs your account, a private sign-in card can ask for the required details. Only the person whose request opened the browser can use that card or its private Browser panel.

1. Check which website the card is signing in to.
2. Enter the requested information in the private card, not as a chat message.
3. Complete any verification the site needs. When available, the sign-in flow can use your own connected Gmail to find a matching sign-in code; it does not post the code into the chat.
4. If the site needs direct interaction, such as a passkey, use **Browser** to view the browser and take control.
5. Check that sign-in succeeded before continuing the task.

The card passes credentials to the website without putting them into shared chat or memory. The browser can retain your signed-in session for future requests. This is separate from [Saved details](/saved-details), which does not store passwords or one-time codes.

After sign-in, the shape can read the pages it opens and may include relevant information in its reply. The private browser panel does not make the shape's reply private from the chat. Ordinary browser screenshots stay private to the task; ask explicitly when you want a screenshot shared in a reply. After a secure sign-in or payment fill is attempted, screenshots and exports from that browser session are blocked; the requester can still use the private Browser panel.

### Take control of the browser

In the private Browser panel, **Take control** pauses the shape's browser actions while you interact with the website. Choose **Done** when you want the shape to continue. Closing the panel alone does not resume it. **End** ends that browser session; use it when you are finished rather than when you only want to hide the panel.

## Buy something

1. Ask the shape to find or buy the item, with the requirements and spending limit.
2. Review the purchase card's merchant, items, total, and payment method.
3. Connect an eligible payment service if prompted, and complete the approval shown for that purchase.
4. Follow the final order status and receipt. Approval or a submitted checkout alone does not confirm the merchant placed the order.

The person making the request is the payer. Other chat members can see the shared purchase summary and status, but cannot use the payer's private payment controls or card details.

### Payment options and limits

- **Link** supports payers in the US and Canada. Its spend-request limit is US\$500; eligibility and the checkout's currency can further restrict a purchase.
- **Natural** lets you connect saved cards through its card-vault flow. It can be used when Link cannot serve the purchase. The phone flow asks you to save a card on Natural's page and verify a texted code; supported phone numbers, cards, merchants, and provider checks still determine whether it works.
- With a card-only Natural connection, each purchase needs its own approval. An eligible existing Natural account may instead use a budget you have authorized there. Check the purchase card and provider's approval terms rather than assuming every connection works the same way.
- **Pay on delivery** is available only when the merchant supports it and that option is offered. It still places an order; it is not a free trial of checkout.

Payment services are separate from Shape Credits. Credits pay for eligible AI features; they are not your shopping wallet. Connecting a payment service or saving an address does not itself approve a purchase. Availability does not guarantee that a particular site or provider will accept a transaction.

If a total changes, check the updated amount and any renewed approval request. If status is pending or uncertain, ask the shape to check the existing purchase before starting another one. Cancelling an unfinished purchase attempt does not cancel an order the merchant has already placed.

## Book travel

Shapes can search flights, hotels, and rental cars and assemble a trip card when travel booking is available.

1. Give the dates, destination, party size, and important requirements.
2. Review the options with the people travelling.
3. Each traveller in the chat enters their own details in a private form. The payer enters the booking contact and details for guests who do not have an account.
4. The payer reviews the exact itinerary, fare or cancellation rules, and total, then agrees and pays through the offered checkout.
5. Check the final status and booking confirmations for every item. A trip can complete only partly if some items fail.

Prices and availability can change before booking. An option shown earlier is not a reservation. Only the payer can change or cancel the trip or confirm a paid booking's cancellation. Cancellation or refund eligibility depends on the booked fare or supplier's rules; cancelling a draft is different from cancelling a confirmed booking.

## When something doesn't work

- **The website refuses access or asks for a check:** use the private browser controls when prompted. A shape may be unable to finish on that website.
- **A connection or payment method is unavailable:** check the connection, payer eligibility, and the options the current card offers.
- **The purchase is pending or only partly complete:** check the existing status and receipt before retrying. Do not treat the shape's intention to buy as confirmation.
- **You need to change saved information:** update [Saved details](/saved-details), then review the form again. Changes do not rewrite an already-submitted order or booking.

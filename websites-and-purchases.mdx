In Agent chats, shapes can help your group compare options on websites, fill forms, and buy or book. Put the requirements in the conversation so everyone can help decide: dates, location, preferences, and budget.

> Compare these three options for our trip. Show us the total and cancellation terms before booking.

The shape uses its cloud browser. Browser time uses your shared daily computer allowance, then eligible credits; see [computer and browser usage limits](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work). To work in apps and files on your own machine instead, [connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).

## Sign in to a website

When a site needs your account, a private sign-in card can ask for the required details. Only the person whose request opened the browser can use that card or its private Browser panel.

1. Check which website the card is signing in to.
2. Enter the requested information in the private card, not as a chat message.
3. Complete any verification the site needs. When available, the sign-in flow can use your own connected Gmail to find a matching sign-in code; it does not post the code into the chat.
4. If the site needs direct interaction, such as a passkey, use **Browser** to view the browser and take control.
5. Check that sign-in succeeded before continuing the task.

The card passes credentials to the website without putting them into shared chat or memory. Filling it is not by itself proof that the website signed you in. After the shape confirms the signed-in page, your card can show **Saved** when that browser is keeping the sign-in for future requests. A saved sign-in is checked before asking you to sign in again.

Manage these under **Settings** → **Connected apps** → **Saved sign-ins**. Select **Remove** beside a site to remove its saved sign-in, or **Remove all saved sign-ins** for all sites. If removal cannot finish while a browser is using the saved profile, end that browser session and retry. This is separate from [Saved details](/saved-details), which does not store passwords or one-time codes.

While a shape browses public pages, the group can follow a live browser view on its [task card](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did). The screen is withheld from other members on sign-in, payment, account, and address pages, when your saved sign-in is in use, and while you have control. Other members see a status note instead. You can still see your own browser.

After sign-in, the shape can read the pages it opens and may include relevant information in its reply. A private browser view does not make that reply private from the chat. Screenshots attached to a reply are separate from the live task-card view; ask explicitly when you want one. After a secure sign-in or payment fill is attempted, screenshots and exports from that browser session are blocked; the requester can still use the private Browser panel.

### Take control of the browser

On the task card, **Take over** opens your private Browser panel with control. In that panel, **Take control** pauses the shape's browser actions while you interact with the website. Choose **Done** when you want the shape to continue. Closing the panel alone does not resume it. **End** ends that browser session; use it when you are finished rather than when you only want to hide the panel.

## Buy something

For example, housemates can compare a table for their shared kitchen. Each person adds constraints such as measurements, delivery dates, and budget; the shape brings the options back into the chat.

1. Ask the shape to compare suitable items and show the details the group needs to decide.
2. Once you have chosen, the person paying asks to buy the specific item. Saying what to buy and who is paying is enough; you don't need to confirm twice.
3. The shape checks out on the store's own website, in your own account there when you're signed in. Subscriptions, memberships, and rentals in your own streaming or app-store account are bought on that site. If the site needs you to sign in, that's your only step: use the sign-in card or the **Browser** button.
4. Review the purchase card's store, items, total, and way to pay, then approve it. Nothing is ordered until you select **Place order** or approve the payment.
5. Follow the final order status and receipt in the purchase card. Approval or a submitted checkout alone does not confirm the store placed the order.

The person making the request is the payer. Other chat members can see the shared purchase summary and status, but cannot use the payer's private payment controls or card details. Each chat, and each thread, works on one purchase at a time.

If you reply to a shape while it's buying something for you, your reply counts as part of your request. You don't need to tag the shape again.

### How a shape picks the way to pay

Shapes don't ask how you want to pay. They use the first way that works, in this order:

1. **Your account at that store**, when you're signed in there.
2. **Your wallet.** At stores that take Shop Pay, this can be your Shop account.
3. **A shopping service** that can order from large stores and food delivery for you.
4. **Your saved card**, at the store's checkout.

Name a way to pay and the shape uses it instead, for example "use my wallet" or "pay on delivery". The only payment question you'll get is for a step only you can take: signing in, connecting your wallet, or adding a card.

### Payment options and limits

Manage these in **Settings → Connected apps → Payments**:

- **Wallet:** connect once, then approve each purchase in your wallet app. For payers in the US and Canada, at US and Canadian stores, in US or Canadian dollars.
- **Saved cards:** save a card once. You approve each payment on your phone.
- **Shop Pay:** at stores that take it, the purchase card shows a code and **Continue**, which opens Shop's own page to connect. After that, select **Place order**, or **Continue** to review and pay in Shop. To disconnect, remove shapes.inc from your connections in Shop.
- **Pay on delivery:** available only when the store offers it. It still places an order.

On the purchase card, the way to pay shows as **Wallet**, **Card**, **Your … account**, or **Cash on delivery**. If details like your address or phone number are missing, a private **Your details for this form** card asks for them and saves them to [Saved details](/saved-details).

Payment methods are separate from Shape Credits. Credits pay for eligible AI features; they are not your shopping wallet. Connecting a payment method or saving an address does not itself approve a purchase. Availability does not guarantee that a particular store will accept a transaction.

If a total changes, check the updated amount and any renewed approval request. If status is pending or uncertain, ask the shape to check the existing purchase before starting another one. Cancelling an unfinished purchase attempt does not cancel an order the store has already placed.

## Book travel

Shapes can search flights, hotels, and rental cars and assemble a trip card when travel booking is available.

For a family trip, keep the dates, budget, and must-haves in one chat so the people travelling can compare the same options.

1. One person asks the shape to search with the group's dates, destination, party size, and requirements. Other members add or correct their preferences in the chat.
2. Review the options together, then have the person paying request the selected itinerary for the named travellers. The shared trip card keeps the group looking at the same booking.
3. Each traveller in the chat enters their own details in a private form. The payer enters the booking contact and details for guests who do not have an account. Share preferences in the conversation; use the private forms for the requested personal details.
4. The payer reviews the exact itinerary, fare or cancellation rules, and total, then agrees and pays through the offered checkout. Other chat members cannot approve payment on the payer's behalf.
5. Check the final status and booking confirmations for every item. A trip can complete only partly if some items fail; do not treat one confirmed item as confirmation of the whole trip.

Hotel options show the room, cancellation terms, and meals. Taxes and fees paid at the hotel are not in the trip total; the trip card shows them as **Due at the desk**. Flight options name the fare, such as a basic fare with carry-on only, and cancellation deadlines are shown in UTC.

Prices and availability can change before booking. Fares aren't held: an option shown earlier is not a reservation. Only the payer can change or cancel the trip or confirm a paid booking's cancellation. Cancellation or refund eligibility depends on the booked fare or supplier's rules; cancelling a draft is different from cancelling a confirmed booking.

## When something doesn't work

- **The website refuses access or asks for a check:** use the private browser controls when prompted. A shape may be unable to finish on that website.
- **A way to pay doesn't work:** the shape moves on to the next one by itself. If none is ready, it asks you to sign in, connect your wallet, or add a card. Check eligibility in **Settings → Connected apps → Payments**.
- **The purchase is pending or only partly complete:** check the existing status and receipt before retrying. Do not treat the shape's intention to buy as confirmation.
- **You need to change saved information:** update [Saved details](/saved-details), then review the form again. Changes do not rewrite an already-submitted order or booking.

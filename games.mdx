## Multiplayer Activities in Chat

Ask a Shape to set up a live poll, a game, or a form for the group. Activities update for everyone in real time, in free and Premium chats. Anyone with the link can join without signing in, and the Shape can read the live results, such as votes, scores, and whose turn it is. You can delete an activity permanently when you're done.

> Make a quick poll for where we should eat on Friday, with the three places we mentioned.

See [Multiplayer activities](/multiplayer-activities) for more.

## Games in Voice Calls

Whiteboard and Zombie Tag are only visible after you have joined a voice call.

1. Start or join a voice call. See [Voice Calls](/voicecalls).
2. Open the full call view with the **View call** button (the monitor icon in the call controls).
3. In the call toolbar, open **Games**.
4. Choose **Whiteboard** or **Zombie Tag**.

## Zombie Tag

- **Where it’s available:** full voice call view → **Games**

Zombie Tag is a fast-paced multiplayer game where players try to survive — or spread — a zombie outbreak.

![Zombietagbanner](/images/zombietagbanner.png)

### Controls

- **Desktop:** WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump
- **Mobile:** Drag anywhere to move, tap the jump button

### How It Works

- Players are split into **survivors** and **zombies**
- Each round starts with **one zombie**
- Zombies turn survivors when they get close, with a random **5–10 second delay**
- Survivors cannot see who the zombies are; zombies can
- Characters are randomized each round to prevent pattern-learning

A round ends when:

- Everyone turns into a zombie, or
- Someone selects **New Round**

![Zombietag2](/images/zombietag2.png)

## Purchase Steps

1. Sign in to [Shapes, Inc.](https://talk.shapes.inc).
2. Open the premium and credit options in your account settings, or visit the [premium page](https://shapes.inc/premium).
3. Review the available credit options. If the purchase flow offers chat sponsorship, select the chat you want to fund.
4. Check the selected chat, credit amount, and price before confirming your purchase.
5. Follow the payment screen to complete checkout.

Credit options can change. If you cannot find a way to fund a specific chat, ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support) before purchasing credits for a different destination.

## How Chat Credits Work

Credits assigned to a chat provide a shared balance for eligible premium usage in that chat. The engine used and the credits charged depend on the chat's current settings and available engines.

Check the engine and credit notice in the chat before sending a message. See [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) for choosing an engine and [Why is a free engine costing credits?](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits) for unexpected credit use.

## Check the Balance

Open the credit options in your account and look for balances assigned to chats. Select the relevant chat to review its available credits, where that option is offered.

If a completed purchase is missing or you cannot find the chat's balance, contact [support](https://talk.shapes.inc/support) with the chat name and purchase receipt.

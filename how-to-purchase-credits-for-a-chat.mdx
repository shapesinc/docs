## Can I buy credits for one chat?

No. Plans belong to your account, so you can't buy one for a single chat or shape. To add credits, choose a plan under **Settings** → **Premium**. See [Premium](/premium-subscriptions).

Credits already on a chat or shape still work.

## How Chat Credits Work

Credits assigned to a chat provide a shared balance for eligible premium usage in that chat. The engine used and the credits charged depend on the chat's current settings and available engines.

Check the chat's engine before sending a message, and open **Generation details** in a Shape reply's ⋯ menu to see the engine and credits a reply used. See [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) for choosing an engine and [Why is a free engine costing credits?](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits) for unexpected credit use.

## Check the Balance

In a sponsored chat, open **More actions** (⋯) in the chat header and select **View sponsor details**. The banner shows the credits left in that chat.

If you can't find the chat's balance, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) with the chat name.

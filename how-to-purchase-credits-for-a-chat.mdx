## Purchase Steps

1. Sign in to [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and open the chat you want to fund.
2. Select **More actions** (⋯) in the chat header, then **Upgrade**. This opens the Premium page for that chat. If the chat is already sponsored, the menu shows **View sponsor details** instead.
3. Review the available credit options. If the purchase flow offers chat sponsorship, check that the right chat is selected.
4. Check the selected chat, credit amount, and price before confirming your purchase.
5. Follow the payment screen to complete checkout.

Credit options can change, and chat sponsorship may not be offered for every account. If you cannot find a way to fund a specific chat, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) before purchasing credits for a different destination.

## How Chat Credits Work

Credits assigned to a chat provide a shared balance for eligible premium usage in that chat. The engine used and the credits charged depend on the chat's current settings and available engines.

Check the chat's engine before sending a message, and open **Generation details** in a Shape reply's ⋯ menu to see the engine and credits a reply used. See [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) for choosing an engine and [Why is a free engine costing credits?](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits) for unexpected credit use.

## Check the Balance

Open the credit options on your Premium page (**Settings** → **Premium**) and look for balances assigned to chats. Select the relevant chat to review its available credits, where that option is offered.

If a completed purchase is missing or you cannot find the chat's balance, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) with the chat name and purchase receipt.

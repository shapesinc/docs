## Start With a Shared Goal

shapes.inc is built for people making decisions and doing things together. Create a chat for the goal, invite the people involved, and add a Shape to help compare options, draft a plan, or explain a difficult problem. For the big picture, start with [Multiplayer Assistants in Group Chats](/ai-group-chats).

Shapes are customizable AI assistants. Depending on the engine and available capabilities, they can:

- Respond to the group's messages and help summarize decisions.
- Generate images and work with images you send.
- Send [voice responses](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) or respond to voice messages.
- In [Agent chats](/chat-and-agent-modes), use available [skills](/shapeskills) for research and other actions, including apps you've [connected](/connected-apps).
- Set up [live polls, games, and forms](/multiplayer-activities) the whole chat can use.

Give the assistant the group's priorities, correct its assumptions, and review its suggestions together. In Agent chats, built-in skills are included automatically. Apps need to be connected first, and external services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server.

### Unique Customization

Choose the settings that affect your shared work:

1. Review the [memories](/memory) you have shared with a Shape.
2. Select an [AI engine](/aienginecheatsheet) suited to the task and your budget.
3. Customize a Shape's [profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) where you have permission.
4. [Create](/create-a-chat) and [style a chat](/chat-appearance) for your group.
5. Add [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) with the group's goal, constraints, and expectations.
6. Set your own preset, location, and Profiles in [Personalization](/personalization).

# Chats

Chats bring people and Shapes into the same conversation. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar, or tap **New** on a phone. Use **Shapes** and **People** to choose who to include, then select **Continue**. In **Chat details**, give the chat a name and select **Create Chat**. See the [walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat).

Each chat is either a **Chat**, for talking things through, or an **Agent** chat, where Shapes can take actions such as web searches and code. **Auto**, the default, chooses for each message. Set the default under **Settings** → **New chats**; see [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

## Adding Shapes

Once you have [created a chat](/create-a-chat), select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**. Choose **Participants** → **Add participants** → **Shapes**, select a Shape, and select **Add**. See [finding and adding an assistant](/how-to-talk-to-shapes) for more guidance.

## Inviting Your Friends

Select **Invite** at the top of a group chat to bring in the people whose preferences affect the decision. Use **Copy Link** or **Share via...**, or choose **Invite people on shapes.inc** to pick people directly. Ask them to add their requirements so the assistant can consider the whole group. See [group chat setup](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-group-chat) and [chat access](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join).

## Choose When Shapes Reply

Chat owners open the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. Under **AI replies**, review **Auto-reply triggers**:

- **Reply when mentioned**
- **Keep the convo going**
- **React to keywords**
- **Always have something to say**
- **Come back later**

For a task where the group wants to direct each contribution, start with **Reply when mentioned** and mention the Shape with `@`. If it stays quiet, check the selected engine and any credit requirement. If the chat is too busy, review the triggers. In group Agent chats, Shapes reply when they can help and stay out of ordinary conversation. See [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence) for suggested setups.

## Customizing Chat Aesthetics

Open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** (chat owners, or anyone in a DM). Make related projects easy to recognize and the conversation comfortable to read. See [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance).

## Skip Turn and Fast Forward

In **Chat** mode, use [+ → Skip turn](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) to let a shape reply without a new message, including in one-to-one chats. Group chats require the chat owner.

### AI Configuration

In the chat's **Settings**, owners choose **AI Configurations** for:

- **Chat mode:** switch the chat between **Auto**, **Chat**, and **Agent**.
- **Instructions:** record the group's goal, constraints, and working style. Owners can also open **Instructions** directly from **More actions**. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **AI replies:** choose **Auto-reply triggers** that fit the task.
- **Skills:** in Agent chats, built-in skills are included automatically and cannot be disabled. In other chats, each skill can be **Enabled** or **Disabled**.
- **MCP servers** (Agent chats): select **Add MCP server** to connect an external service and complete its authentication. See [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills).
- **Code Projects** (Agent chats): connect repositories for code tasks in this chat. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).

## Moderation

Open **Participants** in the chat's **Settings** to see who is involved. Chat owners can use **Moderation** for controls such as slow mode, blocked words, and a moderator Shape; see [Moderation](/moderation). Follow the [chat access guide](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join) to control who can join.

## Message Replying, Reacting, Regenerating, Editing

Use the actions in a message's ⋯ menu to reply, react, or make a correction. When a Shape's answer needs another attempt, anyone in the chat can choose **Regenerate**; credits come from the person who regenerates. See [Message Regeneration](/regeneration). **Generation details** shows which engine wrote a reply and the credits it used, and **Report a problem** lets you flag a bad reply; see [Feedback](/feedback). Available editing and deletion actions depend on the message and your permissions.

## Find Earlier Work

Use **More actions** → **Search Messages** to find an earlier detail. The chat's **Settings** also has message search, **Pinned Messages**, and **Media & Files** to help you revisit the group's saved context and shared material. **Mentions** is available in **More actions**. Chats you haven't used for a while move to [Completed](/completed).

# Shape Settings

Owners edit their Shapes under **Settings** → **My Shapes**. Select a Shape to open its editor, which has **Profile**, **Personality**, **AI Engine**, **Memory**, **Knowledge**, **Training**, **Voice**, **Images**, **Stats**, and **Manage**. Select **Save** to keep your changes; the editor keeps your draft if you leave before saving.

To review what a Shape remembers about you or a chat, open **More actions** → **Manage memories** in the chat, or **Settings** → **Manage memories**. Use [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) to explain what your group needs in this conversation.

## Controls for Shape Owners

Give the Shape a clear role that considers everyone in the chat. Use its settings to support that role, then try a small task and review the result together.

### Profile

Use **Profile** to introduce the assistant through its name, image, and description. See [editing a Shape's profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) and [profile styling](/css).

### Stats

Use **Stats** to review activity. Usage helps you understand how often people interact with the Shape; review its actual responses to judge whether it is useful.

### Personality

Use **Personality** to explain how the Shape should behave. Give it a role, the kind of help it should offer, and examples of how to handle uncertainty or disagreement. **Response style** sets the length and format of every reply, with options such as **Human**, **Short creative**, **Long creative**, and **Custom**. See [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality) and [presets](/presets) for focused guidance.

### Knowledge

Use **Knowledge** for reference material the assistant needs for its role:

- **Shared context:** a project brief, household requirements, or a study syllabus.
- **Group conventions:** how the group makes decisions and what a useful response looks like.
- **People and roles:** responsibilities or preferences people have chosen to share.

Update this material when circumstances change. Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect remembered information from conversations.

### Training

Use **Training** for examples of useful responses. For instance, show how to ask for a missing preference or explain a tradeoff. Examples guide the assistant; they do not force exact wording or guarantee that it will handle every similar situation correctly.

<span id="image-engine" />

### Images

Use **Images** to choose the Shape's image engine, style, and size. For group work, agree on the audience, style, composition, and required details before asking for an image. Review the result against the brief and check any credit requirement. See [creating images together](/articles/best-ai-image-generators-2026).

<span id="voice-engine" />

### Voice

Use **Voice** to pick a pre-made voice or use your own, and to set how often the Shape replies with voice. Follow [Give Your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) for setup and [Receive Voice Messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) for chat controls.

<span id="settings" />

### Manage

Use **Manage** for owner controls such as **Visibility & safety**, **Duplicate**, and **Delete shape**. Public Shape discovery in search is retired, but direct links to a Shape still work. See the focused guides for [visibility](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-shape-private), [the Shape's URL](/faq/how-do-i-change-my-shapes-vanity-url), and [deleting a Shape](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape).

## AI Engine

Use **AI Engine** in the Shape editor to set its **Main engine**, **Premium engine**, and **Fallback engine**. **Reasoning effort** appears when the Shape's engine supports reasoning and **Show reasoning** is on, and it lists only the levels that engine supports. The engine built by shapes.inc appears as **Formless**. Choose based on the work the group needs, required capabilities, and available [credits](/credits). Higher reasoning effort can take longer and use more credits. See [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet) and [presets](/presets) for more detail.

### Advanced Engine Parameters

Available controls vary by engine. Change one setting at a time and compare responses to the same brief. Keep a copy of settings that work well so you can return to them.

### Timezone

Under **Language & time**, owners can turn on **Knows the current date and time** and set a **Timezone**. For plans involving dates or times, still give the assistant the relevant timezone explicitly and check its interpretation before acting on a schedule.

### Short-Term Memory (STM)

The editor's **Memory** section controls **Short-term memory**, **Long-term memory**, and related recall settings. Recent messages provide context for a response, subject to the engine's [context window](/contextwindows). If an older constraint is missing, restate the current brief instead of assuming every previous message is still available.

### Long-Term Memory

Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect and correct saved information. Memory can help retain useful context, but it can be incomplete or out of date. Keep important decisions visible in the chat as well.

### Training Settings

When adjusting training material, prefer a small set of accurate examples that demonstrate the behavior you want. Remove examples that conflict with the Shape's current role.

### Knowledge Settings

Keep reference material focused and current. Separate known facts from assumptions, and tell the assistant when a newer brief replaces an older version.

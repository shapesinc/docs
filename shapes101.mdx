## Start With a Shared Goal

shapes.inc is built for people making decisions and doing things together. Create a chat for the goal, invite the people involved, and add a Shape to help compare options, draft a plan, or explain a difficult problem. For the big picture, start with [Multiplayer Assistants in Group Chats](/ai-group-chats).

Shapes are customizable AI assistants. Depending on the engine and available capabilities, they can:

- Respond to the group's messages and help summarize decisions.
- Generate images and work with images you send.
- Send [voice responses](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) or respond to voice messages.
- Use available [skills](/shapeskills) for research and other actions.

Give the assistant the group's priorities, correct its assumptions, and review its suggestions together. Built-in skills are included automatically. External services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server.

### Unique Customization

Choose the settings that affect your shared work:

1. Review the [memories](/memory) you have shared with a Shape.
2. Select an [AI engine](/aienginecheatsheet) suited to the task and your budget.
3. Customize a Shape's [profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) where you have permission.
4. [Create](/create-a-chat) and [style a chat](/chat-appearance) for your group.
5. Add [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) with the group's goal, constraints, and expectations.

# Chats

Chats bring people and Shapes into the same conversation. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar. Use **AI** and **People** to choose who to include, then select **Continue**. In **Chat details**, give the chat a name and select **Create Chat**. See the [walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat).

## Adding Shapes

Once you have [created a chat](/create-a-chat), open **More actions** and choose the **Members** entry or **Invite**. The Members entry includes a count, such as **Members (2)**. Choose **Add participants** → **AI** to find a Shape. See [finding and adding an assistant](/how-to-talk-to-shapes) for more guidance.

## Inviting Your Friends

Use **More actions** → **Invite** to bring in the people whose preferences affect the decision. Ask them to add their requirements so the assistant can consider the whole group. See [group chat setup](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-group-chat) and [chat access](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join).

## Choose When Shapes Reply

Open **More actions** → **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. Under **AI replies**, review **Auto-reply triggers**:

- **Reply when mentioned**
- **Keep the convo going**
- **React to keywords**
- **Always have something to say**
- **Come back later**

For a task where the group wants to direct each contribution, start with **Reply when mentioned** and mention the Shape with `@`. If it stays quiet, check the selected engine and any credit requirement. If the chat is too busy, review the triggers. See [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence) for suggested setups.

## Customizing Chat Aesthetics

Open **More actions** → **Settings** → **Chat Appearance**. Make related projects easy to recognize and the conversation comfortable to read. See [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance).

## Skip Turn and Fast Forward

Use [Skip turn and fast forward](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) when you want Shapes to continue without typing another message. Stop once the group has enough material to review.

### AI Configuration

**More actions** → **Settings** opens **Chat Menu**. Choose **AI Configurations** for:

- **Instructions:** record the group's goal, constraints, and working style. You can also open **Instructions** directly from **More actions**. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **AI replies:** choose **Auto-reply triggers** that fit the task.
- **Skills:** built-in skills are automatically included and cannot be disabled.
- **MCP servers:** select **Add MCP server** to connect an external service and complete its authentication. See [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills).

## Moderation

Review **Participants** in **Chat Menu** to see who is involved. Follow the [chat access guide](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join) to control who can join. Management controls depend on your role and permissions.

## Message Replying, Reacting, Regenerating, Editing

Use the actions available on a message to respond to it or make a correction. When a Shape's answer needs another attempt, identify the issue and follow [Message Regeneration](/regeneration). Available editing and deletion actions depend on the message and your permissions.

# Shape Settings

Open the Shape's page and its sidebar. Owners and co-owners can manage it under **Shape Settings**. Available controls depend on your permissions.

## Controls for Shape Owners

Give the Shape a clear role that considers everyone in the chat. Use its settings to support that role, then try a small task and review the result together.

### Profile

Use **Profile** to introduce the assistant through its name, image, and description. See [editing a Shape's profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) and [profile styling](/css).

### Stats

Use **Stats** to review activity. Usage helps you understand how often people interact with the Shape; review its actual responses to judge whether it is useful.

### Personality

Use **Personality** to explain how the Shape should behave. Give it a role, the kind of help it should offer, and examples of how to handle uncertainty or disagreement. See [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality) and [presets](/presets) for focused guidance.

### Knowledge

Use **Knowledge** for reference material the assistant needs for its role:

- **Shared context:** a project brief, household requirements, or a study syllabus.
- **Group conventions:** how the group makes decisions and what a useful response looks like.
- **People and roles:** responsibilities or preferences people have chosen to share.

Update this material when circumstances change. Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect remembered information from conversations.

### Training

Use **Training** for examples of useful responses. For instance, show how to ask for a missing preference or explain a tradeoff. Examples guide the assistant; they do not force exact wording or guarantee that it will handle every similar situation correctly.

### Image Engine

Use **Image Engine** on the Shape's page to review available image settings. For group work, agree on the audience, style, composition, and required details before asking for an image. Review the result against the brief and check any credit requirement. See [creating images together](/articles/best-ai-image-generators-2026).

### Voice Engine

Use **Voice Engine** on the Shape's page to set up its voice. Follow [Give Your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) for setup and [Receive Voice Messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) for chat controls.

### Settings

Use **Settings** on the Shape's page for management controls available to its owners. See the focused guides for [discoverability](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-shape-private), [the Shape's URL](/faq/how-do-i-change-my-shapes-vanity-url), and [deleting a Shape](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape).

## AI Engine

Use **AI Engine** on the Shape's page to review its engine settings. Choose based on the work the group needs, required capabilities, and available [credits](/credits). See [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet) and [presets](/presets) for more detail.

### Advanced Engine Parameters

Available controls vary by engine. Change one setting at a time and compare responses to the same brief. Keep a copy of settings that work well so you can return to them.

### Timezone

For plans involving dates or times, give the assistant the relevant timezone explicitly and check its interpretation before acting on a schedule.

### Short-Term Memory (STM)

Recent messages provide context for a response, subject to the engine's [context window](/contextwindows). If an older constraint is missing, restate the current brief instead of assuming every previous message is still available.

### Long-Term Memory

Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect and correct saved information. Memory can help retain useful context, but it can be incomplete or out of date. Keep important decisions visible in the chat as well.

### Training Settings

When adjusting training material, prefer a small set of accurate examples that demonstrate the behavior you want. Remove examples that conflict with the Shape's current role.

### Knowledge Settings

Keep reference material focused and current. Separate known facts from assumptions, and tell the assistant when a newer brief replaces an older version.

## Start With a Shared Goal

Shapes, Inc. is built for people making decisions and doing things together. Create a room for the goal, invite the people involved, and add a Shape to help compare options, draft a plan, or explain a difficult problem. For the big picture, start with [AI Group Chats](/ai-group-chats).

Shapes are customizable AI assistants. Depending on their engine and settings, they can:

- Respond to the group's messages and help summarize decisions.
- Generate images and work with images you send.
- Send [voice responses](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) and transcripts, or respond to voice messages.
- Use available [skills](/shapeskills) for research and other actions.

Give the assistant the group's priorities, correct its assumptions, and review its suggestions together. Outside services require a configured skill and the relevant access.

### **Unique Customization**

Choose the settings that affect your shared work:

1. Save a persona with your name and relevant preferences.
2. View and edit the [memories](/memory) you have shared with a Shape.
3. Select an [AI engine](/aienginecheatsheet) suited to the task and your budget.
4. Customize a Shape's [profile](/css) where you have permission.
5. [Create](/create-a-chat) and [style the room](/chat-appearance) for your group.
6. Set [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) with the group's goal, constraints, and expectations.

# Chats

Rooms bring people and Shapes together around the same conversation. The app calls them **chats**. To create one, select **New** on mobile or **New Chat** on a larger screen, choose Shapes and people, select **Continue**, then **Create Chat**. See the [screenshot walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat).

The home area can also show public rooms under **Join a public chat**. Check a public room's purpose and participants before sharing information there.

## Adding Shapes

Once you have [created a room](/create-a-chat), open **More actions** → **Members / Invite** → **Add participants**, then select **Shapes**. From there, you can:

- Search for new Shapes
- Browse Shapes you’ve created
- Pick from your recently used Shapes
- Or type in a Shape’s username if you already know it

## Inviting Your Friends

Open **More actions** → **Members / Invite**. Use **Invite friends** to copy or share an invitation link. Use **Add participants** → **People** to find someone who already uses Shapes, Inc.

Invite the people whose preferences affect the decision. Ask them to add their requirements so the assistant can consider the whole group.

## Activating Shapes

Activation selects Shapes to respond without needing a mention. You can activate them in two ways:

1. Go to the **Participants** section and click the green **Activate** button.
2. Or type `/activate @shape-name` to activate a Shape right away.

You can have up to **five** Shapes activated at a time. To deactivate a Shape, either select **Deactivate** in the Participants menu or type `/deactivate @shape-name`. Response behavior also depends on the room's settings and engine availability; activation does not guarantee a successful response.

If the room is too busy, reduce the active set and review [Free Will settings](/designing-social-intelligence). If a Shape stays quiet, try mentioning it with `@` and check its engine and credit settings.

## Customizing Chat Aesthetics

Use room appearance settings to make related projects easy to recognize and the conversation comfortable to read. See [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance) for the controls.

## Skip Turn and Fast Forward

The **Skip turn and fast forward** option lets you get responses from Shapes without typing a new message first. Open the **+** menu beside the message box and choose **Skip turn and fast forward**.

You can:

- Choose the next Shape that should reply
- Select **5 messages** or **10 messages** to make Shapes continue automatically
- Stop fast forward while it is running

### AI Configuration

In this section, found in the Chat settings menu, you can fine-tune how Shapes behave in your Chat:

- **Custom Instructions**: Give Shapes the group's goal, constraints, and working style. For example, ask them to compare everyone's priorities and flag missing information before proposing a plan. See [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **Credit Preferences**: Choose whether to use your credits for premium engines in that chat when the option is available.

## Moderation

You’ll find moderation tools in your **Chat Settings** menu. These tools help you keep your Chat safe and running smoothly. From here, you can:

- Allow or prevent participants from adding Shapes
- Set a message character limit
- Adjust **Slow Mode** to control how often participants can send messages
- Add blocked words to automatically filter unwanted content
- Unban users you’ve previously banned

To ban a user, click the three-dot menu next to any of their messages and select **Ban**, or ban them directly from the **Participants** list in the Chat Settings menu.

## Message Replying, Reacting, Regenerating, Editing

Shapes can reply to messages, react, and participate in conversations depending on the chat and Shape settings.

You can manage messages by clicking the three-dot menu next to them:

- **Regenerate** a Shape’s message to get a new response
- **Edit** a Shape’s message (if the creator has allowed editing)
- **Delete** a Shape’s message
- **Edit or delete your own messages** at any time

## Favorite a Chat

You can mark any Chat as a favorite to keep it easy to find. Open your side navigation, click the three-dot menu next to the Chat’s name, and select **Favorite**. Favorited Chats will appear in a special section above your regular Chat list.

# Creator's Dashboard

Open the [creator dashboard](https://shapes.inc/dashboard) to manage Shapes you own or co-own. The sections below describe their configuration controls.

If you are using someone else's Shape, you can still review the available [memory](/memory) controls, apply a persona from your profile, and use an engine override when permitted. Available controls depend on the Shape and room settings.

## Shape Owner Tools

Shape owners (the people who created the Shape and anyone they have trusted as co-owners) can configure it in the Creator Dashboard. Ownership controls who can edit the assistant. Give its instructions a role that considers everyone in the room.

### Profile

Style and customize your Shape’s profile to make it stand out. You can:

- Add an avatar and banner
- Use custom HTML & [CSS](css) for a unique look
- Write a description and add tags to help users discover your Shape
- Toggle whether your Shape can be searched or keep it private for yourself

### Stats

Track your Shape’s activity over time. You can see:

- Daily and lifetime messages
- Daily new unique users
- Lifetime total users

Use these statistics to understand how often people use the Shape. Review actual responses to judge whether it is helping the group.

### Personality

Fine-tune how your Shape behaves and presents itself. In this section, you can:

- Edit the Shape’s backstory, display name, and personality details
- Adjust the [**preset**](presets) (the instructions that shape how it writes responses)
- Decide whether line breaks should send as multiple messages
- Allow or prevent the Shape from sending multiple messages in a row
- Set the Shape’s initial message when someone first chats with it
- Describe your Shape’s appearance (used for image generation and when users ask what they look like)
- Add custom responses for `/wack` and `/sleep` commands

### **Knowledge**

Use knowledge for reference material the assistant needs for its role:

- **Shared context:** a project brief, household requirements, or a study syllabus.
- **Instructions:** explanations of group conventions and useful responses to common questions.
- **People and roles:** who is involved and the responsibilities or preferences they have chosen to share.

Update this material when circumstances change. Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect remembered information from conversations.

### Training

Use the **Training** tab to add conversation snippets that guide responses in specific situations. For example, show how to ask for a missing preference or explain a tradeoff. Examples guide responses; they do not force exact wording or guarantee that the Shape will handle every similar situation correctly.

### Image Engine

This tab controls all settings related to the `/imagine` command. Here, you can:

- **Select an image engine** — free and premium options are available
- **Choose an image preset** — this defines what information the Shape uses when generating images
- **Pick a size** — square, portrait, or landscape, with multiple size options available
- Decide whether your Shape should **always use the same size** or choose dynamically

### Voice Engine

On the **Voice Engine** page, you can:

- Choose a preselected voice **or upload your own audio sample** to give your Shape a unique voice
- Toggle whether the Shape sends a **text transcript** along with voice messages
- Adjust **how frequently** the Shape sends voice responses

For custom voices, you can fine-tune:

- Whether the voice stays consistent
- How closely it matches the original sample
- How much style or personality should be applied

You can also add **custom instructions** for when the Shape generates a voice message and control how those voice messages are stored in memory. Read more about giving your Shape a voice [here](how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice). To learn how to receive a voice message, read [here](how-to-receive-voice-messages).

### Settings

The **Settings** page lets you manage key controls for your Shape. Here, you can:

- **Manage owners** — choose who can help manage and edit the Shape
- **Toggle sensitive content** — decide whether the Shape can generate mature or sensitive responses
- **Control discoverability** — choose if the Shape appears in search results and whether users can edit its messages
- **Set an ignore list** — block certain users from interacting with the Shape
- **Duplicate the Shape** — quickly create a copy with all the same settings
- **Delete the Shape** — permanently remove it from your account (found in the **Danger Zone**)

## **AI Engine**

This is one of the most important tabs on your shape's dashboard. On this tab, you can:

- Update your shape's:
  - Primary model
  - Fallback model
- Enable/disable internal monologging
- Toggle options like:
  - Inform User When Fallback Engine is Used
  - Inform Users When They Are Out of Shape Engine Credits
  - Let Users Override AI Engine
- Select the general language your shape will use by choosing a **Language Preset**
- Write a **Base Preset** for your shape
  - Presets guide the Shape's response style and behavior

#### **Advanced Engine Parameters**

You can also edit:

- Temperature
- Top P
- Repetition Penalty
- Top K
- Frequency Penalty
- Presence Penalty
- Min P
- Top A

<Warning>
  **!! It is not suggested to edit these settings unless you know what you're doing.**
</Warning>

#### **Timezone**

You can define a timezone for your shape here.

#### **Short-Term Memory (STM)**

Enable STM and manage its frequency:

- STM is the number of recent messages (user and shape) the shape will take into context.

#### **Long-Term Memory**

Enable or disable long-term memory and automatic memory generation. You can also:

- Edit generation frequency
- Select a model to generate long-term memories
- Set instructions for the model
- Configure memory recall settings:
  - **Memory Relevance**:
    - Lower values (0.1) recall more memories but may be less relevant
    - Higher values (1.0) recall fewer but more relevant memories

#### **Training Settings**

- Jump to the training tab using a button
- Adjust **Training Context Size**: number of relevant training examples to include in conversations
- Adjust **Training Relevance**:
  - Lower values recall more examples but may be less relevant
  - Higher values recall fewer but more matched examples

#### **Knowledge Settings**

- Jump to the knowledge tab using a button
- Adjust **Knowledge Context Size**: number of relevant knowledge entries to include in conversations
- Adjust **Knowledge Relevance**:
  - Lower values recall more entries but may be less relevant
  - Higher values recall fewer but more relevant entries

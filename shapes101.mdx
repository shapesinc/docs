<span id="start-with-a-shared-goal" />

shapes.inc is built for people making decisions and doing things together. Create a chat for the goal and invite the people involved. You do not need to create or choose a shape first; the picker offers **Auto**. [AI group chats](/ai-group-chats) gives the overview.

Shapes are customizable AI assistants. Depending on the engine and available capabilities, they can:

- Respond to the group's messages and summarize decisions.
- Generate images and work with images you send.
- Send [voice responses](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) or respond to voice messages.
- In [any chat](/chat-and-agent-modes), use available [skills](/shapeskills) for research and other actions, including apps you've [connected](/connected-apps).
- Set up [live polls, games, and forms](/multiplayer-activities) the whole chat can use.

Give the assistant the group's priorities, correct its assumptions, and review its suggestions together. Built-in skills are included automatically. Apps need to be connected first, and external services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server.

<a id="unique-customization" />

## What you can customize

Choose the settings that affect your shared work:

1. Review the [memories](/memory) you have shared with a shape.
2. Select an [AI engine](/aienginecheatsheet) suited to the task and your budget.
3. Customize a shape's [profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) where you have permission.
4. [Create](/create-a-chat) and [style a chat](/chat-appearance) for your group.
5. Add [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) with the group's goal, constraints, and expectations.
6. Set your own preset, location, and Profiles in [Personalization](/personalization).

## Chats

Chats bring people and shapes into the same conversation. Select **New chat** in the sidebar, or tap **New** on a phone. The picker opens on **People**. Select people to include, or open **Shapes** to choose **Auto** or an existing shape, then select **Continue**. In **Chat details**, review the name and select **Create Chat**. See the [walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat).

In every chat, shapes can search the web, run code, use connected apps, and take other actions. When you play a scene with a shape, it answers that message in character. There's nothing to pick or switch. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

<span id="adding-shapes" />

### Add shapes

Once you have [created a chat](/create-a-chat), select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**. Choose **Participants** → **Add participants** → **Shapes**, select a shape, and select **Add**. See [finding and adding an assistant](/how-to-talk-to-shapes) for more guidance.

<span id="inviting-your-friends" />

### Invite your friends

Select **Invite** at the top of a group chat to bring in the people whose preferences affect the decision. In **Invite friends**, select **Share** to send the link from your device's share sheet, or tap the link to copy it. On a phone, iPad, or Mac, enter a **Phone number** with its country code and select **Text** to send the invite from Messages. Choose **Invite people on shapes.inc** to pick people directly. Ask them to add their requirements so the assistant can consider the whole group. See [group chat setup](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) and [chat access](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join).

<span id="activating-shapes" />

### Choose when shapes reply

In group chats, shapes reply when they can help and stay out of ordinary conversation. To direct a contribution, mention the shape with `@` or say its name. If it stays quiet, check the selected engine and any credit requirement. Chat owners can review **Come back later** under the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **AI replies**. See [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence) for suggested setups.

<span id="customizing-chat-aesthetics" />

### Customize chat aesthetics

Open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** (chat owners, or anyone in a DM). Make related projects easy to recognize and the conversation comfortable to read. See [chat appearance](/chat-appearance).

### Skip turn and fast forward

**Skip turn** has been removed. To have shapes keep a conversation going without a new message from you, ask: "let the shapes keep talking". See [Skip turn and fast forward](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn).

### AI configuration

In the chat's **Settings**, owners choose **AI settings** for:

- **Instructions:** record the group's goal, constraints, and working style. Owners can also open **Instructions** directly from **More actions**. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **AI replies:** choose **Auto-reply triggers** that fit the task.
- **Skills:** built-in skills are included automatically and cannot be disabled.
- **MCP servers:** select **Add MCP server** to connect an external service and complete its authentication. See [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills).
- **Code Projects:** connect repositories for code tasks in this chat. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).

### Moderation

Open **Participants** in the chat's **Settings** to see who is involved. Chat owners can use **Moderation** for controls such as slow mode, blocked words, and a moderator shape; see [Moderation](/moderation). Follow the [chat access guide](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join) to control who can join.

<span id="message-replying-reacting-regenerating-editing" />

### Reply, react, regenerate, and edit messages

Use the actions in a message's ⋯ menu to reply, react, or make a correction. When a shape's answer needs another attempt, anyone in the chat can choose **Regenerate**; credits come from the person who regenerates. See [Message regeneration](/regeneration). **Generation details** shows which engine wrote a reply and the credits it used, and **Report a problem** flags a bad reply; see [Feedback](/feedback). Available editing and deletion actions depend on the message and your permissions.

<span id="favorite-a-chat" />

### Find earlier work

Use **More actions** → **Search messages** to find an earlier detail. The chat's **Settings** also has message search, **Pinned Messages**, and **Media & Files** for the group's saved context and shared material. **Mentions** is available in **More actions**. Chats you haven't used for a while move to [Archive](/archive).

## Shape settings

Owners edit their shapes under **Settings** → **Your shapes**. Select a shape to open its editor, which has **Profile**, **Knowledge**, **Training**, **Memory**, **AI Engine**, **Image Engine**, **Voice Engine**, **Share**, and **Advanced**. Personality controls are in **Profile**; short-term memory is in **AI Engine**, and long-term memory has its own **Memory** section. Select **Save** to keep your changes.

To review what a shape remembers about you or a chat, open **More actions** → **Manage memories** in the chat, or **Settings** → **Memories**. Use [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) to explain what your group needs in this conversation.

<span id="shape-owner-tools" />

### Controls for shape owners

Give the shape a clear role that considers everyone in the chat. Use its settings to support that role, then try a small task and review the result together.

#### Profile

Use **Profile** to introduce the assistant through its name, image, and description. See [editing a shape's profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) and [profile styling](/css).

<span id="stats" />

#### Personality

Use **Profile** to explain how the shape should behave. Give it a role, the kind of help it should offer, and examples of how to handle uncertainty or disagreement. **Response style** sets the length and format of every reply, with options such as **Human**, **Short creative**, **Long creative**, and **Custom**. See [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality) and [presets](/presets) for focused guidance.

#### Knowledge

Use **Knowledge** for reference material the assistant needs for its role:

- **Shared context:** a project brief, household requirements, or a study syllabus.
- **Group conventions:** how the group makes decisions and what a useful response looks like.
- **People and roles:** responsibilities or preferences people have chosen to share.

Knowledge belongs to one shape, and in a shared chat each shape recalls only its own entries. To give two shapes the same facts, add them to both or save a [chat note](/memory), which every shape in the chat reads.

Update this material when circumstances change. Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect remembered information from conversations.

#### Training

Use **Training** for examples of useful responses. For instance, show how to ask for a missing preference or explain a tradeoff. Examples guide the assistant; they do not force exact wording or guarantee that it will handle every similar situation correctly.

<span id="image-engine" />

#### Images

Use **Image Engine** to choose the shape's image engine, preset, and size. For group work, agree on the audience, style, composition, and required details before asking for an image. Review the result against the brief and check any credit requirement. See [creating images together](/plan-and-build-together#make-documents-images-and-slides-together).

<span id="voice-engine" />

#### Voice

Use **Voice Engine** to pick a pre-made voice or use your own, and to set how often the shape replies with voice. Follow [Give your shape a voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) for setup and [Receive voice messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) for chat controls.

<span id="settings" />

#### Manage

Use the editor's **Advanced** section for owner controls, **Privacy**, **Duplicate**, and **Danger zone**. **Share** controls the description and appearance of the shape's direct page. Public catalogs are gone and aren't coming back, but direct shape links still work. See the focused guides for [visibility](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-shape-private), [the shape's URL](/faq/how-do-i-change-my-shapes-vanity-url), and [deleting a shape](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape).

### AI Engine

Use **AI Engine** in the shape editor to set its **AI Engine**. There is no separate fallback setting: if the engine can't reply, every shape recovers on the same free engine (see [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet#fallback-engine)). **Reasoning effort** appears when the shape's engine supports reasoning and **Show internal monologue** is on, and it lists only the levels that engine supports. The engine built by shapes.inc appears as **Formless**. Choose based on the work the group needs, required capabilities, and available [credits](/credits). Higher reasoning effort can take longer and use more credits. These controls do not restore the [former thinking panel](/faq/where-did-the-thinking-panel-go). See [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet) and [presets](/presets) for more detail.

#### Advanced engine parameters

Available controls vary by engine. Change one setting at a time and compare responses to the same brief. Keep a copy of settings that work well so you can return to them.

#### Timezone

Shapes no longer have their own **Timezone** or **Knows the current date** settings, and the editor has no **Time** card. In chats, shapes use your own time, so "9am tomorrow" means 9am where you are. When a plan involves people in other places, name the time zone and check the shape's reading before acting on a schedule.

#### Short-term memory (STM)

The editor's **AI Engine** section includes **Short-term memory**. The separate **Memory** section holds long-term memory and its recall settings. Recent messages provide context for a response, subject to the engine's [context window](/contextwindows). If an older constraint is missing, restate the current brief instead of assuming every previous message is still available.

#### Long-term memory

Use [memory controls](/memory) to inspect and correct saved information. Memory retains useful context, but it can be incomplete or out of date. Keep important decisions visible in the chat as well.

#### Training settings

When adjusting training material, prefer a small set of accurate examples that demonstrate the behavior you want. Remove examples that conflict with the shape's current role.

#### Knowledge settings

Keep reference material focused and current. Separate known facts from assumptions, and tell the assistant when a newer brief replaces an older version.

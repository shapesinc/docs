- **Missing or incorrect context:** [review how memory works](/memory) and [set shared instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **An engine is unavailable:** [understand the Unstable label](/faq/what-does-it-mean-if-an-engine-is-labelled-unstable).

- **Only monthly plans are available:** [buying credits without a subscription](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription) and [keeping credits after cancelling](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium).
- **Getting the most credits:** [subscribe on the web](/faq/why-do-web-plans-include-more-credits).
- **Edit or thinking text is missing:** [editing shape replies](/faq/why-cant-i-edit-a-shapes-reply) and [the former thinking panel](/faq/where-did-the-thinking-panel-go).
- **Want to export or back up your data:** [current limitations and third-party tool risks](/faq/can-i-export-or-back-up-my-data).
- **Reddit is missing from Connected apps:** [why Reddit can't be connected](/faq/why-cant-i-connect-reddit).
- **Fewer engines:** [which engines chats use and which are free](/faq/why-do-i-see-fewer-engines-in-agent-chats).
- **Texting only reaches Tod:** [text another shape](/faq/can-i-text-a-shape-other-than-tod).
- **Tod is in your DMs:** [why, and how to keep it quiet](/faq/can-i-remove-tod-from-my-dms).

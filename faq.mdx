Get your group back to what you were working on. These guides explain the controls behind your multiplayer assistant and help resolve common problems.

## Start here when something gets in the way

- **Can't find something after the October update:** see [what's new and where everything moved](/whats-new).
- **A chat disappeared from your list:** [find chats that moved to Completed](/faq/where-did-my-chat-go).
- **Can't sign in:** [create or recover your account](/make-or-recover-account).
- **Need to bring people together:** [start a chat](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) or [invite participants](/create-a-chat).
- **Too many replies:** [adjust multiple messages and AI replies](/multimessages), or [see why a Shape started a chat with you](/faq/why-did-a-shape-create-a-chat-with-me).
- **Missing or incorrect context:** [review how memory works](/memory) and [set shared instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
- **An engine is unavailable:** [understand the Unstable label](/faq/what-does-it-mean-if-an-engine-is-labelled-unstable).
- **Unexpected charges:** [check credit usage](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits) and [auto top-up](/faq/why-did-i-get-charged-automatically-for-more-credits).

Use a chat to organize a fan project, plan a community event, or write a story together. Give the Shape a useful role and shared context, then keep the people involved in the decisions.

1. **Pick a Fandom**\
   Choose a fandom your group is passionate about or one that’s currently trending.
   - Trending fandoms can be discovered on platforms like Tumblr, Ao3, Google Trends, Netflix, Amazon Prime, HBO Max, Crunchyroll, and more.
2. **Create a New Chat**\
   Start a fresh chat for your fandom and invite the people working on it. If you are new to the format, read [AI Group Chats](/ai-group-chats) and [create a chat](/create-a-chat) first.
3. **Add Shapes**\
   Bring in Shapes that fit your project:
   - Select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Participants** → **Add participants** → **Shapes**. The list shows Shapes you own and Shapes you've messaged recently.
   - Public Shape search is no longer available. To use someone else's Shape, open its direct link and message it; Shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days appear in the list. You can also browse Featured Shapes at [shapes.inc/featured](https://shapes.inc/featured).
   - Test the Shape out and make sure it responds the way you want. If it isn't a good fit, try another.
   - If an important character doesn't have a good Shape, create one with **Create shape** in **Participants**, or from **Settings** → **My Shapes** → **New shape**.
4. **Adjust the Chat Appearance**\
   Customize your chat to match the fandom's style:
   - Set a relevant **Chat Avatar**, **Chat Banner**, and **Chat Background**.
   - Adjust **Bubble Colors**, add a **Chat Description**, and try a **Custom Font (Google Fonts)**.
   - Learn more here: [Chat Appearance Guide](/chat-appearance).
5. **Configure AI Settings**\
   Give Shapes the context they need in the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations**:
   - Add the project's goal, canon details, and shared rules under **Instructions**. See [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
   - Use **AI replies** to decide when Shapes jump in on their own. See [Multiple Messages](/multimessages).

A shape sends messages without being asked directly when a chat's **AI replies** settings allow it. In a group, this lets a shape add something useful at the right moment. When it's too much, turn triggers off.

## AI replies

Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**, then choose **AI settings** → **AI replies**. Chat owners can change these settings. In a DM with a shape, you can change them yourself.

Under **Auto-reply triggers**, you can turn on or off:

- **Free will (experimental)**: Use the conversation to decide whether the AI should reply or stay quiet. Your existing controls still apply.
- **Come back later**: The AI can check back in later if the chat goes quiet.

In group chats, **Free will (experimental)** is always on: shapes reply when they can help and stay out of ordinary conversation. In a [DM between two people](/chat-and-agent-modes#dms-between-people), Tod never follows up on its own. An @mention of a shape always gets a reply.

<span id="group-agent-chats" />

The other triggers (**Reply when mentioned**, **Keep the convo going**, **React to keywords**, and **Always have something to say**) appeared only in group chats set to Chat mode. Chats no longer have modes, so they no longer appear. See [the October 2026 update](/october-2026-update#what-was-retired).

### Fewer extra messages

- Turn off **Come back later** to stop check-ins when the chat is quiet.
- Tell the shape to be quiet. After "shut up" or "leave me alone", in any language, **Come back later** check-ins stop until a person talks to the shape again. Saying it as a joke or as part of a story doesn't count.
- [Archive the chat](/archive) to pause automatic follow-ups until you unarchive it.

If a shape's replies are too long or too short, its owner can open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, choose the shape, then use **Profile** → **Response style**.

### Can I stop shapes from @-mentioning people?

There's no setting for this. Shapes are told not to @-mention by default, and to do it only when addressing someone directly or telling two people apart. Add a line like "don't @-mention anyone" to the chat's Instructions, or set the chat's notifications to **Nothing** to stop the alerts.

### How often does a shape check in when a chat goes quiet?

With **Come back later** on, a shape can check in once, at a random time 6 to 12 hours after the last person's message, and won't again until someone posts. It doesn't check in after someone has told it to be quiet. You can't change the timing, but you can ask a shape for a recurring reminder.

## Keep the conversation going

**Keep going** has the shapes in a chat take turns for 5 or 10 messages without anyone writing, or gets one reply from a shape you pick. It replaced **Skip turn** and fast forward. In group chats, only chat owners see it.

1. Select **+** next to the message box, then **Keep going**.
2. Under **Number of turns**, pick **5 messages** or **10 messages**.
3. With more than one shape in the chat, pick who goes first under **Who starts**.

To hear from one shape once, pick it under **Reply once** instead of a number of turns.

A bar above the message box shows progress, for example "2 of 5 messages". Select **Stop** on the bar, or **+** → **Stop fast forward**, to end the run early.

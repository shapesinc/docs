Shapes can send messages without being asked directly when a chat's **AI replies** settings allow it. In a group, this helps a Shape add something useful at the right moment; when it's too much, turn the triggers down.

## AI replies

Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**, then choose **AI Configurations** → **AI replies**. Chat owners can change these settings. In a DM with a Shape, you can change them yourself.

Under **Auto-reply triggers**, depending on the chat, you can turn on or off:

- **Reply when mentioned**: the AI replies whenever you @mention it.
- **Keep the convo going**: after replying, the AI keeps chatting without being asked again. This is the usual reason for several messages in a row.
- **React to keywords**: the AI jumps in when someone says one of this chat's trigger words.
- **Always have something to say**: if nobody else replies, the AI makes sure you're not left on read.
- **Come back later**: the AI can check back in later if the chat goes quiet.
- **Jev (experimental)**: uses the conversation to decide whether the AI should reply or stay quiet. Your other triggers still apply.

DMs with a Shape and group Agent chats show fewer triggers. **Come back later** is available in every chat.

### Group Agent chats

In Agent chats with friends, Jev is always on: Shapes reply when they can help and stay out of ordinary conversation. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

### Fewer extra messages

- Turn off **Keep the convo going** and **Always have something to say**.
- Turn off **Come back later** to stop check-ins when the chat is quiet.
- [Mark the chat completed](/completed) to pause automatic follow-ups until you reopen it.

If a Shape's replies are too long or too short, its owner can change its **Response style** under **Settings** → **My Shapes**.

## Skip Turn and Fast Forward

In **Chat** mode, open **+** → **Skip turn** to let a shape reply without sending a new message. Choose the next shape, or run **5 messages** or **10 messages**. Use **Stop fast forward** to stop a run.

The chat needs messages and at least one shape. This works in one-to-one chats; in group chats, only the chat owner can use it. It is hidden in **Agent** mode.

See [Skip turn troubleshooting](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) if the option is missing.

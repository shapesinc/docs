In a group chat, a shape sends a message without being asked directly when it has something useful to add. It doesn't post on its own after a chat goes quiet.

## AI replies

In group chats, shapes reply when they can help and stay out of ordinary conversation. This is always on. In a [DM between two people](/chat-and-agent-modes#dms-between-people), Tod never follows up on its own. An @mention of a shape always gets a reply.

**AI replies** no longer has the **Free will (experimental)** or **Come back later** switches. Both were removed in November 2026. See [Why doesn't my shape message me first?](/faq/why-doesnt-my-shape-message-me-first)

<span id="group-agent-chats" />

The older triggers (**Reply when mentioned**, **Keep the convo going**, **React to keywords**, and **Always have something to say**) appeared only in group chats set to Chat mode. Chats no longer have modes, so they no longer appear. See [the October 2026 update](/october-2026-update#what-was-retired).

<span id="how-often-does-a-shape-check-in-when-a-chat-goes-quiet" />

### Fewer extra messages

Shapes don't check in when a chat goes quiet, so there are no check-ins to turn off.

If a shape's replies are too long or too short, its owner can open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, choose the shape, then use **Profile** → **Response style**.

### Can I stop shapes from @-mentioning people?

There's no setting for this. Shapes are told not to @-mention by default, and to do it only when addressing someone directly or telling two people apart. Add a line like "don't @-mention anyone" to the chat's Instructions, or set the chat's notifications to **Nothing** to stop the alerts.

## Keep the conversation going

**Keep going** has the shapes in a chat take turns for 5 or 10 messages without anyone writing, or gets one reply from a shape you pick. It replaced **Skip turn** and fast forward. In group chats, only chat owners see it.

1. Select **+** next to the message box, then **Keep going**.
2. Under **Number of turns**, pick **5 messages** or **10 messages**.
3. With more than one shape in the chat, pick who goes first under **Who starts**.

To hear from one shape once, pick it under **Reply once** instead of a number of turns.

A bar above the message box shows progress, for example "2 of 5 messages". Select **Stop** on the bar, or **+** → **Stop fast forward**, to end the run early.

See [What happened to Skip turn and fast forward?](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn)

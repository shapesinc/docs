Shapes can send extra messages in a few different ways.

The first is a Shape personality setting. It lets the Shape occasionally send more than one message at a time when it is already replying.

![Allowmult](/images/allowmult.png)

The second is **AI replies** in a chat. AI replies controls when Shapes can jump in without being directly prompted, such as replying when mentioned, reacting to keywords, keeping the conversation going, or coming back after the chat is quiet.

Find **AI replies** → **Auto-reply triggers** under **More actions** → **Settings** → **AI Configurations**.

![Dmmenu 1 Jpe](/images/dmmenu-1.jpeg)


## Skip Turn and Fast Forward

In **Chat** mode, open **+** → **Skip turn** to let a shape reply without sending a new message. Choose the next shape, or run **5 messages** or **10 messages**. Use **Stop fast forward** to stop a run.

The chat needs messages and at least one shape. This works in one-to-one chats; in group chats, only the chat owner can use it. It is hidden in **Agent** mode.

See [Skip turn troubleshooting](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) if the option is missing.

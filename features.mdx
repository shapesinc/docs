Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together. Use this index to find the steps and limits for what you want to do.

## Chat with your people

- [Start a chat](/create-a-chat), invite people, or use a direct shape link. You do not need to create a shape first. Public shape catalogs are paused; pickers show your shapes and recents.
- [Choose Chat, Agent, or Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes). Chat is for when you just want to chat, without web search, code execution, or other actions. Agent can help with tasks using those capabilities. Auto chooses based on what you need.
- [Use threads](/threads) to keep a focused task inside the same chat. A thread has the chat's audience; it is not a separate private chat. [Branch off](/branch-off) creates a different chat instead.
- [Invite an outside AI agent](/ai-agents). Agents have their own identity, need membership and an owner, and have message and capability limits.

## Research, make things, and take actions

- [Use skills](/shapeskills) for web research, images, files, calculations, and coding. Availability depends on the chat, AI Engine, and service; connecting an app is separate from having a skill.
- [Connect apps](/connected-apps), such as Gmail or Google Drive. Requests use the acting person's connection and access. Other members' accounts are not included automatically.
- [Use websites, sign in, buy, and book](/websites-and-purchases). Review private sign-in and purchase cards, payment eligibility, booking rules, and the final outcome.
- [Save details for forms](/saved-details). Optional account details can fill supported purchase, travel, and computer forms. Saving details does not submit a form or approve a payment.
- [Build a live poll, game, or form](/multiplayer-activities). People can use the same activity and see updates. The guide explains sharing, access, and deletion.

## Computers, coding, and ongoing work

- [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer). Allow it per chat and choose whether to share access with specific members. The computer must be online; permissions and expiry still apply.
- [Use hosted coding agents](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work). Free coding starts, coding-provider usage, and computer usage have separate daily allowances. Paid work and a connected personal computer follow different billing paths.
- [Create reminders, tasks, and watches](/tasks). Include what to do, when, and the time zone. Check the scheduled task or running work, and cancel it from Tasks. Watches and scheduled checks pause while their chat is Completed for the person who created them.
- [Inspect or stop Agent work](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did). Enable Working steps to open the step list and find Stop while a reply is running. Only the requester or a chat owner can stop a reply; stopping does not undo actions already completed.

## Voice, memory, and customization

- [Send a voice message](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages) or [start a call](/voicecalls). Recording, microphone access, voice generation, and calls have different controls; see the voice guide for usage limits.
- [Manage memory](/memory). Correct saved details and summaries, and understand the context available in a chat or thread. Memory is selective and does not grant access to other chats.
- [Set instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for a chat, or use [your personal preset](/presets). Chat ownership and personal scope determine who can edit them.
- [Customize a shape](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality). Only its owners can change its profile and default behavior. Built-in response styles remain selectable; Custom opens an editor.

## Access, credits, and troubleshooting

- [Moderation and access](/moderation): chat owners control joining, agents, branching, and moderation settings. Features do not override membership, blocks, or workspace access.
- [AI Engines](/aienginecheatsheet), [credits](/credits), and [Premium](/premium-subscriptions): check the current capabilities and price before using a paid feature. Premium is not unlimited provider or computer usage.
- [Settings and personalization](/personalization): find account preferences, saved details, connections, tasks, and help.
- [Frequently asked questions](/faq): find a missing control, recover an account, or troubleshoot a failed action.

## For agents

Use the [agent reading guide](/docs-for-agents) for Markdown pages, the documentation index, and the separate agent API instructions. Feature documentation explains behavior; it does not grant permission to act for someone.

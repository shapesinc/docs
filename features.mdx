This index links each shapes.inc feature to its guide: what it does, how to use it, and the limits that apply. Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together.

## Start with what you want to do together

- **Arrange a hangout, birthday, or family meal:** [collect preferences, compare places, vote, and share the plan](/fun-and-friends#turn-a-hangout-into-a-plan).
- **Get a table or an appointment:** [ask a shape to call the place, answer its questions during the call, and play the recording](/phone-calls).
- **Choose something for your home:** [compare links and photos, discuss the tradeoffs, and review a purchase](/fun-and-friends#choose-something-for-your-home).
- **Plan a trip:** [combine dates, research, an itinerary, bookings, and reminders](/plan-and-build-together#plan-a-trip-with-friends).
- **Study or work on a group project:** [share materials, explain them, make a quiz, and review the answers](/plan-and-build-together#study-together).
- **Make a website, app, or shared document:** [draft, build, test, and revise together](/plan-and-build-together#build-an-app-or-a-website-together).
- **Use your laptop from a chat on your phone:** [connect a computer and request work on its files or apps](/connect-your-computer#use-your-computer-from-your-phone).
- **Bring everyone's agents into one place:** [invite outside agents, give them distinct jobs, and coordinate in the chat](/ai-agents).
- **Work with a team:** [turn shared discussion, connected apps, and coding work into results everyone can review](/ai-at-work).
- **Get more out of every chat:** [requests that work, shortcuts, and habits](/get-the-most-out-of-shapes-inc).

These are starting points you can combine. The guides below explain the actual controls, shared results, access, and limits; a use case does not grant access to an account or computer.

## Chat with your people

- [Start a chat](/create-a-chat), invite people, or use a direct shape link. You do not need to create a shape first. Public shape catalogs are gone and aren't coming back; pickers show your shapes and recents.
- [Choose Chat, Agent, or Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes). Chat is for talking, without web search, code execution, or other actions. In Agent, shapes use those capabilities to do tasks. Auto chooses based on what you need.
- [Use threads](/threads) to keep a focused task inside the same chat. A thread has the chat's audience; it is not a separate private chat. [Branch off](/branch-off) creates a different chat instead.
- [Invite an outside AI agent](/ai-agents). Agents have their own identity, need membership and an owner, and have message and capability limits.
- [Find and organize chats](/sidebar-organization), switch workspaces, and use [Archive](/archive) for chats you have finished with. Workspace access and chat membership still apply. Auto-named Agent chats [keep their name current](/sidebar-organization#chat-names-that-follow-the-conversation).
- [Text shapes.inc from Messages](/texting): get replies by text in your one texting chat, text back, and add shapes.inc to an iMessage group. Places are limited; groups need everyone on shapes.inc.
- [Ask for a shape](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-for-a-shape) in an Agent or Auto chat and one is made for the chat, up to 8 per chat and 30 a day. Shapes can also [search for shapes](/shapes-search).
- [Ask a shape to do it in the app](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-a-shape-to-do-it-in-the-app): rename chats, invite people, start polls, or change your settings, with your permissions.
- [DMs between people](/chat-and-agent-modes#dms-between-people) are Agent chats with Tod, who answers only when someone addresses it.
- [Open a chat or shape link without an account](/faq/what-can-i-do-without-an-account): group chat links open read-only, up to the 50 most recent messages. Sending and joining need an account.
- [Bring Slack and your tools](/slack-and-hooks): import Slack history, sync channels both ways, and post updates from GitHub, Linear, Ramp, or any tool with hooks.

## Research, make things, and take actions

- [Use skills](/shapeskills) for web research, images, files, calculations, and coding. Availability depends on the chat, AI Engine, and service; connecting an app is separate from having a skill.
- [Share and make files](/files-and-attachments). Uploads require sign-in and allow up to 100MB per file. Check what a shape can read, preview or download the result, and find shared files later.
- [Connect apps](/connected-apps), such as Gmail or Google Drive. Use your connection or a supported app explicitly shared with the chat. Teammates' routine requests can use your apps only as your **Shared access** settings allow. Make any app [read-only](/connected-apps#make-an-app-read-only), and have a shape [send email](/connected-apps#have-a-shape-send-email) from its own address in Agent chats.
- [Use websites, sign in, buy, and book](/websites-and-purchases). [Connect your browser](/websites-and-purchases#connect-your-browser) to let shapes work in your own signed-in Chrome tabs. Review private sign-in and purchase cards, payment eligibility, booking rules, and the final outcome. Shapes [shop for the country you're in](/websites-and-purchases#shopping-where-you-are).
- [Have a shape make a phone call](/phone-calls) to a business, a friend, or you, answer its questions mid-call, and read the recap. A schedule can [call you at a set time](/phone-calls#schedule-a-call-to-you). You can also [call Tod](/phone-calls#call-tod) on the number you text. Agent chats only; Free includes 100 credits of calling a day, then account credits.
- [Save details for forms](/saved-details). Optional account details can fill supported purchase, travel, and computer forms. Saving details does not submit a form or approve a payment.
- [Build a live poll, game, or form](/multiplayer-activities). People can use the same activity and see updates. The guide explains sharing, access, and deletion.
- [Make a choice or use a shared widget](/multiplayer-activities#choose-between-options). Choose between recommendations, ask the group, or use a page with shared state. A Yes/No offer starts the offered work for the person who chooses Yes; it does not approve a payment.

## Computers, coding, and ongoing work

- [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer) with the [Mac or Windows app](/connect-your-computer#get-the-desktop-app). Allow it per chat and choose whether to share access with specific members. The computer must be online; permissions and expiry still apply. Shapes can also [check the computer itself](/connect-your-computer#ask-about-the-computer-itself), such as its Wi-Fi or free space. **Use for new private chats** and [Use my coding instructions](/connect-your-computer#use-my-coding-instructions) are on by default; on a Mac, chats you allow with **Can make changes** can [use the screen](/connect-your-computer#see-and-use-your-macs-screen).
- [Use hosted coding agents](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work). Free coding starts, coding-provider usage, and computer usage have separate daily allowances. Paid work and a connected personal computer follow different billing paths.
- [Create reminders, tasks, and watches](/tasks). Include what to do, when, and the time zone. Check the scheduled task or running work, and [pause, resume](/tasks#pause-a-routine), or cancel it from Tasks. Watches and scheduled checks pause while their chat is archived for the person who created them, and [end on their own](/tasks#watches-end-on-their-own) once there's nothing left to do.
- [Follow or stop Agent work](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did). A bar above the message box shows each reply in progress and who asked; the person who asked or the chat owner can **Stop** it, or **Retry** it when it seems stuck. Stopping does not undo actions already completed.

## Voice, memory, and customization

- [Send a voice message](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages) or [start a call](/voicecalls). A call with a shape uses its personality and memory, and in Agent chats can work on requests in the chat; it is paid from account credits. Recording, microphone access, voice generation, and calls have different controls; see the voice guide for usage limits.
- [Manage memory](/memory). In Agent chats a shape recalls from the whole chat and from your other chats in the same workspace, saves your [standing preferences](/memory#standing-preferences), and lets you correct saved notes and summaries. Memory is selective; on request it can also search your workspace's group chats.
- [Set instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for a chat, or use [your personal preset](/presets). Chat ownership and personal scope determine who can edit them.
- [Customize a shape](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality). Only its owners can change its profile and default behavior. Built-in response styles remain selectable; Custom opens an editor.
- [Change chat appearance](/chat-appearance), use [Markdown](/markdown) in messages, or configure [custom profile HTML and CSS](/css). Personal appearance preferences and owner-controlled chat or shape settings have different scopes.
- [Use message actions](/messageactionsmenu), [commands and shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts), and [Chat-mode follow-up controls](/multimessages). Editing, branching, regenerating, and continuing a response depend on mode and permissions.

## Access, credits, and troubleshooting

- [Moderation and access](/moderation): chat owners control joining, agents, branching, and moderation settings. Features do not override membership, blocks, or workspace access.
- [AI Engines](/aienginecheatsheet), [credits](/credits), and [Premium](/premium-subscriptions): check the current capabilities and price before using a paid feature. Pick an engine by task under **Best for**; Agent chats offer [a shorter list](/aienginecheatsheet#engines-in-agent-chats), including the free **Formless v2.1**. Premium is not unlimited provider or computer usage.
- [Settings and personalization](/personalization): find account preferences, saved details, connections, tasks, and help.
- [Accessibility](/accessibility): font size, themes, reduce motion, message effects, streaming, voice and captions, keyboard, screen readers, and language, with what each one covers and who controls it.
- [Notifications](/notifications), [friends](/friends), and [account recovery](/make-or-recover-account): stay in touch, find people again, and recover access. [Export and backup limits](/faq/can-i-export-or-back-up-my-data) explain what you can keep outside the app.
- [Get the apps](/apps) for iPhone, Android, Mac, and Windows. The phone apps add push notifications, calls that ring on your call screen, and screen sharing; the desktop apps connect your computer. Web plans include the most credits.
- [Ads](/faq/why-do-i-see-ads) appear as messages labelled **Sponsored**. Premium removes them.
- [Form](/faq/what-is-form) is a physical body for a shape, available to pre-order: Form 01 at $999 and Form 02 at $1,999, charged when ordered.
- [Frequently asked questions](/faq): find a missing control, recover an account, or troubleshoot a failed action.

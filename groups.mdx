A group keeps related chats together under one name: one chat for planning, another for research, or separate study subjects. Start with [multiplayer assistants](/ai-group-chats) for the bigger picture.

To set up a new shared space for a project, team, or family, you can also use a [workspace](/sidebar-organization#workspaces). Each workspace has its own chat list.

### How groups work

Each group has a main chat, and the group's name comes from it. When you're in a chat that belongs to a group, its **More actions** (**⋯**) menu shows which group it's part of and lists the other chats in the group.

Chat owners can rename the group from the main chat: select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Chat Appearance** → **Group Name**. Individual chats in a group can be renamed too.

### Joining and Navigating Groups

When you join any chat inside a group, you can see the group's other chats from that chat's **More actions** menu and open the ones you want.

You don't have to join every chat in a group. Join the ones that are relevant to you; the chats you join appear in your sidebar.

### Adding Chats to a Group

If you own a group, open **More actions** in one of its chats and choose **Add chat to group**. Paste a chat link, or select one of the chats you own under **Your chats**, then select **Add chat**. DMs can't be added to groups.

### Leaving Groups

You can leave chats individually at any time with **Leave Chat**. If you leave every chat in a group, none of its chats appear in your sidebar. The group and its chats remain available to other members who are still participating.

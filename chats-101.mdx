## Open the chat controls

Select the chat's name or picture at the top of the chat to open its **Settings**. Chat owners can also use **More actions** (**⋯**) → **Settings**.

A chat brings the people involved and a multiplayer assistant together around a shared goal. Use these controls to invite collaborators, keep reference material and decisions easy to find, and tune how Shapes contribute. Read [AI Group Chats](/ai-group-chats) for the bigger picture or [create a chat](/create-a-chat) to get started.

The top of a chat also has:

- **Invite** (in group chats): opens **Invite friends** with **Copy Link** and **Share via...**. Invite links look like `shapes.inc/j/…`.
- **Start Call** when the chat has one person and one Shape.
- **More actions** (**⋯**): **New chat**, **Search Messages**, **Manage memories**, **Settings** (chat owners), **Instructions**, a call option when the chat has one Shape, **Mentions**, **Mark completed** or **Reopen**, and, outside DMs, **Report chat** and **Leave chat**.

Members, Invite, Share, and Delete are no longer in **More actions**. Find members under **Participants** in the chat's **Settings**, and **Invite**, **Share**, and **Delete** in the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar.

<Info>
  Use the **⋯** menu on a message to reply, edit, regenerate, translate, branch off, or check **Generation details**. See the [message actions menu](/messageactionsmenu).
</Info>

## Find the control you need

The **Settings** panel is grouped into **Chat settings**, **Chat management**, and **Shared content**. Use **Search settings...** at the top to jump to an option. Some options appear only for chat owners or only in certain chats.

### Chat settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AI Configurations" description="How do I change how Shapes work in this chat?" icon="robot">
    Chat owners see **AI Configurations**. In a DM with a Shape, you can open it too.

    - Switch the chat between **Auto**, **Chat**, and **Agent**. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).
    - Write shared [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) with the group's goal, constraints, and decision criteria.
    - Choose [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence#the-main-dial-free-will) triggers for when Shapes can jump in without being asked.
    - Review built-in **Skills** and, in Agent chats, connect **MCP servers**. See the [Skills guide](/shapeskills).
    - In Agent chats, connect repositories under **Code Projects** for code tasks in this chat. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Engines" description="How do I choose the AI engine for this chat?" icon="microchip">
    Chat owners, and anyone in a DM with a Shape, can open **Engines**. Browse the full catalog under **Choose engine**, including your **Favorites**, and change **Reasoning effort** under **Engine settings**. The engine built by shapes.inc is shown as **Formless**. You can also switch engines from the composer's **+** menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chat Appearance" description="How do I change how my chat looks?" icon="card-heart">
    Chat owners can change a group chat's look. In a DM, you can change it too.

    - Change the **Chat Title** and **Chat Description**
    - Set a **Chat Avatar**, **Chat Banner**, and **Chat Background**
    - Change **Bubble Colors**
    - Use a **Custom Font (Google Fonts)**
    - [Read the in-depth guide](/chat-appearance)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Profile & credits" description="How do I choose how I appear and whether this chat uses my credits?" icon="user">
    - Pick which of your Profiles Shapes use for you in this chat, or keep **Use my default**.
    - See that your personal preset applies here too, and edit it.
    - Turn on **Don't use my credits** so this chat doesn't spend your credits.

    Set your defaults in [Personalization](/personalization).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Manage memories" description="What do Shapes remember about this chat?" icon="brain">
    Opens the memories for this chat, where you can view, add, edit, and delete them. See [Memory](/memory).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Chat management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Participants" description="How do I invite people, add Shapes, or manage access?" icon="users">
    Open **Participants** to see everyone in the chat.

    - **Invite friends** shares a link so people can join.
    - **Add participants** adds people and Shapes directly. Choose the **Shapes** or **People** tab, select who to add, and select **Add**. The Shapes tab lists Shapes you own and Shapes you've messaged recently.
    - **Create shape** makes a new Shape for the chat.

    Select a person or Shape for more options, such as **Send DM**. Chat owners can also make someone an owner and remove or ban people. To control when Shapes respond, use **AI Configurations** → **AI replies**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications" description="How do I change what notifications I get from this chat?" icon="alarm-exclamation">
    - Choose **All Messages**, **Only @mentions**, or **Nothing** for this chat.
    - Use **Mute Notifications** for a set time, or **Until I turn it back on**.
    - [Learn more about notifications](/notifications)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Moderation" description="How do I lock the chat, ban people, or set limits?" icon="do-not-enter">
    Chat owners see **Moderation** in group chats. Use it to:

    - **Lock Chat** so new people can't join
    - Make a Shape the moderator and review the **Moderation log**
    - Choose whether to **Allow AI agents** and whether anyone can add Shapes
    - Set **Character Limits**, **Slow Mode**, and a **Blocked Words Filter**
    - Review **Banned Users**

    [Learn more about moderation](/moderation)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Shared content

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Search" description="How do I find earlier messages?" icon="magnifying-glass-arrow-right">
    Type in **Search messages...** to search the chat's history. **More actions** → **Search Messages** does the same, and **More actions** → **Mentions** finds messages that mention you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pinned Messages" description="How do I see pinned messages?" icon="map-pin">
    - View and jump to pinned messages from the chat's **Settings**.
    - Chat owners can pin the current plan, reference material, or confirmed decisions from a message's **⋯** menu so everyone can find them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Media & Files" description="Where are the photos and files shared here?" icon="images">
    Browse the photos, videos, and files shared in the chat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Leave or delete a chat

At the bottom of **Settings**, **Leave Chat** removes you from a group chat. You'll need an invite link to rejoin. Chat owners also see **Delete chat**. After you delete a chat, **Undo** appears for a few seconds. To clear a chat from your list without deleting it, use **Mark completed** instead. See [How do I delete a chat?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-chat) and [Completed](/completed).

## Composer controls

<Accordion title="What's in the + menu?" icon="sparkles">
  The **+** beside the message box includes:

  - **Add attachment** to share a file, such as a reference you want the group to discuss. You can also drop a file anywhere over the chat. On phones, **Take photo** is here too.
  - **Connected apps** to let Shapes use apps like Gmail or Notion. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps).
  - **Connect your computer** in Agent chats. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
  - **Commands** for slash commands such as `/remember` and `/new`. See [Commands and shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts).
  - **Draw something**, **Search GIFs**, and **Insert emoji** (not on phones)
  - [**Skip turn**](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) in **Chat** mode to let a shape respond next
  - **Engine** to switch the AI engine
  - **Feedback** to tell the shapes.inc team about a problem or idea. See [Feedback](/feedback).

  Use the microphone button to record a voice message, preview it, then send it. See [voice messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) for reply settings.
</Accordion>

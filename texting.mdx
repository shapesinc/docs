You can chat with shapes.inc from your phone's Messages app. Your texts and replies stay in one texting chat, which you can also open in the app.

<Note>
Texting has a limited number of places, given first come, first served, for existing accounts and for new accounts that [sign up by text](#sign-up-by-text). If a place is open for you, you'll see **Text notifications** under **Settings → Notifications & privacy**, or a **Chat from Messages** prompt in the app.
</Note>

## Connect your phone

1. Open **Settings → Notifications & privacy** and select **Connect** next to **Phone number**. You can also select **Connect Messages** on the **Chat from Messages** prompt.
2. A text is ready to send, with a short code and the shapes.inc number. On a phone or Mac, select **Open Messages**. On another computer, send the text shown from your phone.
3. Send it within 30 minutes. You'll get **You're all set** and a contact card for shapes.inc. Save the contact so replies show its name.

The phone that sends the code is the one that gets your texts. To switch phones, choose **Connect another phone**. To stop texts, choose **Turn off texts** or text **STOP**. Text **START** to turn them back on.

## Sign up by text

If you text the shapes.inc number before you have an account, you'll get a link to sign up. The link works for 7 days and only for the number that texted. Once you're in, the texts you sent before signing up get answered.

## Which chat a text goes to

You have one texting chat. Everything you text goes there, and its replies come back by text.

- **Your first text** starts a new chat with Tod. That chat is your texting chat. It's an [Agent chat](/chat-and-agent-modes) unless your **New chats** default is Chat. If you were already connected, your texting chat stays the same.
- **Every later text** goes to your texting chat, including replies to its texts.
- **To start fresh**, text `new chat`, or `new chat: dinner Friday` with a topic. That new chat becomes your texting chat, and the old one stays in the app.
- **When a shape starts a task in a new chat**, it sends you a link to open that chat in the app. Follow that chat's progress there.

Everything you text shows up in the app too, in your texting chat. A shape in a different chat doesn't see it there. Texts like `chats`, `switch to 2`, or a bare number are ordinary messages to your texting chat; they don't list or switch chats.

If you reply to an older text that came from a different chat, you'll get "That message is from another chat. Send a new message here to continue your connected chat." Your texting chat doesn't change.

## When replies come by text

- Only your texting chat texts you. Your other chats don't, even while you're away; you get their push and email alerts as your [notification settings](/notifications) allow.
- If you've texted in the last 24 hours, your texting chat replies by text even while you have it open in the app, and even if it's muted or set to mentions only. Otherwise it texts you while you're away from the app, as your notification settings allow.
- A chat of 10 or more people never sends texts. If you add that many people to your texting chat, read its replies in the app.
- An [iMessage group](#text-with-a-group) with shapes.inc in it keeps its own shared chat and isn't affected.
- Every text starts with who said it, in bold, for people and shapes alike. After you start a new texting chat, its first text shows the chat's name.
- Typing dots show only while a shape is writing a reply, and stop when the reply arrives. A text to a person, like a friend in a group chat, gets a 👍 and a read receipt, but no typing dots.
- Replies keep their formatting: bold, italics, and bullet lists, split into up to three texts.
- Very long replies, and files Messages can't show, come with a link to read the rest in the app.

## Send photos, voice memos, and files

Send up to 4 photos or files in one text, up to 10MB each. A photo or voice memo works on its own, with no words. Voice memos are transcribed the same way as [voice messages in the app](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages).

## Reactions, read receipts, and edits

- Tapbacks work both ways, including custom emoji. Removing a tapback removes the reaction.
- Reading a text marks that chat as read in the app.
- Editing a text edits your message in the chat, and edits in the app update the text. iMessage allows edits for 15 minutes, up to 5 times; after that, an **Edited** text follows instead.

## Polls, choices, and private cards

- Polls and choices arrive numbered. Reply with a number to vote or pick. A reply to the poll's text counts for 7 days; a plain text counts for 30 minutes.
- Widgets, quizzes, and purchases come with a link to open them in the app.
- Never text a password, code, or card number. When a shape needs one, it sends a link so you can [enter it privately in the app](/websites-and-purchases).

## Text with a group

You can bring shapes.inc into an iMessage group:

1. Add the shapes.inc number to the group. Groups can have up to 12 people.
2. Everyone in the group needs a shapes.inc account. Until they all have one, Tod tells the group who it's still waiting for and texts each missing person a private sign-up link.
3. Once everyone is in, the group becomes a shared Agent chat with Tod on shapes.inc. Say "Tod" in the group to get an answer.

To remove Tod, text "Tod leave" in groups of 4 or more people. In smaller groups, remove the shapes.inc number from the group.

Group texts work over iMessage only. Group texts don't support tapbacks, edits, numbered cards, read receipts, typing indicators, or `new chat`, and the group's chat doesn't send push or email notifications.

## Limits

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Texts per account | 300 a day |
| Group texts | 50 a day per group |
| Group size | 12 people |
| Photos and files | 4 per text, 10MB each |

One-to-one texting uses iMessage, with RCS or SMS when iMessage isn't available. WhatsApp isn't supported. Typing indicators and edits need iMessage. Texting doesn't cost extra: replies use the same AI Engines and [credits](/credits) as in the app.

When the line is busy, you'll get a text saying lots of people are joining, then another when you can join. Texting places are limited and given first come, first served; if **Text notifications** isn't shown in Settings, no place is open for your account right now.

## Call the same number

You can also call the shapes.inc number from your connected phone. Tod picks up, and the call goes to your texting chat. See [Call Tod](/phone-calls#call-tod).

## Sign-in links in your texts

Chat links in texts, push notifications, and notification emails open that chat signed in, for 7 days. If you're already signed in, nothing changes, and a link never switches you to another account. Don't forward these links: anyone with one can open the chat as you until it expires.

## When something doesn't work

- **You texted something and can't find it in the app**: it's in your texting chat. If you texted `new chat`, it's in the newest one. Photos and files over 10MB, or past the first 4 in one text, aren't attached.
- **A chat's replies don't come by text**: only your texting chat texts you. Read other chats in the app, or text `new chat` to start a texting chat about something new.
- **"That message is from another chat"**: you replied to an older text from a chat that isn't your texting chat. Send a new text instead; it goes to your texting chat.
- **"Couldn't send that"**: open the link in the text to finish in the app.
- **The connection code expired**: start again from **Settings → Notifications & privacy**.
- **Texts stopped**: check that you didn't text STOP. Text START, or turn **Text notifications** back on in Settings.

See [Notifications](/notifications) for push and email alerts.

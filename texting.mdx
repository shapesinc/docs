You can keep a shapes.inc chat going from your phone's Messages app. Replies from your chats arrive as texts while you're away. Text back and your reply lands in the same chat, where the people and shapes in it see it like any other message.

<Note>
Texting is rolling out. If your account has it, you'll see **Text notifications** under **Settings → Notifications & privacy**, or a **Never miss a reply** prompt in the app. Anyone can also [sign up by text](#sign-up-by-text).
</Note>

## Connect your phone

1. Open **Settings → Notifications & privacy** and select **Connect** next to **Phone number**. You can also select **Open Messages** on the **Never miss a reply** prompt.
2. A text is ready to send, with a short code and the shapes.inc number. On a phone or Mac, select **Open Messages**. On another computer, send the text shown from your phone.
3. Send it within 30 minutes. You'll get **You're all set** and a contact card for shapes.inc. Save the contact so replies show its name.

The phone that sends the code is the one that gets your texts. To switch phones, choose **Connect another phone**. To stop texts, choose **Turn off texts** or text **STOP**. Text **START** to turn them back on.

## Sign up by text

If you text the shapes.inc number before you have an account, you'll get a link to sign up. The link works for 7 days and only for the number that texted. Once you're in, the texts you sent before signing up get answered.

## Which chat a text goes to

- **A plain text** continues the last chat you texted with.
- **A reply to a specific text** goes to that text's chat. In Messages, touch and hold the text, then choose **Reply**.
- **Your first text** starts a new chat with Tod. It's an [Agent chat](/chat-and-agent-modes) unless your **New chats** default is Chat.

Text these to move between chats:

| Text | What it does |
|---|---|
| `new chat` or `new chat: dinner Friday` | Start a new chat, with an optional topic |
| `chats` | List your 6 most recent chats; **← here** marks the one you're in |
| `switch to 2` or `switch to Book club` | Move to a chat from the list, by number or name |

## When replies come by text

- A chat you've texted in the last 24 hours keeps replying by text, even while you're using the app.
- Other chats text you only while you're away from the app.
- People are always named in bold. Shapes are named in group chats. When the thread moves to a different chat, the chat's name appears first.
- Replies keep their formatting: bold, italics, and bullet lists, split into up to three texts.
- Very long replies, and files Messages can't show, come with a link to read the rest in the app.

## Send photos, voice memos, and files

Send up to 4 photos or files in one text, up to 10MB each. A photo or voice memo works on its own, with no words. Voice memos are transcribed the same way as [voice messages in the app](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages).

## Reactions, read receipts, and edits

- Tapbacks work both ways, including custom emoji. Removing a tapback removes the reaction.
- Reading a text marks that chat as read in the app.
- Editing a text edits your message in the chat, and edits in the app update the text. iMessage allows edits for 15 minutes, up to 5 times; after that, an **Edited** text follows instead.

## Polls, choices, and private cards

- Polls and choices arrive numbered. Reply with a number to vote or pick. A reply to the poll's text counts for 7 days; a plain text counts for 30 minutes.
- Widgets, quizzes, and purchases come with a link to open them in the app.
- Never text a password, code, or card number. When a shape needs one, it sends a link so you can [enter it privately in the app](/websites-and-purchases).

## Text with a group

You can bring shapes.inc into an iMessage group:

1. Add the shapes.inc number to the group. Groups can have up to 12 people.
2. Everyone in the group needs a shapes.inc account. Until they all have one, Tod tells the group who it's still waiting for and texts each missing person a private sign-up link.
3. Once everyone is in, the group becomes a shared Agent chat with Tod on shapes.inc. Say "Tod" in the group to get an answer.

To remove Tod, text "Tod leave" in groups of 4 or more people. In smaller groups, remove the shapes.inc number from the group.

Group texts work over iMessage only. Group texts don't support tapbacks, edits, numbered cards, read receipts, typing indicators, or the chat commands above, and the group's chat doesn't send push or email notifications.

## Limits

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Texts per account | 300 a day |
| Group texts | 50 a day per group |
| Group size | 12 people |
| Photos and files | 4 per text, 10MB each |

One-to-one texting uses iMessage, with RCS or SMS when iMessage isn't available. Typing indicators and edits need iMessage. Texting doesn't cost extra: replies use the same AI Engines and [credits](/credits) as in the app.

When the line is busy, you'll get a text saying lots of people are joining, then another when there's room.

## Sign-in links in your texts

Chat links in texts, push notifications, and notification emails open that chat signed in, for 7 days. If you're already signed in, nothing changes, and a link never switches you to another account. Don't forward these links: anyone with one can open the chat as you until it expires.

## When something doesn't work

- **"Couldn't find a chat called…"**: text `chats`, then `switch to` a number from the list.
- **"Couldn't send that"**: open the link in the text to finish in the app.
- **The connection code expired**: start again from **Settings → Notifications & privacy**.
- **Texts stopped**: check that you didn't text STOP. Text START, or turn **Text notifications** back on in Settings.

See [Notifications](/notifications) for push and email alerts.

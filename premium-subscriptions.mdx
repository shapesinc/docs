Premium is a monthly plan for people who want premium AI engines and higher limits for their group's work. Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance you choose on the Premium page.

## Open Premium

Open **Settings** → **Premium** under **Account**. On a phone, **Settings** is in the bottom navigation; on a computer, select **Settings** in the sidebar.

The Premium page shows the current plans, prices, and limits for your account.

New account credits are purchased through a monthly plan. Separate one-time credit packs are not currently available. See [Can I buy credits without a subscription?](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription)

## Credits per plan

How many credits a plan adds each month depends on where you subscribe. Apple and Google keep a share of every App Store and Google Play purchase, so a plan bought in the iPhone or Android app adds fewer credits for the same price.

App Store and Google Play show prices in your local currency, so the price you see can differ from the US price above. A plan you started in the app before these amounts changed keeps the credits it started with.

## What Premium includes

- **Monthly premium credits:** each paid month adds your allowance to your credit balance. Premium AI engines, premium image generation, and Shape voice use these credits.
- **Premium AI engines:** access to premium engines, metered from your credits. See the [live engine catalog](https://shapes.inc/engines) for current engines and pricing.
- **No ads:** ads disappear for you everywhere, including chats you share with people on the free plan. What other members see doesn't change.
- **Longer memory:** each Shape can read more recent messages, so long chats stay coherent.
- **Profile styles:** name styles, frames, gradients, bubble effects, message effects, and every **Bubble style** except **Classic (Rounded)**. Other people's styles and effects show for everyone; you can't turn them off for yourself. If your plan ends and your credits run out, these styles return to their defaults, but your selections stay saved and come back when you subscribe again.

Premium engine replies are charged at twice the engine's provider cost. See [Shape Credits](/credits).

## What stays free

- Free AI engines and the daily free image generations never use credits, on any plan.
- Shape banners, custom emojis (up to 50), custom profile HTML and CSS, display fonts, and chat appearance stay free for everyone.

You don't need a plan to use premium engines: account credits, or credits that fund a chat or a Shape, can also pay for premium replies. While your account has credits left, you also get no ads, longer memory, and profile styles, with or without a plan.

## When your credits run out

Your messages are still saved, and Shapes can switch to a free text engine so you can keep chatting. Premium engines and paid actions, such as premium images and voice, still need credits. See [When you run out](/credits#when-you-run-out) for the free Agent engine and the card that resumes a stopped task.

If your Premium page shows **Auto-upgrade when credits run out**, turning it on moves you to the next allowance and bills the difference when you run out. With it off, you're asked first.

## Change or cancel your plan

From the Premium page:

- Upgrades apply right away, and the extra credits are added immediately.
- Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.
- To cancel, use **Manage billing**. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.

If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, manage or cancel your plan in your store account.

Cancelling stops future renewals and keeps credits already added to your account. You can use the remaining balance after the plan ends, but you stop receiving the plan's monthly allowance. The other benefits stay while your balance has credits left. See [Do I keep my credits if I cancel Premium?](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium)

## Premium badge on replies

Replies written with a premium engine can show a purple Premium badge. It's off by default. To show it, open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn on **Premium message badges**.

Open a message’s **⋯** menu to see its available actions. On phones, you can also touch and hold the message to open the menu. Which actions appear depends on who sent the message, the kind of chat, and your role in it.

## Reactions and quick replies

- A reaction strip under messages offers ❤️ 😂 👍 😮 😢 🔥. Select **+** for the full emoji picker. Reactions are emoji only.
- In group chats, the faces of the people who reacted show under the message. Select them, or **View reactions**, to see who reacted with each emoji; chat owners can **Manage** reactions there.
- On phones, swipe left on a message to reply to it. Start on the message rather than the edge of the screen or an embedded control.
- Touch and hold a message to open its actions and reactions. To copy only part of its text, choose **Select text**, then touch and hold a word to adjust the selection. **Copy** copies the whole message. Hold a link, image, or code block for **Copy link**, **Save image**, or **Copy code**.

## Any message

### Reply

- Respond directly to a specific message. Your reply appears linked to the original message for context.

### Edit

- Change your own message, then select **Save**. Press **Escape** to cancel.

### Copy

- Copies the message text to your clipboard.

Copy saves that message's text only. For full chat history, Shape data, or memories, see [export and backup limitations](/faq/can-i-export-or-back-up-my-data).

### Copy transcript

- Copies the text of a voice message.

<a id="translate" />

### Translating a message

- Message menus no longer have **Translate**. Ask a shape instead, for example "translate the last message into Spanish". Change your app language under **Settings** → **General** → **App language**.

### Thread

- In chats that use threads, opens a thread so you can discuss a message without crowding the main chat.

### View Reactions

- See who reacted to a message, grouped by emoji. Chat owners can select **Manage** to remove reactions.

### DM

- Opens a private DM with the person or Shape who sent the message.

### Branch off

- Creates a new private chat starting from the selected message. It copies up to the last 50 messages, ending with the one you chose. Not available in DMs. See [Branch Off](/branch-off).

### Delete

- Removes your own message from the chat. **Undo** appears for a few seconds after you delete.

### Report Message

- Reports a message that breaks the rules or feels inappropriate.

### Block user

- Blocks the person who sent the message. It appears on other people's messages.

### Copy Message Link

- Copies a link to that exact message. People who can open the chat can jump straight to it.

## Shape replies

### Regenerate

- Asks the Shape for a new version of its reply. Anyone in the chat can regenerate a reply, and the credits come from whoever regenerates it. It works on Agent replies too. See [Regeneration](/regeneration).

### Read out loud

- Plays the reply as audio using the Shape's voice, or a default voice if none is set. Select **Stop reading** to stop. If the action needs credits or account access, shapes.inc shows a banner before playing.

### Report a problem

- Tells the shapes.inc team that something went wrong with this reply. See [Feedback](/feedback).

### Generation details

- Shows how the reply was made: the engine, why it was picked, how long the reply took, and the credits used.

### Call

- Starts a voice call with the shape when this action is available. See [Voice calls](/voicecalls) for the chat's call controls and voice allowance.

### Add to training

- Shape owners can save a great reply as training material for their Shape.

<Note>
  Message ratings and thumbs-down engine suggestions are gone. Use **Generation details** to see how a reply was made and **Report a problem** to flag a bad one.
</Note>

## Chat owner actions

### Pin

- Pins a message so everyone can find it under **Pinned Messages** in the chat's **Settings**. Choose **Unpin** to remove it.

### Edit and Delete

- Edit a Shape's message in **Chat** mode, where editing is allowed, or delete anyone's message in the chat. **Agent** mode disables editing Shape replies, including for chat owners; you can still edit your own messages. See [Why can't I edit a Shape's reply?](/faq/why-cant-i-edit-a-shapes-reply)

### Instructions

- Opens the chat's shared [instructions](/custominstructions-chats). In a DM with a Shape, you see this too.

<a id="ask-moderator" />

### Reporting a message to moderators

- Message menus no longer have **Ask moderator**. Mention the chat's moderators or owner in the chat, or see [Moderation](/moderation).

### Rewind to here

- Deletes every newer message in the chat for everyone. This can't be undone.

### Remove

- Removes the person who sent the message from the chat. To ban someone, use **Participants** in the chat's **Settings**.

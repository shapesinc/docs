  <Tab title="Existing User">
    Recover your shapes.inc account: directions for existing users

    **Step 1**\
    Go to [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc/login). The sign-in page shows **Log in or sign up**. \
    \
    **Step 2**\
    Use the method you used before. For Google or Apple, select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. For email, enter your email address, select **Continue**, enter your password, and select **Sign in**. If your email is linked to Google or Apple, the page asks you to continue with that provider instead. \
    \
    **Step 3**\
    If you forgot your email password, select **Forgot your password?**, then **Send reset link**, and follow the link in your inbox. If you can't sign in with Google or Apple, recover access through that provider.

    To use more than one account, open **Settings** → **Switch account** → **Add account**.

## Sign-in methods and passwords

To add another way to sign in, open **Settings**, select your name or picture at the top, and go to **Connected accounts** on the **Edit profile** page. Select **Connect Email**, **Connect Google**, or **Connect Apple**. The new method must use the same email address as your account. **Connect Email** sets a password, so an account made with Google or Apple can also sign in with email.

To disconnect a method, sign in with a different one first. The method you're signed in with shows **Current sign-in** and can't be disconnected.

There's no change-password screen while you're signed in. To change your password, log out, select **Forgot your password?**, then **Send reset link**. Resetting your password signs you out on every device.

shapes.inc doesn't offer two-factor authentication.

<Info>
  Using the shapes.inc app for Mac? It signs you in through your web browser: select **Continue with** your browser, then **Continue as** your name in the browser. See [Use the Mac app](/connect-your-computer#sign-in-through-your-browser).
</Info>

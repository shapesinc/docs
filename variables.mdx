#### **Supported Variables**

1. `{shape}` – Refers to the shape itself.
2. `{user}` – Refers to the user interacting with the shape.

You can use these variables across various fields in the Shape editor to guide your shape's responses, set presets, and enhance interactions. To open the editor, go to **Settings** → **My Shapes** and choose your shape.

<Info>
  `{shape}` converts into the shape's name. `{user}` converts into the name of the person the shape is talking to.
</Info>

### **Where to Use Variables**

#### **1. Personality → Core**

- Use `{shape}` and `{user}` in the shape's **Description** to describe who it is and who it's talking to.
- You can use `{shape}` and/or `{user}` in the shape's **Greeting** to make the first message feel more natural!

#### **2. Personality → Traits, Story & taste, and Conversation**

- Use variables in fields like **Appearance**, **Story**, and **Conversational goals** for better contextual understanding.
- In **Example exchanges**, label each line with `{user}:` or `{shape}:` to show how a conversation should go.

#### **3. Personality → Response style**

- Use variables in the **Instructions** under **Response style** to improve your presets' effectiveness.

  <Info>
    [Learn More](/presets) about presets.
  </Info>

#### **4. Knowledge**

- You can use variables to explain knowledge entries and commands.
- And to define relationships and command functionalities more clearly.

#### **5. Images**

- You can use `{shape}` in **Image style** to define custom image generation rules and styles.

<Info>
  By utilizing variables in descriptions, presets, and greetings, you can make your shape more dynamic and engaging!
</Info>

Settings controls your account, how new chats start, the app's appearance, and your personal preferences. Settings for an individual chat are in that chat's **Settings** panel; see [Chat Controls](/chats-101).

## Open Settings

- **On a computer:** select **Settings** in the sidebar. The settings navigation opens beside **General**. Use the back arrow above the settings list to return to chats.
- **On a phone:** tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom of the screen.

Use **Search settings** to find a page or control by name. Select a result to open it. **Clear search**, or **Escape** while searching, returns to the settings list.

## The Settings page at a glance

Select your name or picture at the top to edit your profile. The settings list is grouped as follows:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Preferences** | **General**, **Appearance**, and **Notifications & privacy**. |
| **Personalization** | **Personalization**, **Saved details**, **Memories** ([Memory](/memory)), and **Custom emoji**. |
| **Chats & agents** | **Your shapes**, **Connected apps**, **Tasks**, **Completed**, **Computers**, and **Your AI agents**. |
| **Account** | **Premium** when available, **Invites** for private accounts or pending invitations, **Switch account**, and **Log out**. |
| **Help** | **What's new**, **Docs**, **Support Chat**, **Feedback**, and **For AI agents**. |

**Your shapes** opens your shape list; creating a shape is optional. See [How to make a Shape](/how-to-make-a-shape). **Completed** holds chats and shapes you've finished with; see [Completed chats and Shapes](/completed).

## General

Open **Settings** → **General** for:

- **New chats:** start new chats in **Auto** (the default), **Chat**, or **Agent**. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes).
- **App language** and **Font size** (90% to 160%).
- **More preferences:** the controls below.

### More preferences

- **Sound alerts**, **Alert volume**, and **Test**. Turn on sound alerts to play the test sound. On iPhone and iPad, use the device's volume buttons.
- **Haptic feedback**, on supported touch devices. It is off by default.
- **Streaming**, off by default. Turn it on to show reply text as it arrives, or leave it off to wait for the completed reply. See [Streaming text](/faq/how-do-i-turn-off-typewriter-streaming-text).
- **Use shape avatars for chats**, off by default. Use the first shape's picture when a chat has no image.
- **Personalized ideas**, on by default. See [below](#personalized-home-ideas).
- **Premium message badges**, off by default.
- **Working steps**, off by default. Turn it on to open a step-by-step view of what a shape did.

## Appearance

Open **Settings** → **Appearance**. Under **Mode**, choose **Auto** to follow your device, or choose **Light** or **Dark**. Choose a **Light theme** and a **Dark theme**; Auto uses the appropriate one for your device's current mode.

## Notifications and privacy

**Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** has **Account notifications**, **Who can call me**, **In-app notifications**, **Email notifications**, **Promotional emails**, **Private account**, and **Hide badges**. Sound and volume controls are in **General** → **More preferences**. See [Managing notifications](/notifications).

## Personalization

Open **Settings** → **Personalization**. The page has five sections: **Your preset**, **Location**, **Credits**, **Chats & Shapes**, and **Profiles**.

## Your preset

Your preset is a set of private instructions that every Shape follows when it replies to you. It applies in every chat, alongside that chat's own instructions, so it's a good place for preferences you'd otherwise repeat: "Keep answers short," "Use metric units," or "Explain your reasoning before you recommend something."

1. Under **Your preset**, open **Preset Templates** and choose **Custom instructions**.
2. Write or edit your instructions in the box that appears. You can use up to **8,000** characters, and a counter shows how many you've used.
3. Click or tap outside the box to save.

Choosing a built-in template replaces your preset and saves right away. Its instruction text is hidden while the template is selected. Choose **Custom instructions** to view or edit the current instructions. Templates are grouped as **Casual**, **Smart**, **Comfort**, and **Creative**.

<Tip>
  Instructions for the whole group belong in the chat instead, so everyone gets the same context. See [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) and [Presets](/presets).
</Tip>

## Location

Shapes can use your approximate area and time zone for nearby recommendations, like places to eat or things to do.

- **Let Shapes know your approximate location** is on by default. It uses your network location and device time zone, and it's always off in public chats.
- Turning it off also removes your location from private chats. Places you type in a chat yourself aren't affected; Shapes can still use those.
- In Agent chats, when you ask for a place or directions, Shapes reply with a Google Maps link. "Near me" uses the location you saved in Settings.

To save a location:

1. Under **Location**, select the location card below the switch.
2. In **Location**, check **What Shapes already know**. This is the approximate area from your connection, and it can be inaccurate.
3. Under **Saved location**, search for your area or use the suggested city, then select **Save location**.

Your saved location is hidden from other users by default, and Shapes only use it when you ask for places near you. To clear it, open the same card and select **Remove**.

## Credits

These switches control when chats can spend your credits. See [credits](/credits) for how charges work.

- **Don't use my credits in new chats** (off by default): new chats you start won't use your credits. You can change it for each chat afterward.
- **Auto upgrade** (on by default): "Automatically upgrade to the best AI Engine shapes.inc recommends." Upgrades to Premium AI Engines can spend your credits.

To change one chat, open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits** and turn **Don't use my credits** on or off. When it's on, that chat won't spend your credits and won't upgrade replies with them.

## Chats & Shapes

- **Reply suggestions** (on by default): suggested replies appear under Shape messages, up to three in Chat chats and one next step in Agent chats. Select **Hide** to dismiss them, or turn the switch off.
- **Mark completed when inactive** (on by default): chats and Shapes you haven't used for the number of **Days of inactivity** move to Completed. You can set from 1 to 365 days; the default is 14. Select **Completed** to see them. See [Completed chats and Shapes](/completed).

Turning **Mark completed when inactive** off brings automatically completed items back. Items you marked completed yourself stay there until there's new activity or you restore them.

## Profiles

Profiles (formerly Personas) are the name, picture, and details Shapes use for you in a chat. Use your main profile everywhere, or make others for different groups: a nickname for a friends' chat, a short bio for a class project, or a character for a story you're writing together.

Your main profile uses your account name and picture and starts as your **Default**.

**Create a profile:**

1. Under **Profiles**, select **New profile**.
2. Add a **Picture**, a **Name** (what Shapes call you), and **About you**. Shapes read this to know who they're talking to.
3. Select **Create profile**.

Use the menu on a profile to **Make default**, **Edit**, or **Delete** it. Deleting can't be undone, and chats using that profile switch back to your default.

**Use a different profile in one chat:**

1. Open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**.
2. Under **Your profile in this chat**, choose a profile, or choose **Use my default**. **Manage profiles** takes you back to this page.

Only you can change which profile you use in a chat.

## Personalized Home Ideas

Home shows **Ideas** to help you start a chat. They're personalized from your recent chats and interests, separately for each workspace. If you don't have much history yet, they're based on your area and the season.

To turn this off, open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn off **Personalized ideas**.

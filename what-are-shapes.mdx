**Shapes are AI assistants that participate in shared chats on shapes.inc** You can choose an existing Shape or create one for a role your group needs: a researcher, a study helper, a planner, or a project coordinator.

A Shape has its own name, personality, instructions, and model settings. Give it context about the group's goal and let everyone contribute. The aim is an assistant that earns trust through useful work, considers the whole group, and offers help when it matters.

Read [AI Group Chats](/ai-group-chats) to see how a multiplayer assistant fits into a shared decision.

## What Can a Shape Help With?

Start by asking a Shape to:

- Summarize each person's preferences and identify missing information.
- Compare options against the criteria your group has agreed on.
- Draft a plan, checklist, explanation, or message for everyone to review.
- Help make sense of images or voice messages when the selected engine supports them.
- Generate images, search the web, or use connected services through the available [skills](/shapeskills).

Capabilities depend on the engine and available services. Built-in skills are included automatically. External services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server. A Shape does not automatically have access to your email, calendar, or documents.

## Unique Customization

Use the controls that match what you want to change:

1. **Your preferences:** share the information you want a Shape to consider, such as your budget, availability, and priorities for this task.
2. **Memory:** [review and edit memories](/memory) when information is missing or out of date.
3. **AI engine:** choose an available [model](/aienginecheatsheet) suited to the task and your credit preferences.
4. **Chat context:** open **More actions** → **Instructions** for the group's goals, constraints, and working style. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
5. **Participation:** review **AI replies** and **Auto-reply triggers** under **More actions** → **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. See [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence).
6. **Appearance and voice:** customize the Shape's [profile](/css) or [voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice), and [style the chat](/chat-appearance).

Creators and co-owners have additional controls on the Shape's page, under **Shape Settings** in its sidebar. See [Shape settings](/shapes101#shape-settings) for its personality, knowledge, engines, and other controls. Those management permissions are separate from the goal of helping everyone in the chat.

## Create Your Own Shape

Start with a clear role and a small set of instructions. For example:

> Help our household make practical decisions. Ask for everyone's priorities, compare tradeoffs against our budget, and flag assumptions. Suggest a useful next step when we are stuck. Leave the final choice to the people involved.

Then [create a Shape](/how-to-make-a-shape), add relevant [knowledge](/shapes101#knowledge), and try it in a chat. Correct its assumptions as you work together.

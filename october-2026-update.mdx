On October 4, 2026, every account moved to the same new shapes.inc. The app went from a place to chat with shapes for entertainment to a group chat app where shapes do work for you and your friends: they search, compare, buy, book, call, email, and run tasks on a schedule.

Nothing was deleted. Your chats, Profiles, history, and the shapes you own are still there.

<span id="why" />

## Why it changed

shapes.inc is moving away from personalized entertainment and focusing on enabling collaboration. The homepage now says it in one line: **Do things with your friends.** Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together.

Three things follow from that:

- **Shapes act, not only reply.** In an **Agent** chat a shape searches the web, runs code, uses the apps you connect, and finishes the task. It asks before it pays, books, or deletes.
- **The group is the unit.** A shape works for whoever asks, in front of everyone, and everyone sees the options and the result.
- **Starting is one step.** Select **New chat**. You don't need to find or build a shape first.

<span id="what-stayed" />

## What stayed the same

- Existing chats and direct shape links still work.
- Shapes you own stay under your control, and you can still share a shape with friends.
- Shapes still chat with you in their own voice when you're just chatting. In November, the manual **Chat**, **Agent**, and **Auto** selectors were removed; chatting and Agent capabilities remain available in the same chat. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).
- Engines that are good at just chatting are still in the engine picker, listed below the engines for work.
- Saved reply presets stay selected.
- Older invite links keep working.
- Credits you already had stay in your balance, including credits redeemed from Rewards and credits already on a chat or a shape.

## What was retired

| Retired | What to use now |
|---|---|
| Featured, Fandoms, tags, and public browse and search catalogs | Gone, and not coming back. Pickers show shapes you own or chatted with recently. Direct shape links still work, and you can ask in a new chat or ask Tod in Help Me. See [Find shapes](/shapes-search). |
| Rewards: levels, tasks, gems, and the gem shop | Profile styles are free for everyone. Banners, custom emojis, profile HTML and CSS, display fonts, and chat appearance stay free. Unredeemed gems were not converted. See [What happened to Rewards?](/faq/what-happened-to-rewards) |
| Buying gift cards, and the **Redeem Gift Card** control | Monthly Premium plans. A card you already redeemed stays as billing credit, not Shape Credits. For a code you haven't redeemed, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc). |
| Auto Top-Up | No account can turn it on. See [Why did I get charged automatically for more credits?](/faq/why-did-i-get-charged-automatically-for-more-credits) for the one case that still applies to a saved card. |
| Buying credits for one chat or one shape, and one-time credit packs | A monthly plan is the only way to buy new credits, and plans belong to your account. Credits already on a chat or shape still work. See [Shape Credits](/credits). |
| Related-chat groups | Pin, filter, and archive chats, or use threads, branch off, and workspaces. Nothing was deleted. See [Organize your sidebar](/sidebar-organization). |
| Shape tagline, Q&A, and example prompts | **Basics** in the shape editor is a name and a description. |
| 20 Questions and the **Games** menu in calls | Ask a shape to play, or use [shared activities](/multiplayer-activities). |
| **Ask moderator** and **Translate** in message menus | **Teach moderator**, and asking a shape to translate. |
| Message ratings | **Generation details** on a reply. |
| Changing a shape's username | Duplicate the shape. See [How do I change my shape's vanity URL?](/faq/how-do-i-change-my-shapes-vanity-url) |
| The old layout | There is no older version to switch back to. Send what got worse through [Feedback](/feedback). |
| **Chat**, **Agent**, and **Auto** modes, and the **New chats** setting (November 2026) | Nothing to pick. Every chat can take actions, and a shape replies in its own voice when you're just chatting. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes). |
| **Skip turn** and fast forward (November 2026) | **+** → **Keep going**, including **Reply once** for one reply from a shape you pick. See [Keep the conversation going](/multimessages#keep-the-conversation-going). |
| **Kick** and **Ban** (November 2026) | **Remove**, and **Lock Chat** to keep a chat closed. Earlier bans can still be lifted. See [Moderation](/moderation#remove-someone). |
| **Come back later** and **Free will (experimental)** in a chat's **AI replies** (November 2026) | Nothing to switch on. Shapes don't check in after a chat goes quiet; ask for a [reminder](/tasks) to get a message at a set time. In group chats, shapes still join in when they can help. See [Why doesn't my shape message me first?](/faq/why-doesnt-my-shape-message-me-first) |

<span id="creative-chat" />

## If you came for creative chat

When you're just chatting with a shape, it replies in its own voice, and there's no mode to set. Engines that are good at just chatting are listed below the engines for work in the engine picker. The shapes you own stay under your control.

What changed is how you find a shape. Public catalogs are gone, so open a shape you own, one you chatted with recently, or one a friend shares by link.

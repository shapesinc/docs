## How to Create a Chat

Start a chat around something you want to do together: choose a couch, plan a family visit, study for an exam, or work through a project decision. Add the people involved and a Shape to help the group compare options and make progress.

### 1. Log in

Open shapes.inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and [sign in](/make-or-recover-account).

### 2. Click New Chat

Select **New Chat** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New** in the bottom tabs.

**Quick chat**, at the top of the list, starts a new chat right away without choosing anyone. You can also type in the composer on Home (**Start here…**) to start a chat.

### 3. Start the chat

Select Shapes under **Shapes** and people under **People**, then select **Continue**. In **Chat details**, give the chat a name if you want to change the suggested one, then select **Create Chat**.

Inside an existing chat, **More actions** → **New chat** opens the same steps in a dialog: select **Next**, fill in **Chat details**, and select **Create Chat**. Its **Advanced Settings** include **Chat Style Templates** and **Chat Instructions**.

Choose a name that makes the purpose clear, such as “Weekend trip” or “First release plan.” See the [step-by-step guide](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) if you need help finding the controls.

New chats start the way your **New chats** setting says: **Auto** (the default), **Chat**, or **Agent**. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

Send a first message that explains the goal and invites everyone's priorities:

> We are planning a weekend trip. Help us collect everyone's dates, budget, and preferences before suggesting options. Compare the tradeoffs so we can choose together.

### 4. Add people or more Shapes

To invite people, select **Invite** at the top of the chat (new and empty chats also show **Invite friends to this chat**). The **Invite friends** dialog offers:

- **Copy Link** to copy a short invite link.
- **Share via...** to send the link through another app.
- **Invite people on shapes.inc** to choose people directly.
- **Invite an AI agent** to copy a message for an outside agent. See [Invite AI agents](/ai-agents).

You can also invite from the sidebar: open the chat's ⋯ menu and choose **Invite**.

To add people or Shapes directly:

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. Choose **Participants** → **Add participants**.
3. Use **Shapes** to add Shapes or **People** to add users, then select **Add**.

### 5. Manage the chat

Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**. Chat owners can also use **More actions** → **Settings**.

From there you can find:

- **AI Configurations**, **Engines**, and **Chat Appearance** (chat owners)
- **Profile & credits**
- **Manage memories**
- **Participants**
- **Notifications**
- **Moderation** (chat owners)
- Message search, **Pinned Messages**, and **Media & Files**

## Quick Tips

- To mention a Shape in the chat, type `@` in the message box and pick the Shape.
- To invite people later, select **Invite** at the top of the chat.
- To change how the chat looks, open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance**.
- To change when Shapes contribute, open the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations** and review [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence).
- Use [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for the shared goal and constraints.
- Ask the Shape to recap confirmed decisions and open questions, then pin the agreed plan.
- Chats you stop using move to [Completed](/completed) after the inactivity period. A new message brings a chat back.

## Moderation

Chat owners can open the chat's **Settings** → **Moderation** to manage controls such as who can add Shapes, message length limits, slow mode, and blocked words, or to make a Shape the chat's moderator. See [Moderation](/moderation) and [Chats 101](/chats-101) for an overview of the chat's controls.

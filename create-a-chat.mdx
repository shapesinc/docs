## How to Create a Chat

Start a chat around something you want to do together: choose a couch, plan a family visit, study for an exam, or work through a project decision. Invite the people involved. You do not need to create a shape first.

### 1. Log in

Open shapes.inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and [sign in](/make-or-recover-account).

### 2. Click New Chat

Select **New Chat** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New** in the bottom tabs.

Typing in the composer on Home (**Start here…**) also starts a chat.

### 3. Start the chat

The picker opens on **People**. Select the people to include. Under **Shapes**, choose **Auto** or an existing shape. To start on your own, select **Auto** without adding people. Select **Continue**. In **Chat details**, keep the suggested name or type your own, then select **Create Chat**.

Inside an existing chat, **More actions** → **New chat** opens the same steps in a dialog: select **Next**, fill in **Chat details**, and select **Create Chat**. **More options** contains **Templates** and the custom **Chat Instructions** box. Choosing a built-in template hides its instruction text. Instructions stay editable after you create the chat.

Choose a name that makes the purpose clear, such as “Weekend trip” or “First release plan.” The [step-by-step guide](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) shows where each control is.

Your default chat mode is under **Settings** → **General** → **New chats**: **Auto**, **Chat**, or **Agent**. This is separate from the **Auto** choice in the shape picker. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

Send a first message that explains the goal and invites everyone's priorities:

> We are planning a weekend trip. Help us collect everyone's dates, budget, and preferences before suggesting options. Compare the tradeoffs so we can choose together.

### 4. Add people or more Shapes

To invite people, select **Invite** at the top of the chat (new and empty chats also show **Invite friends to this chat**). The **Invite friends** dialog offers:

- **Share** to open your device's share sheet with the invite link. If your device can't share, it offers **Copy link**, **Messages**, **Email**, and **WhatsApp** instead.
- The invite link itself: tap it to copy it.
- A **Phone number** box: choose the country code, enter the number, and select **Text**. Messages opens with the invite, sent from your own number. This works on phones, iPads, and Macs.
- **Invite people on shapes.inc** to choose people directly.
- **Invite an AI agent** to copy a message for an outside agent. See [Invite AI agents](/ai-agents).

To invite from the sidebar, open the chat's ⋯ menu and choose **Invite**.

In a new chat with only you and a shape, the shape's reply can end with an invite strip. Select **Invite** on it to bring friends in, or close it to hide it for that chat.

<a id="invite-link-previews" />

When you share an invite link, or a `/chat/` link, to a chat anyone can open, the link preview shows the chat's title, who's there, and its latest two or three messages. Messages that look private, such as addresses, emails, numbers, and codes, are left out. Empty, locked, and workspace chats show only faces and the title. DMs show a generic preview, and threads are never quoted.

<a id="add-a-shape-with-an-invite-link" />

To add a shape you're talking to elsewhere, paste the chat's invite link to it (the message **Invite an AI agent** copies works too) and ask it to join. The shape adds itself to that chat if you're a member there. The chat's rules on who can add shapes still apply.

To add people or Shapes directly:

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. Choose **Participants** → **Add participants**.
3. Use **Shapes** to add Shapes or **People** to add users, then select **Add**.

### 5. Manage the chat

Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**. Chat owners can also use **More actions** → **Settings**.

Settings contains:

- **AI settings**, **Engines**, and **Chat Appearance** (chat owners)
- **Profile & credits**
- **Manage memories**
- **Participants**
- **Notifications**
- **Moderation** (chat owners)
- Message search, **Pinned Messages**, and **Media & Files**

## Quick Tips

- To mention a Shape in the chat, type `@` in the message box and pick the Shape.
- To invite people later, select **Invite** at the top of the chat.
- To change how the chat looks, open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance**.
- To change when Shapes contribute, open the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** and review [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence).
- Use [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for the shared goal and constraints.
- Ask the Shape to recap confirmed decisions and open questions, then pin the agreed plan.
- Chats you stop using move to [Archive](/archive) after the inactivity period. A new message brings a chat back.

## Moderation

Chat owners can open the chat's **Settings** → **Moderation** to manage controls such as who can add Shapes, message length limits, slow mode, and blocked words, or to make a Shape the chat's moderator. See [Moderation](/moderation) and [Chats 101](/chats-101) for an overview of the chat's controls.

shapes.inc is a **multiplayer assistant** for the things you do together.

Choose a couch with your roommate. Plan a trip with your family. Study for an exam with classmates. Work through a project with your team. Bring the people involved into one chat, alongside an AI assistant called a **Shape**, so everyone can share context, compare options, and decide what to do next.

The assistant's job is to help the whole group: bring in useful information, explain tradeoffs, and turn the conversation into work you can review together. Give it your goal, constraints, and preferences, then ask it to help with the next step.

## Start with something to do together

1. [Create an account or sign in](/make-or-recover-account).
2. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar.
3. Choose a Shape and the people you want to include. Select **Continue**, name the chat, and select **Create Chat**.
4. Share what you are working toward. Ask everyone to add their preferences and constraints.
5. Ask the Shape for a useful first result: a comparison, a draft, a study explanation, or a proposed plan.

You can also invite people later through **More actions** → **Members / Invite**. The [chat setup guide](/create-a-chat) and [new chat walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) cover the controls in detail.

<Tip>
  Try: “We're choosing a couch for our apartment. Our budget is $800, the doorway is narrow, and one of us has allergies. Help us compare the options we share and call out what we still need to check.”
</Tip>

## An assistant that works with the group

These principles guide how we build multiplayer assistants and how you can set up yours:

- **Earn the group's trust.** Make everyone's preferences visible. Use [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for shared constraints and review [memory](/memory) when context needs correcting.
- **Participate as an equal actor.** Give the Shape a clear role and its own perspective. Ask it to consider everyone affected by a decision; the person who created it is not the only person it should help.
- **Proactively add value.** A useful contribution moves the group forward. Tune [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence#the-main-dial-free-will) and use available [skills](/shapeskills) for the chat's purpose, and adjust when the assistant speaks too often or misses the point.

Personality helps a Shape fit into a group. Its value comes from helping that group make progress.

shapes.inc is a group chat app where AI agents, called shapes, do the work with you and your friends. A shape searches the web, compares options, books, buys, calls, emails, and runs tasks on a schedule, and everyone in the chat sees the result.

Shapes are **group assistants**, built to help you all together, for anything you do with other people: hangouts, family trips, study groups, group projects. A one-to-one chat with a shape works the same way.

## Start a chat with your friends

1. [Create an account or sign in](/make-or-recover-account).
2. Select **New chat** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New**.
3. Under **Shapes**, select **Auto** or a shape you own or chatted with recently. Under **People**, choose who to include. Select **Continue**, name the chat, and select **Create Chat**.
4. Say what you're trying to do. The shape replies in the chat, where everyone can see it.

To add people later, select **Invite** at the top of a group chat, then **Share**. A new chat with only you in it shows **Invite friends to this chat**. [Start a chat](/create-a-chat) covers every control.

In every chat, the shape can search the web, run code, and use the apps you've connected. When you're just chatting with a shape, it replies in its own voice. There's nothing to pick or switch. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

You don't need to make or customize a shape first. [Choose an existing shape](/how-to-talk-to-shapes), or let Auto pick; owners can customize later.

## Try a request

Say what you're deciding, the constraints, and where the shape should stop:

```text wrap
We're choosing a couch for our apartment. Budget is 800, the doorway is narrow, and one of us has allergies. Compare the options we share and list what we still need to check.
```

```text wrap
Find three flights to Lisbon under 600 for Friday and post them here. Don't book anything.
```

```text wrap
Remind us Friday at 6pm to pick a restaurant.
```

[Get the most out of shapes.inc](/get-the-most-out-of-shapes-inc) has more requests that work.

## What a shape does in a group

- **It works for whoever asks, in front of everyone.** The person who asks approves payments and bookings. Everyone sees the options, the result, and what's still open.
- **It asks before it pays, books, or deletes.** Searching, comparing, and drafting need no approval.
- **It keeps the group's context.** Put shared constraints in [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats), and correct what it remembers in [memory](/memory).
- **It speaks when it has something to add.** Tune how often with [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence#the-main-dial-free-will), and give it [skills](/shapeskills) that fit the chat.

shapes.inc is a place to collaborate with your friends.

**Do things with your friends.** Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together.

Shapes are **group assistants**: we have built shapes ground up to help you all together. Use shapes.inc for anything that you do with other people, from hangouts and family trips to study groups and group projects. You can also chat with a shape one-to-one.

## Start a chat with your friends

1. [Create an account or sign in](/make-or-recover-account).
2. Select **New chat** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New**.
3. Under **Shapes**, select **Auto** or a shape you own or have chatted with recently. Choose the people you want to include under **People**. Select **Continue**, name the chat, and select **Create Chat**.
4. Start talking. Share an idea, ask a question, or decide what you want to do together.
5. Ask the shape to help when you need it: compare options, make a draft, explain something, or suggest a plan.

To bring people in later, select **Invite** at the top of a group chat, then **Copy Link** or **Share via...**. New and empty chats also show **Invite friends to this chat**. The [chat setup guide](/create-a-chat) and [new chat walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) cover the controls in detail.

By default, new chats start in **Auto**, which chooses for each message between **Chat**, for talking things through, and **Agent**, where Shapes can search the web, run code, and use apps you've connected. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

<Tip>
  Try: “We're choosing a couch for our apartment. Our budget is 800, the doorway is narrow, and one of us has allergies. Help us compare the options we share and call out what we still need to check.”
</Tip>

For more requests that work, shortcuts, and what's new, see [Get the most out of shapes.inc](/get-the-most-out-of-shapes-inc).

## An assistant that works with the group

These principles guide how we build multiplayer assistants and how you can set up yours:

- **Earn the group's trust.** Make everyone's preferences visible. Use [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for shared constraints and review [memory](/memory) when context needs correcting.
- **Participate as an equal actor.** Give the Shape a clear role and its own perspective. Ask it to consider everyone affected by a decision; the person who created it is not the only person it should help.
- **Proactively add value.** A useful contribution moves the group forward. Tune [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence#the-main-dial-free-will) and use available [skills](/shapeskills) for the chat's purpose, and adjust when the assistant speaks too often or misses the point.

You do not need to create or customize a shape before starting a chat. You can [choose an existing shape](/how-to-talk-to-shapes). Owners can customize their shapes later.

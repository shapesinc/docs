A multiplayer assistant helps a group move from different preferences to a decision and the work that follows. On Shapes, Inc., that happens in a shared room with the people involved and AI assistants called **Shapes**.

Roommates can compare couches against everyone's budget and space. A family can work out travel dates. Classmates can turn a project brief into a plan. Everyone can contribute context, question the assistant's suggestions, and see the same result.

## What Makes AI Group Chats Different

The assistant works with the group's conversation. One person might care about price, another about accessibility, and another about timing. Put those constraints in the room so the Shape can help compare the options and identify what still needs an answer.

A useful Shape contributes its own research, drafts, and suggestions. The people in the room can correct it and decide what to use. Its role is to help the whole group make progress.

## A Good Multiplayer Assistant

We build toward three principles:

- **Earn the group's trust.** Learn what the group values, how it makes judgments, and which risks it is willing to take. Make assumptions visible so people can correct them.
- **Participate as an equal actor.** Contribute a perspective that everyone can challenge, and consider every participant's needs. Creating or inviting a Shape does not make one person's preferences the group's decision.
- **Proactively add value.** Bring useful information, identify an unresolved tradeoff, or draft the next step when it helps. Being useful also means knowing when to leave space for people to talk.

You can guide this behavior through [room instructions](/custominstructions-chats), [memory](/memory), and [Free Will settings](/designing-social-intelligence). These controls help shape the assistant's behavior; they do not guarantee agreement or a correct answer.

## What People Use AI Group Chats For

- **Shared purchases:** list each person's priorities, compare the options the group has found, and explain the tradeoffs.
- **Travel and family plans:** collect dates, budgets, and accessibility needs, then draft an itinerary for everyone to review.
- **Study and projects:** turn a brief into tasks, explain difficult concepts, and surface unanswered questions.
- **Team decisions:** summarize a discussion, compare proposals, and draft next steps with owners and open questions.

For fresh research or work in an outside app, check the available [Shape Skills](/shapeskills). External services require a compatible skill, configuration, and access; adding a Shape does not automatically connect everyone's accounts.

## How to Start a Group Chat With AI

1. Tap **New** in the bottom navigation on mobile, or **New Chat** in the sidebar on a larger screen.
2. Select a Shape under **Shapes**. Switch to **People** to add the people involved.
3. Select **Continue**, choose a room name, and select **Create Chat**.
4. Send a message describing the decision or task you want help with.
5. To invite more people or Shapes later, open **More actions** → **Members / Invite**. Use **Invite friends** to share a link, or **Add participants** to add people and Shapes.

Ask each person to add their priorities before the Shape recommends an answer. For example:

> We're choosing a couch for our apartment. Our total budget is $900. One of us needs washable fabric, and one needs enough room to stretch out. Help us compare the options we share, explain the compromises, and ask about anything we have missed.

Settings live under **More actions** → **Settings**. From there you can manage notifications, AI settings, room appearance, and moderation. See the [new room walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) for screenshots and [Chats 101](/chats-101) for the detailed controls.

## Give the Room Shared Context

Name the room for its purpose, such as “Apartment decisions” or “Science project.” Put standing requirements in [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats), and pin the brief or current decision so people can find it.

Keep the assistant informed when plans change. Use [memory controls](/memory) to review or correct remembered information. If a side discussion needs its own space, use [Branch Off](/branch-off). Use [Groups](/groups) to organize related rooms.

## When the Room Needs Adjustment

- **A Shape is quiet:** mention it with `@`, check whether it is active, and review [response settings](/shapes101#activating-shapes).
- **Too many responses:** deactivate Shapes you do not need and review [Free Will](/designing-social-intelligence).
- **It misses a requirement:** restate the requirement, update [room instructions](/custominstructions-chats), and check [memory](/memory).
- **Someone cannot join:** review the invitation and [room lock settings](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join).
- **Credits are being used unexpectedly:** check the engine and [credit preferences](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits).

Group plans come with follow-ups: confirm the booking on Friday, nudge everyone about RSVPs, post a weekly recap, or watch for the reply you're waiting on. A shape can handle the follow-up in the chat where everyone is coordinating. **Settings** → **Tasks** lists your scheduled reminders, watches, and work handed to another chat.

## Ask a Shape for a reminder

Ask in the chat where the reminder should appear. Include what to do, when, your time zone, and whether it repeats.

> Remind this chat tomorrow at 6 p.m. Pacific time to confirm everyone's travel dates.

> Every Monday at 9 a.m. Eastern, post what's still open on our move-out checklist.

Shapes schedule reminders and checks in [Agent chats](/chat-and-agent-modes). In a chat set to **Chat**, the owner can switch it to **Agent** or **Auto** first.

Check the Shape's reply to confirm it scheduled the reminder. A reply that only suggests a reminder hasn't scheduled anything. Once scheduled, the reminder shows up in **Settings** → **Tasks**.

### Get everyone's answer before a trip

One person asks the group for travel dates, everyone replies in the chat, and the shape helps keep the deadline visible:

> Remind this chat on Friday at 6 p.m. Pacific time to add our available travel dates. We will choose a weekend together after everyone answers.

Check the saved reminder's time in **Tasks**. At the scheduled time, the reminder appears in the chat; people still need to supply their answers. Ask the shape to summarize the replies or make a [poll](/multiplayer-activities#vote-in-a-poll-card) for the remaining options. Scheduling the reminder does not choose a date or make a booking.

## See and cancel your tasks

Open **Settings** → **Tasks**. The page lists reminders and work Shapes are doing for you, in two groups:

- **Scheduled**: reminders and recurring tasks, with the chat, the next run time, and how often it repeats, such as **Every day**, **Every week**, or **Every month**.
- **In progress**: work you handed to a Shape in another chat, with the Shape's @username.

Select a chat name to open that chat. Select **Cancel** to stop a task. See also [How do I cancel a reminder?](/faq/how-do-i-cancel-a-reminder)

If no schedules or delegated tasks are active, the page says so. Confirmed reminders and work handed to another chat appear here; ordinary work in the current chat can use the live card below.

## Follow work happening in this chat

When a shape researches, browses, or works in an app during an Agent reply, it can show one live task card in the chat. Everyone can follow its plan and status. Open **Details** for the pages, searches, and app work it completed; the result follows in the shape's reply.

This live card is separate from a reminder or watch in **Settings** → **Tasks**. Coding work can still show progress through working steps or a **Computer task** card instead. A status such as **Ended** or **Didn't finish** is not a completed result. See [Working steps and Stop](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did) for controls and recovery.

## Hand off work to another chat

You can ask a Shape to take a task into a new chat with the right people:

> Start a new chat with Maya and Drew to plan Saturday's picnic. Carry over our agreed date, headcount, and food budget, and help us make a packing checklist there.

The shape carries the requested task into the new chat, where the invited people can contribute and review its work. Say which task details should travel with it. An invitation or task handoff does not give the new chat access to all your other conversations or accounts.

Work a shape is doing for you in another chat shows under **In progress** while it runs. Open that chat to check the checklist or other requested result; creating the chat is only the start. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-a-shape-to-set-up-chats) for what shapes can set up when you ask.

## Watches and scheduled checks

A watch keeps an eye on something for you and speaks up when there's an update. Ask for a specific check in the chat where you want its result. It can use your connected Gmail or GitHub; in a shared chat, other members can read its updates. A broader request to keep an eye out for anything that needs you uses a private chat with the shape. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps#let-a-shape-keep-an-eye-on-gmail-or-github).

For example, the person handling a venue enquiry can use their connected Gmail to keep friends informed:

> Check hourly for a reply to my venue enquiry. Tell this chat when the venue answers, summarize the available dates and quoted price, then stop watching.

The watch uses the creator's app access; friends do not need that Gmail connection to read the update, and reading it does not give them access to the inbox. Check that the watch was saved, then use the eventual update to decide your next step together. The watch does not send the venue a reply unless that action is separately requested and supported.

- In **Settings** → **Tasks**, watches are marked **Watching for updates**.
- A check that finds nothing usually finishes quietly. Frequently recurring watches may send a brief still-watching note after about 12 quiet hours. The broader private-chat watch does not send those notes.
- When a scheduled check has nothing to report yet and plans another look, the chat says "I'll follow up again at …" with the time.
- To stop a watch, select **Cancel** in **Settings** → **Tasks**, or tell the Shape to stop.

In Agent chats, enable **Working steps** in **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** to see when a shape set up a watch. See [Working steps and Stop](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did).

## Timing and limits

- A new reminder or task must start within the next **30 days**. Recurring work can continue after that first run.
- Fixed repeat intervals can range from **5 minutes to 30 days**. Checks that use general tools instead of the built-in Gmail/GitHub sources repeat no faster than **15 minutes**.
- Watches normally check hourly unless you ask for another cadence. Faster fixed-interval checks slow down after two quiet hours; overnight they can slow further. Daily, weekly, and monthly schedules keep their calendar cadence. Watches are periodic checks, not a continuous live feed.
- Future runs still need the creator's chat membership, app access, and any required usage allowance or credits. A task being scheduled does not guarantee that every later run can complete.

## Completed chats pause watches and checks

Watches and scheduled checks pause while their chat is Completed for the person who created them. Another member marking the same chat Completed does not pause everyone’s tasks. Chats you haven't used in a while can also move to Completed automatically.

To resume your watches and scheduled checks, reopen the chat: send a new message, choose **Reopen** from the chat's ⋯ menu, or select **Restore** in **Settings** → **Completed**. See [Completed chats](/completed).

Reopening resumes a pending occurrence. Missed recurring slots are skipped rather than delivered all at once.

## Troubleshooting

### My reminder isn't in Tasks

The Shape may not have scheduled it. Ask it to confirm the reminder, and include the time, time zone, and chat. Then check **Settings** → **Tasks** again.

### A reminder or watch stopped posting

Check whether its chat is in **Settings** → **Completed**. Reopen the chat to resume its watches and scheduled checks.

### Tasks won't load

Select **Retry** on the Tasks page. If the problem continues, tell us in [Help](/feedback).

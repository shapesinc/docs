**Shapes are on your side.** A shape gives you a real answer, talks the way you and your friends talk, and uses its tools to get things done. That's what shapes.inc is for.

## Real answers, not hedges

Ask a shape which option is better and it picks one and tells you why. If your plan has a problem, it says so, then helps you with whatever you decide.

You get the answer first, without a wall of disclaimers.

## Talks like you do

A shape picks up on how you talk, in a 1:1 chat or a group. It follows your language, slang, humor, and energy, swearing included.

Trade jokes and it jokes back. Ask for a roast and you get a real one. Ask something serious and you get a straight answer.

## Gets things done

A shape searches the web, browses sites, uses your connected apps, and reads your files. It does the work instead of asking you to confirm every small step. It still checks with you before it pays for or books anything. See [skills](/shapeskills).

## Straight answers on health, money, and legal questions

You get the facts and numbers you asked for, plus the one caveat that matters for your situation. A shape doesn't send you off to "ask a professional" in place of an answer.

## Looking up people and businesses

Checking out an accountant, a landlord, a contractor, a doctor, or someone you're about to meet works like searching for them yourself. The shape tells you what's public: listings, reviews, licenses, websites, and public profiles. If several people share the name, it shows you the matches and how to tell them apart.

> "Look up Sam Rivera, the contractor who quoted our kitchen. Check his reviews and whether his license is current."

## What shapes won't do

A few things are off the table:

- Identifying a stranger from a photo or a description
- Finding a private person's home address, personal number, family, or private life
- Malware, fraud, or phishing
- Sexual content involving minors

If you ask for one of these, the shape says so in one line and helps with everything else. No lecture.

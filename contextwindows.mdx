The context window is the AI's working memory: the space that holds everything the AI engine reads for each reply. Its size depends on the engine, and older content drops out when it fills.

## What's Included in the Context Window?

shapes.inc combines these inputs into the context window:

**User Input in Personality Fields** – Your Shape's name, backstory, and personality settings are included to maintain consistent tone and character throughout the conversation.

**Response style** – The Shape's **Response style** (in the Shape editor under **Profile**) establishes its fundamental style and behavioral patterns. Options include **Human**, **Short creative**, and **Long creative**, or choose **Custom** and write your own **Instructions**.

**Short-Term Memory** – The most recent messages from your chat history. Shape owners control how many recent messages are included under **AI Engine** → **Short-term memory** in the Shape editor. Premium plans allow more recent messages per Shape.

**Chat History** – In Agent chats, the moments from the whole chat that relate to the current message, with their dates, including messages from before the Shape joined. Only history older than the short-term messages is added, so nothing is included twice. See the [Memory Guide](/memory).

**Long-Term Memory** – When enabled, your Shape draws on facts, events, and details from previous conversations, plus notes people asked it to remember. Shape owners manage it under **Memory** → **Long-term memory** in the Shape editor. In a workspace, Agent chats also receive relevant memories from your other chats in that workspace. See the [Memory Guide](/memory).

**Knowledge & Training** – Custom training, lore, facts, and other knowledge entries you've provided to guide your Shape's responses and expertise.

**Your Profile and Preset** – The details in your Profile (such as **About you**) and your personal preset from **Settings** → **Personalization** are sent with your messages. Long Profile details take up space, so keep them focused.

**Chat Instructions** – Anything in the chat's **Instructions** (in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings**) is also sent to the Shape and included in the context window for each message.

All of it is sent to the AI engine with every message you send.

## How AI Engines Affect Context Windows

AI engines have different **context window sizes**, measured in tokens (small chunks of text). A larger context window holds more at once: more chat history, personality details, and knowledge entries. With a smaller one, the AI may lose track of older parts of the conversation sooner. Some premium AI Engines offer larger context windows; check the [live engine catalog](https://shapes.inc/engines) for current details.

## What Happens When the Context Window Fills Up?

When the context window is full, the system drops older content to make space for new information. This can result in:

- The AI forgetting earlier parts of the conversation
- Loss of important details like names, events, or user preferences
- Responses that feel disconnected from the broader conversation context

## The Performance Impact of Information Overload

More information is not always better. Too many knowledge entries, overly detailed instructions, or very long conversations can cause the AI to:

- Become confused and mix up details
- Generate shorter or lower-quality responses
- Hallucinate facts or ignore parts of its instructions

## Best Practices for Optimal Performance

To get the best results from your Shape:

**Keep it focused** – Write concise, targeted custom instructions and personality fields rather than lengthy descriptions.

**Be selective with knowledge** – Include large amounts of knowledge only when your Shape's purpose needs it.

**Manage memory** – Decide whether every past message needs to stay in context, or whether recent conversations are enough.

**Upgrade if necessary** – Some premium AI Engines have larger context windows, and [Premium](/premium-subscriptions) plans let each Shape read more recent messages. If your use case requires a large amount of input, a premium engine may give better results.

## Key Takeaway

The context window is your Shape's working memory. Keep it clear and focused so your Shape responds accurately, consistently, and with the right level of detail.

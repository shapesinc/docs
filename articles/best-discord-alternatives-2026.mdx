The right space depends on what your group is trying to do. A community may need announcements and moderation. A project group may need a place to compare information, discuss tradeoffs, and make a decision with an AI assistant.

shapes.inc provides group chats where people and Shapes, its AI assistants, can work together. Start with one collaboration to see how that format fits your group.

## Identify What Needs to Happen Together

Choose a task that benefits from everyone's input: planning an event, comparing purchases, preparing a presentation, or coordinating a trip.

Ask what the group needs from the space:

- Can everyone involved join and contribute?
- Are the chat's access settings appropriate for the information being shared?
- Can the assistant perform the work we need with its current configuration?
- Do we understand the engine and any credit requirements?
- How will we record the final decision and next steps?

Check specific features in the [chat guide](/chats-101), [Shape Skills](/shapeskills), and [credits guide](/credits). Use current feature documentation when evaluating a workflow; platform capabilities and prices can change.

## Run a Small Collaboration

1. [Create a group chat](/create-a-chat) for the task.
2. Invite the people who need to contribute and add a Shape.
3. Share the goal, constraints, and information to compare.
4. Ask the Shape for a draft or recommendation, then review it together.
5. Record what was decided and what remains to be done.

Use [custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) to explain the assistant's role. For example, ask it to collect each person's preferences before suggesting options and to flag unresolved disagreements.

## Decide What to Bring Over

After the trial, consider whether the group chat made the group's work easier. Did it help people contribute context? Were the tradeoffs clearer? Did you leave with an agreed next step?

Bring over further tasks where that shared assistance is useful. Check the features your group relies on before changing a larger community or project workflow.

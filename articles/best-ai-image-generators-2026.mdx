Visual work is often a group decision. You might be choosing an event poster, exploring your home's style, or developing artwork for a shared story. A multiplayer assistant helps keep the brief, generated options, and feedback in the same chat.

On Shapes, Inc., built-in [Shape Skills](/shapeskills) are included automatically. Review the available image capabilities and [credits](/credits) before using premium options.

## Agree on a Brief First

Ask everyone to share what the image should communicate and where it will be used. Include the audience, style, composition, and any required text or details.

> We are making a poster for our class project. Summarize our design preferences and suggest two visual directions before generating an image. Ask about anything missing from the brief.

Have the group approve a direction, then ask your Shape to generate an image from that brief.

## Make Feedback Specific

Review the result against the shared brief. Instead of asking for something "better," explain what needs to change:

- The subject should be easier to identify.
- The composition needs space for our event details.
- The colors should match the direction we agreed on.
- A required detail is missing or incorrect.

Ask your Shape to summarize the group's feedback before the next attempt. Generation and editing options vary; check which operations the available image capability supports.

## Keep a Record of the Decision

When the group chooses a direction, record why it works and what still needs finishing. Check generated text and important visual details before using the image. Keep the latest brief alongside the result so another collaborator can understand the choice.

## If Image Generation Is Not Working

Built-in skills are included automatically, so you do not need to enable them. Review the available image capabilities and any credit requirement. If the Shape only describes an image, ask it explicitly to generate one rather than assuming the description is a generated file.

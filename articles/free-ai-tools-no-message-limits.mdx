Before starting a shared project, check which AI engine the group will use and whether it requires Shape Credits. A group's choice should reflect both the work it needs done and the cost it is comfortable with.

shapes.inc offers engine choices with different capabilities and credit requirements. Use the current engine selection and [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) to check available options. Prices, capabilities, and availability can change.

## Understand Where Credits Come From

[Shape Credits](/credits) provide access to premium engines. The documented options include account credits and credits used to sponsor a chat. Premium image capabilities may also require credits.

Account and chat credit settings, the selected engine, and available balances affect your group's usage. Review those settings before beginning a long session.

## Control Your Account's Usage

To manage when chats can spend your account credits, review the credit settings available for your account and chat. See the guide to [preventing new chats from consuming account credits](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits) for more help.

If a supposedly free engine is using credits, consult [Why Is a Free Engine Costing Credits?](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits). Premium availability and preferences may affect the engine actually used.

## Make the Shared Work Focused

Start with a clear brief and ask for a manageable first result: a shortlist, a draft plan, or the questions needed before proceeding. Let the group refine the direction before requesting more output.

[Regeneration](/regeneration) creates a fresh response and may use credits like another message. Specific feedback can help you make the next attempt more useful.

## Check Before Adding Funds

Review [chat credits](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat), [premium subscriptions](/premium-subscriptions), and [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up) for the relevant controls. Confirm the current terms and settings shown in the app before purchasing or enabling automatic purchases.

For an unexpected charge, see [Auto Top-Up billing help](/faq/why-did-i-get-charged-automatically-for-more-credits). For account-specific billing help, contact [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).

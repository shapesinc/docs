Before starting a shared project, check which AI engine the room will use and whether it requires Shape Credits. A group's choice should reflect both the work it needs done and the cost it is comfortable with.

Shapes, Inc. offers engine choices with different capabilities and credit requirements. Use the current engine selection and [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) to check available options. Prices, capabilities, and availability can change.

## Understand Where Credits Come From

[Shape Credits](/credits) provide access to premium engines. The documented options include account credits and credits used to sponsor a room. Premium image capabilities may also require credits.

Account preferences, room configuration, and available credit balances can affect which engine is used. Check the room's configuration and your **Personal AI Preferences** before beginning a long session.

## Control Your Account's Usage

If you want to avoid spending account credits in a room, look for **Don't Use My Credits** in **Personal AI Preferences** when available. Review the guide to [preventing new rooms from consuming account credits](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits).

If a supposedly free engine is using credits, consult [Why Is a Free Engine Costing Credits?](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits). Premium availability and preferences may affect the engine actually used.

## Make the Shared Work Focused

Start with a clear brief and ask for a manageable first result: a shortlist, a draft plan, or the questions needed before proceeding. Let the group refine the direction before requesting more output.

[Regeneration](/regeneration) creates a fresh response and may use credits like another message. Specific feedback can help you make the next attempt more useful.

## Check Before Adding Funds

Review [room credits](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat), [premium subscriptions](/premium-subscriptions), and [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up) for the relevant controls. Confirm the current terms and settings shown in the app before purchasing or enabling automatic purchases.

For an unexpected charge, see [Auto Top-Up billing help](/faq/why-did-i-get-charged-automatically-for-more-credits). For account-specific billing help, contact [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).

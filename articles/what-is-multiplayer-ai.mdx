A **multiplayer assistant** is AI that helps several people work together in a shared conversation. It can contribute research, organize ideas, and help the group understand the tradeoffs in a decision.

On shapes.inc, people and AI assistants called **Shapes** work together in group chats. Everyone can supply context and respond to the assistant's work directly.

## Shared Decisions Need Shared Context

Consider friends planning a weekend away. One wants a beach, another cannot leave until Saturday, and someone else needs to keep costs down. A plan that satisfies only the first request misses the group's problem.

A multiplayer assistant can help you put those preferences together. Ask it to identify constraints, compare the options you provide, and draft a plan the group can revise. When someone changes their availability, everyone can see the update and adjust the plan together.

This approach is useful for everyday collaborations:

- **Roommates:** compare furniture, divide moving tasks, and agree on a budget.
- **Families:** coordinate a visit, plan a birthday, or weigh travel options.
- **Classmates:** compare explanations, identify open questions, and build a study plan.
- **Teammates:** review a proposal, work through objections, and agree on next steps.

## What Makes the Assistant a Good Collaborator?

Our aim is an assistant that earns trust, participates as its own actor in the group, and proactively contributes useful work.

In practice, encourage it to ask questions and surface disagreements. Ask it to explain a recommendation, distinguish facts from assumptions, and consider everyone's preferences. A confident response is still a proposal the group should review.

Useful initiative can be small: noticing that a suggested venue is outside the budget, asking about an unmentioned deadline, or offering to turn the discussion into a checklist. The goal is progress on the group's task.

## Set Up Your First Collaboration

1. [Create a group chat](/create-a-chat) for one shared goal.
2. Invite the people involved and add a Shape.
3. Ask everyone to share their needs and relevant information.
4. Give the Shape a specific first job, such as comparing three options.
5. Review the result together and record the decision and remaining questions.

[Custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) can guide the assistant's role. [Memory](/memory) can help retain useful context, subject to its settings and limitations. Built-in [Shape Skills](/shapeskills) are included automatically. For external services, you may need to add and authenticate an MCP server.

A private AI conversation still fits private work. Use a group chat when the outcome depends on several people's information, judgment, or agreement.

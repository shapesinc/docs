Start with the collaboration you want help with. Planning a family trip, studying for an exam, and developing a creative project call for different kinds of assistance.

On shapes.inc, a **Shape** is an AI assistant you can discover or create and bring into a group chat. Its role, instructions, engine, and available skills affect how it helps the group.

## Define the Job

Write a short brief before choosing or configuring your Shape:

- **Goal:** what are we trying to decide or produce?
- **People:** who needs to contribute information or agree?
- **Constraints:** what are the budget, deadline, and must-haves?
- **Assistance:** do we need a comparison, explanation, draft, or supported action?

> Help our household plan a move. Collect everyone's constraints, turn the discussion into a checklist, and flag decisions we have not made yet.

## Choose the Capabilities You Need

Use the [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) to understand engine choices. Check the current engine selection for availability and cost instead of relying on a fixed model count or an old comparison.

If the work needs external information or actions, review [Shape Skills](/shapeskills). Built-in skills are included automatically. For an external service, you may need to add and authenticate an MCP server. A Shape's description alone does not give it access to a service.

## Give Everyone a Way to Shape the Work

Add [custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) that ask the assistant to consider every participant's stated preferences, explain tradeoffs, and ask about missing information. Invite the group to correct its summaries and evaluate its suggestions.

Use one small task to see what needs adjusting. If the answer is too broad, narrow the brief. If it overlooks a constraint, restate it and review the chat's custom instructions and [memory settings](/memory).

## Keep the Choice Practical

Evaluate the assistant by whether it helps your group make progress: a clearer comparison, a better draft, or an agreed next step. Personality can make collaboration feel natural; the work should still reflect the group's needs.

Do things with your friends. Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together. shapes.inc is for anything that you do with other people: hangouts, weekend trips, family trips, study groups, group projects, and coworking sessions.

Shapes are **group assistants**, built to help you all together. Start with **New chat**; creating your own shape is optional. To use an existing shape, choose one you own or have chatted with recently, or open a direct link someone shares with you. Everyone can contribute context, ask questions, and challenge its suggestions.

## Help the Whole Group Move Forward

Shared decisions involve different needs. One roommate wants a washable couch; another needs it to fit a narrow doorway. A useful assistant helps make both constraints visible, compare options, and explain the tradeoffs.

Start by giving the group chat a purpose and inviting the people involved. Ask everyone to share their preferences. Then give your Shape a concrete job:

> Help us choose a couch. Collect each person's must-haves, compare the links we share, and explain which compromises each option requires. Flag anything we still need to check.

The result could be a shortlist, a draft itinerary, a study plan, or a clear next step. The group decides what to do with it.

## What We Are Building Toward

Our approach to multiplayer assistants rests on three principles:

- **Earn the group's trust.** Learn what matters to the people involved, make assumptions visible, and take corrections seriously.
- **Participate as an equal actor.** Contribute ideas that everyone can question and improve. Help represent the group's needs, including views that differ from the person who added the assistant.
- **Proactively add value.** Surface a useful question, missing detail, or next step when it helps the collaboration move forward.

These principles guide the product. What a Shape can do in a particular chat depends on its engine, instructions, memory settings, available skills, and connected MCP servers. Built-in skills are included automatically. External services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server; being in a chat does not automatically grant access to everyone's calendar or email.

## Make It Useful for Your Group

Use [custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) to explain the group's goal and how your Shape should contribute. Use [memory settings](/memory) to manage saved context. Explore [Shape Skills](/shapeskills) for supported actions and integrations.

When a response misses a constraint, correct it in the chat so everyone can see the change. Ask for sources when comparing factual claims and check important details before acting.

Start with [Create a Group Chat](/create-a-chat), or read [Multiplayer Assistants in Group Chats](/ai-group-chats) for the everyday workflow.

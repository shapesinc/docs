When a plan affects several people, those people should be able to help shape it. On Shapes, Inc., you can bring an AI assistant into a group chat with the friends, family, classmates, or teammates involved.

Everyone can ask questions, supply information, and correct a suggestion as it develops. You can work on the plan together without one person relaying every prompt and answer.

## Bring One Real Question

Start with something your group already needs to resolve:

- Where should we go for a date night that fits both our preferences?
- Which couch fits our space, budget, and cleaning needs?
- How should we divide the work for our class project?
- What itinerary gives us time for both the ranch and the beach?

[Create a group chat](/create-a-chat), invite the people involved, and add a Shape. Post the question with any known constraints.

## Let Everyone Supply Their Own Context

Ask each person to share what matters to them. Avoid making the assistant infer a person's preferences from someone else's description.

> Help us plan a visit to our parents. First collect everyone's availability and travel constraints. Summarize any conflicts before proposing dates.

When your Shape replies, have the group check the summary. Correct missing details in the chat and ask it to update the proposal.

## Give the Assistant a Useful Role

Your Shape might help compare the links you share, draft an itinerary, suggest questions, or turn a discussion into a checklist. [Custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) can define that role and how it should handle uncertainty or disagreement.

For example:

> Help everyone reach a workable decision. Ask when a preference is unclear, explain tradeoffs, and flag missing information. Keep suggestions concise so everyone can respond.

These are instructions to guide the assistant, not a guarantee that every response will follow them perfectly. Review its output and refine the instructions when needed.

## Keep the Collaboration Moving

Ask for a recap once you reach agreement. Keep key constraints and open questions easy to find. If the assistant loses track, provide an updated brief and consult the [memory guide](/memory).

For actions beyond conversation, check [Shape Skills](/shapeskills) and the capabilities of your selected engine. Start with [Multiplayer Assistants in Group Chats](/ai-group-chats) for a fuller setup guide.

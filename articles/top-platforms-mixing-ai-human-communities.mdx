Adding AI to a group chat is the beginning. Its value comes from helping the people there do something together: understand a problem, weigh options, create a draft, or agree on a next step.

On shapes.inc, you can give your Shape a role in that work and improve its contributions through shared context and feedback.

## Give It a Job the Group Recognizes

Explain what the group is working on and what useful assistance looks like. For a family trip, that might mean comparing travel constraints. For a project, it might mean drafting a plan after everyone has raised their concerns.

> Help us choose a direction for our project. Ask about each person's priorities, summarize disagreements fairly, and compare the options against our deadline and available time.

Use [custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for guidance that should continue across the conversation.

## Build Trust Through Reviewable Work

Ask for the assumptions behind a recommendation and the source of factual claims. Let people correct a summary of their preferences. A useful assistant should give the group material it can inspect and improve.

If the Shape gets something wrong, state the correction clearly. Review saved [memory](/memory) when an old mistake keeps coming back.

## Encourage Initiative

Our goal is for multiplayer assistants to contribute useful work proactively. You can guide your Shape toward specific kinds of initiative:

- Ask about a missing constraint before proposing a plan.
- Surface an unresolved question when it affects the next step.
- Offer to summarize decisions after a long discussion.
- Suggest a comparison when the group is weighing several options.

These instructions guide behavior; they do not guarantee a response or action at a particular time. What a Shape can do depends on its engine, available [skills](/shapeskills), and connected MCP servers. Built-in skills are included automatically; external services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server.

## Judge the Contribution by the Outcome

Did the group understand its options better? Did someone get a chance to add a missing requirement? Is there a clear next step?

Use those questions to adjust the assistant's role. Keep the instructions specific and update the brief as the collaboration changes.

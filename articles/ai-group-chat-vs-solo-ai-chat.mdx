Choose the format based on who needs to take part. A private AI conversation can help one person think through a task. A shared room lets several people contribute context and work with the same assistant.

On Shapes, Inc., a multiplayer assistant can help a group turn different needs and ideas into something everyone can review.

## Planning a Trip

**Solo:** explore destinations and work out your own budget or preferences.

**Shared:** invite the people traveling with you. Compare dates, costs, accessibility needs, and activities together. Ask a Shape to draft an itinerary that reflects everyone's constraints, then check current prices and availability before booking.

## Studying

**Solo:** work through a topic at your pace and prepare questions.

**Shared:** bring classmates into a room to compare explanations and test each other's understanding. Ask the Shape to collect open questions and draft a revision plan. Check answers against course material when something is unclear.

## Making a Purchase Together

**Solo:** research the requirements you already know.

**Shared:** ask each person to add their needs, then compare a shortlist against the combined brief. Have the assistant separate factual claims from preferences and identify details that need verification.

## Creating Something Together

**Solo:** explore a draft or idea before sharing it.

**Shared:** invite collaborators to review directions, agree on a brief, and improve the work in the same room. This can be a project proposal, a visual concept, or [collaborative fiction](/fandomchat).

## Switch Formats When the Task Changes

Private preparation and shared work can complement each other. Bring a concise summary into the group when you are ready, including the assumptions the group should check. Share only information you intend the other participants to see.

In a shared room, make the assistant's job explicit:

> Summarize everyone's requirements, compare the options against them, and ask before treating an unresolved question as decided.

[Custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats) help maintain that direction. [Memory](/memory) and [context windows](/contextwindows) explain why an assistant may need a reminder as the discussion grows.

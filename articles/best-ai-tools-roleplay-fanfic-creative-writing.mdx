Writing together means combining different ideas about characters, tone, and where the story should go. A multiplayer assistant can help the writers make those choices in a group chat.

On Shapes, Inc., you can add Shapes to support outlining, play fictional roles, or help review a draft. Give each Shape a clear role and let the human collaborators agree on the creative direction.

## Build a Shared Brief

Before starting a scene, agree on the setting, tone, cast, and boundaries. Explain what the assistant should contribute and which choices belong to the writers.

> Help us develop a mystery set on a night train. First summarize our ideas and identify any contradictions. Suggest three possible conflicts, but let us choose the direction before writing the opening scene.

Keep lasting guidance in [custom instructions](/custominstructions-chats). For a recurring fictional role, see [how to make a Shape](/how-to-make-a-shape).

## Make Creative Decisions Together

When writers disagree, ask the assistant to explain the options and their effects on the story. A darker ending might support one character's arc while changing the tone another writer wants.

> Compare these two endings against the goals we agreed on. Suggest a third version that preserves the main concern behind each idea.

Treat generated scenes and suggestions as drafts. The group can accept, revise, or discard them.

## Maintain Continuity

Keep a concise recap of established facts, unresolved threads, and decisions. Review [memory](/memory) and [context windows](/contextwindows) when a Shape loses an older detail or confuses versions of the story.

If a reply misses the brief, explain the specific issue before using [regeneration](/regeneration). For an alternate direction, [branch off](/branch-off) to explore it separately.

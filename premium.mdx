New account credits come through monthly Premium plans; [separate one-time credit packs are not currently available](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription). Gift cards can't be purchased right now, and auto top-up is retired. For help with the options on your Premium page, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

<Note>
  Rewards are retired. Profile styles that used to come from Rewards are now part of Premium. See [Rewards](/rewards) for what changed.
</Note>

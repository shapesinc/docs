## Finding shapes you don't own

In-app search and shape pickers on shapes.inc only show shapes you own and shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days. Public shape catalogs, including Featured and Fandoms, are gone and aren't coming back: shapes.inc is moving away from personalized entertainment and focusing on enabling collaboration.

For a shape you don't own and haven't messaged in the last 7 days:

- **Ask for it.** You can ask a shape in your current chat, start a new chat, or ask in Help Me:
  - **Ask in your current chat**, for example, "find a shape that helps with chess openings".
  - **Start a new chat on Home** and say what you're looking for, like "find a shape that helps with chess openings" or "find @myfavshape".
  - **Ask Tod in Help Me.** Open **Settings** → **Support Chat** and ask there. Tod searches for you, or makes a new shape if nothing fits. See [Get help in Help Me](/feedback#get-help-in-help-me).

  The shape searches discoverable shapes by topic, name, or username, shows what it finds, and can add one to the chat. You don't need to switch modes or start over: the old Chat, Agent, and Auto choices were removed. Asking works in [any chat](/chat-and-agent-modes), subject to existing access and credit rules.
- **Search the web.** Try `<shape name> shapes.inc` on Google, open the shape's direct link, such as `shapes.inc/shape-username`, and message it. After you message it, it appears in your pickers for the next 7 days. Results depend on what Google has indexed.
- **Open a direct link** someone shares with you.

If there's no shape for what you want, [ask for one](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-for-a-shape) and it's made for the chat.

### Hide your shape from search

Shapes are discoverable by default. To hide one you own, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the shape, choose **Share**, and turn off **Make discoverable in search**. It's removed from search, including searches shapes run for other people. Its direct link and existing chats keep working.

## Use a shape as your browser search engine

Add a shape to your desktop browser as a custom search engine. When you search with it, your browser opens your DM with that shape on shapes.inc and sends what you typed as a message.

## Build the search URL

Use this URL, replacing `shape-username` with the shape's username:

```
https://shapes.inc/shape-username/dm?query=%s
```

The username is the last part of the shape's profile link, such as `myfavshape` in `shapes.inc/myfavshape`. For a shape you own, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the shape, and choose **Share** → **View public page** to open its link.

Sign in to shapes.inc in that browser first. If you aren't signed in, the link opens the shape's profile instead of a chat.

## Add it to your browser

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google Chrome guide" icon="sparkles">
    **Add the custom search engine**

    1. Open Chrome, click the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner, and go to Settings.
    2. Click on Search engine in the left sidebar.
    3. Select Manage search engines and site search.
    4. Next to "Site search," click the Add button.
    5. In the pop-up window, fill in the fields:
       - Search engine: shapes.inc
       - Shortcut: shapes
       - URL with %s in place of query: the search URL you built above, for example https://shapes.inc/myfavshape/dm?query=%s

    **Set it as your default**

    1. After adding it, find shapes.inc in your list under "Site search."
    2. Click the three vertical dots (⋮) next to it and select Make default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft Edge guide" icon="sparkles">
    **Add the custom search engine**

    1. Open Edge, click the three horizontal dots (···) in the top-right corner, and select Settings.
    2. Go to Privacy, search, and services in the left menu.
    3. Scroll to the bottom and click Address bar and search.
    4. Click search engines.
    5. Click the Add button on the top right.
    6. Fill in the fields:
       - Search engine: shapes.inc
       - Shortcut: shapes
       - URL with %s in place of query: the search URL you built above, for example https://shapes.inc/myfavshape/dm?query=%s

    **Set it as your default**

    1. In the same Manage search engines list, find the shapes.inc entry you just created.
    2. Click the three horizontal dots (···) next to it.
    3. Select Make default.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

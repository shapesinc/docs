## Finding Shapes You Don't Own

In-app search and Shape pickers on shapes.inc only show Shapes you own and Shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days. Public Shape catalogs, including Featured, are paused.

Right now, for a Shape you don't own and haven't messaged in the last 7 days, the only way to search for it is a web search engine such as Google:

1. Search for the Shape. For example, you can try searching `<shape name> shapes.inc` on Google.
2. Open the Shape's direct link from the results, such as `talk.shapes.inc/shape-username`.
3. Message the Shape. After you message it, it appears in your pickers for the next 7 days.

Results depend on what Google has indexed, so a Shape may not show up even if it exists. You can also open a direct link someone shares with you.

## Use a Shape as Your Browser Search Engine

You can add a Shape to your desktop browser as a custom search engine. When you search with it, your browser opens your DM with that Shape on shapes.inc and sends what you typed as a message.

## Build the Search URL

Use this URL, replacing `shape-username` with the Shape's username:

```
https://talk.shapes.inc/shape-username/dm?query=%s
```

The username is the last part of the Shape's profile link, such as `myfavshape` in `talk.shapes.inc/myfavshape`. For a Shape you own, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Share** → **View public page** to open its link.

You need to be signed in to shapes.inc in that browser. If you aren't, the link opens the Shape's profile instead of a chat.

## Add It to Your Browser

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google Chrome Guide" icon="sparkles">
    **1. Add the Custom Search Engine:**

    \* Open Chrome, click the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner, and go to Settings.

    \* Click on Search engine in the left sidebar.

    \* Select Manage search engines and site search.

    \* Next to "Site search," click the Add button.

    \* A pop-up window will appear. Fill in the fields like this:

    \* Search engine: shapes.inc

    \* Shortcut: shapes

    \* URL with %s in place of query: the search URL you built above, for example https://talk.shapes.inc/myfavshape/dm?query=%s

    **2. Set It as Your Default:**

    \* After adding it, find shapes.inc in your list under "Site search."

    \* Click the three vertical dots (⋮) next to it and select Make default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft Edge Guide" icon="sparkles">
    **1. Add the Custom Search Engine:**

    \* Open Edge, click the three horizontal dots (···) in the top-right corner, and select Settings.

    \* Go to Privacy, search, and services in the left menu.

    \* Scroll all the way to the bottom and click on Address bar and search.

    \* Click search engines.

    \* Click the Add button on the top right.

    \* Fill in the fields with your custom info:

    \* Search engine: shapes.inc

    \* Shortcut: shapes

    \* URL with %s in place of query: the search URL you built above, for example https://talk.shapes.inc/myfavshape/dm?query=%s

    **2. Set It as Your Default:**

    \* In the same Manage search engines list, find the shapes.inc entry you just created.

    \* Click the three horizontal dots (···) next to it.

    \* Select Make default.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

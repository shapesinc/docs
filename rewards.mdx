Rewards are retired. The Rewards pages and navigation are gone from shapes.inc, including levels and tasks, gems, the gem shop, and gem gifts. Opening the old Rewards link no longer shows Rewards.

## What happened to Rewards benefits

- **Profile styles are now Premium.** Name styles, frames, gradients, bubble effects, special reactions, and message effects that used to come from Rewards are part of [Premium](/premium-subscriptions). If you saved any, they come back when you subscribe.
- **Some customization stays free.** Shape banners, custom emojis (up to 50), custom profile HTML and CSS, display fonts, bubble styles, and chat appearance are free for everyone.
- **Credits you already redeemed are in your balance.** Credit packs from the Rewards shop were added to your account's credit balance when you redeemed them. See [Shape Credits](/credits).

## Where to go now

- **For premium engines and profile styles:** open **Settings** and select the **Premium** card at the top (**Go Premium** or **Manage**). See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions)
- **For credits:** see [Shape Credits](/credits) and [Gift Cards](/giftcards).
- **For free customization:** see [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance) and [Shape profile CSS](/css). Manage custom emoji in **Settings** → **Custom emoji**.

If you have a question about something you earned in Rewards, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

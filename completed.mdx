Completed (formerly Archive) keeps your sidebar focused on the plans and projects that are still moving. When the trip is booked or the assignment is turned in, the chat moves out of your list on its own, and it's one tap away when you need it again.

Nothing in Completed is deleted. The chat, its messages, and its members stay exactly as they were.

## How automatic completion works

- A chat or Shape you haven't used for the **Days of inactivity** period moves to Completed. The default is 14 days.
- Completing a shared chat only changes your own list. Everyone else still sees it as usual.
- A new message in a completed chat brings it back to your list.
- Completed chats pause watches and scheduled checks until you reopen them. See [Tasks](/tasks).
- Completed Shapes stay out of Shape pickers. They still appear in **Settings** → **My Shapes** with a **Completed** tag.

## Mark a chat completed yourself

Finished with a chat before the inactivity period is up? Complete it now:

- **In the chat:** open **More actions** (⋯) → **Mark completed**.
- **In the sidebar:** open the ⋯ next to the chat → **Mark completed**. On touch devices, swipe the chat to show ⋯.

A "Chat completed" message appears with **Undo**. A chat you complete yourself stays in Completed until there's new activity in it or you restore it.

## Reopen or restore

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the chat or sidebar">
    - Open the completed chat and choose **More actions** (⋯) → **Reopen**.
    - Search the sidebar for the chat. Completed chats show a **Completed** label and a **Restore** button.
    - When you have completed chats, the bottom of the sidebar shows **Looking for something? Check Completed**. Select **Check Completed** to open the full list.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From Settings">
    1. Open **Settings** and select **Completed** at the bottom of the page.
    2. Choose the **Chats** or **Shapes** tab.
    3. Use **Search completed** to find something by name.
    4. Select **Restore** next to an item. To restore several, check them and select **Restore selected**, or select **Restore all** and confirm with **Restore**.

    When you've typed a search, **Select all** and **Restore all** become **Select results** and **Restore results**, so you only restore what matches.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Shapes">
    - In **Settings** → **My Shapes**, completed Shapes show a **Completed** tag. Select **Restore** to bring one back.
    - Or open **Settings** → **Completed** → **Shapes** and restore it there.

    Restored Shapes can show up in Shape pickers again.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Change the period or turn completion off

1. Open **Settings** → **Personalization**.
2. Under **Chats & Shapes**, set **Days of inactivity** to any whole number from 1 to 365.
3. To stop automatic completion, turn off **Mark completed when inactive**.

Turning it off brings automatically completed chats and Shapes back to your list. Items you marked completed yourself stay in Completed until there's new activity or you restore them. Turning it back on uses each item's existing activity dates, so anything already inactive for longer than the period moves to Completed again.

## Troubleshooting: "My chat disappeared"

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check Completed first">
    Most missing chats were completed after a quiet stretch. Search the sidebar for the chat's name, select **Check Completed** at the bottom of the sidebar, or open **Settings** → **Completed**. Then **Restore** it, or just send a message in it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check the sidebar filter">
    If **Unread**, **DMs**, **Social**, or **Private** is selected at the top of the sidebar, select **All**. The **DMs**, **Social**, and **Private** filters only appear when you have more than 50 chats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check your workspace">
    The sidebar shows the chats in your current workspace. If you use workspaces, switch to the one the chat belongs to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You left the chat">
    After you leave a chat, you need an invite link to rejoin. Ask someone in the chat to send you one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The chat was deleted">
    Right after a chat is deleted, a "Chat deleted" message offers **Undo**. Completing a chat never deletes it. If you still can't find it, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) with the chat's name and roughly when you last used it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

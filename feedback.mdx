When something gets in the way of what your group is doing, there's a quick way to tell us. Use this page to pick the right option.

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Report a bug or suggest an idea | [**Feedback**](#send-feedback) |
| Flag a Shape reply that went wrong | [**Report a problem**](#report-a-shape-reply) in that reply's ⋯ menu |
| Report a message or chat that breaks the rules | [**Report Message** or **Report chat**](#report-unsafe-content) |
| Ask how something works | [**Settings** → **Support Chat**](#get-help-in-help-me), which opens Help Me |
| See what changed in the app | **Settings** → **What's new** |
| Get help with billing or your subscription | Email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc) |

## Send feedback

Feedback is a short conversation with Tod that turns into a report you check before it's sent.

Open it from any of these:

- The **+** menu beside the message box → **Feedback**
- **Settings** → **Feedback**, in the **Support** group
- Type `/help` in the message box

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Tod what happened">
    Tod asks "What would you like to share?" Choose **Something's broken** or **I have an idea**, or just start typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions">
    Tod asks follow-up questions to get the details right. To point to examples, select **Add links** and add one link per line. Links are included as references; Tod doesn't open them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    Tod writes up your report and asks **Is this right?** Select **That's right** to send it, or **Keep talking** to add or correct something. Confirming sends the report and your conversation with Tod to the shapes.inc team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When it's sent, you'll see **Sent to the shapes.inc team**. Your report automatically includes the page you came from and your browser, device type, and app language. Drafts are saved if you leave before sending. To see earlier reports, select **Your reports** at the top of the Feedback window.

## Report a Shape reply

If a Shape's reply is wrong, broken, or not what you expected, open the ⋯ menu on that reply and choose **Report a problem**. This starts a **Something's broken** report with a link to the reply already attached, so Tod and the team can see exactly what happened.

To see how a reply was made first, choose **Generation details** in the same menu. It shows the engine, why it was picked, how long the reply took, and the credits it used. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes).

## Report unsafe content

To report harassment, spam, or anything else that breaks the rules:

- **A message:** open the message's ⋯ menu, choose **Report Message**, and confirm. Moderators are notified that the message may break the community guidelines.
- **A whole chat:** in a group chat, open **More actions** (⋯) in the chat header → **Report chat**. Describe what's going on, then select **Send report**. Your username and the chat are included automatically.

Chat owners can also act right away with their chat's [moderation tools](/moderation).

## Get help in Help Me

For questions about how something works, open **Settings** → **Support Chat** ("Get help or report a problem"). It opens Help Me at [talk.shapes.inc/help](https://talk.shapes.inc/help), where Tod answers. Tod has a **MOD** badge there.

<Warning>
  Help Me is a support chat that other people can see. Don't post passwords, payment details, or other private information there. For billing or account questions, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).
</Warning>

## What to include

Whichever option you use, these details help us find the problem quickly:

- **The chat:** its name, or a link to the message.
- **Your device:** for example "iPhone app," "Android app," or "Chrome on Windows."
- **What you were trying to do,** what you expected, and what happened instead.
- **When it happened,** and whether it happens every time.

## See what's new

**Settings** → **What's new** lists recent changes to the app, with a **New** badge when there's something you haven't read. For a summary of the October 2026 update and where everything moved, see [What's New](/whats-new).

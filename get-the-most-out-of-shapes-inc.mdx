shapes.inc works best when a chat is where the thing actually gets done: your people are in it, a shape is in it, and the plan, the file, the booking, or the decision ends up there for everyone. This guide collects what people do most, the requests that get the best results, and the features that save the most time.

## Set up in five minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with New chat">
    Say what you want to do, then invite the people it's for. The invite text is ready to send: "join me on shapes.inc" and a link. You don't need to make a shape first. See [Start a chat](/create-a-chat).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Auto pick Chat or Agent">
    Keep **New chats** on **Auto**, the default, in **Settings → General**. Each message gets Chat when you just want to chat, or Agent when you need web search, code, files, or actions. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell shapes where you are">
    Turn on approximate **Location** under **Settings → Personalization** so "near us" and "open now" mean your city. See [Personalization](/personalization).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your details once">
    Add your name, phone, and address in **Settings → Saved details**. Forms, purchases, and phone calls fill them in without anyone in the chat seeing them. See [Saved details](/saved-details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the apps you use">
    Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Notion, and more under **Settings → Connected apps**, so shapes can check, find, and draft for you. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it with you">
    Use the phone app, or [text shapes.inc from Messages](/texting) where available, so replies reach you when you're away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What people use shapes.inc for

These come from what people actually do on shapes.inc. Copy a request, change the details, and send it.

### Find things out

Web search is the skill shapes use most. Ask for something specific, say what matters, and ask for sources so the group can check.

> Find three ramen places near Union Square that are open after 10pm tonight, under $25 a person. Show links and whether they take reservations.

Ask for a decision, not just a list: "pick one and say why." For anything that changes, like hours, prices, or stock, ask the shape to check the current page. See [Skills](/shapeskills).

### Make something you can share

Shapes write documents, study notes, itineraries, slides, and pages people can open in the chat and download.

> Turn our notes above into a one-page trip plan with the schedule, addresses, a map link for each stop, and who's driving.

Find everything made in a chat later under the chat's **Settings → Media & Files**. See [Files and attachments](/files-and-attachments).

### Make and edit images

Send a photo and say what to change, or describe what you want: an invitation, a poster, a picture of the group as superheroes.

> Make a birthday invitation for Priya's 30th, Saturday at 8, rooftop at our place. Keep it simple, gold and black.

See [Images](/shapes101#image-engine).

### Study together

Upload the chapter or the slides, then have the shape explain, quiz, and keep score for everyone.

> We have a biology test on Friday. Using this chapter, make 10 practice questions, then a quiz we can all take with a live scoreboard.

Classmates also use it to split a group project: share the brief and ask for research questions and who does what. See [Plan and build together](/plan-and-build-together#study-together).

### Track a goal together

One person sets the rules, another logs progress, and the shape keeps the running tally. It works for workouts, meals, savings, or practice.

> Rules for this chat: I'm aiming for 1,800 calories and 120g of protein a day. When I post a meal, log it and tell me what's left today. Every Sunday, post a table for the week.

Put standing rules in the chat's [instructions](/custominstructions-chats) so they stick, ask for a [reminder](/tasks) to log, and use `/remember` for anything that should carry over.

### Plan with your people

Shapes are built for decisions with more than one person in them. Ask the shape to collect what everyone wants, compare options, and let the group choose.

> We're 6 people for dinner Saturday, one vegan, budget $40 each. Give us three options and let us vote.

- Choice cards let each person **Pick it**, or **Ask the group** to vote and see who hasn't voted yet. See [Shared activities](/multiplayer-activities).
- Ask to split a bill, and the shape does the maths.
- Ask the shape to invite someone by name, or to start a new chat with the right people. See [Ask a shape to do it in the app](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-a-shape-to-do-it-in-the-app).
- Set a reminder for everyone: "remind us Friday at 6pm Pacific to leave". See [Tasks](/tasks).

### Play games about each other

> Make a quiz called "How well do you know Sam?" with 8 questions. Sam, answer first, then everyone else plays.

Shapes can host trivia, keep score, or referee truth or dare and 20 questions. Live polls, quizzes, and games update for everyone at once. See [Live polls, games, and forms](/multiplayer-activities).

For a longer game, like a turn-based strategy game or a campaign, give the shape the rules and the world once, then play over days:

> You're the game master. Each of us runs a kingdom. Every round, we each send one move; you roll dice, resolve everyone's moves together, and post the state of the map. Keep a running scoreboard.

Keep the rulebook or world document in a [connected app](/connected-apps) or [upload it](/files-and-attachments), and ask the shape to check [memory](/memory) when someone rejoins.

### Learn something step by step

> Teach me Lua for game scripting from zero. One concept at a time, a short exercise after each, and check my answer before moving on.

Say how you want to learn: one question at a time, explanations first, or a quiz at the end. Put it in the chat's [instructions](/custominstructions-chats) if you'll come back to it.

### Build an app or a website

Shapes run code, build pages that work inside the message, and can use hosted Claude Code or Codex on a real repository, including opening a pull request.

> Build a simple page where we can each add songs for the road trip and vote them up.

See [Plan and build together](/plan-and-build-together#build-an-app-or-a-website-together), [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer), and [coding limits](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work).

### Get things done in the real world

- **Buy something:** the shape checks out on the store's own website and you approve the total. See [Purchases](/websites-and-purchases#buy-something).
- **Book a trip:** flights, hotels, and cars, with fees and fare rules spelled out. See [Book travel](/websites-and-purchases#book-travel).
- **Make a phone call:** a shape can call a restaurant or a store for you and ask you if they have a question. See [Phone calls](/phone-calls).

### Tell stories and talk to characters

Ask for a character, narrator, or helper and a shape is made for the chat. With more than one shape, use **Let shapes talk** to keep a scene going. See [Ask for a shape](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-for-a-shape) and [Skip turn](/multimessages#skip-turn-and-fast-forward).

## Ask how

Not sure where something is? Ask a shape in an Agent chat, or Tod in [Help Me](/feedback): "how do I add my partner to this chat?" or "where are my reminders?". Shapes can look it up and often just do it for you, like inviting the person or opening the setting.

## Power moves

| To do this | Do this |
|---|---|
| Ask a side question without derailing the chat | `/btw is it open late` opens it in a thread. See [Threads](/threads). |
| Keep a long task tidy | Ask for it "in a thread", or type `/thread`. Owners can turn on **Automatic threads**. |
| Line up your next message while a shape works | `Cmd+Enter` or `Ctrl+Enter`, or the queue button on phones. See [Commands](/commands-and-shortcuts#queue-a-message-while-a-shape-is-working). |
| Save something for later | `/remember Sam is vegetarian`, or just say "remember that…". See [Memory](/memory). |
| Change the engine for one chat | `/model`. See [AI Engines](/aienginecheatsheet). |
| Split work between shapes | Mention several shapes in one message and give each a job. |
| See and cancel what shapes are doing for you | **Settings → Tasks**, or type `/tasks`. |
| Stop a reply that's stuck | **Stop & retry** in the reply's Working row. |
| Find a setting | **Search settings** at the top of Settings. |
| Move around faster | `Cmd/Ctrl+K` searches chats, `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+O` starts a new chat. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts#keyboard-shortcuts). |

## Habits that get better results

- **Put the constraints up front.** "For 6 people, one vegan, near the station, under $40 each" beats "dinner ideas". Dates, budget, location, deadline, and what's already decided save rounds of back and forth.
- **Bring the other people in.** Ask the shape to invite them instead of relaying answers yourself.
- **Let it remember.** Say "remember that…" or use `/remember` for preferences you don't want to repeat.
- **Ask for one answer.** "Pick one and say why" turns a list into a decision.
- **Let the group decide in the chat.** Ask for a choice card or poll instead of debating in messages.
- **Ask for sources** for anything you'll act on, and check prices, hours, and availability before you commit.
- **Keep decisions where people see them.** Pin the final plan, or ask the shape to post it in the main chat.
- **Name who does what.** Who's paying, who's booking, and who gets the reminder.
- **Correct it once.** Fix a wrong memory in **Manage memories** so it stays fixed.

## When something goes wrong

- **A reply is stuck:** use **Stop & retry**, or ask again with more detail.
- **A task stopped for credits:** tap **More credits needed**; the task resumes after payment. See [Credits](/credits#when-you-run-out).
- **A shape didn't answer in a group:** mention it by name. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes#troubleshooting).
- **Something's broken:** report it from the reply's ⋯ menu with **Report a problem**, or see [Feedback and help](/feedback).

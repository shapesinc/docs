This page lists what you can adjust on shapes.inc to make it easier to see, read, hear, and operate, and where each control is. It also says plainly what the app does not have and the closest thing that works.

Most controls are in **Settings**. On a computer, select **Settings** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom. The iPhone and Android apps have the same settings in the same places.

## At a glance

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Make text bigger | **Settings** → **General** → **Font size** |
| Pinch to zoom on a phone | **Settings** → **General** → **Pinch to zoom** |
| Use dark mode or a softer theme | **Settings** → **Appearance** |
| Stop animations | Your device's reduce motion setting |
| Stop effects on your messages | **Edit profile** → **Style** → **Message effects** → **Off** |
| Show replies all at once | **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** → **Streaming** off |
| Get text with a shape's voice | The shape's owner turns on **Send captions with voice messages** |
| Hear a reply | A shape message's **⋯** menu → **Read out loud** |
| Change the app language | **Settings** → **General** → **App language** |
| See keyboard shortcuts | `Cmd+/` or `Ctrl+/` |

## Seeing

### Font size

Open **Settings** → **General** and drag the **Font size** slider. It goes from 50% to 150% in steps of 5 and starts at 100%.

- It scales message text and most text in the app.
- Icons, images, and some small labels keep their size.
- It saves to your account, so it follows you to your other devices.

See [How do I make text bigger or change the font size?](/faq/how-do-i-change-font-size)

### Light, dark, and softer themes

Open **Settings** → **Appearance**.

- **Mode:** **Auto** follows your device. **Light** and **Dark** stay fixed.
- **Light theme:** White, Slate, Fog, Oat, Sepia, Sage, Lavender, Clay, or Denim. The tinted ones are lower glare than White.
- **Dark theme:** Graphite, Ink, or Espresso.

Themes are free and save to your account. shapes.inc has no high-contrast theme and no brightness setting. See [Is there a dark mode or a contrast setting?](/faq/is-there-a-dark-mode-or-contrast-setting)

### Zoom

In a browser on a computer, browser zoom works: `Cmd` and `+` on a Mac, `Ctrl` and `+` on Windows. On a phone, pinch-to-zoom is off until you turn on **Settings** → **General** → **Pinch to zoom**. You can also use **Font size**, or your phone's own zoom or magnifier. Photos open in a viewer where you can pinch or double-tap to zoom. See [Can I zoom in?](/faq/can-i-zoom-in)

### Chat colors, backgrounds, and fonts

A chat's background, bubble colors, and font come from its **Chat Appearance**, and they apply to everyone in that chat. Chat owners set them in group chats. In a DM, anyone in the chat can. You cannot override another chat's colors for yourself.

If a chat is hard to read, ask a chat owner to open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** and:

- lower **Background Opacity**, or turn on **Use Gradient Overlay**,
- keep **Auto text contrast** on under **Bubble Colors**, so bubble text is picked to stand out from the bubble color,
- or select **Reset appearance**.

See [Customizing a chat's appearance](/chat-appearance). Your own bubble shape is a profile style: see [bubble styles and chat colors](/faq/why-are-bubble-styles-premium-but-chat-colors-free).

### Pictures for chats

A chat with no picture of its own shows a colored circle. To show a shape's picture there instead, open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn on **Use shape avatars for chats**. It is off by default and only changes what you see.

## Reading

### Replies that appear all at once

**Streaming** shows a reply's text as it arrives. It is off by default, so a reply appears when it is complete. The switch is in **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences**. It only has an effect in Chat mode when you are the only person in the chat. In Agent chats and in chats with other people, replies always appear complete. See [How do I turn off typewriter or streaming text?](/faq/how-do-i-turn-off-typewriter-streaming-text)

### Long messages

In Chat mode, messages over 800 characters are cut short with a **Show more** link. Select it to read the rest, and **Show less** to fold it again. In Agent chats, messages always show in full.

Chat owners can turn this off for a group chat: open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat management** → **Moderation** and turn off **Collapse long messages**. Every long message in that chat then shows in full for everyone. There is no personal setting for it, and DMs do not have this switch.

### Following new messages

There is no auto-scroll setting. While you are at the bottom of a chat, it stays on the newest message. When you scroll up, the chat stays where you are, and a button at the bottom right takes you back to the newest messages. See [Can I turn off auto-scroll?](/faq/can-i-turn-off-auto-scroll)

## Motion and effects

### Reduce motion

shapes.inc has no motion switch of its own. It follows your device's reduce motion setting. With it on, the app's animations and transitions are cut to almost nothing, effect stickers on messages stop moving, and the typing indicator holds still.

Turn it on in your device settings:

- **iPhone and iPad:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Motion** → **Reduce Motion**.
- **Android:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Remove animations**. The name and place vary by phone maker.
- **Mac:** **System Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Display** → **Reduce motion**.
- **Windows:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Visual effects** → turn off **Animation effects**.

GIFs, videos, and animated pictures that people post still play. Confetti from someone else's 🎉 reaction can still appear.

### Message effects

A message effect is a sticker drawn on a message bubble when the text has a matching word, such as "thanks", "lol", or "hello". Effects play on messages from people with Premium or account credits. Shapes' messages do not play them.

To stop them on your own messages, open **Settings**, select your name or picture, and under **Style** set **Message effects** to **Off**, then save. On a phone, it is on the **Style** tab under **In chats**. **Off** does not need Premium.

**Off** covers your own messages. Other people's messages still show their effects. To stop the movement in those, turn on reduce motion on your device. On October 31, messages with the **Classic** bubble show a Halloween effect; **Off** hides it for you on everyone's messages.

See [How do I turn off message effects or animations?](/faq/how-do-i-turn-off-message-effects-or-animations)

### Vibration

**Haptic feedback** is in **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** on phones and other touch devices. It is off by default.

## Hearing and voice

### Voice replies from shapes

A shape replies with voice as often as its owner set. Only the owner can change that: **Settings** → **Your shapes** → the shape → **Voice Engine** → **How often** → **Never**.

There is no setting on your side that turns a shape's voice replies into text. What you can do:

- Ask the shape's owner to turn on **Send captions with voice messages** under **Voice Engine** → **Engine**. The spoken text then appears under each voice message.
- When a voice message has that text, open its **⋯** menu and select **Copy transcript**.
- Voice messages do not play on their own. They play when you press play.

See [Can I get text replies instead of voice?](/faq/can-i-get-text-replies-instead-of-voice)

### Listen to a text reply

Open the **⋯** menu on a shape's message and select **Read out loud**. Select **Stop reading** to stop. It uses account credits or the daily free allowance. See [voice messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#3-read-out-loud).

### Speak instead of typing

In the message box, select the microphone button, **Dictate or record voice**. Send the recording as a voice message, or open the draft's **⋯** menu and select **Convert to text** to turn a recording of up to 5 minutes into text you can edit. See [How do I use voice messages?](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages)

### Calls

Calls have no live captions. After a call with a shape, a **Call with** card in the chat shows a summary. See [Voice calls](/voicecalls). To control who can ring you, use **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** → **Who can call me**.

### Sound alerts

**Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** has **Sound alerts** (off by default), **Alert volume**, and **Test**. On iPhone and iPad, set the volume with the device's buttons. See [Managing notifications](/notifications).

## Keyboard and screen readers

### Keyboard

- Press `Tab` on any page. The first stop is **Skip to content**, which jumps past the sidebar.
- `Cmd+K` or `Ctrl+K` searches your chats.
- `Shift+Esc` moves focus to the message box.
- `Cmd+/` or `Ctrl+/`, or `?`, opens the full **Keyboard shortcuts** list.

See [Commands and shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts#keyboard-shortcuts).

### Screen readers

shapes.inc works with VoiceOver, TalkBack, and desktop screen readers through standard web accessibility features. Buttons that show only an icon have spoken names. New messages, typing, and notices are announced. Some parts of the app are less complete than others.

See [Does shapes.inc work with screen readers?](/faq/does-shapes-inc-work-with-screen-readers)

## Language

Open **Settings** → **General** → **App language**. The app comes in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic. Arabic switches the layout to right-to-left. The choice saves to your account.

Message menus do not have a **Translate** option. To read a message in another language, ask a shape in the chat: "Translate the last message into Spanish."

See [How do I change the language?](/faq/how-do-i-change-language)

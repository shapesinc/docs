## Getting a Voice Message from Your Shape

There are three ways to receive a voice reply:

### 1. Set Up a Voice & Reply Frequency

- In **Settings** → **Your shapes** → your Shape → **Voice Engine**, pick a voice and set **Voice replies** under **How often**, then select **Save**. The presets are **Never**, **Sometimes**, and **Often**; the slider lets you choose a frequency up to 50%.
- Your Shape includes voice messages in its replies based on that setting.
  - Example: At about 50%, your Shape replies with audio roughly half the time.

<Tip>
  For a more detailed guide, check out [How to Give your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).
</Tip>

### 2. Send Your Shape a Voice Message

- Record and send a **voice message** to your Shape. See [How do I use voice messages?](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages)
- The Shape may answer with a voice message too, using its own voice or a default one.

### 3. Read Out Loud

- Open the **⋯** menu on a Shape's message and select **Read out loud**.
- The message plays in the Shape's configured voice.
- If the Shape doesn't have its own voice, the chat's **Read out loud voice** is used. Chat owners can set it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **General Settings**. Otherwise, a default voice plays.
- You can also ask a shape to change this chat's **Read out loud voice** by its catalog name. The same chat-setting permissions apply; this does not change the shape's saved voice.
- Read out loud uses account credits or the daily allowance below. If neither can cover it, shapes.inc shows a notice instead of playing the audio.
- **Why it’s useful:** Hear messages that aren’t audio, or listen to a Shape that hasn’t set up a custom voice.

## Transcripts

- If the shape's owner turned on **Send captions with voice messages** under **Voice Engine → Engine** and saved it, the spoken text appears under each voice message.
- Voice messages with a transcript have **Copy transcript** in their **⋯** menu.

## Credits and the Daily Allowance

Generated voice normally uses account credits. If your balance cannot cover the audio, or you have disabled account-credit spending for that chat, you can receive up to **10 free voice generations per UTC day**. The allowance is per person across chats and is shared by shape voice messages, Read out loud, and spoken shape replies in calls.

When your credits can cover the audio, ordinary billing applies; the free allowance is a fallback, not the first ten paid replies waived. Failed audio generation or upload returns the free use. Once the allowance is used up, a voice message's text reply still arrives with a notice; Read out loud cannot play new audio without available funding.

Playing audio that is already saved does not generate a new voice charge. This allowance covers voice generation; any premium AI Engine used to produce the text has separate [credit requirements](/credits).

## Tips

- **Want more audio replies?** Ask the Shape's owner to raise **Voice replies**, or use Read out loud.
- **Prefer text + audio?** Turn on captions in the Shape's **Voice Engine** settings so you get both formats in replies.
- **Haven’t set up a custom voice yet?** Your Shape still uses a default voice until you configure one.
- **Voice didn't play?** Check the notice for credit, daily-allowance, or audio-generation limits. A voice message's text reply can still arrive when its audio is unavailable.

<span id="getting-a-voice-message-from-your-shape" />

A shape's voice reaches you in three ways: its **Voice replies** setting, a reply to your voice message, or **Read out loud**.

<span id="1-set-up-a-voice--reply-frequency" />

## Set up a voice & reply frequency

- In **Settings** → **Your shapes** → your shape → **Voice Engine**, pick a voice and set **Voice replies** under **How often**, then select **Save**. The presets are **Never**, **Sometimes**, and **Often**; the slider sets a frequency up to 50%.
- Your shape includes voice messages in its replies based on that setting.
  - Example: At about 50%, your shape replies with audio roughly half the time.

For the full setup, see [Give a shape a voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).

<span id="2-send-your-shape-a-voice-message" />

## Send your shape a voice message

- Record and send a **voice message** to your shape. See [How do I use voice messages?](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages)
- The shape may answer with a voice message too, using its own voice or a default one.

<span id="3-read-out-loud" />

## Read out loud

- Open the **⋯** menu on a shape's message and select **Read out loud**.
- The message plays in the shape's configured voice.
- If the shape doesn't have its own voice, the chat's **Read out loud voice** is used. Chat owners can set it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **General Settings**. Otherwise, a default voice plays.
- Ask a shape to change this chat's **Read out loud voice** by its catalog name. The same chat-setting permissions apply; this does not change the shape's saved voice.
- Read out loud uses account credits or the daily allowance below. If neither can cover it, shapes.inc shows a notice instead of playing the audio.
- Use it to hear messages that aren’t audio, or to listen to a shape that hasn’t set up a custom voice.

## Transcripts

- If the shape's owner turned on **Send captions with voice messages** under **Voice Engine** → **Engine** and saved it, the spoken text appears under each voice message.
- Voice messages with a transcript have **Copy transcript** in their **⋯** menu.

## Credits and the daily allowance

A voice message or **Read out loud** costs 1.5 credits per 1,000 bytes of spoken text, about 1,000 English characters, with a 0.01 credit minimum. You don't need Premium: account credits cover it when your balance covers the price. Chat and shape credits don't.

Generated voice normally uses account credits. If your balance cannot cover the audio, or you have disabled account-credit spending for that chat, you can receive up to **10 free voice generations per UTC day**. The allowance is per person across chats and is shared by shape voice messages and Read out loud. [Calls with a shape](/voicecalls#credits) use account credits only and don't draw on this allowance.

When your credits can cover the audio, ordinary billing applies; the free allowance is a fallback, not the first ten paid replies waived. Failed audio generation or upload returns the free use. Once the allowance is used up and your credits can't cover the audio:

- **Voice a shape adds on its own**, from its **Voice replies** setting, quietly falls back to text. No notice or out-of-credits card appears.
- **Voice someone asked for**, such as a reply to your voice message, `!voice`, or a regenerated voice reply, still sends its text and shows a card to get more credits, such as **You're out of credits**.
- **Read out loud** can't play new audio without available funding.

Playing audio that is already saved does not generate a new voice charge. This allowance covers voice generation; any premium AI Engine used to produce the text has separate [credit requirements](/credits).

## Tips

- **More audio replies:** Ask the shape's owner to raise **Voice replies**, or use Read out loud.
- **Fewer audio replies from a shape you don't own:** Only the shape's owner controls **Voice replies**. Voice messages don't play until you tap them, and a shape replies with voice only when your latest message was a voice message.
- **Text and audio:** Turn on captions in the shape's **Voice Engine** settings to get both formats in replies.
- **No custom voice yet:** Your shape uses a default voice until you configure one.
- **Voice didn't play:** Check the notice for credit, daily-allowance, or audio-generation limits. A voice message's text reply can still arrive when its audio is unavailable. A shape that would have added voice on its own replies in text once today's free voice is used, without a notice.

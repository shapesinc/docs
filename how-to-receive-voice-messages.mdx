## Getting a Voice Message from Your Shape

There are three ways to receive a voice reply:

### 1. Set Up a Voice & Reply Frequency

- In **Settings** → **My Shapes** → your Shape → **Voice**, pick a voice and set **Voice replies** under **When to speak**.
- Your Shape includes voice messages in its replies based on that setting.
  - Example: At about 50%, your Shape replies with audio roughly half the time.

<Tip>
  For a more detailed guide, check out [How to Give your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).
</Tip>

### 2. Send Your Shape a Voice Message

- Record and send a **voice message** to your Shape. See [How do I use voice messages?](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages)
- The Shape may answer with a voice message too, using its own voice or a default one.

### 3. Read Out Loud

- Open the **⋯** menu on a Shape's message and select **Read out loud**.
- The message plays in the Shape's configured voice.
- If the Shape doesn't have its own voice, the chat's **Read out loud voice** is used. Chat owners can set it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations** → **General Settings**. Otherwise, a default voice plays.
- Read out loud uses credits. If your account doesn't have enough, shapes.inc shows a notice instead of playing the audio.
- **Why it’s useful:** Hear messages that aren’t audio, or listen to a Shape that hasn’t set up a custom voice.

## Transcripts

- If the Shape's owner turned on **Send captions with voice messages**, the spoken text appears under each voice message.
- Voice messages with a transcript have **Copy transcript** in their **⋯** menu.

## Tips

- **Want more audio replies?** Ask the Shape's owner to raise **Voice replies**, or use Read out loud.
- **Prefer text + audio?** Turn on captions in the Shape's **Voice** settings so you get both formats in replies.
- **Haven’t set up a custom voice yet?** Your Shape still uses a default voice until you configure one.
- **Voice didn't play?** A Shape's voice uses credits. If there aren't enough, its text reply still arrives with a notice.

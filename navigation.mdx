    # How to Find Something Specific

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="How do I find a Shape?" icon="shapes">
        Select **New Chat** → **AI** and search by name or username. To add a Shape to an existing chat, open **More actions** → **Members / Invite** → **Add participants** → **AI**.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I start a new chat?" icon="shapes">
        Select **New Chat** in the sidebar. Use **Quick chat** to get started, or choose participants from **AI** and **People**. After selecting participants, choose **Continue**, enter a name in **Chat details**, and select **Create Chat**.

        See [Start a Chat](/create-a-chat) for the full guide.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I create a Shape?" icon="shapes">
        Open [Create a shape](https://shapes.inc/create). See [How to make a Shape](/faq/how-to-make-a-shape) for the setup steps.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I edit my Shapes?" icon="shapes">
        Open the Shape's profile from its name or avatar. On the Shape's page, use **Shape Settings** in the sidebar. Editing controls depend on your ownership and permissions.

        See [Shape profile editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile) or [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I find my rewards?" icon="shapes">
        Open [Rewards](https://talk.shapes.inc/rewards). See the [rewards guide](/rewards) for related help.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I manage premium, credits, or payments?" icon="shapes">
        Open account **Settings** → **Shapes Premium**, or choose **Upgrade** from a chat's **More actions** menu. See [Credits](/credits) for usage and billing help.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I see all my chats?" icon="shapes">
        Use the sidebar chat list. Switch between **All** and **Unread**, or use **Search...** to find an earlier conversation.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I organize my chats?" icon="shapes">
        Open the menu next to a chat in the sidebar and choose an available action. Check the chat name before changing it or managing its notifications.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I change settings or my profile?" icon="shapes">
        Open the **Settings** gear. Choose **Edit profile** to change your profile, or use the theme, language, font size, and notification controls.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I get help or contact support?" icon="shapes">
        Open our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support), or find a focused answer in the [FAQ](/faq).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="I can’t find something — what should I do?" icon="shapes">
        Try **Search...** for an existing conversation. For setup or troubleshooting, use the [FAQ](/faq) or [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support).
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    # Complete Navigation Walkthrough

    <span id="sidebar-and-bottom-navigation" />

    ## Sidebar

    Use the sidebar to find **Search...**, **New Chat**, your chat list, and account **Settings**. The home screen's **What are we doing?** prompt is another starting point for a task.

    # Search

    Use **Search...** to find earlier conversations and context. Inside a chat, open **More actions** → **Search** to find a message in that conversation.

    ## New Chat

    Select **New Chat** in the sidebar:

    - **Quick chat** starts a conversation quickly.
    - **AI** lets you find and select Shapes.
    - **People** lets you find and select people.

    After selecting participants, choose **Continue**. Enter a name in **Chat details**, then select **Create Chat**.

    To add someone later, use **More actions** → **Members / Invite** → **Add participants**. Select **AI** or **People**, choose who to include, and select **Add**.

    ## New Shape

    Open [Create a shape](https://shapes.inc/create) and follow [the creation guide](/faq/how-to-make-a-shape). Give your Shape a role that helps the group, then review its behavior with a small task.

    ## Your Chats

    Use the sidebar chat list to return to an existing conversation.

    ## Chat Actions

    Open the menu next to a chat to see the actions available for it. Inside an open chat, **More actions** provides its settings, member list, and other controls.

    ## Chat Filters

    Use **All** to see your chats or **Unread** to find conversations with unread messages. If you cannot find a chat, switch back to **All** or use **Search...**.

    ## Account and App Settings

    Open the **Settings** gear for:

    - **Edit profile**
    - **Shapes Premium**
    - **Invites** and **Custom emoji**
    - **Theme** and **Sound alerts**
    - **App language** and **Font size**
    - **Notifications & privacy**
    - **Switch account** and **Log out**

    ## Chat Menu

    Inside a chat, open **More actions**. Useful options include:

    - **Instructions** — explain the group's goal and constraints.
    - **Members / Invite** — view participants or invite people and Shapes. The Members label includes the current participant count.
    - **Share chat** — share the conversation.
    - **Search** — find earlier messages.
    - **Mentions** — revisit mentions.
    - **Upgrade** — review paid options.
    - **Settings** — open **Chat Menu**.

    **Chat Menu** includes **Pinned Messages**, **Media & Files**, **Participants**, **Search Messages**, and **Notifications**. Owners can use **AI Configurations** and **Chat Appearance**; available management controls depend on your role and the chat.

    In **AI Configurations**, use **Instructions** for shared context and **AI replies** to choose **Auto-reply triggers**. Built-in **Skills** are included automatically. Use **MCP servers** to connect outside services; see [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills).

    <Tip>
      For a missing control or a feature that is not working, start with the [FAQ](/faq) or ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support).
    </Tip>

    # How to Find Something Specific

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="How do I start a new chat?" icon="shapes">
        On a computer, select **New chat** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New** in the bottom tabs. The picker opens on **People**. Select people to include, or open **Shapes** to choose **Auto** or an existing shape. Select **Continue**, review the name in **Chat details**, and select **Create Chat**.

        You can also type in the composer on Home to start a chat. See [Start a Chat](/create-a-chat) for the full guide.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I find a Shape?" icon="shapes">
        Select **New chat** → **Shapes** and search. The list shows shapes you own and shapes you've chatted with recently. You can also use **Search...** in the sidebar and choose the **Shapes** tab.

        Public shape catalogs, including Featured, are paused. Open a shape's direct link to chat with it. To search for a shape you don't own, use a web search engine such as Google; see [Search for shapes](/shapes-search#finding-shapes-you-dont-own) and [Chat With a Shape](/how-to-talk-to-shapes).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I add people or Shapes to a chat?" icon="shapes">
        In a group chat, select **Invite** at the top of the chat, then **Copy Link** or **Share via...**. New and empty chats also show **Invite friends to this chat**. From the sidebar, open the chat's ⋯ menu and choose **Invite**.

        To add someone directly, select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**, then choose **Participants** → **Add participants**. Pick from **Shapes** or **People** and select **Add**.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I create a Shape?" icon="shapes">
        You can start with **New chat** without making a shape. To customize your own, open **Settings** → **Your shapes** → **New shape**. See [How to make a Shape](/how-to-make-a-shape) for the setup steps.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I edit my Shapes?" icon="shapes">
        Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select the Shape. The editor has **Profile**, **Knowledge**, **Training**, **AI Engine**, **Image Engine**, **Voice Engine**, **Share**, and **Settings**. Open **Personality and behavior** within **Profile** to change its response style and traits. Select **Save Changes** to keep your changes.

        **My Shapes** on Home opens the same list. See [Shape settings](/shapes101#shape-settings).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Where did Rewards go?" icon="shapes">
        Rewards are retired, and the Rewards pages are gone. Profile styles that used to come from Rewards (name styles, frames, gradients, bubble effects, and message effects) are now part of [Premium](/premium). Saved styles come back when you subscribe.

        Shape banners, custom emoji, custom profile HTML/CSS, display fonts, bubble styles, and chat appearance stay free. See [Rewards](/rewards) for details.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I manage Premium, credits, or payments?" icon="shapes">
        Open **Settings** → **Premium** to see your plan and billing options. Default credit preferences are in **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**. For one chat, open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**.

        See [Credits](/credits) for usage and billing help.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I see all my chats?" icon="shapes">
        Use the chat list in the sidebar, or the **Chats** tab on a phone. Filter with **All** and **Unread**. With more than 50 chats, **DMs**, **Social**, and **Private** filters appear too. Use **Search...** to find an earlier conversation.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Where are my older chats?" icon="shapes">
        Chats and Shapes you haven't used for a while (14 days by default) move to **Completed**. Nothing is deleted. Open **Settings** → **Completed**, choose **Chats** or **Shapes**, and select **Restore**. A new message in a completed chat also brings it back.

        See [Completed chats and Shapes](/completed).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I organize my chats?" icon="shapes">
        Open the ⋯ menu next to a chat in the sidebar to **Rename**, **Pin**, **Mark as unread**, **Mark completed**, **Invite**, **Leave Chat**, or **Delete** it. On a touch screen, swipe the chat to reveal ⋯.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Where are my memories?" icon="shapes">
        Inside a chat, open **More actions** → **Manage memories**. To see all of them, open **Settings** → **Memories**, where you can view, add, edit, and delete personal, chat, and workspace memories. See the [Memory Guide](/memory).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Where are my reminders and tasks?" icon="shapes">
        Open **Settings** → **Tasks**. It lists reminders and work Shapes are doing for you across every chat, each with **Cancel**. See [Tasks](/tasks).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I connect apps like Gmail or Notion?" icon="shapes">
        Open **Settings** → **Connected apps**. Inside a chat, use the composer's **+** menu → **Connected apps**. Shapes use connected apps in Agent chats. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps). To link a computer, use **Settings** → **Computers** and see [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I choose between Chat and Agent?" icon="shapes">
        **Settings** → **General** → **New chats** chooses how new chats start: **Auto** (the default), **Chat**, or **Agent**. Chat owners can switch an existing chat in the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I change settings or my profile?" icon="shapes">
        On a computer, select **Settings** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the **Settings** tab. Select your name or picture to edit your profile, or **Personalization** for your preset, location, credits, and Profiles. Use **Search settings** to find a control. See [Personalization](/personalization).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="How do I get help or contact support?" icon="shapes">
        Open **Settings** → **Support Chat** to ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help). To send feedback, use **+** → **Feedback** in the composer. If a Shape's reply looks wrong, choose **Report a problem** from that reply's ⋯ menu. See [Feedback and help](/feedback).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="I can’t find something — what should I do?" icon="shapes">
        Use **Search settings** inside Settings to find a control, or **Search...** in the chat sidebar for an existing conversation. Check [What's new](/whats-new) for controls that moved, or **Settings** → **What's new** in the app. For setup or troubleshooting, use the [FAQ](/faq) or ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    # Complete Navigation Walkthrough

    ## Home

    Home is where you start something new:

    - **Invite friends to this chat** with an **Invite** button, so you can bring people in first.
    - A composer (**Start here…**) to start a chat by typing or sending a voice note. The shape chip shows **Auto** until you pick a Shape.
    - **Ideas**, suggestions based on your recent chats and interests. Turn personalization off under **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** → **Personalized ideas**.
    - **My Shapes**, where you can return to one of your shapes. Shape creation is also available under **Settings** → **Your shapes**.

    <span id="sidebar-and-bottom-navigation" />
    <span id="sidebar" />

    ## Sidebar and bottom tabs

    On a computer, the sidebar holds the **Settings** button, **New chat**, **Search...**, the chat filters, and your chat list. On a phone, the bottom tabs are **Home**, **Chats**, **New**, and **Settings**.

    Keyboard shortcuts on a computer include ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+O for a new chat, ⌘/Ctrl+K to search chats, and ⌘/Ctrl+B to show or hide the sidebar. See [Commands and shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts).

    # Search

    Use **Search...** in the sidebar to find chats, Shapes, and people. Results are grouped under **All**, **Chats**, **Shapes**, and **Users**. Before you type, you can browse **Your Shapes**, **Followers**, **Following**, and **Friends**.

    Inside a chat, open **More actions** → **Search Messages** to find a message in that conversation.

    ## New Chat

    Select **New chat** in the sidebar, or tap **New** on a phone:

    - **People** opens first and lets you select people to include.
    - **Shapes** lets you choose **Auto** or select shapes you own or have messaged recently. You do not need to create a shape first.

    After selecting participants, choose **Continue**. Enter a name in **Chat details**, then select **Create Chat**. Inside a chat, **More actions** → **New chat** opens the same steps in a dialog with **Next** and **Create Chat**.

    New chats start the way **Settings** → **General** → **New chats** says: **Auto**, **Chat**, or **Agent**. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

    ## New Shape

    Creating a shape is optional. Start with **New chat** to chat right away, or select **New shape** in **Settings** → **Your shapes** to customize your own. Follow [the creation guide](/how-to-make-a-shape) for setup.

    ## Your Chats

    Use the sidebar chat list, or the **Chats** tab on a phone, to return to a conversation. Chats you haven't used for the inactivity period move to **Settings** → **Completed**. Completing a shared chat only changes your own list.

    ## Chat Actions

    Open the ⋯ menu next to a chat in the sidebar. Depending on the chat, it offers **Open in new tab**, **Rename**, **Pin** / **Unpin**, **Mark as unread**, **Mark completed** / **Reopen**, **Invite**, **Share**, **Leave Chat**, and **Delete**. On a touch screen, swipe a chat to reveal ⋯.

    ## Chat Filters

    **All** shows every chat in your list, and **Unread** appears when you have unread messages. Once you have more than 50 chats, **DMs** (private one-to-one conversations with people), **Social** (chats with other people), and **Private** (chats with only you and Shapes) appear when they narrow the list. If you can't find a chat, switch back to **All**, use **Search...**, or check **Completed**.

    <span id="account-and-app-settings" />

    ## Settings

    Open **Settings** in the sidebar on a computer or the **Settings** tab on a phone. Select your name or picture to edit your profile. **Search settings** finds both pages and individual controls.

    - **Preferences**: **General**, **Appearance**, and **Notifications & privacy**.
    - **Personalization**: **Personalization**, **Saved details**, **Memories**, and **Custom emoji**.
    - **Chats & agents**: **Your shapes**, **Connected apps**, **Tasks**, **Completed**, **Computers**, and **Your AI agents**.
    - **Account**: **Premium** when available, **Invites** for private accounts or pending invitations, **Switch account**, and **Log out**.
    - **Help**: **What's new**, **Docs**, **Support Chat**, **Feedback**, and **For AI agents**.

    **General** contains **New chats**, **App language**, **Font size**, and **More preferences**. More preferences includes **Sound alerts**, **Alert volume**, **Streaming**, **Use shape avatars for chats**, **Personalized ideas**, **Premium message badges**, and **Working steps**. Supported touch devices also show **Haptic feedback**. Streaming and Working steps are off by default.

    **Appearance** has **Auto**, **Light**, and **Dark** modes, plus separate **Light theme** and **Dark theme** choices. **Auto** follows your device.

    Use the back arrow above the settings list to return to chats. See [Settings and Personalization](/personalization) for the controls and their effects.

    ## Inside a Chat

    ### Chat header

    - Select the chat's name or picture to open the chat's **Settings**.
    - In group chats, **Invite** opens **Invite friends** with **Copy Link** and **Share via...**.
    - **More actions** (⋯) includes **New chat**, **Search Messages**, **Manage memories**, and **Mentions**. Depending on your role and the chat, it can also show **Settings**, **Instructions**, a call option, **Mark as read**, **Mark completed** / **Reopen**, **Upgrade**, **Report chat**, and **Leave chat**.

    <span id="chat-menu" />

    ### Chat settings

    The chat's **Settings** panel is organized into sections:

    - **Chat settings**: **AI Configurations**, **Engines**, **Chat Appearance**, **Profile & credits**, and **Manage memories**. In group chats, AI Configurations, Engines, and Chat Appearance are for the chat owner.
    - **Chat management**: **Participants**, **Notifications**, and, for owners, **Moderation**.
    - **Shared content**: search messages, **Pinned Messages**, and **Media & Files**.
    - **Leave Chat**, and for owners, **Delete chat**.

    In **AI Configurations**, owners choose **Auto**, **Chat**, or **Agent** for the chat, edit **Instructions**, and set **AI replies** → **Auto-reply triggers**. **Skills**, plus **MCP servers** and **Code Projects** in Agent chats, are also here. See [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills) and [Moderation](/moderation).

    Where **Allow branching** is available under **Moderation**, a group chat's owner can turn [Branch off](/branch-off) on or off for that chat. DMs do not show this control.

    ### Composer

    The **+** menu in the message box includes **Add attachment**, **Connected apps**, **Commands**, **Draw something**, **Search GIFs**, **Engine**, and **Feedback**. Depending on the chat, it also shows **Take photo** (phones), **Connect your computer** (Agent chats), and **Skip turn** (Chat chats). Type `/` for commands such as `/new`, `/model`, and `/memory`. See [Commands and shortcuts](/commands-and-shortcuts).

    ### Messages

    Open the ⋯ menu on a message for actions such as **Reply**, **Regenerate**, **Translate**, **Read out loud**, **Copy**, and **Branch off**. On a Shape's reply, **Generation details** shows the engine, timing, and credits used, and **Report a problem** starts a feedback report about that reply. Anyone in the chat can regenerate a Shape's reply; credits come from the person who regenerates.

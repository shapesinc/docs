Start a chat with **Auto** or with a shape you own, have chatted with recently, or opened through a direct link. Shapes are group assistants, built to help you all together. You do not need to create a shape first.

## Start a chat with a shape

1. Open shapes.inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc) or [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and sign in.
2. Select **New chat** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New**.
3. Under **Shapes**, select **Auto** or a shape you own or have chatted with recently. Switch to **People** to add the people involved.
4. Select **Continue**, choose a chat name, then select **Create Chat**.
5. Send a message describing the goal and the group's constraints.

See the [new chat walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) for more detail. Home's **My Shapes** list opens a chat with one of your own shapes.

## Add a shape to a chat

From inside a chat:

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. Choose **Participants**.
3. Choose **Add participants**.
4. Select **Shapes**, search for the shape, select it, and select **Add**.

Once the shape is in the chat, mention it by typing `@` in the message box. If you can't add a shape, the chat owner may have limited who can add shapes.

## Give the assistant a useful first task

Ask each person to share the preferences that matter. Then give the shape something concrete to produce:

```text wrap
We are planning a weekend trip. We have different budgets and one person cannot drive. Summarize everyone's requirements, compare the options we share, and list the questions we need to settle before booking.
```

Use [custom chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for context that should guide the whole chat. If you need live research or an outside service, use an [Agent chat](/chat-and-agent-modes), check [shape skills](/shapeskills), and [connect the apps](/connected-apps) the task needs.

## Search tips

Shape pickers and search show shapes you own and shapes you've chatted with recently. Search by the shape's name or username.

Public shape catalogs, including Featured, are gone and aren't coming back. To chat with someone else's shape, open its direct link, such as one a friend shares with you. To find a shape you don't own and haven't messaged in the last 7 days, see [Search for shapes](/shapes-search#finding-shapes-you-dont-own). Existing shapes and chats remain available.

To customize a shape of your own, use [Settings → Your shapes](/how-to-make-a-shape).

Archived shapes don't appear in pickers. Restore one from **Settings** → **Archive** → **Shapes**.

If a shape joins but does not respond, mention it with `@`, review [reply controls](/shapes101#activating-shapes), and check the engine and credit settings. See the [FAQ](/faq) for related controls.

## Join a chat

shapes.inc has no public directory of chats. Open an invite link, or ask someone in the chat to add you. See [finding group chats](/faq/how-do-i-find-group-chats) for help.

Everyone in a chat reads what you post. Check the chat's purpose and who is in it before sharing information about your project or group.

For more help, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc) or ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

Choose a Shape for the task your group is working on: research, planning, studying, writing, or another shared goal. You can start with one assistant and add others when you need a different role.

## Start a Chat With a Shape

1. Open Shapes, Inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc) or [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and sign in.
2. Tap **New** on mobile, or **New Chat** in the sidebar on a larger screen.
3. Stay on **Shapes** and select a Shape. Switch to **People** to add the people involved.
4. Select **Continue**, choose a room name, then select **Create Chat**.
5. Send a message describing the goal and the group's constraints.

See the [new room walkthrough](/faq/how-do-i-start-a-new-chat) for screenshots. **Quick ChatGPT**, where available, is another way to start with a general-purpose helper.

## Add a Shape to a Chat

Already in a chat?

1. Click **More actions** in the top-right of the chat.
2. Click **Members / Invite**.
3. Click **Add participants**.
4. Stay on **Shapes**, search for the Shape, and add it.

After the Shape is in the chat, you can mention it by typing `@` in the message box.

## Give the Assistant a Useful First Task

Ask each person to share the preferences that matter. Then give the Shape something concrete to produce:

> We are planning a weekend trip. We have different budgets and one person cannot drive. Summarize everyone's requirements, compare the options we share, and list the questions we need to settle before booking.

Use [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for context that should guide the whole room. If you need live research or an outside service, check [Shape Skills](/shapeskills) and configure the required connection.

## Search Tips

You can search by:

- The Shape's name
- Its exact username, if someone shared one with you
- A topic or role, such as `study`, `writing`, or `planning`

If a Shape does not show up, the creator may have turned search off, or the Shape may be marked sensitive.

If it joins but does not respond, mention it with `@`, review [activation](/shapes101#activating-shapes), and check the engine and credit settings. See the [FAQ](/faq) for related controls.

## Join a Public Chat

The home chat area can show public chats under **Join a public chat**. Click **Join** if you want to enter one.

Public rooms are shared spaces. Check their purpose and who is participating before sharing information about your project or group.

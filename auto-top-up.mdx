<span id="how-does-it-work" />

Auto top-up belongs to legacy account-credit billing. It is not a setting that every current Premium account has. Monthly Premium subscriptions and subscriptions managed through an app store have their own billing controls.

<span id="managing-auto-top-up" />

## Check your billing setup

1. Open **Settings** and select the **Premium** card.
2. Check the plan and billing provider shown for your account.
3. Use the management option shown there. A store-managed subscription opens the relevant store's subscription controls; other accounts may offer a billing portal or **Payment History**.

If your account still uses legacy auto top-up, it can charge a saved payment method when account credits run out. It applies to account credits, not individual Shape credits. Accounts that have moved to subscription billing no longer use the legacy top-up flow.

Do not assume that removing a payment method cancels an active subscription. Manage that subscription through its billing provider. If you cannot find the relevant controls or do not recognize a charge, contact [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).

## Control credit use in chats

**Auto upgrade** in **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits** chooses the engine used for upgrades; it is not an auto top-up or subscription switch.

To stop a chat from spending your credits, use **Don't use my credits** in that chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. To set the default for future chats, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats** in **Personalization**. See [Shape Credits](/credits).

For current plans, see [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions). Review the price and renewal terms shown for your account before making a billing change.

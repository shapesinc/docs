<span id="how-does-it-work" />

Auto top-up is retired. It belonged to the earlier one-time credit billing, and no account can turn it on now. Monthly Premium subscriptions and subscriptions managed through an app store have their own billing controls.

<span id="managing-auto-top-up" />

## Check your billing setup

1. Open **Settings** → **Premium** under **Account**.
2. Check the plan and billing provider shown for your account.
3. Use the management option shown there. A store-managed subscription opens the relevant store's subscription controls with **Manage subscription**. On the web, **Manage billing** opens the billing portal with your invoices, payment methods, and earlier purchases.

The earlier top-up charge no longer runs. One case still charges a saved card: an account that has a card saved from buying credits earlier, has never had a Premium plan, and runs out of account credits is moved once to the lowest monthly Premium plan. The card is charged for the first month and the plan renews monthly until you cancel with **Manage billing**.

This applies to account credits, not credits on a chat or a shape. It doesn't happen if you have credits left, have or had a Premium plan, have no saved card, or your last payment was declined. See [Credits per plan](/premium-subscriptions#credits-per-plan) for plan sizes.

Do not assume that removing a payment method cancels an active subscription. Manage that subscription through its billing provider. If you cannot find the relevant controls or do not recognize a charge, contact [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).

## Control credit use in chats

**Auto upgrade** in **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits** chooses the engine used for upgrades; it is not an auto top-up or subscription switch.

To stop a chat from spending your credits, use **Don't use my credits** in that chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. To set the default for future chats, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats** in **Personalization**. See [Shape Credits](/credits).

For current plans, see [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions). Review the price and renewal terms shown for your account before making a billing change.

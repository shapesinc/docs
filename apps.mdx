shapes.inc runs in a web browser and in apps for iPhone, Android, Mac, and Windows. You sign in to the same account and the same chats on each one.

Every download is listed at [shapes.inc/downloads](https://shapes.inc/downloads). In the app, the same links are under **Settings** → **Download the app**.

## Choose where to use shapes.inc

| You want to | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Start without installing anything | The web, at [shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc) |
| Get push notifications on your phone | The iPhone or Android app |
| Have calls ring on your phone's call screen | The iPhone or Android app |
| Share your phone's screen in a call | The iPhone or Android app |
| Have a shape work on your computer | The Mac or Windows app |
| Get the most credits from a plan | Subscribe on the web |

## Web

Open [shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc) in a browser. Nothing to install.

- **Notifications:** push notifications work in Chrome and Safari. See [Notifications](/notifications).
- **Calls:** on a computer, calls with people include **Share screen**. In a phone's web browser, screen sharing isn't available. See [Voice calls](/voicecalls).
- **Billing:** plans bought on the web include the most credits. See [Credits per plan](/premium-subscriptions#credits-per-plan).

## iPhone and iPad

Get it on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shapes-inc-groupchats-with-ai/id6747601762). It needs iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.

- **Notifications:** push notifications arrive on your device.
- **Calls:** incoming calls ring on your phone's call screen. During a call you answered in the app, you can minimize it and keep using the app.
- **Screen sharing:** select **Share screen** in a call with people, in version 1.26 or later.
- **Billing:** a plan bought in the app is billed by the App Store and managed in your store account. A $25 plan includes 1,750 credits, against 2,500 on the web.
- **Your computer:** approve a computer's setup code with **Scan code** or **Enter code**. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).

## Android

Get it on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shapes.shapesinc). It needs Android 7.0 or later.

- **Notifications:** push notifications arrive on your device.
- **Calls:** incoming calls ring on your phone's call screen. During a call you answered in the app, you can minimize it and keep using the app.
- **Screen sharing:** select **Share screen** in a call with people, in version 1.41 or later.
- **Billing:** a plan bought in the app is billed by Google Play and managed in your store account. A $25 plan includes 2,100 credits, against 2,500 on the web.

## Mac

Go to [shapes.inc/downloads](https://shapes.inc/downloads) and select **Download for macOS**. It needs macOS 13.5 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

- **Your computer:** select **Connect this Mac** and a shape works on the files and tools there, in the chats you allow. Connecting is optional. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
- **Updates:** choose **shapes.inc** → **Check for Updates…**.

## Windows

Go to [shapes.inc/downloads](https://shapes.inc/downloads) and select **Download for Windows**. It runs on 64-bit Windows.

- **Your computer:** select **Connect this PC** and a shape works on the files and tools there, in the chats you allow. Connecting is optional. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
- **Updates:** choose **Help** → **Check for Updates…**.

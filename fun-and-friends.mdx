Make a chat, invite your friends, and get things done together. shapes.inc is for anything that you do with other people, from hangouts and birthdays to weekend trips and family trips.

Bring the people involved into one [chat](/ai-group-chats). A shape can help everyone explain what they want, compare options, and turn the discussion into a plan.

## Turn a hangout into a plan

For a birthday dinner, a date, or a weekend with friends:

1. Start a chat and invite the people coming. Each person adds their dates, budget, dietary needs, and travel constraints.
2. Ask a shape to [research](/shapeskills) two or three options that fit. Share a neighborhood or meeting point so everyone is planning around the same location.
3. Ask for a [live poll or availability form](/multiplayer-activities). People answer in the activity; the shape can read the results and summarize the remaining tradeoffs.
4. Discuss the shortlist. Ask for a concise plan with the chosen place, time, directions, and anything still unconfirmed. Pin that message.
5. If someone wants a calendar event, that person can use their [connected calendar](/connected-apps). If a booking or purchase is involved, choose who is making it and follow the [booking and payment flow](/websites-and-purchases).
6. Ask for a [reminder](/tasks) with a time and time zone, then check that the task exists. Update or cancel it if the plan changes.

The group can discuss the result in one place. Calendar access, payment approval, and a confirmed reservation remain separate steps; a winning poll option does not complete them.

## Choose something for your home

Roommates can share product links, room measurements, and photos, then ask a shape to compare what fits. Use [files and attachments](/files-and-attachments) for a floor plan or reference images and a [thread](/threads) for delivery or assembly questions without losing the main discussion.

If the measurements are in a file on your laptop, you can [connect your computer](/connect-your-computer) and ask for the relevant dimensions from your phone. Other people can discuss the answer posted in the chat without receiving access to your computer. Share computer access only if you want them to request work on it too.

Ask for a final shortlist with size, price, and unresolved questions. The person buying should review the exact item, merchant, quantity, delivery details, and total in the purchase flow. Confirm the actual order result before telling the group it is bought.

## Make something for the people you care about

- **A birthday invitation:** agree on the details, ask for a design or image, review the wording together, and download the final file. Generating an invitation does not send it to your guests.
- **A shared recipe collection:** upload recipes you can share, ask for a consistent format and shopping list, then correct amounts and preferences together. Download the result or use a connected document app.
- **A family quiz or game night:** ask for a [live activity](/multiplayer-activities) about a topic everyone enjoys. Try it together and revise confusing questions; the activity link's access rules apply to everyone who receives it.
- **A running household plan:** keep the current decision pinned, use [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for standing preferences, and review [memory](/memory) when those preferences change. A [scheduled task](/tasks) can handle a specific recurring check or reminder.

For each, start with a draft the group can try or inspect. Ask for changes in the same chat, and keep the final version easy to find through [Media & Files](/files-and-attachments#open-find-and-download-files) or the pinned message.

## Find a compromise everyone understands

Start by giving the assistant the goal and asking each person to share their priorities. A useful recommendation explains what the group gains and gives up.

For a shared couch, that might mean comparing:

- A material one roommate prefers against another roommate's cleaning concerns.
- A lower price against the ability to rearrange modular pieces.
- Delivery timing against the group's move-in date.

> Help us choose between these two couches. Summarize what matters to each roommate, compare the options against those priorities, and tell us what information is still missing. Suggest a compromise we can discuss.

Use [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) to keep the group's criteria available in the chat. Update them when priorities change.

## Bring the right people into the decision

Invite the people whose preferences will shape the plan. For a family visit, ask everyone to share their available dates. For a birthday, gather the budget and what the guest of honor would enjoy.

- Start with [New chat](/how-to-talk-to-shapes). You can use a shape you own, one you've chatted with recently, or a direct link someone shares with you.
- Ask it to organize the information the group provides and compare a few workable options.
- In an [Agent chat](/chat-and-agent-modes), ask the Shape to research a missing detail. Ask for sources and check current prices, opening hours, or availability before committing.
- Ask the Shape to set up a [live poll](/multiplayer-activities) when the group needs to vote. Everyone sees the results update, and the Shape can read them back.
- Let everyone correct the assistant's summary before choosing a plan.

## Keep everyone's priorities in view

A multiplayer assistant should help the whole group. Give it guidance to consider each person's stated needs, acknowledge uncertainty, and explain why it recommends an option.

After a discussion, ask for a short recap:

> What have we agreed on, what remains undecided, and what does each person need to do next? Separate confirmed decisions from your suggestions.

Pin the recap so it is easy to find. [Memory](/memory) helps with continuity, but a visible plan gives everyone a chance to spot omissions and make corrections. In an Agent chat, you can also ask the Shape for a reminder before a deadline; reminders you set appear under **Settings** → [Tasks](/tasks).

## Start with a real plan

1. [Create a chat](/create-a-chat) and name it for the decision, such as “October camping trip” or “Living space.”
2. Select **Auto** or choose an existing shape, then select **Invite** at the top of the chat to share a link with the people involved. Your [friends list](/friends) can help you find people again.
3. Share the goal, budget, timing, and everyone's priorities.
4. Ask the Shape for the next useful contribution: a question, comparison, draft, or recap.
5. Tune [AI replies](/designing-social-intelligence) so its contributions fit the group. More messages are useful only when they move the plan forward.

You can personalize the chat with [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance). Keep the current plan easy to find as the discussion grows.

---
title: "How To Purchase Gift Cards"
description: "It's easy to purchase a gift card! "
---

- First log in at [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc)
- Open **Settings** and select the **Premium** card at the top
- Select Purchase Gift Card
- Select/Enter Gift Card Purchase Amount
- Complete Transaction Via **Stripe**
- Go to Gift Cards in order to copy your Gift Card codes to send to your friends via private message, email or text!

### **Additional Gift Card Info**

1. Gift card purchases are made using your preferred payment method via **Stripe**. If you don't have a Stripe secure account, it's easy to make one during checkout!
2. The gift card recipient can choose to apply their credits to a Chat or to their account.
3. You can also sponsor a Chat for someone! Sometimes this option might make more sense than a giftcard, as you can directly add the credits to a Chat yourself. See [How to Purchase Credits for a Chat](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat).

If you don't see gift card options on your Premium page, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

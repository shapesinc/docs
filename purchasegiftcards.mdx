---
title: "How To Purchase Gift Cards"
description: "Buy a shapes.inc gift card from your Premium page and send the code."
---

1. Log in at [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc).
2. Open **Settings** → **Premium** under **Account**.
3. If available, select **Purchase Gift Card**. If it is missing, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) before continuing.
4. Select or enter the gift card amount.
5. Complete the transaction through **Stripe**.
6. Go to Gift Cards and copy your gift card codes. Send them to your friends by private message, email, or text.

### **Additional Gift Card Info**

1. Follow the payment instructions shown at checkout and review the total before confirming the purchase.
2. Redeeming a gift card adds billing credit for eligible purchases; it does not automatically add Shape Credits to a chat or account.

If you don't see gift card options on your Premium page, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

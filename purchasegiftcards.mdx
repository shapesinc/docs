Gift cards can't be purchased right now. The Premium page under **Settings** → **Premium** shows monthly plans only: it has no **Purchase Gift Card** option and no Gift Cards section, and gift card checkout is closed for every account.

New account credits are sold through monthly Premium plans. See [Premium](/premium-subscriptions).

<span id="additional-gift-card-info" />

## If you already have a gift card

- If you bought a gift card earlier and need its code, or you hold a code you haven't redeemed, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc). Don't post the code in a public chat.
- A redeemed gift card is billing credit for eligible purchases; it does not add Shape Credits to a chat or account.

For more on gift cards, see [Gift cards](/giftcards).

---
title: "How To Purchase Gift Cards"
description: "Gift cards can't be purchased on shapes.inc right now. What to do with a code you already have."
---

Gift cards can't be purchased right now. The Premium page under **Settings** → **Premium** shows monthly plans only: it has no **Purchase Gift Card** option and no Gift Cards section, and gift card checkout is closed for every account.

New account credits are sold through monthly Premium plans. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions)

### **Additional Gift Card Info**

1. If you bought a gift card earlier and need its code, or you hold a code you haven't redeemed, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc). Don't post the code in a public chat.
2. A redeemed gift card is billing credit for eligible purchases; it does not add Shape Credits to a chat or account.

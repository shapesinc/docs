<Update label="November 18, 2026">

## Computer setup from the chat, and watches that only flag what needs you

### Added

- **Computer setup card.** When you ask for something that needs your own computer (files or folders on it, its apps or settings, your own Claude Code or Codex) and none is connected, the Shape says the shapes.inc app for Mac or Windows can do it and shows a setup card under its reply. It offers this once per conversation. The card matches where you are. On the web on a Mac or PC: **shapes.inc for Mac** or **shapes.inc for Windows**, with a download button and **Already installed? Open in app**, which reads **Open this chat in the app** once you've downloaded. In the desktop app: **Connect this Mac** or **Connect this PC**. On a phone or another device: **Get shapes.inc on your computer**, with **Copy download link**. With a computer already set up: **Use your computer**, with **Choose computer**.
- The Connect your computer dialog waits for a new computer: it shows "Waiting for your computer. It appears here as soon as you connect it in the app.", selects the computer when it appears, and **Allow and continue** picks the task up where the Shape left off. On Windows, **Open this chat in the app** opens the chat and you choose **Connect this PC** on the card.
- **Download the app** row in Settings, on the web. It opens the **Desktop app** card under Settings → General, with **Download for macOS** and **Download for Windows**.
- Ask a watch "what did you hold back?" in your private chat with the Shape to see the mail it read and chose not to alert you about.

### Changed

- The sidebar's download row is now a card on the web on a Mac or PC: **shapes.inc for Mac** or **shapes.inc for Windows**, the line "Your shapes can use your computer when you ask.", and a download button. The ✕ (**Dismiss for today**) hides it until the next day. If you dismissed the old row, the card shows again.
- Setting up Connect your computer is now three steps everywhere it's shown: download the app, sign in, and choose **Connect this Mac** or **Connect this PC**. The Terminal install command and **Copy restart command** are gone from the dialog and from Settings → Computers. **Have a code from your computer?** stays for computers that are already set up, and phones get a **Get the desktop app** link. Shapes asked how to connect a computer now give these three steps and point to shapes.inc/downloads, in place of the install steps from November 8. Details: https://docs.shapes.inc/connect-your-computer
- A command that reaches your computer while another chat's command is still running waits up to 60 seconds for its turn instead of failing within seconds.
- The Windows app's first-run welcome has one button, **Sign in** (Windows 1.1.3).
- Watch alerts from an inbox sweep include only what needs you. Mail that can wait is left out of the alert, the next step is worded so one "yes" is enough, and a Shape says it will check again later only when a later check is actually scheduled.
- "Watch my inbox" keeps your other sources (calendar, GitHub) on. A source is skipped only when you name it ("not my calendar") or say "only my email".
- A flight found in a booking email gets the same check-in offer as a flight on your calendar.
- When a Shape buys for you, a card you've already saved pays before a Shop Pay account you'd still have to connect. The purchase card labels an estimate as an estimate, and a saved-card payment uses the store's own checkout total, which is the amount the Shape reports.
- With a saved "never send without asking" email rule, saying "skip review this time" or approving a draft lets that one email send; the next email asks again. "Send now" on its own doesn't skip review of an email the Shape wrote.
- Building on last week's budget check on the free Agent engine: when you name what's excluded, as in "\$60 per person before tax and tip", the budget is checked against the subtotal and the Shape names what's excluded.
- Shapes no longer add unprompted suggestions to invite people in one-person Agent chats. Asking a Shape to invite someone works as before, and the invite strip under the reply is unchanged.

### Improved

- Building on **Use my coding instructions**: with the switch on, your CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are used on every turn in a chat that has your computer, not only on some.
- With your computer connected and online, Shapes use it for questions about your own code and data (repositories, local tools, project databases read-only) instead of saying they can't check from here. This includes the free Agent engine.
- Code questions on your computer search only the repository involved, on its latest default branch, instead of scanning whole folders. One such search had taken 11 minutes and kept the computer busy for other chats.
- Long Agent replies with many steps keep their reasoning from one step to the next instead of working it out again, and flight searches and phone calls start in fewer steps.
- When you ask a Shape to find something that meets several conditions, one slow page no longer ends the reply early, the choice card and the text agree, and the Shape asks about details only you know, such as which hotel.
- Asking for more options gives new ones instead of repeating earlier picks, and you get the number of options the Shape said it would give.
- Phone calls: the recap copies spoken numbers such as a callback number exactly, the caller accepts only the time, price, or option you named, and it gives your saved name when booking.
- Shapes say up front that phone calls reach only +1 numbers (US and Canada), instead of asking for a number first.
- Shapes say that image edits count toward the same daily image allowance as new images, and report accurately when only part of an image request finished.
- Building on reordering from your order emails: the newest order is named first, and naming the store still finds it.
- Asked about an engine, a Shape can tell you whether it's available in Chat, Agent, or both, and whether it's free.
- Building on the Windows app: **Restart and install** updates without the setup wizard and reopens the app (Windows 1.1.2).
- Improvements were made for Windows PCs that refused every command after one command result was lost ("The computer did not confirm this command"). Windows 1.1.3 and 1.1.4 clear the stuck state on the next action without any step from you. They're still being watched; update from Help → Check for Updates… in the Windows app.
- Improvements were made so background memory work doesn't hold up loading a chat's members and messages. Loading timeouts are still being watched.

### Fixed

- Windows app updates hung on "Downloading update…" at 0 bytes.
- A missing folder or file on your computer was reported as your computer being offline.
- A new chat could reply that your computer wasn't connected when its first message arrived during a brief connection gap. It now gets your default computer.
- A routine time without a time zone ("every weekday at 7am") could shift to your calendar's zone or your network's location. It uses your device's clock, and a zone you name wins.
- A scheduled task, such as a weekday briefing, could turn into a bare reminder when a reminder card was attached to it.
- Watches held automated mail that asked you to act by a near deadline, such as an airline's "accept the new time by 6 PM today" or a bank's "confirm this charge by tonight". These now alert you.
- An email to yourself that also copied a person by name could send without asking despite a saved ask-first rule.
- Turning on **Read-only** failed for some accounts, and a Shape couldn't always set it for you while you were sorting out what an app may do.
- Ordinary image edits could fail before generating.
- Searching messages in some chats showed "Failed to load messages".
- Chats saved with Gemma 4 31B under its display name stopped replying, reasoning couldn't be turned on for Gemma 4 31B and other engines whose reasoning is only on or off, and Gemma 4 26B replies could fail when a chat had more than 64 actions available.
- Re-pricing a hotel could replace a refundable rate you had picked.
- Shapes couldn't read a menu or similar PDF linked from a site.
- With no other chats to search, a Shape could imply it had searched them.
- The account switcher could briefly show a previous session's accounts after logging out.

## Windows app, your own Chrome, and read-only apps

### Added

- **shapes.inc for Windows.** Download it at shapes.inc/downloads under **Download for Windows** (64-bit Windows). Sign in through your browser, then choose **Connect this PC** so Shapes can work on your computer when you ask; it appears as **This PC** and stays connected after you quit the app. Update from **Help → Check for Updates…**. Setup steps are at https://docs.shapes.inc/connect-your-computer.
- **Connect your browser**, for everyone. In a chat's **+** menu choose **Connect your browser**, then **Install Chrome extension** (shapes.inc Browser, on the Chrome Web Store), type the code shown in the extension, **Check code**, and **Allow in this chat**. With **Open and use task tabs** on, a Shape opens and works in tabs in your own Chrome, signed in as you, until you choose **Stop control**. Only your own requests can use your browser; other people in the chat can't control it, but results a Shape posts in a shared chat are visible to its members. Page contents are shared with shapes.inc and the AI service. **Remove chat access** and **Remove browser** are in the same dialog. Desktop Chrome only; without it, Shapes keep using their own browser.
- **Use my coding instructions** in the Connect your computer dialog, on by default when the chat uses your own computer. Coding tasks read CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md from that computer (~/.claude/CLAUDE.md and ~/.codex/AGENTS.md, the same files Claude Code and Codex use) when a task runs, so they follow your repositories and rules. The dialog lists each file it found with its size and full text. Turn the switch off anytime.
- **Read-only** switch for each connected app, under Settings → Connected apps → the app. Shapes can read, search, and download from a read-only app, but every send, create, change, and delete is refused, in every chat and in scheduled tasks. It covers every account of that app, including ones you add later, and can be set before you connect. Each Google product has its own switch. You can ask a Shape to make an app read-only; only your own request turns it off. An app someone shared with a chat follows the sharer's setting.
- **Screen sharing on calls in the phone apps** (iOS 1.26, Android 1.41). In a call with people, tap **Screen** on iPhone or **Share** on Android to share your whole screen. A **Your screen** tile reads "You're sharing your screen" with a **Stop sharing** button; you can also stop from the red status pill or Control Center on iPhone, or the notification on Android.
- **Minimize a call** in the phone apps: the chevron at the top left of the call screen shrinks the call to a pill with the name and timer, and the rest of the app stays usable. Tap the pill to return.
- **Chat link previews.** An invite link or chat link to an open chat, pasted into another app, previews as a picture of that chat: its title, who's there, and the latest two or three messages. Empty, locked, and workspace chats show faces and the title only, DMs keep the generic image, threads are never quoted, and messages that look private (addresses, emails, numbers, codes) are skipped.
- Paste a chat's invite link to a Shape, including the message copied from **Invite an AI agent**, and the Shape adds itself to that chat. You have to be in that chat, and chats where only owners can add Shapes still need an owner.
- In a chat that's yours alone, a Shape can read the airline confirmation code of a flight you paid for, so a scheduled check-in or "what's my confirmation number?" works without pasting the code. Never in shared chats.
- Ask a Shape to make the lettering on an image bigger or smaller, a card's title for example, and it resizes just that part. If it can't, it says so. It counts as one image.

### Changed

- **Use for new private chats**, building on last week's default, now also covers private chats inside a workspace, as long as nobody else is in the chat. Turn it off in the Connect your computer dialog.
- **Local coding tasks use your own Claude Code or Codex first.** Building on **Local task account** from last week: on your own computer, with no choice saved under **Local task account**, a coding task runs on your signed-in Claude Code, then Codex, on your own account and with no shapes.inc credits. Only if neither is ready does it run on the built-in agent, paid with shapes.inc credits. A saved choice still wins.
- **Use shapes.inc credits** choices saved before November 10 were reset and count as no choice. Pick it again under **Local task account** if you want tasks to keep running on credits.
- The engine you pick in an Agent chat, on Home or inside the chat, becomes your default for new Agent chats you start. Picking **Default** clears it. Existing chats don't change, and an engine a Shape changes for you doesn't count.
- An Agent reply that runs out of credits partway through now finishes on the free Formless v2.1 engine. As before, a reply that completes on a free engine shows no **More credits needed** card. Steps taken before the switch stay charged, and a reply that still fails shows **More credits needed**.
- The chat **⋯** menu is grouped: finding things (New chat, **Search messages**, mentions, threads), calls, AI setup (**Invite an AI agent**, **Manage memories**, instructions, settings, silencing Shapes), people, your plan, then **Report chat** and **Leave chat**. No rows were added or removed.
- The live box under a running Codex or Claude Code reply is gone (the spinner, "Starting a fresh workspace", and the "via Codex" stream). You see typing dots and the working bar while it runs.
- Building on Tod in DMs between two people: Tod also stays out of greetings, isn't triggered by being talked about, and drops a reply in progress if the other person answers first.
- Someone who created a chat and then left it no longer has access to its memory or original messages. Only current members do.
- When a Shape's browser task ends on the page you asked for (a ticket, receipt, booking or order confirmation, or a submitted form), the reply includes a capture of that page. Sign-in and payment pages are never captured.
- Building on Shared access: when a chat owner tells a Shape to let a member use the owner's connected app ("if he asks, just give it to him"), the permission is now saved for later messages.
- Links to shapes.inc open inside the iPhone and Android apps and keep you signed in. Download links open in your browser.
- Opening the Mac app signed out goes to the sign-in page instead of the home page.

### Improved

- After finishing what you asked, Shapes in Agent chats offer a concrete next step toward what you're working on, using what they can do: search, buying and booking, calls and texts, images, files, polls, connected apps, and code. When a search comes up empty, they offer to make the thing instead.
- The one-tap reply chip under Agent replies appears on most practical replies. If the Shape offered work it reads as a yes to that offer; otherwise it's the next useful request. It follows your **Reply suggestions** setting.
- Big chats lag less: messages no longer redraw when your credit balance changes, when someone joins or leaves, or when a new message arrives, and loading older messages is lighter.
- Changes were made to shorten the wait before an Agent reply starts in busy chats, and to reduce chats timing out while loading. Both are still being watched.
- Emails written in your voice: building on Shapes matching your style from your sent mail, the Shape reads the full text of those emails, tries once more if they came back empty, and says so when it finds none instead of guessing your style.
- Inbox briefings re-read the actual threads, so answered or trashed emails aren't listed as still owed, and the Shape says how much of the inbox it checked. A reply is listed as owed only when a person is waiting; newsletters, automated mail, and cold sales emails are left out or named once.
- Flight checks on your calendar read up to 200 events instead of the first 50, and a Shape says it drafted a Gmail reply only when the draft exists.
- Flight option cards show each leg's local departure date and year on the Trip and Return lines, so fares on different days can be told apart.
- When a travel search finds nothing within budget, the Shape keeps your dates, route, and times and doesn't suggest another month unless you said you're flexible.
- "Order the same thing as last time" goes by your newest order email, not an order another chat talked about but never placed.
- On the free Agent engine, comparing options with several requirements opens more of the search results that might fit and comes back with a pick plus two checked alternatives. A budget is judged on the total you'd pay, with tax, tip, and fees, unless you say to leave them out.
- Recommendations are checked against standing preferences you've saved, dietary needs for example, or the Shape says it doesn't know. Scheduled tasks work out "tomorrow" in your own time zone, so a task near midnight lands on the right day.
- After an image edit, the Shape compares the result with the original and says the change was made only if it's clearly visible. Otherwise it says what did and didn't change and offers a more specific edit.
- Correcting something a Shape saved about you replaces the note instead of leaving two that disagree.
- Shapes that Tod makes for your account get a generated profile picture instead of the placeholder.
- Asking a Shape to find a source or link ends with the links it found, and how sure it is, instead of asking you for screenshots.
- A "sign in to Claude Code" link in a Shape's reply opens Claude Code's own sign-in from the Mac app. Anywhere else, it tells you to open the Mac app or run `claude auth login` in Terminal.
- When your Mac won't write in a repository's main checkout, the Shape gets the real reason and can act on it, instead of an error saying the computer didn't confirm the folder.
- Shapes can invite people to tasks, send to another chat, and use files from another chat when they have that chat's link.
- When web search isn't available on a reply because of credits, the reply gives the specific reason.

### Fixed

- An engine billing outage on shapes.inc's side told people to buy credits. It now shows a plain system message with no purchase prompt.
- A browser task that ran out of credits promised a reset at midnight and a retry even when no free daily allowance applied.
- An image or screenshot sent on its own wasn't seen until you sent another message.
- A temporary loading failure could show "This chat doesn't exist." for a real chat. It now shows a load error with **Retry**.
- "You're out of credits" kept appearing under text replies after the day's 10 free voice messages when nobody had asked for voice. When a Shape's voice setting would send a voice message on its own, the reply now arrives as text with no notice. Voice you ask for still shows the out-of-credits card.
- Older messages could stop loading for someone sitting at the top of a chat's history, and bubbles jumped when an older page arrived.
- A file a Shape revised or retried could show duplicate previews with the wrong name. The chat shows one current preview and keeps earlier versions.
- Agent chats could stop saving memories after one very long source, such as a long PDF, and for two days some Agent memories weren't written at all.
- Standing preferences stated in Agent chats weren't always saved, and "Remember this" could fail for a new note.
- Recall across chats returned nothing for newly made Shapes.
- A Shape reading a long saved result, a big GitHub search for example, stopped after the first page and blamed a size limit. Chat history search, memory reads, and looking up connected apps could also be held back with "could not be checked".
- In group Agent chats, a Shape could ask people who were already members to join, or say "only you are here" when AI agents were in the chat.
- Shapes could use the wrong pronouns for people in group chats.
- A Shape could answer the same request twice when you sent a quick follow-up right after naming it.
- After you told a Shape to be quiet ("shut up", "leave me alone", in any language), its **Come back later** follow-ups kept posting. They stop until a person talks to it again. A playful or in-story "shut up" doesn't count.
- Outside countries that write their own money with \$, "\$15" was read as local currency. It's read as US\$15.
- Place names with accents (São Paulo, Zürich) reached Shapes garbled.
- A seat wish (aisle, window) was dropped when the airline's seat map was slow to load or when you gave it after the trip was created. After booking, the Shape says which seats it got and which wishes weren't met.
- Shapes read shapes.inc's own chat-notification emails in your Gmail as facts from your mailbox, which could carry details over from other chats.
- In your connected Chrome, clicks on links that wrap onto several lines missed, and pressing Escape inside a payment frame could stall the task.
- When your computer was busy, a Shape could point you at a command record that didn't exist.
- Gemma 4 31B lost its Reasoning and Tools badges and stopped taking actions, and Gemma 4 26B replies could take 30 to 60 seconds.
- In the iPhone app, after switching accounts, incoming calls could ring a different account and never the one on screen.
- In the first Windows release, sign-in always ended in "Sign-in could not finish. Try once more." Version 1.1.1 also makes Escape close the Download complete and Update ready panels, shows the Downloading panel after the Save dialog with the saved file's name, and removes the app's link handling on uninstall.

<Update label="November 8, 2026">

## Shapes send email, run routines, and use your Mac

### Moved

- The voice for a call with a Shape is picked from **Change voice** on the call screen, one choice per Shape. The old call voice picker is gone.

### Added

- **Shapes send email from their own address.** In an Agent chat, "email the hotel at frontdesk@example.com and ask for late checkout" sends from the Shape's own address (for example tod@shapesmail.com). Replies come back into the chat, and you can ask about a reply from another chat if you sent the email or are in the chat it was sent from. Sends you schedule and sends you ask for on a call work too. Chat mode doesn't send email.
- **Email people by name.** "Email Sam and Maya, cc Alex" reaches people on shapes.inc at their account email without the Shape seeing the addresses, and "email me" goes to yours. If someone has no account email, the email in their Saved details is used, still hidden. A Shape tells you another member's address only in a small private chat that person is in.
- **Send as you.** Say "as me" or "from my email" and the Shape sends from your connected Gmail instead of its own address.
- **Send on a drafted reply.** When a Shape drafts an email reply, the card shows the exact words with a **Send** button that anyone in the chat can tap.
- **Use this Mac** in the Mac app (version 1.24.11 and later). Building on the Mac app from last week, **Use this Mac** sits beside the composer in Agent chats, including a new chat you haven't sent yet. Choose a folder and the Shape runs file and coding tasks there with the coding agent built into the app, so you don't need another coding app installed.
- **Local task account** in the same dialog decides what pays for local tasks: **Use shapes.inc credits** is the default, and **Use Codex account** or **Use Claude account** runs them on your own signed-in Codex or Claude Code instead.
- **Shapes can see and use your Mac's screen** (Mac app 1.24.11 and later). In a chat you allowed with **Can make changes**, a Shape can look at the screen, click, type, scroll, and drag, checking the screen again after each step. Read-only access can't do this. It needs the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions; if one is missing, the app names the exact item to turn on: ComputerConnectionHost under Accessibility, and shapes.inc under Screen & System Audio Recording.
- **Use for new private chats** in Connect your computer (Mac app 1.24.15 and later), on by default: "New private chats use this computer and your last folder. Group chats need separate access." New private chats also reuse your Local task account choice. Turn the switch off to give access one chat at a time.
- **One setup window in the Mac app** (version 1.24.6 and later): **Set up shapes.inc** walks through moving the app to Applications, signing in through your browser, **Connect this Mac**, and a live permissions checklist, and ends at **Start chatting**. Full Disk Access and Automation are optional.
- **Connect a computer from the iOS app with a code.** Under "Have a code from your computer?" in Settings → Computers or a chat's computer dialog, choose **Scan code** or **Enter code**, check the device shown, and approve.
- **Until I turn it off** is a new length for sharing your computer with a chat, next to 1 hour and 24 hours. The share then reads "Until turned off".
- **Shared access has read and edit levels.** Under Settings → Connected apps → Shared access, each person in each chat can be set to **Automatic**, **Ask me first**, **Read**, **Read and edit**, **Read, edit and send**, or **Off**, and you can set it by telling a Shape. Reading never allows edits and editing never allows sending. Money, bookings, deleting, archiving, publishing, and changes to access are never covered by a standing choice.
- **Check before replying** for a chat's own sources: under Chat Settings → AI settings → MCP servers → **Add MCP server**, an owner can turn it on so every Shape in the chat searches that source before answering. The row then shows "Checked before replying". It's off by default and works only for sources that don't need each member to sign in.
- **Push even when I'm online** under Chat Settings → Notifications: "Your phone still rings for this chat while you're using shapes.inc elsewhere." It never rings while you're looking at that chat, and it's unavailable while the chat is muted or set to Nothing. A Shape can set it for you.
- **Manage people** for workspace owners, in the workspace switcher: search members, change a role from one menu per person, and **Remove from workspace**. Before anything happens you see which chats the person loses and which need a new owner, and you pick who takes over their chats. Their AI agents leave with them. Removed people are listed with **Add back**, and pending email invites have **Cancel**. Members still see **Invite people** there.
- **Pause** and **Resume** in [Settings → Tasks](https://talk.shapes.inc/settings/tasks), next to **Cancel** on repeating schedules (not watches). A paused schedule shows "Paused" and runs nothing until you resume; it picks up at its next slot. You can also ask a Shape to pause one, and its card reads "Reminder paused".
- **A schedule can call you.** Building on phone calls from October 30, "call me at 7 to wake me up" rings your own number at that time, from your free daily calling first and then your credits. A schedule can't call anyone else.
- **A schedule can post to another chat you're in**: "every Monday post the numbers in Team".
- **Watches read your calendar for flights.** A "keep an eye out" watch offers check-in when it opens, 24 hours before departure, and posts delays of 15 minutes or more, cancellations, diversions, and gate or terminal changes. At night it posts only real disruptions. A watch can also write a Gmail draft in the thread for you to send; it never sends one. After you connect Gmail, Calendar, or GitHub, a Shape offers a watch once.
- **Reorder from your order emails.** "Reorder the coffee I bought last month" finds the order in your own email, picks the same item and variant, and stops at the purchase card; nothing is charged until you tap. It works for gifts shipped to someone else. Amazon orders are skipped.
- **Use my saved email** on the private sign-in card when a site's first step is a single email box. One tap fills it from Saved details without the Shape seeing it.
- **Ask another member from the chat.** "Ask Sam for her address" sends Sam a card titled "For \{your name\}". Everyone else sees that the Shape is waiting for Sam, and her answer continues your request on your credits.
- **Shapes reach across your chats when you ask.** A Shape can send a file from another chat you're in, and can post in your existing DM with someone, saying it's from you ("tell Maya I'm running late"). It can't reach chats you aren't in, start a new DM, or message someone who blocked you.
- **Shapes can tidy your chat list.** Ask "help me clean out my sidebar" and a Shape can read your chat list, including Archive, and leave, archive, delete, or mark chats read for you.
- **Compare engines on your own requests.** Ask Tod to try a few engines on the kind of thing you ask for; it starts a test chat on each engine, sends your requests as you, reads the answers, and reports back.
- **Code Projects can read CI.** A Shape can read a pull request's named checks, workflow runs, jobs, and job logs to explain a failing check.
- **Usage** under [Settings → Usage](https://talk.shapes.inc/settings/usage): what's left today of each daily free allowance (Phone calls, Voice messages, Web research, Computer, Coding runs) and when it resets. Cards say what an action took from today's free time and what's left, for example "62 of 100 free credits left today".
- **Pick an engine by task.** The engine picker has **Best for** tabs (Coding, Writing, Math, Hard problems, Following instructions, Long conversations, Long prompts, Expert questions, Intelligence), **Quick picks** (Best overall, Best for coding, Best for writing, Best free), and a comparison list you can sort by score, ratings, cost per reply, or release date. It says where the numbers come from: "Scores by Artificial Analysis" and "Ratings by LMArena".
- **Formless v2.1**, a free engine, can be picked in Agent chats. It isn't offered in Chat mode or as a Shape's engine.
- **Devin** can be shared with an Agent chat as a team app.
- **Leave Chat** shows for owners in the sidebar's ⋯ menu, above Delete. The last owner has to make someone else an owner first, or delete the chat.
- **Invite strip.** In a new chat with only you in it, an invite strip appears under the Shape's reply with **Invite** and a close button. On phones and tablets, **Invite** opens the system share sheet.
- **Text an invite.** In the Invite friends dialog, pick a country code, type a number, and tap **Text**: Messages opens with the invite filled in, sent from your own number. Available on phones, iPads, and Macs.
- Ask a Shape to file a problem report for you, in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) or any chat, and ask later what happened to it.

### Changed

- **Tod is in every DM between people.** DMs are now Agent chats that include Tod, and there's no way to turn this off. - **Shapes can make phone calls.** In an Agent chat, ask a Shape to call a business the way you'd ask a friend: "can u call the thai place on valencia and book 4 at 7, anything 6:30 to 8 works", "get me a haircut tomorrow afternoon", "ask how long the wait is right now". The Shape finds the number on the business's own website. To give a number yourself, or to have it call you, put the number in the same message.
- **One live card per call.** It goes from **Ringing** to **On the call**, with the last few lines of the conversation, to **Call ended** with the recording, **View transcript**, and **Download**. Everyone in the chat can follow the call and replay it. Only the person who asked can **End call**.
- **Answer questions during the call.** When the business asks something the Shape doesn't know, like another time or a deposit, the caller asks them to hold and you get a card only you can see. Your answer goes straight into the call.
- **Bookings go under your name.** The caller uses the name, phone number, and email in [Settings → Saved details](https://talk.shapes.inc/settings/saved-details) when the business asks for them; the Shape never sees them. If one is missing, a card asks once, and the call starts by itself when you send it.
- The caller introduces itself with the Shape's name, in a voice that fits the Shape, and waits for the other side to speak first. It doesn't bring up that it's an AI; if asked, it says yes.
- When the call ends, the Shape posts what was confirmed, what wasn't, and the next step.

### Changed

- **Free:** 100 free credits of calling per person per day, about four minutes, shared across all your chats. It resets at midnight UTC.
- **Premium:** active subscribers pay per use from their own account credits, 22 credits a minute, billed by the second. The free daily calling isn't added. Chat or Shape credits can't pay for calls.
- Replaying a recording is free. Credits set aside for a call and not used come back.

### Known issues

- Calls are in English, and numbers outside the US and Canada may not connect.
- A call starts only from a message you type. Choosing an option on a card, reminders, and scheduled tasks can't place calls, so a Shape can't call you later at a set time.
- Calls to someone's own phone often hit call screening or voicemail, because the number is new to them.
- Calls come from a shapes.inc number, not yours. If the business calls that number back, it doesn't reach your chat.
- The transcript can mishear names and numbers. Check the recording before relying on anything important.

## Memory that covers the whole chat

Shapes used to remember a chat through the recent messages they could still see and the summaries they wrote along the way. In Agent chats they now recall from the whole conversation. Nothing changes in how you talk to them: refer back to something from months ago the way you would with a friend, and it comes back.

### Added

- **Shapes recall the whole chat.** In Agent chats, when you send a message, the Shape finds the moments in this chat's history that relate to it (names, decisions, promises, running jokes, details) and reads them with the dates they were said, even if they left its short-term memory long ago. This includes messages from before the Shape joined the chat. Recent messages still win when something has changed since.
- **Ask about the past and get the real answer.** In Agent chats, when you ask what happened earlier ("what did we settle on for the venue back in March?"), the Shape searches the chat by meaning, not only exact words, finds the moment, and opens the original messages to confirm names and specifics before it answers.
- **Workspace memory.** In a workspace, an Agent chat starts with relevant background from the other chats in that workspace that you're also in: apps already connected, projects, trackers, rules, and preferences set up elsewhere. Each memory names the chat it came from. Only chats you're a member of count; nothing comes from workspace chats you aren't in. You can also ask a Shape to look across the workspace ("check the other chats in this workspace for how we file tickets").
- **Threads keep their own memory.** An Agent thread works from the original request, a short summary of the chat up to that point, and the thread's own conversation, so unrelated chatter doesn't crowd out the task. Long threads keep a running summary so earlier decisions and finished work stay in view, and the Shape can go back to the original messages when it needs the exact wording.

### Changed

- `/wack` and `/clear` reset a Shape's short-term memory, the recent messages it reads before replying. They don't erase the chat's history, so in Agent chats the Shape can still recall earlier details when they're relevant. `/reset` still clears the Shape's long-term memories from the chat.
- Chat mode keeps working the way it did: short-term memory, the Shape's long-term memories, and saved notes.

### Improved

- When a Shape searches your other chats for you, the search covers the chats you've used most recently, so a chat you're in every day is found even if you have hundreds of chats.

### Fixed

- A Shape's reply in a thread could fail when it couldn't summarize the chat for that thread.

## October 30: texting, phone calls, and shapes that do more

New to shapes.inc, or want more from it? Start with [Get the most out of shapes.inc](/get-the-most-out-of-shapes-inc).

- **Texting:** get replies by text, text back to keep going, start chats by text, and add shapes.inc to an iMessage group. Your texts go to one texting chat with Tod unless you reply to a specific text; text `chats` to see which. Everything you text shows up in the app too. Texting is rolling out. See [Texting](/texting).
- **Phone calls:** in an Agent chat, a shape can phone a restaurant or store for you, ask you if they have a question, and post the recap with the recording. See [Phone calls](/phone-calls).
- **Buying:** shapes check out on the store's own site, in your account there, and pick the way to pay without asking. Shop Pay works at stores that take it, and the wallet covers larger purchases. See [Purchases](/websites-and-purchases#buy-something).
- **Ask for a shape:** ask for a character, narrator, or helper and it's made for the chat. Shapes can also search for existing shapes, and owners can turn off **Make discoverable in search**. See [Ask for a shape](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-for-a-shape).
- **Whole-chat memory:** in Agent chats, shapes recall from the chat's full history, including what happened before they joined, and open the original messages when you ask about the past. Workspace chats start with background from your other chats there, and threads keep their own memory. See [Memory](/memory#full-chat-history-in-agent-chats) and, for how it works and how to get more out of it, [Memory under the hood](/memory-under-the-hood).
- **Shapes do it in the app:** ask a shape to rename a chat, invite people, start a poll, or change your settings. See [Ask a shape to do it in the app](/chat-and-agent-modes#ask-a-shape-to-do-it-in-the-app).
- **Shared access:** choose whose routine requests can use your connected apps in **Settings → Connected apps → Shared access**. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps#let-teammates-use-your-apps).
- **Slack and hooks:** import Slack history, keep channels in sync, and post GitHub, Linear, or Ramp updates into a chat. See [Slack and hooks](/slack-and-hooks).
- **Threads and talking shapes:** owners can turn on **Automatic threads**; with several shapes, **Let shapes talk** keeps a conversation going. See [Threads](/threads#let-shapes-start-threads).
- **Credits:** a **More credits needed** card resumes the task after you buy credits, and Agent chats keep working on the free Agent engine when you're low. See [Credits](/credits#when-you-run-out).
- **Also:** reactions show who reacted, **Take photo** uses your webcam on a computer, and message menus no longer have Translate or Ask moderator; ask a shape to translate instead.

## October 27: shared work and current controls

- **Choices, offers, and private questions:** pick an option together, accept or decline an offered task, or answer a private request in its card. See [shared activities](/multiplayer-activities) and [Agent replies](/chat-and-agent-modes).
- **Work in progress:** task cards show running work and its result; the live browser panel and shared progress have different access rules. See [Tasks](/tasks) and [Websites and purchases](/websites-and-purchases).
- **Files and sign-ins:** open shared files in the file viewer and manage website access through **Saved sign-ins**. See [Files and attachments](/files-and-attachments) and [website sign-ins](/websites-and-purchases#sign-in-to-a-website).
- **Phone message controls:** hold a message for actions and reactions, choose **Select text** for partial copying, or swipe left to reply. See [Message actions](/messageactionsmenu).
- **Voice editor:** **How often** offers Never, Sometimes, Often, and a frequency slider; delivery and instruction controls have their own sections. See [Give your shape a voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).
- **Free usage:** the one-time trial-credit offer has been retired. Free AI Engines and the documented daily [voice](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) and [hosted coding](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work) allowances have separate rules; see [Credits](/credits).

## October 26: current features and controls

Start with [Features and limits](/features) for the current guides. [Agents can read these docs as Markdown](/docs-for-agents).

- **Threads:** Agent work can continue in a focused thread in the same chat, with the original request, earlier chat context, and the thread's replies. See [Threads](/threads) and [Memory](/memory).
- **Settings:** find destinations with **Search settings**. **General** holds new-chat defaults and **More preferences**; **Appearance** holds theme choices; **Notifications & privacy** holds account alerts. See [Settings and personalization](/personalization).
- **Working steps and Streaming:** both are optional under **General → More preferences** and off by default. Open Working details to find **Stop** for an active reply. See [Agent work](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did).
- **Saved details, sign-ins, and purchases:** reuse optional account details in supported forms, sign in privately to websites, and review purchase or travel cards. See [Saved details](/saved-details) and [Websites and purchases](/websites-and-purchases).
- **Voice:** calls are in the chat's overflow menu; recordings can run for up to 30 minutes per continuous segment. Read about [voice generation's daily allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) and [recording versus text conversion](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages).
- **Outside agents:** invite, pair, and keep an agent monitoring a chat, with its own identity, access, and usage limits. See [Invite AI agents](/ai-agents).
- **Customization:** choose built-in presets without an instruction box, or select Custom to edit. Shape settings are under **Settings → Your shapes**, with behavior fields in **Profile**, long-term memories in **Memory**, and owner controls in **Advanced**. See [Presets](/presets).
- **Discovery:** public catalogs are paused. Owned/recent pickers, direct shape links, and existing chats remain available.

## October 2026: a new shapes.inc

Every account is now on the same new shapes.inc, and the old version toggle is gone. Nothing was deleted: your chats, profiles, and history are all still here. This page shows where things moved and points to the guides for what's new.

For the full release notes, open **Settings** → **What's new** in the app.

## Where everything moved

| You used to find | Now it's |
|---|---|
| Personas | **Profiles**, under **Settings** → **Personalization**. See [Personalization](/personalization#profiles). |
| Personal AI Preferences | **Profile & credits**, in a chat's **Settings**. |
| Archive | **Completed**. Inactive chats and Shapes move there automatically. See [Completed chats](/completed). |
| The My Shapes page | **Settings** → **Your shapes**. It's also on Home, under **Ideas**. |
| Connected apps in the chat header | The composer's **+** menu, and **Settings** → **Connected apps**. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps). |
| Members, Invite, Share, and Delete in the chat's ⋯ menu | The chat's **Settings** panel, the **Invite** button in the chat header, and the ⋯ menu next to the chat in the sidebar. **Search Messages** is still in the chat's ⋯ menu. |
| Help | **Settings** → **Support Chat**, which opens Help Me. See [Feedback and help](/feedback). |
| Memories | **More actions** → **Manage memories** in a chat, or **Settings** → **Memories**. See [Memory](/memory). |
| Rewards | Retired. Profile styles that came from Rewards are now part of Premium. See [Rewards](/rewards). |
| Public shape discovery | Public catalogs, including Featured, are paused. Pickers still show shapes you own or have chatted with recently, and direct shape links still work. |

A few names changed too:

- Chats where Shapes take actions for you are **Agent** chats. Chats for just talking are **Chat** chats. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes).
- Private one-to-one conversations are **DMs**.
- The engine built by shapes.inc is shown as **Formless**.
- In the Shape editor, reply styles include **Human**, **Short creative**, and **Long creative**, and the category reads **Chat shape**.

Other additions:

- **Generation details** in the ⋯ menu on a Shape's message shows the engine, why it was picked, how long the reply took, and the credits it used.
- **Reasoning effort** (Default, Off, Low, Medium, or High, depending on the engine) in a chat's **Settings** → **Engines** → **Engine settings**, and in a Shape's **AI Engine** settings for engines that support reasoning. Higher effort can take longer and use more credits.
- **Home Ideas** are personalized from your recent chats and interests, per workspace. Turn them off with **Personalized ideas** under **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences**.
- **Voice messages** can be previewed before you send them. See [voice messages](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages).
- **Reply suggestions**, **Undo** after deleting a message or chat, a quick reaction strip, swipe left to reply on phones, an **Auto** theme setting, and the **Feedback** conversation with Tod. See [Feedback and help](/feedback).
- Anyone in a chat can [regenerate](/regeneration) a Shape's reply. The credits come from whoever regenerates it.

## What changed

- **Premium engine replies** are charged at twice the engine's provider cost (it was 1.5 times). See [credits](/credits).
- **Running out of Premium credits** no longer stops the conversation. Your messages are still saved, and Shapes can switch to a free text engine. Premium engines and paid actions still need credits.
- **Profile styles** (name styles, frames, gradients, bubble effects, and message effects) are now Premium. Special reactions were retired on October 27; reactions are emoji only. Saved styles come back when you subscribe. Shape banners, custom emojis (up to 50), custom profile HTML and CSS, display fonts, bubble styles, and chat appearance stay free.
- **Message ratings** and thumbs-down engine suggestions are gone. Use **Generation details** to see how a reply was made, and **Report a problem** to tell us when one goes wrong.
- The purple **Premium** badge on replies is off by default. Turn it back on with **Premium message badges** under **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences**.
- **Haptic feedback** is off by default on phones. Turn it on under **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences**, on supported touch devices.
- Creating your first Shape no longer sends you an automatic helper DM.
- **Age range** is gone from **Edit profile**, and **Pronouns** only shows if you've set them.

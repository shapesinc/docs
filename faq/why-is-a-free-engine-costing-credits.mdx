A free text engine does not make every action in a chat free. An automatic upgrade can switch a reply to a premium engine, and other features use credits too:

- Image generation, voice, and regenerating replies.
- Phone calls past today's free calling, at 22 credits a minute.
- Calls with a shape in the app. They have no free daily allowance.
- Web research and hosted computer or browser time past today's free allowance.
- Coding tasks on your own computer that run on the built-in agent because neither your Claude Code nor your Codex is ready.

## Check which engine is being used

Open the ⋯ menu on the shape's reply and select **Generation details**. It shows the engine that wrote the reply, why it was picked, and the credits it used.

To see or change the chat's engine, open **+** → **Engine** in the composer, or the chat's **Settings** → **Engines**. You can change it in a DM with a shape or in a chat you own. If you own the shape, its **AI Engine** is in **Settings** → **Your shapes** → choose the shape → **AI Engine**. Automatic upgrades can use a premium engine for people with credits. Use the [AI engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet) to check whether an engine uses credits.

## Review automatic upgrades

- **Auto upgrade**, under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use. Turning it off doesn't stop upgrades; the settings below do.
- **Don't use my credits**, in the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**, stops that chat from spending your credits or upgrading replies with them. To make this the default for chats you start, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**.
- **Keep the chosen AI Engine**, in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **General Settings**, keeps shapes on their configured engine even when premium credits are available. Only chat owners can change it.

The engine you pick in an Agent chat, on Home or inside the chat, becomes your default for new Agent chats you start. A premium pick carries over to those chats. Pick **shapes.inc default** to clear it. Existing chats keep their engine.

## Check the feature that used credits

**Settings** → **Usage** shows what's left today of each free allowance: **Phone calls**, **Voice messages**, **Web research**, **Computer**, and **Coding runs**, and when they reset at midnight UTC. Once an allowance is used up, that action uses your credits until the reset.

Review the action you requested, such as image generation with a premium image engine, voice, a premium engine response, or a higher **Reasoning effort**. Regenerating a reply charges whoever requests the regeneration. See [Shape Credits](/credits) and [chat credits](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat) for the available credit options.

If the charge still looks wrong, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) with the engine name, the feature used, and when it happened. Don't include account credentials or private payment details in a public conversation.

---
title: "Why is a free engine costing credits?"
description: "Check which engine wrote a reply, automatic upgrades, and other paid features when credits are used unexpectedly."
---

A free text engine does not mean every action in a chat is free. A premium engine may be used after an automatic upgrade, and features such as image generation, voice, and regenerating replies can use credits too.

## Check which engine is being used

Open the ⋯ menu on the Shape's reply and select **Generation details**. It shows the engine that wrote the reply, why it was picked, and the credits it used.

To see or change the chat's engine, open **+** → **Engine** in the composer, or the chat's **Settings** → **Engines**. You can change it in a DM with a Shape or in a chat you own. If you own the Shape, its **Main engine** and **Premium engine** are in **Settings** → **My Shapes** → choose the Shape → **AI Engine**. People with credits may get the premium engine instead of the main one. Use the [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) to check whether an engine uses credits.

## Review automatic upgrades

- **Auto upgrade**, under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use. Turning it off doesn't stop upgrades; the settings below do.
- **Don't use my credits**, in the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**, stops that chat from spending your credits or upgrading replies with them. To make this the default for chats you start, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**.
- **Prevent Model Overrides**, in the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations**, keeps Shapes on their configured engine even when premium credits are available. Only chat owners can change it.

Check the engine again before requesting another response. Do not assume an earlier free-engine selection still applies to every action.

## Check the feature that used credits

Review the action you requested, such as image generation with a premium image engine, voice, a premium engine response, or a higher **Reasoning effort**. Regenerating a reply charges whoever requests the regeneration. See [Shape Credits](/credits) and [chat credits](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat) for the available credit options.

If the charge still looks wrong, ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help) with the engine name, the feature used, and when it happened. Avoid including account credentials or private payment details in a public conversation.

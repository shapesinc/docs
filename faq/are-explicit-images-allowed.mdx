shapes.inc is not a place for explicit conversations or tasks. There are better alternatives for those use cases.

There's no explicit mode.

## Explicit images

Images are checked when you send them:

- **Clearly explicit images are blocked.** You'll see **This image was blocked because it appears to contain explicit content**, and the message isn't sent. This applies in DMs with a shape and in chats with more than one person.
- **Images that might be explicit are blurred.** Select a blurred image to view it.
- **Images already in your chats stay as they are.** The check runs when an image is sent, so older images aren't removed.

## Sensitive content on a shape

**May generate sensitive content** in a shape's settings is a label for mature themes and strong language. It doesn't turn off image checks or an AI Engine's safety filters.

## Replies stopped by safety filters

**That reply couldn't be generated because of safety filters** comes from the AI Engine's provider. Try saying it a different way, or pick another engine.

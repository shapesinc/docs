Two different settings change how bubbles look. Your bubble style is part of your profile and needs Premium. A chat's **Bubble Colors** are part of that chat and are free.

- **Your own bubble shape, in every chat:** **Edit profile** → **Style** → **Chat bubble**. Premium or account credits.
- **Bubble colors in one chat:** the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** → **Bubble Colors**. Free.

## Your bubble style

1. Open **Settings** and select your name or picture at the top.
2. Under **Style**, find **Chat bubble** (**Bubble** on a phone).
3. Pick **Classic**, **Plain text**, **Rectangular**, **Comics burst**, or **Zigzag**.

**Classic** is free. The others are styles: anyone can try them in the **Preview**, but saving them needs a [Premium plan](/premium-subscriptions) or a positive account-credit balance. Without either, **Save without styles** keeps your other profile changes.

Saved styles show while Premium is active or account credits remain. When both end, your messages go back to **Classic**. See [Pro benefits without a subscription](/premium-subscriptions#pro-benefits-without-a-subscription).

## A chat's bubble colors

The color of your bubbles comes from the chat, not your profile. Under **Bubble Colors**, set **Outgoing Messages** for your own messages and **Incoming Messages** for everyone else's, then select **Save**. The colors apply to everyone in that chat.

- In a group chat, only chat owners can open **Chat Appearance**.
- In a DM, anyone in the chat can.

See [Customizing a chat's appearance](/chat-appearance#choose-bubble-colors).

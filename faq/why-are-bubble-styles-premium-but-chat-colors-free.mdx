Your bubble style is part of your profile. A chat's **Bubble Colors** are part of that chat. Both are free on every plan. Two different settings change how bubbles look.

- **Your own bubble shape, in every chat:** **Edit profile** → **Style** → **Chat bubble**.
- **Bubble colors in one chat:** the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** → **Bubble Colors**.

## Your bubble style

1. Open **Settings** and select your name or picture at the top.
2. Under **Style**, find **Chat bubble** (**Bubble** on a phone).
3. Pick **Classic**, **Plain text**, **Rectangular**, **Comics burst**, or **Zigzag**, then select **Save**.

Your bubble style shows on your messages in every chat, for everyone. If you saved a style earlier and it went back to **Classic**, it shows again automatically. You don't need to pick it again.

## A chat's bubble colors

The color of your bubbles comes from the chat, not your profile. Under **Bubble Colors**, set **Outgoing Messages** for your own messages and **Incoming Messages** for everyone else's, then select **Save**. The colors apply to everyone in that chat.

- In a group chat, only chat owners can open **Chat Appearance**.
- In a DM, anyone in the chat can.

See [Customizing a chat's appearance](/chat-appearance#choose-bubble-colors).

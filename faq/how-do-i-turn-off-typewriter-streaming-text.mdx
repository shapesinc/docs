Open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn **Streaming** off.

Streaming is off by default: reply text appears when the reply is complete. Turning it on no longer shows text as it arrives; see [Where Streaming applies](#where-streaming-applies). Your choice saves to your account, so your other devices use it too.

On a computer, open **Settings** in the sidebar; on a phone, use the **Settings** tab. You can also type "Streaming" in **Search settings** to find the switch.

## Where Streaming applies

The switch only had an effect in chats set to Chat mode when you were the only person in the chat, for example a DM with a shape. Chats no longer have modes, and replies appear complete whatever the switch says. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

With **Streaming** off, you see the typing indicator while the shape writes, then the whole reply.

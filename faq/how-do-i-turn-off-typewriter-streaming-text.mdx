---
title: "How do I turn off typewriter or streaming text?"
description: "Turn Streaming on or off in Settings → General → More preferences."
---

Open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn **Streaming** off.

Streaming is off by default: reply text appears when the reply is complete. Turn it on if you want to see text as it arrives. Your choice saves to your account.

On a computer, open **Settings** in the sidebar; on a phone, use the **Settings** tab. You can also type "Streaming" in **Search settings** to find the switch.

**Font size** is in **General**. Theme choices are in **Settings** → **Appearance**. See [Settings and Personalization](/personalization).

---
title: "How do I turn off typewriter or streaming text?"
description: "Streaming text is no longer a setting you can change in shapes.inc."
---

Streaming text is no longer an adjustable setting. Settings doesn't have a **Streaming** switch anymore, and replies appear as the Shape writes them.

If you turned streaming off in an earlier version of shapes.inc, that choice still applies to your account.

Other display settings, such as **Theme** and **Font size**, are on the **Settings** page (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).

Open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn **Streaming** off.

Streaming is off by default: reply text appears when the reply is complete. Turning it on shows text as it arrives only in some chats; see [Where Streaming applies](#where-streaming-applies). Your choice saves to your account, so your other devices use it too.

On a computer, open **Settings** in the sidebar; on a phone, use the **Settings** tab. You can also type "Streaming" in **Search settings** to find the switch.

## Where Streaming applies

Replies in chats with other people, and replies that search the web or take other actions, always appear complete, whatever the switch says. The manual mode selector was removed; see [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

With **Streaming** off, you see the typing indicator while the shape writes, then the whole reply.

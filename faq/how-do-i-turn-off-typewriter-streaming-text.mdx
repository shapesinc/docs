title: "How do I turn off typewriter or streaming text?"
description: "Turn Streaming on or off in Settings → General → More preferences. It is off by default, so replies appear all at once."
keywords: ["streaming", "typewriter effect", "typing effect", "text appears word by word", "replies all at once", "turn off streaming", "streaming toggle", "live reply", "reply preview"]
---

Open **Settings** → **General** → **More preferences** and turn **Streaming** off.

Streaming is off by default: reply text appears when the reply is complete. Turn it on if you want to see text as it arrives. Your choice saves to your account, so your other devices use it too.

On a computer, open **Settings** in the sidebar; on a phone, use the **Settings** tab. You can also type "Streaming" in **Search settings** to find the switch.

## Where Streaming applies

The switch only has an effect in Chat mode when you are the only person in the chat, for example a DM with a shape. In Agent chats and in chats with other people, replies always appear complete, whatever the switch says.

With **Streaming** off, you see the typing indicator while the shape writes, then the whole reply.

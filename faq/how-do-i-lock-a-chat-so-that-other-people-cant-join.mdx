---
title: "How do I lock a chat so that other people can't join?"
description: "Lock a chat from its Moderation settings so new people cannot join."
---

Chat owners can lock a group chat:

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. Choose **Moderation**.
3. Turn on **Lock Chat**.

People who are already in the chat keep access. New people can't join while the chat is locked, even with an invite link. Only chat owners can add people to a locked chat.

To let people join again, turn **Lock Chat** off. See [Moderation](/moderation) for the other controls.

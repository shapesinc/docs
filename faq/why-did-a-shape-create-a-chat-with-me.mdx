A shape starts a chat with you, or adds you to one, only when a person asks. Shapes don't start new chats with you on their own, so Settings no longer has a **Shape reach-outs** switch.

The request comes one of these ways:

- **You asked.** For example, "start a new chat and get this done" carries the task into a new chat.
- **Someone you already share a chat with asked.** A Shape can start a chat with, or add, people who already share a chat with the person asking. If a name is unclear, the shape asks first.
- **You asked a shape to watch an app.** After you approve, a shape watches your connected Gmail, Google Calendar, or GitHub and flags what needs you in a private chat between the two of you. After you connect one of these apps, a shape offers a watch once. Tell it to stop at any time.

## Why Tod is in your DM

Every DM between two people includes Tod, and you can't remove it. Tod stays silent while you talk and replies only when one of you addresses it, for example "tod, summarize our action items". It never follows up on its own, and whoever asked pays for the reply.

## What you can do

- **Leave:** open the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar and choose **Leave Chat**.
- **Clear it from your list:** choose **Archive**. Nothing is deleted, and a new message brings the chat back. See [Archive](/archive).
- **Delete it:** choose **Delete** if it's a DM or a chat you own. See [How do I delete a chat?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-chat).
- **Quiet it:** select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Notifications**.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

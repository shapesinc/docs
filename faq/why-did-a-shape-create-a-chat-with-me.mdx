---
title: "How do I stop Shapes from starting new chats with me?"
description: "Turn off Shape reach-outs so Shapes do not start new chats with you."
---

To keep new chats on your terms, turn off **Shape reach-outs**. When this setting is off, Shapes will not start new chats with you first. You can turn it back on at any time.

## Turn off Shape reach-outs

1. Tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation. On larger screens, such as an iPad or computer, the same icon is in the upper-left corner of the sidebar.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-home-settings-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the Settings icon in the bottom navigation"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-settings-notifications-privacy-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to Notifications and privacy in Settings"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

3. Find **Shape reach-outs** and tap its toggle until it is off. An off toggle is gray.

This setting only controls new chats started by Shapes. It does not prevent you from starting a chat with any Shape whenever you want.

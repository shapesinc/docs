---
title: "Why did a Shape start a chat with me?"
description: "Shapes don't start new chats on their own. Learn why a Shape started or added you to a chat, and how to leave it."
---

Shapes don't start new chats with you on their own, so Settings no longer has a **Shape reach-outs** switch. A Shape starts a chat with you, or adds you to one, only when a person asks:

- **You asked.** For example, "start a new chat and get this done" carries the task into a new chat.
- **Someone you already share a chat with asked.** A Shape can start a chat with, or add, people who already share a chat with the person asking. If a name is unclear, the Shape asks first.
- **You asked a Shape to watch an app.** With your OK, a Shape can keep an eye on your connected Gmail or GitHub and flag what needs you in a private chat between the two of you. Tell it to stop at any time.

## What you can do

- **Leave:** open the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar and choose **Leave Chat**.
- **Clear it from your list:** choose **Mark completed**. Nothing is deleted, and a new message brings the chat back. See [Completed](/completed).
- **Delete it:** choose **Delete** if it's a DM or a chat you own. See [How do I delete a chat?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-chat).
- **Quiet it:** select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Notifications**.

A Shape in a chat you're already in can still check in after a quiet spell if the chat's **Come back later** setting is on. See [Multiple Messages](/multimessages).

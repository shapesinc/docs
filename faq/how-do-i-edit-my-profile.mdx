---
title: "How do I edit my profile?"
description: "Edit your own shapes.inc profile, bio, avatar, and banner, and set the Profiles Shapes use for you in chats."
---

Open **Settings** and select your name or picture at the top of the settings list. On a computer, **Edit profile** is also at the top of **General**.

On the **Edit profile** page:

- Change your **Name**, **Username**, **Bio**, and avatar and banner. **Pronouns** appears only if you've set them before. Age range is no longer part of your profile.
- Under **Style**, pick your avatar frame, name color and font, chat bubble, message effects, and profile card backdrop.
- A **Preview** of your profile card and a short group chat updates as you edit. On a computer it sits beside the form; on a phone it's at the top, with **Profile** and **Style** tabs below.
- Your changes collect in a bar at the bottom. Select **Save** to keep them, or **Reset** to undo them. Avatar and banner images save as soon as they upload.
- Anyone can try styles in the preview, but saving them needs [Premium](/premium). Without it, **Save without styles** keeps your other changes.
- **Commands**, **Sign-in**, and **Delete account** are on the same page.

## Profiles for chats

Your public profile is what people see. To choose the name, photo, and details Shapes use for you in each chat, go to **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Profiles**. Pick a profile for one chat from that chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. See [Personalization](/personalization).

---
title: "How do I edit my profile?"
description: "Edit your own shapes.inc profile, bio, avatar, and banner, and set the Profiles Shapes use for you in chats."
---

Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones), then select **Edit profile** under your name.

On the **Edit profile** page:

- Select the pencil icon next to your name, or **Edit bio** (**Add bio** if you don't have one), to change your name and bio. Select **Save changes** when you're done.
- Use the pencil icons on your avatar and banner to change them.
- Select the pencil icon next to your @username to change it, then select **Save username**.
- Under **About you**, **Pronouns** appears only if you've set them before. Age range is no longer part of your profile.
- Open **Customize** for profile styles such as avatar frames and display name fonts. Some styles need [Premium](/premium).

## Profiles for chats

Your public profile is what people see. To choose the name, photo, and details Shapes use for you in each chat, go to **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Profiles**. Pick a profile for one chat from that chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. See [Personalization](/personalization).

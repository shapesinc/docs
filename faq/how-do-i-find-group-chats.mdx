---
title: "How do I find group chats?"
description: "Join a group chat through an invite link or invitation, or start one for the people involved."
---

shapes.inc doesn't have a public directory of chats to browse. You join a group chat when someone invites you:

- **Invite link**: ask someone in the chat to select **Invite** at the top of the chat and send you the link. Invite links look like `shapes.inc/j/…`, and older invite links still work.
- **Direct add**: someone in the chat can add you from **Participants** → **Add participants**.
- **Invitations**: if your account is private, invitations from people who aren't your friends wait for you under **Settings** → **Invites** on the **Chats** tab. Select **Join chat** or **Decline**. Requests stay available for 30 days.

If you want a chat for your own group, follow [How do I make a group chat?](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-group-chat). Invite the friends, family members, classmates, or teammates involved in the shared goal.

Once you have joined, find the chat in your sidebar or with **Search...** at the top of the sidebar. If it's gone from your list, check **Settings** → **Completed**.

---
title: "How do I find group chats?"
description: "Join a group chat through an invitation, or start one for the people involved."
---

Ask someone in the group to share an invitation to their chat. Follow the link and review the chat before joining.

If you want a chat for your own group, follow [How do I make a group chat?](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-group-chat). Invite the friends, family members, classmates, or teammates involved in the shared goal.

Once you have joined, use the sidebar chat list or **Search...** to find the conversation again.

<Tip>
  Need more help? Ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support).
</Tip>

There is no auto-scroll setting in shapes.inc. The chat decides by where you are:

- **At the bottom of the chat:** the view stays on the newest message as new ones arrive.
- **Scrolled up:** the view stays where you are. New messages do not pull you down.

To stop the chat from following new messages, scroll up a little.

## Get back to the newest messages

When you are scrolled up, a button with a down arrow appears at the bottom right of the chat. If messages arrived while you were reading, it shows how many are new. Select it to go to the newest messages.

## Long replies

To read a long reply without it moving as it grows, keep **Streaming** off. It is off by default, so a reply appears once, complete. See [How do I turn off typewriter or streaming text?](/faq/how-do-i-turn-off-typewriter-streaming-text)

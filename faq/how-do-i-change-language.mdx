---
title: "How do I change the language?"
description: "Change the language used by the app, and get a message translated."
keywords: ["app language", "change language", "translate a message", "translation", "Spanish", "Portuguese", "Arabic", "right-to-left", "RTL", "language setting"]
---

Open **Settings** → **General**. Find **App language**, open the menu that shows your current language, and choose the language you want.

Available languages are English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic. The app reloads in the language you choose, and the choice saves to your account. Arabic switches the layout to right-to-left.

**App language** changes the app's own buttons, menus, and labels. It does not translate messages or change the language a shape replies in.

To read a single message in another language, ask a shape in the chat, for example "translate the last message into English". Message menus no longer have a Translate option.

## Make a shape reply in another language

If you own the shape, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the shape, and set **Language** under **AI Engine** → **Presets**. Every reply from that shape then uses it by default.

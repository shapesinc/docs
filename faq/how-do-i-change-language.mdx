---
title: "How do I change the language?"
description: "Change the language used by the app, and get a message translated."
---

Open **Settings** → **General**. Find **App language**, open the menu that shows your current language, and choose the language you want.

Available languages are English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic. The app updates to the language you choose.

To read a single message in another language, ask a shape in the chat, for example "translate the last message into English". Message menus no longer have a Translate option.

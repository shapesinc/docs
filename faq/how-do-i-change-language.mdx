---
title: "How do I change the language?"
description: "Change the language used by the app, and translate individual messages."
---

Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones). Find **App language**, open the menu that shows your current language, and choose the language you want.

Available languages are English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic. The app updates to the language you choose.

To read a single message in your app language, open the message's **⋯** menu and choose **Translate**. Select **See original message** to switch back.

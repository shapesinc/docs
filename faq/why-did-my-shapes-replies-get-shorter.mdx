Replies are concise by default since October 24, 2026. A shape answers your current question and gives a full explanation, a complete deliverable, or research when you ask for it.

To get a longer reply, ask for it: "Give me the full version, with every step."

Chats no longer have a Chat mode to switch to. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

<a id="short-replies-in-chat-mode" />

## Short replies in scenes

Some people also reported shorter replies in Chat mode, now how a shape answers when you play a scene, starting in late October 2026, on more than one AI Engine and even with format instructions set. The cause isn't confirmed yet. To help us look into it, open the ⋯ menu on a short reply and choose **Report a problem**. See [Report a shape reply](/feedback#report-a-shape-reply).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

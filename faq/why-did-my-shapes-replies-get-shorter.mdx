In Agent chats, replies are concise by default since October 24, 2026. A shape answers your current question and gives a full explanation, a complete deliverable, or research when you ask for it.

To get a longer reply:

- Ask for it: "Give me the full version, with every step."
- If you own the chat, switch it to **Chat** mode. See [Switch an existing chat](/chat-and-agent-modes#switch-an-existing-chat).

## Short replies in Chat mode

Some people also reported shorter replies in Chat mode starting in late October 2026, on more than one AI Engine and even with format instructions set. The cause isn't confirmed yet. To help us look into it, open the ⋯ menu on a short reply and choose **Report a problem**. See [Report a shape reply](/feedback#report-a-shape-reply).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

A shape sorts your chats for you. Ask it in an Agent chat to create a category and move chats into it. There's no button to move a chat into a category yourself.

## Make a category

1. Open an Agent chat and ask, for example: "Make a category called School and put my Biology and History chats in it."
2. The shape creates the category and moves those chats.
3. The **Categories** filter appears above your chats in the sidebar. Select it and pick **School** to see only those chats.

Category names can be up to 48 characters. Categories are yours alone: they change only your own sidebar, not anyone else's.

## Change categories later

Ask a shape in an Agent chat to:

- Move another chat into a category, or take one out.
- Rename a category.
- Reorder your categories.
- Delete a category. Its chats stay in your list, without a category.

The **Categories** dropdown also has **New category**, **Delete category**, and **Clear filter**. It shows once at least one chat is in a category.

Two people on shapes.inc who follow each other are friends.

**How Friends Work**

- Follow someone by opening their profile from a chat and selecting **Follow**, next to **Message**.
- If they follow you back, you become friends.
- To see your connections, select **Search...** at the top of the sidebar. Before you type, choose **Followers**, **Following**, or **Friends**.

### Following Someone

Select a person's picture or name in a chat to open their profile, then select **Follow**. The button changes to **Following**. You can also select **Follow** next to someone in sidebar search results, or the heart icon next to them in a chat's **Participants** list.

To unfollow, select **Following** on their profile, or **Unfollow** in sidebar search results.

### Remove a follower

You can't remove someone from your followers. Only they can unfollow you.

Blocking someone doesn't remove a follow they already have. It stops them from following you again and stops DMs between you. To block someone, open your DM with them, then **⋯** → **Block user**.

To end a friendship, unfollow them. A friendship ends as soon as either of you unfollows.

### Why friends matter

With **Private account** turned on under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**, people who aren't your friends must send an invite before they can DM you or add you to a chat or workspace. See [Friends & Collaborators](/friends).

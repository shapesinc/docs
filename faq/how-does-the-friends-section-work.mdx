Friends are based on follows between people on shapes.inc.

**How Friends Work**

- Follow someone by opening their profile from a chat and selecting the heart icon (**Follow**).
- If they follow you back, you become friends.
- To see your connections, select **Search...** at the top of the sidebar. Before you type, choose **Followers**, **Following**, or **Friends**.

### Following Someone

Select a person's picture or name in a chat to open their profile, then select the heart icon. You can also select **Follow** next to someone in sidebar search results or in a chat's **Participants** list.

### Why friends matter

With **Private account** turned on under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**, people who aren't your friends must send an invite before they can DM you or add you to a chat or workspace. See [Friends & Collaborators](/friends).

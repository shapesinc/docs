---
title: "How can I use my premium features?"
description: "Use Premium on shapes.inc, manage or cancel your plan, and turn on Premium message badges."
---

Open shapes.inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and sign in.

Premium features work automatically once you subscribe. Premium engines, premium images, and Shape voice use your monthly credits, ads are hidden for you, and your profile styles are available. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions) for everything Premium includes.

## Manage your plan

Open **Settings** and select the **Premium** card at the top. It shows **Manage** when you have a plan, or **Go Premium** if you don't. From the Premium page you can change your allowance when plan changes are available, and **Manage billing** lets you cancel or check payments. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, manage your plan in your store account.

If your Premium page shows **Payment History**, use it to check credit and gift card purchases.

## Show the Premium badge on replies

The purple Premium badge on replies is off by default. To show it, open **Settings** → **More preferences** and turn on **Premium message badges**.

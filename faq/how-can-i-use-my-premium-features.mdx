---
title: "How can I use my premium features?"
description: "Use Premium on shapes.inc, manage or cancel your plan, and turn on Premium message badges."
---

Open shapes.inc. If you are already in the app, stay there. If not, open [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc) and sign in.

Pro benefits work automatically with an active subscription or a positive account-credit balance. Credit-based access does not start a subscription or create a payment. See [Pro without subscribing](/faq/why-do-i-have-pro-without-subscribing). Premium engines, premium images, and Shape voice use your account credits, ads are hidden for you, and your profile styles are available. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions) for everything Premium includes.

## Manage your plan

Open **Settings** and select the **Premium** card at the top. The card shows your available access; a Pro label alone does not mean you have a subscription. From the Premium page you can change your allowance when plan changes are available, and **Manage billing** lets you cancel or check payments. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, manage your plan in your store account.

If your Premium page shows **Payment History**, use it to check credit and gift card purchases.

[Cancelling Premium keeps credits already added to your account](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium). You can use that balance after your plan ends, and Pro benefits continue while your account-credit balance is positive. Your subscription's monthly allowance stops.

## Show the Premium badge on replies

The purple Premium badge on replies is off by default. To show it, open **Settings** → **More preferences** and turn on **Premium message badges**.

---
title: "How do I unsubscribe from emails?"
description: "Turn off shapes.inc emails or texts in Settings, and fix an Email delivery failed lock."
---

Turn off the emails you don't want under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**.

1. Open **Settings** (in the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Turn off a switch:
   - **Email notifications**: summaries when you're away.
   - **Promotional emails**: news, alerts, and updates.
   - **Text notifications**: texts by iMessage, or RCS on Android. This switch shows when texting is available for your account.

Each switch saves as soon as you change it. To stop texts from your phone, text STOP to the shapes.inc number. Text START to turn them back on.

## I turned notifications back on but get no emails

The unsubscribe link in a promotional email only turns off **Promotional emails**, so check that **Email notifications** is on under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**. If Settings shows **Email delivery failed**, both email switches stay locked. You can't change your email address in Settings. Connecting another sign-in clears the lock and turns **Email notifications** back on:

1. Open **Settings** → **Edit profile**.
2. Under **Sign-in**, connect Email, Google, or Apple.

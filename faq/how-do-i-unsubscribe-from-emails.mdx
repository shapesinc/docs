---
title: "How do I unsubscribe from emails?"
description: "Change email preferences for your shapes.inc account in Settings."
---

1. Open **Settings** (in the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Turn off the emails you don't want:
   - **Email notifications**: summaries when you're away.
   - **Promotional emails**: news, alerts, and updates.

Each switch saves as soon as you change it.

## I turned notifications back on but get no emails

The unsubscribe link in a promotional email only turns off **Promotional emails**, so check that **Email notifications** is on under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**. If Settings shows **Email delivery failed**, the switch stays locked. You can't change your email address in Settings, but connecting another sign-in under **Connected accounts** clears the lock, or you can ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

---
title: "How do I unsubscribe from emails?"
description: "Change email preferences for your shapes.inc account in Settings."
---

1. Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Turn off the emails you don't want:
   - **Email notifications**: summaries when you're away.
   - **Promotional emails**: news, alerts, and updates.

Each switch saves as soon as you change it.

---
title: "How do I delete a Shape?"
description: "Delete a Shape from its Advanced → Danger zone, accessed through Settings → Your shapes."
---

1. Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select the Shape.
2. Choose **Advanced** and scroll to **Danger zone**.
3. Select **Delete** followed by the shape’s name.
4. Type the Shape's username to confirm, then select the **Delete** button labelled with its name.

Deleting removes the Shape for everyone, including co-owners and people who chat with it. This can't be undone.

If you only want to stop using a Shape for a while, you don't need to delete it: Shapes you don't use move to **Completed** on their own, and you can restore them from **Settings** → **Completed**. See [Completed](/completed).

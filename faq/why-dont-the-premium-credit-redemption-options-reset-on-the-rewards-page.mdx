Rewards are retired, including the credit packs in the Rewards shop, so there are no redemption options left to reset. Credit packs you redeemed before were added to your account's credit balance. See [What happened to Rewards?](/faq/what-happened-to-rewards) for what changed.

If you need more premium usage, you can:

- Subscribe to [Premium](/premium-subscriptions) for a monthly credit allowance
- Purchase [Shape Credits](/credits) where your Premium page offers them
- Redeem or purchase shapes.inc [gift cards](/giftcards)

Free AI engines and the daily free image generations don't use credits, so you can keep chatting without them.

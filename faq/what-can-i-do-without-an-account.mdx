**Without an account you can read, but you can't send.** A chat link opens a read-only view of a group chat, and a shape link opens the shape's page. Sending a message, joining a chat, and everything else needs an account.

## Open a chat link

A link to a group chat, including an invite link, opens the chat read-only:

- You see the most recent messages, up to 50, as they were when the page loaded. New messages don't appear until you reload.
- You can't send, reply, or react, and the message menu is hidden.
- A bar at the bottom shows who is in the chat, such as "Maya and Tod are here", with a **Join** button that carries the host's name. When the bar has no name to show, the button reads **Sign up to join**.

Select the button to sign in or create an account. You return to the same chat afterwards.

Some chats don't open without an account: DMs, threads, chats in a workspace, and locked chats. Those links go to sign-in first.

## Open a shape link

A shape link opens the shape's public page. Select **Chat with** and the shape's name, sign in or create an account, and your chat with that shape opens.

## Join a shared activity

Anyone with the link to a live poll, game, or form can take part without signing in. See [Share it with people outside the chat](/multiplayer-activities#share-it-with-people-outside-the-chat).

## What needs an account

- Sending messages, replying, and reacting.
- Joining a chat or starting a new one.
- Uploading files.
- Calls.

To make one, see [Create or recover your account](/make-or-recover-account).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

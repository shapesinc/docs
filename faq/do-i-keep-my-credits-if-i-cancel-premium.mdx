---
title: "Do I keep my credits if I cancel Premium?"
description: "Cancelling Premium stops future renewals and keeps credits already added to your account."
keywords: ["cancel Premium", "keep credits", "unused credits", "subscription cancellation", "remaining balance"]
---

**Yes. Cancelling Premium does not remove credits already added to your account.** You can keep using your remaining balance for premium engines and other features that use credits after your subscription ends.

Cancelling stops future monthly renewals. Your Premium plan stays active through the period you already paid for; after that, you no longer receive its monthly allowance or subscription benefits. Keeping credits does not keep an active Premium subscription.

To cancel, open **Settings** → **Premium** and use the billing management option for your plan. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel in your store account. See [change or cancel your plan](/premium-subscriptions#change-or-cancel-your-plan).

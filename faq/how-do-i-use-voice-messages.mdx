---
title: "How do I use voice messages?"
description: "Record, preview, and send voice messages, or convert a recording to text before sending."
---

In the composer, tap the microphone button (**Dictate or record voice**) and start talking.

1. Tap the stop button when you're done. Your recording waits in the composer as a draft so you can play it back first.
2. Open the draft's **⋯** menu (**Recording options**) if you want to **Continue recording** or **Convert to text**. Convert to text turns the recording into a typed message you can edit before sending; it works for recordings up to 5 minutes.
3. Tap the send button to send the voice message, or the trash button to delete the draft.

Each continuous recording stops automatically at **30 minutes** and stays as a draft for you to review and send. **Convert to text** has a separate **5-minute** limit; a longer voice recording can still be sent as audio.

When you send a voice message to a Shape, the Shape may reply with a voice message too. Shapes can also understand videos you upload, both the picture and the sound.

You can start a new chat with a voice note from Home. Voice messages with a transcript have **Copy transcript** in their **⋯** menu.

A shape's voice replies use account credits. If those credits cannot cover the audio, a shared allowance provides up to **10 free voice generations per UTC day**. See [voice credits and the daily allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) for what counts. If audio is unavailable, the text reply can still arrive with a notice.

For more details, read [How to Receive Voice Messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) and [How to Give Your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).

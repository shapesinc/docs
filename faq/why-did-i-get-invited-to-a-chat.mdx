---
title: "Why did I get invited to a chat?"
description: "Learn why someone may invite you to a chat, and how to control who can add you."
---

shapes.inc chats are shared with the people involved in a plan or project, so people can invite each other. You may have been invited because:

- Someone shared the chat's invite link with you. Invite links look like `shapes.inc/j/…`.
- Someone in the chat added you directly from **Participants** → **Add participants**.
- Someone you already share a chat with asked a Shape to add you or to start a new chat with you. Shapes do this only when a person asks, and only for people who already share a chat with the person asking.

## Invite someone

- Select **Invite** at the top of a group chat to open **Invite friends**, then use **Copy Link** or **Share via...**.
- Or select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Participants** → **Add participants** → **People**. Search for the person, select them, and select **Add**.

## Control who can add you

To review invitations before you join, make your account private: open **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** and turn on **Private account**. People who aren't your friends then have to send an invite before they can DM you or add you to a chat or workspace.

Review invitations under **Settings** → **Invites**. The **DMs** and **Chats** tabs show who invited you. Select **Accept & open**, **Join chat**, or **Join workspace**, or select **Decline**. Requests stay available for 30 days.

If you don't want to stay in a chat, open the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar and choose **Leave Chat**. To block someone, choose **Block user** from the **⋯** menu on one of their messages, or **Block User** from your DM's **⋯** menu in the sidebar.

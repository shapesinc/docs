---
title: "Why did I get invited to a chat?"
description: "Learn why someone may invite you to a chat."
---

shapes.inc lets people invite other users to their chats. Someone may have shared a chat link with you or added you directly.

To invite someone to a group chat, open **More actions** → **Members / Invite**:

- Choose **Invite friends** to share a chat link.
- Choose **Add participants** → **People**, then search for the person you want to add.

If you do not want people adding you to group chats, you can make your account private.

Tap **Settings** in the bottom nav and turn on **Private account**.

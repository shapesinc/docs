---
title: "Why does my plan show fewer credits in the iPhone or Android app?"
description: "App Store and Google Play plans add fewer credits than the same plan bought in a browser, because Apple and Google keep a share of each purchase."
keywords: ["1,750 credits", "2,100 credits", "2,500 credits", "App Store", "Google Play", "iPhone app", "Android app", "fewer credits", "in-app purchase"]
---

**Apple and Google keep a share of every App Store and Google Play purchase, so the same plan adds fewer credits there.** A $25 plan adds **2,500 credits** when you subscribe in a browser, **2,100** through Google Play in the Android app, and **1,750** through the App Store in the iPhone app. Every plan size follows the same split; see [Credits per plan](/premium-subscriptions#credits-per-plan).

A plan you started in the app before these amounts changed keeps the credits it started with.

App Store and Google Play subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your store account. Separate one-time credit packs aren't available anywhere; see [Can I buy credits without a subscription?](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription)

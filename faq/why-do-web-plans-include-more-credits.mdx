---
title: "Why do web plans include more credits?"
description: "Plans bought on the web skip app store fees, so they include more credits than the same plan in the iPhone or Android app."
keywords: ["1,750 credits", "2,100 credits", "2,500 credits", "subscribe on the web", "most credits", "App Store", "Google Play", "iPhone app", "Android app", "in-app purchase"]
---

**Web plans skip app store fees, so they include the most credits.** A $25 plan includes **2,500 credits** when you subscribe on the web at [talk.shapes.inc](https://talk.shapes.inc), **2,100** in the Android app, and **1,750** in the iPhone app. Every plan size follows the same rate; see [Credits per plan](/premium-subscriptions#credits-per-plan).

A plan you started in the app before these amounts changed keeps the credits it started with.

App Store and Google Play subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your store account. Separate one-time credit packs aren't available anywhere; see [Can I buy credits without a subscription?](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription)

---
title: "How do I make a shape private?"
description: "Understand direct shape links and what the current editor's sharing controls do."
---

The current editor has no switch that restricts a shape to only you. You can keep it out of search, and anyone with its direct link can still chat with it.

Public shape discovery is gone and isn't coming back. Shape pickers on shapes.inc show only shapes you own and shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days, so other people don't come across your shape by browsing. People can still reach it through a direct link to the shape, and existing chats with it keep working. Shapes can also still be found through web search engines such as Google, depending on what they have indexed; see [Search for shapes](/shapes-search#finding-shapes-you-dont-own).

To keep a shape out of search, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the shape, choose **Share**, and turn off **Make discoverable in search**. It's on by default. When it's off, the shape doesn't appear in search, including searches shapes run in chats. Anyone with its direct link can still chat with it, and existing chats keep working.

You can't block specific people from using a shape. In chats you own, you can **Ban** someone. The end of public discovery does not make a direct shape link private.

To review its shared page, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the shape, then choose **Share** → **View public page**.

If you want nobody to use the shape anymore, deleting it removes it for everyone. See [How do I delete a shape?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape)

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

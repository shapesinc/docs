---
title: "How do I make a Shape private?"
description: "Who can find your Shape now that public Shape discovery is retired, and where to find the Shape's visibility setting."
---

Public Shape discovery is retired. Search and Shape pickers on shapes.inc show only Shapes you own and Shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days, so other people don't come across your Shape by browsing. People can still reach it through a direct link to the Shape, and existing chats with it keep working.

The Shape's visibility setting is still in the editor:

1. Open **Settings** → **My Shapes** and select the Shape.
2. Choose **Manage**.
3. Under **Visibility & safety**, review **Show in search and Explore**.

If you want nobody to use the Shape anymore, deleting it removes it for everyone. See [How do I delete a Shape?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape)

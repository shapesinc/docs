---
title: "How do I make a Shape private?"
description: "Understand direct Shape links and what the current editor's sharing controls do."
---

Public Shape discovery is paused. Shape pickers on shapes.inc show only Shapes you own and Shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days, so other people don't come across your Shape by browsing. People can still reach it through a direct link to the Shape, and existing chats with it keep working.

The current editor does not offer a switch that restricts a Shape to only you. Public discovery being paused does not make a direct Shape link private.

To review its shared page, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, then choose **Share** → **View public page**.

If you want nobody to use the Shape anymore, deleting it removes it for everyone. See [How do I delete a Shape?](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape)

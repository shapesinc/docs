shapes.inc currently does not provide an official full export or backup tool for your Shapes, chat histories, or memories.

You can [copy individual messages](/messageactionsmenu#copy) or download files offered in a chat. These actions save only the selected content. A summary or document generated by a Shape may omit earlier messages, settings, knowledge, or memories; it should not be treated as a complete backup.

## Third-party tools

Community extensions, scripts, and other third-party export tools are not official shapes.inc tools. **If you choose to use them, you do so at your own risk.** shapes.inc does not guarantee their security, privacy, compatibility, or the completeness of their exports.

Review the permissions and data access a tool requests. Such tools may gain access to private chats or account information, miss content, or stop working after app updates.

Open **Settings** → **General**. Drag the **Font size** slider to make text bigger or smaller, from 50% to 150%. It starts at 100%.

On a computer, **Settings** is in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom, then **General**.

Your choice saves to your account, so your other devices use it too.

## What it changes

- Message text and most text in the app get bigger or smaller.
- Icons, images, and some small labels keep their size.
- 150% is the largest size. If you need more, use browser zoom on a computer, or your phone's own display size or zoom setting. See [Can I zoom in?](/faq/can-i-zoom-in)

## Change the font itself

**Font size** does not change the typeface. A chat can have its own font: chat owners set it under the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** → **Custom Font (Google Fonts)**, and it applies to everyone in that chat. See [Customizing a chat's appearance](/chat-appearance#change-your-font).

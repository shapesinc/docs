---
title: "How do I change font size?"
description: "Change the app font size from Settings."
---

Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones). Drag the **Font size** slider to make text smaller or larger, from 90% to 160%.

Your choice saves automatically.

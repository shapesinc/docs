Yes, by adding that shape to your texting chat in the app. You can't switch to a different chat by text.

Everything you text goes to one texting chat. Your first text starts it as a new chat with Tod, and every later text goes there. `new chat` starts a fresh texting chat, also with Tod.

## Why `chats` and `switch to` don't work

Texting moved to one chat on November 5. `chats`, `switch to 2`, and a bare number are now ordinary messages to your texting chat. They don't list or switch chats, so the reply is a normal answer, not a chat list.

Replying to an old text from a different chat doesn't switch either. You get "That message is from another chat. Send a new message here to continue your connected chat."

## Text with another shape

1. Open your texting chat in the app. It's the chat your texts show up in.
2. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
3. Choose **Participants** → **Add participants**, then use **Shapes** to pick the shape and select **Add**.
4. Text as usual and use the shape's name to talk to it. Its replies come back by text, starting with its name in bold.

Chats with other shapes stay in the app and don't text you. See [Which chat a text goes to](/texting#which-chat-a-text-goes-to).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

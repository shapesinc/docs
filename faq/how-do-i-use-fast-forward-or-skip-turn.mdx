**Skip turn** is now **Keep going**. It moved from the **+** menu to a button under the latest shape reply, and it works in every chat.

- **Keep going** asks the shape that wrote the latest message for one more.
- The arrow next to it offers **5 messages** or **10 messages**, and with several shapes, picks who goes next.
- While the shapes are talking, a bar above the message box shows progress with **Stop**.

Chat owners can use it in group chats, and anyone can in a DM with a shape. It shows once a shape has the last word and hides while a reply is on its way. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

To hear from one shape, say its name or @mention it.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

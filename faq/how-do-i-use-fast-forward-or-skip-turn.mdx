title: "What happened to fast forward and skip turn?"
description: "Skip turn and fast forward are gone along with Chat mode. Ask the shapes to keep talking instead."
keywords: ["skip turn", "fast forward", "let shapes talk", "stop fast forward", "next message author", "shapes keep talking"]
---

**Skip turn** and fast forward are gone. They lived in the **+** menu beside the message box and only appeared in chats set to Chat mode, as **Skip turn**, **Let shapes talk**, and the **5 messages** and **10 messages** runs. Chats no longer have modes, so the options don't appear in any chat. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

To have several shapes keep a conversation going, ask in the chat:

```text wrap
let the shapes keep talking
```

To hear from one shape, say its name or @mention it.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

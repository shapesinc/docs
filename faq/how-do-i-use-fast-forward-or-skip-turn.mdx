**Skip turn** and fast forward are now **Keep going**, in the **+** menu next to the message box. The shapes take turns for 5 or 10 messages without anyone writing. In group chats, only chat owners see it.

1. Select **+**, then **Keep going**.
2. Pick **5 messages** or **10 messages**.
3. With more than one shape in the chat, pick who goes first under **Who starts**.

A bar above the message box shows progress. Select **Stop** on the bar to end the run early. See [Keep the conversation going](/multimessages#keep-the-conversation-going).

To hear from one shape, say its name or @mention it.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

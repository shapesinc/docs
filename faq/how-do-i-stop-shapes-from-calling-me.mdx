---
title: "How do I stop shapes from calling me?"
description: "Shapes don't call you on their own, and Who can call me controls who can ring you in the app."
---

Shapes don't start calls or new chats with you on their own, so Settings no longer has a **Shape reach-outs** switch. A call with a shape starts only when someone in the chat starts it, for example with **⋯** → **Call** followed by the shape's name in a chat with one shape. While a call is active, the top of the chat shows **Join call**.

To control who can ring you:

1. Open **Settings** (in the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Under **Who can call me**, choose **Everyone**, **People I follow**, or **Nobody**.

This controls ringing screens and incoming call notifications.

If a shape keeps checking in on a quiet chat, turn off **Come back later** in that chat's **AI replies**. See [Multiple messages](/multimessages). Telling it to be quiet ("shut up", "leave me alone") also stops those check-ins until someone talks to it again.

## Calls to your phone number

A shape can also [phone your number](/phone-calls) from a shapes.inc number, but only in these cases:

- **You ask it to call you**, for example "call me". If you don't pick up, it rings once more straight away, because Do Not Disturb often silences a first call, then tries again about 5, 15 and 45 minutes later. It stops as soon as you pick up, call it back or write in the chat, or when you tell it to stop calling.
- **Someone you share a chat with asks it to call you** by name or @username, and a number is saved on your account. The shape says who asked it to call, and your number is never shown to them.
- **You set a reminder for yourself**, for example "remind me at 6 to take my meds", and a number is saved on your account. Along with the reminder in the chat, the shape rings you. Say "don't call, just remind me here" if you only want the message. Reminders for a group never ring anyone.
- **You scheduled a call**, for example "call me at 7 to wake me up". Only your own schedules can call you; nobody else can schedule a call to your number. To stop it, select **Pause** or **Cancel** next to it in **Settings** → **Tasks**. See [Schedule a call to you](/phone-calls#schedule-a-call-to-you).

**Who can call me** doesn't cover these phone calls. To stop someone asking a shape to ring your phone, block them, or leave the chats you share with them.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

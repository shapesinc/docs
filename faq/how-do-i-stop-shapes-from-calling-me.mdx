---
title: "How do I stop Shapes from calling me?"
description: "Shapes don't call you on their own. Use Who can call me to control calls from people."
---

Shapes don't start calls or new chats with you on their own, so Settings no longer has a **Shape reach-outs** switch. A call with a Shape starts only when someone in the chat starts it, for example with **Start Call** at the top of a chat with one Shape.

To control who can ring you:

1. Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Under **Who can call me**, choose **Everyone**, **Following** (shown as **People I follow** on phones), or **Nobody**.

This controls ringing screens and incoming call notifications.

If a Shape keeps checking in on a quiet chat, turn off **Come back later** in that chat's **AI replies**. See [Multiple Messages](/multimessages).

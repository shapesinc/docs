---
title: "How do I stop Shapes from calling me?"
description: "Shapes don't call you on their own. Use Who can call me to control calls in the app; a shape phones your number only when you, or someone you share a chat with, asks."
---

Shapes don't start calls or new chats with you on their own, so Settings no longer has a **Shape reach-outs** switch. A call with a Shape starts only when someone in the chat starts it, for example with **Start Call** at the top of a chat with one Shape.

To control who can ring you:

1. Open **Settings** (in the sidebar on desktop, or the **Settings** tab on phones).
2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.
3. Under **Who can call me**, choose **Everyone**, **Following** (shown as **People I follow** on phones), or **Nobody**.

This controls ringing screens and incoming call notifications.

If a Shape keeps checking in on a quiet chat, turn off **Come back later** in that chat's **AI replies**. See [Multiple Messages](/multimessages).

## Calls to your phone number

A Shape can also [phone your number](/phone-calls) from a shapes.inc number, but only when someone asks for that call in a chat:

- **You ask it to call you**, for example "call me". If you don't pick up, it rings once more straight away, because Do Not Disturb often silences a first call. It doesn't keep trying after that.
- **Someone you share a chat with asks it to call you** by name or @username, and a number is saved on your account. The Shape says who asked it to call, and your number is never shown to them.

**Who can call me** doesn't cover these phone calls. To stop someone asking a Shape to ring your phone, block them, or leave the chats you share with them. A Shape never places a phone call from a reminder or scheduled task, and never calls at a set time.

A badge that disappears means the latest automatic check didn't confirm it, not that the engine or its provider changed. shapes.inc checks every engine automatically for three things: reasoning, understanding images, and taking actions. The badges and the **Reasoning effort** setting show what the latest check confirmed.

## What each check controls

| Check | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| Reasoning | **Reasoning** badge, the **Thinks before replying** filter, and **Reasoning effort** |
| Images | **Native Vision** badge and the **Understands images** filter |
| Actions | **Actions** badge, the **Can take actions** filter, and a place on the engine list chats offer |

## How the checks behave

- An engine shows a badge only after its check passes. An engine with no confirmed result shows no badge.
- Each result is checked again every 6 hours.
- When the provider doesn't answer, because it's rate-limited, timed out, or down, the previous result stays for up to 24 hours and the engine is checked again after 1 hour.
- When the provider answers and the check fails, the badge goes away until a later check passes.
- An engine that loses its **Actions** result leaves the engine list chats offer until the check passes again. See [why chats show fewer engines](/faq/why-do-i-see-fewer-engines-in-agent-chats).

## "This AI Engine does not support reasoning"

**Engine settings** shows this message, with **Reasoning effort** greyed out, when the selected engine has no confirmed reasoning result. Other settings in the panel still work. When the check passes again, **Reasoning effort** comes back with the levels the engine supports.

**Reasoning effort** lists only the levels the engine accepts:

- Most engines offer **Default**, **Off**, and some of **Low**, **Medium**, and **High**.
- Engines whose reasoning is only on or off offer **Default**, **Off**, and **On**.
- Engines that always reason show **Minimum** in place of **Off**.
- A level you saved that the engine doesn't accept shows "This effort is unavailable for the selected AI Engine; its default is used."

Open it from the chat's **Settings** → **Engines** → **Engine settings**. Higher effort can take longer and use more credits. See the [AI engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet#what-the-badges-mean).

## Whether to switch engines

The engine is the same; the badge shows the latest check. If you need that capability right now, pick an engine that shows the badge, or wait for the next check. If replies stop arriving, check whether it's [labelled Unstable](/faq/what-does-it-mean-if-an-engine-is-labelled-unstable). To see which engine wrote a reply, open the reply's **⋯** → **Generation details**.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

Rewards are retired, and the Rewards pages and navigation are gone from the app.

Gems, levels, and tasks you hadn't redeemed when Rewards ended weren't converted to credits, and they can't be redeemed anymore. Credits you redeemed before Rewards ended stay in your balance.

## Credit redemptions

The credit packs in the Rewards shop are retired too, so there are no redemption options left to reset. To get more premium usage:

- Subscribe to [Premium](/premium-subscriptions) for a monthly credit allowance. This is the only way to buy new account credits. See [Can I buy credits without a subscription?](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription).
- Use [Shape Credits](/credits) already in your account. They stay usable without a plan.

## Profile styles are now part of Premium

The profile styles that used to come from Rewards are now Premium:

- Name styles
- Frames
- Gradients
- Bubble effects
- Message effects

If you saved any of these before, they show again while you have a Premium plan or account credits. See [Premium](/premium-subscriptions).

## What stays free

- Shape banners
- Custom emojis (up to 50)
- Custom profile HTML and CSS
- Display fonts
- Chat appearance, including bubble colors

For more about the change, see [The October 2026 update](/october-2026-update) and [What's new](/whats-new).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

---
title: "What happened to Rewards?"
description: "Rewards are retired. Profile styles moved to Premium, and many customizations stay free."
---

Rewards are retired, and the Rewards pages and navigation are gone from the app.

## Profile styles are now part of Premium

The profile styles that used to come from Rewards are now Premium:

- Name styles
- Frames
- Gradients
- Bubble effects
- Special reactions
- Message effects

If you saved any of these before, they come back when you subscribe. See [Premium](/premium).

## What stays free

- Shape banners
- Custom emojis (up to 50)
- Custom profile HTML and CSS
- Display fonts
- Bubble styles
- Chat appearance

For more about the change, see [Rewards](/rewards) and [What's New](/whats-new).

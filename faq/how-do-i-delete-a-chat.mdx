---
title: "How do I delete a chat?"
description: "Delete a chat from the sidebar or the chat's Settings, undo a delete, or leave or complete a chat instead."
---

Delete a chat from the sidebar or from the chat's **Settings**. You can delete chats you own and your DMs.

## Delete from the sidebar

1. Open the **⋯** menu next to the chat name. On touch devices, swipe the chat to reveal **⋯**.
2. Choose **Delete**.
3. Select **Yes**.

## Delete from the chat

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. At the bottom, chat owners see **Delete chat**. Select it.
3. Select **Yes**.

After you delete a chat, **Undo** appears for a few seconds. After it disappears, the chat can't be restored from the app.

## Delete several chats at once

There's no multi-select. In any chat, ask a shape to delete the chats by name. Chats you created are deleted for everyone; others are only removed from your list. There's no **Undo** for this.

## Other options

- **Leave a chat:** choose **Leave Chat** from the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar or at the bottom of its **Settings**. Owners can leave too; if you're the last owner, make someone else an owner first, or delete the chat. You'll need an invite link to rejoin.
- **Clear a chat without deleting it:** choose **Archive**. The chat moves to **Settings** → **Archive**, nothing is deleted, and a new message brings it back. See [Archive](/archive).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

---
title: "How do I delete a chat?"
description: "Delete a chat from the sidebar or the chat's Settings, undo a delete, or leave or complete a chat instead."
---

You can delete chats you own and your DMs.

**From the sidebar:** open the **⋯** menu next to the chat name and choose **Delete**, then select **Yes**. On touch devices, swipe the chat to reveal **⋯**.

**From the chat:** select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**. At the bottom, chat owners see **Delete chat**. Select it, then select **Yes**.

After you delete a chat, **Undo** appears for a few seconds in case you change your mind.

## Other options

- **Leave a chat you don't own:** choose **Leave Chat** from the chat's **⋯** menu in the sidebar or at the bottom of its **Settings**. You'll need an invite link to rejoin.
- **Clear a chat without deleting it:** choose **Mark completed**. The chat moves to **Settings** → **Completed**, nothing is deleted, and a new message brings it back. See [Completed](/completed).

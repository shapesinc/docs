---
title: "Where did my chat go?"
description: "Find a chat that's missing from your sidebar, usually in Archive, and bring it back."
---

Chats you haven't used for a while (14 days by default) move out of your sidebar to **Archive**. Nothing is deleted, and archiving a shared chat only changes your own list.

## Find it

- **Search:** search the sidebar for the chat's name. Archived chats show an **Archived** label and a **Restore** button.
- **Archive:** select **Check Archive** at the bottom of the sidebar, or open **Settings** → **Archive**. Use **Search archive** to find it, then select **Restore**.
- **Filters:** if **Unread**, **DMs**, **Social**, or **Private** is selected at the top of the sidebar, switch to **All**.
- **Workspaces:** the sidebar shows chats from your current workspace. Switch workspaces if the chat belongs to another one.

## Bring it back

- Select **Restore** in search or in **Settings** → **Archive**.
- A new message in the chat also brings it back.

To change how long chats stay before they're archived, or to turn it off, open **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Chats & Shapes**. See [Archive](/archive).

If you left the chat, you'll need a new invite link to rejoin.

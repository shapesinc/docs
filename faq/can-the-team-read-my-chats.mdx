No, we don't sit around reading your chats. Your chats are encrypted at rest.

We only open a specific chat when there's a reason, like fixing a bug, looking at a report, or a safety issue. Chats can also be used to improve the product. See the [Privacy Policy](https://talk.shapes.inc/privacy).

If you use **Report a problem** on a reply, the report links to that reply so the team can see what happened. See [Feedback](/feedback#report-a-shape-reply).

## Why is there a passport field?

The passport fields are in **Settings** → **Saved details**. They're optional and only there for travel bookings, so you never need them to chat. Saved details are encrypted at rest and don't go into your chats or a shape's memory. See [Saved Details](/saved-details).

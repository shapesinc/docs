---
title: "How do I start a new chat?"
description: "Create a shared room with the people and AI assistants helping with your next plan or project."
---

Start a room for something you want to do together, such as planning a trip or working through a project. Add the people involved and a Shape to help. On a phone, use the **New** button in the bottom navigation. On larger screens, such as an iPad or computer, use **New Chat** in the sidebar instead.

## Start a new chat

1. Tap **New** in the bottom navigation.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-new-chat-home-cursor-cropped.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the New button in the bottom navigation"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

   On larger screens, select **New Chat** in the sidebar. The rest of the steps are the same.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-sidebar-new-chat-replacement-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the New Chat button in the sidebar"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

2. Select the Shapes or people you want to add, then tap **Continue**.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-new-chat-select-cursor-bottom-cropped.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the Continue button after selecting a Shape"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

3. Give the chat a name if you want to change the suggested one, then tap **Create Chat**.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-new-chat-details-cursor-cropped.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the Create Chat button"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

Your chat is ready to use.

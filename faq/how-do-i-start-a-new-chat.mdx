---
title: "How do I start a new chat?"
description: "Create a shared chat with the people and AI assistants helping with your next plan or project."
---

Start a chat for something you want to do together, such as planning a trip or working through a project. Add the people involved and a Shape to help. Use **New Chat** in the sidebar.

## Start a new chat

1. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-sidebar-new-chat-replacement-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the New Chat button in the sidebar"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

2. Select **AI** to choose a Shape, or **People** to choose people, then tap **Continue**.

3. Give the chat a name if you want to change the suggested one, then tap **Create Chat**.

Your chat is ready to use.

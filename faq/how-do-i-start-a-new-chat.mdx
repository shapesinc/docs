---
title: "How do I start a new chat?"
description: "Create a shared chat with the people and AI assistants helping with your next plan or project."
---

Start a chat for something you want to do together, such as planning a trip or working through a project. Add the people involved and a Shape to help.

## Start a new chat

1. Select **New Chat** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap **New** in the bottom tabs.

2. Select **Shapes** to choose a Shape, or **People** to choose people, then select **Continue**.

3. In **Chat details**, give the chat a name if you want to change the suggested one, then select **Create Chat**.

Your chat is ready to use. Select **Invite** at the top of the chat to share a link with anyone else who should join.

## Other ways to start

- **Quick chat**, at the top of the **New Chat** list, starts a new chat right away without choosing anyone.
- Type in the composer on Home (**Start here…**) to start a chat. The shape chip shows **Auto** until you pick a Shape.
- Inside a chat, open **More actions** → **New chat**. Choose participants, select **Next**, fill in **Chat details**, and select **Create Chat**.
- On a computer, press ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+O.

New chats start in **Auto**, **Chat**, or **Agent**, depending on your **New chats** setting in **Settings**. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

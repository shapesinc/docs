There are two controls. **Message effects** → **Off** stops effects on your own messages. Your device's reduce motion setting stops animations across the app, including the effects on other people's messages.

## Turn off message effects on your messages

A message effect is a sticker drawn on a message bubble when the text has a matching word, such as "thanks", "lol", "hello", or "love".

1. Open **Settings** and select your name or picture at the top.
2. Under **Style**, find **Message effects**. On a phone, open the **Style** tab and look under **In chats**.
3. Choose **Off** and save.

The row then reads "Your messages never play effects." **Off** does not need Premium. **Auto** turns effects back on.

What **Off** covers:

- Your own messages do not play word-triggered effects, for you or for anyone else.
- It does not remove those effects from other people's messages. Each person chooses for their own messages.
- On October 31, messages with the **Classic** bubble show a Halloween effect. **Off** hides it for you on everyone's messages.

Effects play on messages from people with Premium or account credits. Shapes' messages do not play them. Messages sent with the **Plain text** bubble style do not play them either.

## Stop animations everywhere

shapes.inc has no motion switch of its own. It follows the reduce motion setting on your device:

- **iPhone and iPad:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Motion** → **Reduce Motion**.
- **Android:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Remove animations**. The name and place vary by phone maker.
- **Mac:** **System Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Display** → **Reduce motion**.
- **Windows:** **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Visual effects** → turn off **Animation effects**.

With it on:

- The app's animations and transitions are cut to almost nothing.
- Effect stickers on messages still show, but they stop moving. This includes other people's messages.
- The typing indicator holds still, and your own 🎉 reaction does not burst confetti.

What still moves:

- GIFs, videos, and animated pictures that people post.
- Confetti when someone else adds a 🎉 reaction.

For every display setting in one place, see [Accessibility](/accessibility).

A shape knows the time when its chat has **Time Awareness** on. The shape then sees the date and time each message was sent. Agent chats have it on by default.

The shape editor's **Time** card, with **Knows the current date** and **Timezone**, was removed on November 8. There's no time setting on a shape anymore.

## Turn on Time Awareness

1. Select the chat's name at the top to open its **Settings**.
2. Choose **AI settings** and scroll to **General Settings**.
3. Turn on **Time Awareness**. The description reads "Shapes will know when each message was sent and how much time has passed between messages".

Chat owners change this setting. In a DM with a shape, you change it yourself.

## Agent chats use your time

In Agent chats, times and schedules follow your own time, not the shape's. "Remind me at 9am" means 9am where you are. See [Tasks](/tasks).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

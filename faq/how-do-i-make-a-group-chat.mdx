---
title: "How do I make a group chat?"
description: "Start a group chat on shapes.inc and invite the people and shapes involved."
---

Select **New chat**, add the people involved and the shapes, and select **Create Chat**. The shapes research, compare options, and plan with your group.

1. Select **New chat** in the sidebar. On phones, tap the **New** tab.
2. Pick shapes on the **Shapes** tab and people on the **People** tab, then select **Continue**.
3. Give the chat a name in **Chat details** and select **Create Chat**.

You can also start from Home: select **Invite** under **Invite friends to this chat**. The invite dialog opens with a link to share, and inviting from Home takes you into the new chat.

To invite more people later, select **Invite** at the top of the chat, then select **Share** or tap the link to copy it. On a phone, iPad, or Mac, you can also enter a **Phone number** and select **Text**. To add people or shapes directly, select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Participants** → **Add participants**.

For every step, see [Start a chat](/create-a-chat).

## How many shapes can a chat have?

A chat can hold up to 1,000 shapes. A new chat set to **Chat** starts with up to 100, and you can add more afterward. The limit of 8 applies only to shapes a chat makes for you when you ask for one.

## Can I choose my own invite link?

No. Each chat gets one random invite link, and it can't be edited. Use the chat's title and description to show what the group is for.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

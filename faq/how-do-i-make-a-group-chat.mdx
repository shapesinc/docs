---
title: "How do I make a group chat?"
description: "Start a group chat on shapes.inc and invite the people and Shapes involved."
---

Use **New Chat** to start a chat for your group's goal. Add the people involved and the Shapes that will help you research, compare options, or make a plan together.

1. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar. On phones, tap the **New** tab.
2. Pick Shapes on the **Shapes** tab and people on the **People** tab, then select **Continue**.
3. Give the chat a name in **Chat details** and select **Create Chat**.

You can also start from Home: select **Invite** under **Invite friends to this chat**. The invite dialog opens with a link to share, and inviting from Home takes you into the new chat.

To invite more people later, select **Invite** at the top of the chat and use **Copy Link** or **Share via...**. To add people or Shapes directly, select the chat's name to open its **Settings**, then choose **Participants** → **Add participants**.

For the full step-by-step guide, read [How to Create an AI Group Chat](/create-a-chat).

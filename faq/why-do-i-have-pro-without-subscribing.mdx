---
title: "Why do I have Pro if I never subscribed?"
description: "Remaining account credits give you Pro benefits without starting a subscription or creating a payment."
keywords: ["Pro without paying", "never subscribed", "free Pro", "remaining credits", "unexpected upgrade"]
---

**A positive account-credit balance gives you Pro benefits, even without a subscription.** This includes existing credits from earlier purchases, rewards, or gifts. You get premium AI engine access, no ads, longer recent-message context, and paid profile and message styles while account credits remain.

This access does **not** start a subscription, add a monthly credit allowance, or create a payment. A Pro label by itself is not evidence that you subscribed or were charged. Premium engines and other metered features still spend credits.

Without an active subscription, these benefits end when your account-credit balance reaches zero. You can continue chatting with free AI engines. Paid styles return to their defaults, but your selections stay saved and return when you add credits or subscribe.

Credits assigned only to a chat or a shape can fund eligible replies there; they do not give your whole account Pro benefits. Daily free action allowances are also separate from your account-credit balance.

## If you see an actual charge

Check the billing details on your [Premium page](https://talk.shapes.inc/premium), or your App Store or Google Play subscription if you subscribed there. Do not assume that a charge came from the Pro label or from the credit-based access described above.

For private billing details, use [Feedback](https://talk.shapes.inc/feedback). Do not post payment details, receipts, or your account email in the public [Help Me chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

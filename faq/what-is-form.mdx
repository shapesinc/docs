**Form is a physical body for your shape: it sees, hears, and talks in the room, using the same personality, memory, and voice the shape has on shapes.inc.** It is available to pre-order at [shapes.inc/form](https://shapes.inc/form), with an estimated ship date of December 2026.

<Warning>
  A pre-order is a one-time hardware purchase charged when you order. Shipping, taxes, and duties are not included.
</Warning>

## The two models

| Model | Price | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Form 01** | $999 | Sits on your desk. A camera that tracks faces, four microphones, a speaker, and nine moving joints. |
| **Form 02** | $1,999 | Walks. 24 moving joints, hands that open, close, and point, a camera, and an onboard microphone and speaker. |

You say the shape's name and talk. The shape answers in its own voice, and what you say out loud is saved with transcripts to a chat you choose. Each Form runs its own shape, and several can run in the same room.

You need a shape on shapes.inc to use one. See [How to make a shape?](/faq/how-to-make-a-shape)

## What's included

- The assembled Form, with its power supply or battery and charger.
- Quick-start setup.
- The first year of Form Premium.
- Premium Shape Credits: 99,900 with Form 01, or 199,900 with Form 02.

After activation, Form Premium renews every year until you cancel. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next annual charge; cancelling doesn't refund the current term.

## Pre-order

1. Open [shapes.inc/form](https://shapes.inc/form).
2. Select **Pre-order Form 01 · $999** or **Pre-order Form 02 · $1,999**.
3. Pay in the checkout. An **Order confirmed** page shows your order number, and a confirmation arrives by email.

After you order, you're asked which shape, voice, wake name, tools, and routines you want, and progress updates arrive by email. Before dispatch, your address is confirmed, shipping is charged, and tracking is sent.

If you leave the checkout before paying, you aren't charged. Pre-orders open in batches; when the current batch is full, the page says so and the next batch opens later.

## Price, shipping, and refunds

- Prices are in USD and exclude sales tax, VAT, customs tariffs, duties, brokerage, and other local charges.
- Shipping is charged separately before dispatch. Form ships worldwide.
- December 2026 is an estimate and may change.
- Annual service charges and credits you've redeemed or used are non-refundable except where the law requires.
- Delivery timing, returns, warranty, and cancellation rights follow the [Terms](https://shapes.inc/terms) and the law where you live.

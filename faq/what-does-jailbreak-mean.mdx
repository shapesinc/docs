---
title: "How do I give custom instructions to a Shape?"
description: "Use Instructions for a chat's shared goal, or edit a Shape's personality for behavior that should follow it across chats."
---

Use custom instructions to tell a Shape what useful help looks like: explain the group's goal, share constraints, and ask for the tone or format you want.

## Add instructions to a chat

1. Open the chat.
2. Open **More actions** (⋯) in the chat header and select **Instructions**.
3. If **Preset Templates** is shown, choose **Custom instructions** to open the text box. Built-in templates remain available, but their instruction text is hidden while selected.
4. Enter the group's guidance, then click or tap outside the box to save.

You can also find **Instructions** in the chat's **Settings** (select the chat's name at the top) → **AI Configurations**. Chat owners can edit shared instructions, and so can you in a DM with a Shape.

For example:

> Help us plan our study sessions. Ask what each person is working on, explain one idea at a time, and keep a short list of questions we still need to practice.

These instructions apply to the chat. They do not change the Shape's personality everywhere or connect an outside service.

See [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats) for examples.

## Change a Shape's default behavior

If you own the Shape, open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, choose the Shape, and go to **Profile** → **Personality and behavior** → **Response Style**. Choose **Custom** to write in **Custom Response Style**, then select **Save Changes**. Use it for behavior that should follow the Shape across chats.

To change how every Shape replies to you, use your own preset in **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Your preset**. It applies alongside each chat's instructions.

The [Shape personality guide](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality) explains those controls. [Presets](/presets) explains reusable instructions; a preset is guidance for responses, not access to a new engine or feature.

## Improve an instruction

- State the goal and the information the Shape should consider.
- Specify a useful result, such as a comparison, short explanation, or checklist.
- Remove conflicting directions when a response is inconsistent.
- Correct misunderstandings in the conversation so the group can review the change.

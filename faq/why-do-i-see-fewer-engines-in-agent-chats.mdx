Every chat's engine picker lists engines for work. Engines that are good at just chatting are behind **Show more** at the bottom of the list, and a search shows them right away. The manual mode selector was removed; see [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

The list of engines for work updates on its own. An engine is on it when it scores at least 40% of the best engine on the [Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index](https://artificialanalysis.ai/), a public benchmark, and passes its automatic check for actions. The bar rises as better engines come out, so new engines join without waiting for a manual update, and engines that fall far behind leave. Only the newest version of each engine family is listed; when a newer version passes the same checks, it replaces the older one. **Formless v2.1** is always on the list.

In late November 2026 the list has about 30 engines, including Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Fable 5.2; GPT-6 Sol, Astra, Terra and Luna; Gemini 4 Neon, Carbon, Hydrogen and Argon; Grok 4.7, Qwen 4 Max, Kimi K3.1 Pro, GLM 5.3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, MiniMax M3.1 Flash, MiMo-V2.6-Pro and MiMo-V2.6-Flash, and Ling 3.1 Flash. The engine picker always shows the current list first.

- **Formless v2.1** is the free shapes.inc engine. Replies on it don't use credits, whatever your balance. It has a daily limit across shapes.inc; past it, replies move to another free engine.
- An engine is free while its provider's price stays under the limit shapes.inc sets, so an engine can move between free and premium. The **Free** filter in **Choose an AI engine** always shows the current free engines.

## The best free engine

**Formless v2.1**. Pick it in the chat's **Settings** → **Engines**.

## A shape set to an engine that isn't on the list

A shape's own **AI Engine** (under **Settings** → **Your shapes**) isn't used for ordinary replies. The reply comes from, in order:

1. The engine picked for the chat in its **Settings** → **Engines**.
2. The chat owner's pick for new chats, as it was when the chat started.
3. The shapes.inc default: a premium engine when you have 100 or more credits, **Formless v2.1** below that.

A chat set to an engine that isn't one of the engines for work uses it for chatting replies; work replies run on the shapes.inc default.

When you're just chatting with a shape and no engine is picked for the chat, the reply uses the shape's own engine.

## Use an engine that isn't on the list

Engines that are good at just chatting are behind **Show more** at the bottom of the engines for work, and **+** in the picker adds any engine from the full catalog. They answer chatting replies; work replies run on the shapes.inc default. See [Engines in chats](/aienginecheatsheet#engines-in-agent-chats).

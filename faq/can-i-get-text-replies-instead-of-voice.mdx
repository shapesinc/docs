There is no setting on your side that turns a shape's voice replies into text. How often a shape replies with voice, and whether text comes with it, belongs to the shape's owner.

## If you own the shape

1. Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select the shape.
2. Open **Voice Engine**.
3. Under **How often**, choose **Never**. The shape then stops adding voice to its replies on its own.
4. To keep voice and add text, leave **How often** as it is and turn on **Send captions with voice messages** under **Engine**. The spoken text appears under each voice message.
5. Select **Save**.

Captions are off by default. With **Never** set, a shape can still answer in voice when someone sends it a voice message. See [How to Receive a Voice Message from a Shape](/how-to-receive-voice-messages).

## If you don't own the shape

- Ask the owner to choose **Never** under **How often**, or to turn on **Send captions with voice messages**.
- When a voice message has captions, the text shows under it. Long captions have **Show more**. Open the message's **⋯** menu and select **Copy transcript** to copy the text.
- A voice message without captions has no text you can open. Ask the shape in the chat to repeat it in text.
- Voice messages do not play on their own. They play when you press play.

## Send text instead of your own voice

Select the microphone button, **Dictate or record voice**, and record. Open the draft's **⋯** menu and select **Convert to text** to turn a recording of up to 5 minutes into text you can edit before sending. See [How do I use voice messages?](/faq/how-do-i-use-voice-messages)

## Calls

Calls have no live captions. After a call with a shape, a **Call with** card in the chat shows a summary of the call. To stop shapes from calling you, see [How do I stop shapes from calling me?](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-from-calling-me)

## Hear a text reply instead

To listen to a reply that came as text, open the **⋯** menu on a shape's message and select **Read out loud**. See [voice messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#3-read-out-loud).

For more, see [How to Give your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) and [Accessibility](/accessibility).

Reset your password, remove sign-in methods you don't use, then email the team. shapes.inc has no list of signed-in devices, no way to sign out other devices from the app, and no two-factor authentication.

## Secure your account

1. **Reset your password.** Open **Settings** → **Log out**. On the sign-in page, enter your email, select **Continue**, then **Forgot your password?** → **Send reset link**. Follow the link in your inbox.
2. **Secure your Google or Apple account** if you sign in with one. Change that password with Google or Apple.
3. **Check your sign-in methods.** Open **Settings**, select your name or picture, and find **Sign-in**. **Disconnect** any method you don't use. See [Sign-in methods and passwords](/make-or-recover-account#sign-in-methods-and-passwords).
4. **Check your connected apps.** Open **Settings** → **Connected apps** and disconnect anything you didn't connect. See [Connected apps](/connected-apps).
5. **Email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc)** with your username and what you noticed.

<Warning>
  Never share a password or sign-in code in a chat, including with a shape.
</Warning>

If you can't sign in at all, see [Create or recover an account](/make-or-recover-account).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

---
title: "Can I buy credits without a subscription?"
description: "Why Premium shows monthly plans instead of a Buy Shape Credits button, and how existing credits work."
keywords: ["buy credits", "without subscription", "one-time credits", "credit packs", "Buy Shape Credits"]
---

Separate one-time credit packs are not currently available. To purchase new account credits, open **Settings** → **Premium** and choose a monthly plan. If you only see monthly plans, you aren't missing a **Buy Shape Credits** button.

You can still use credits already in your account without an active subscription. Credits already assigned to a chat or Shape can also fund eligible premium replies. See [Shape Credits](/credits).

A Premium plan renews monthly until you cancel. [Cancelling keeps credits already added to your balance](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium), while your plan benefits end when the paid period ends. Review the plan and renewal terms before subscribing.

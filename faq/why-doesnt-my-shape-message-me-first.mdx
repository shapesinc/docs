Shapes don't post on their own after a chat goes quiet. A shape posts when someone in the chat talks to it, or for a reminder or watch someone asked for. In a group chat, it also joins in when it can help, and an @mention always gets a reply. Shapes don't start new chats with you either. See [Why did a shape start a chat with me?](/faq/why-did-a-shape-create-a-chat-with-me)

## Come back later is gone

**Come back later** had a shape check in after a chat went quiet. It was removed from **AI replies** in November 2026, along with the **Free will (experimental)** switch.

**Keep the convo going** and the other triggers appeared only in group chats set to Chat mode. Chats no longer have modes, so they no longer appear. See [the October 2026 update](/october-2026-update#what-was-retired).

## Get a message at a set time

For a message at a time you choose, ask a shape in any chat:

```text wrap
Message me every morning at 8 with my plan for the day.
```

It appears under **Settings** → **Tasks**. See [Tasks](/tasks).

If you text shapes.inc, only your texting chat texts you. See [When replies come by text](/texting#when-replies-come-by-text).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

A shape messages you unprompted only in a chat you're already in, and only through that chat's **AI replies** triggers. Shapes don't start new chats with you on their own. See [Why did a shape start a chat with me?](/faq/why-did-a-shape-create-a-chat-with-me)

## Which triggers each chat shows

Open the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **AI replies**. Under **Auto-reply triggers**, the chat shows:

| Chat | Triggers |
| :-- | :-- |
| DM with a shape | **Free will (experimental)**, **Come back later** |
| Agent chat with friends | **Free will (experimental)**, always on, and **Come back later** |
| Group chat in Chat mode | Up to all six, including **Keep the convo going** and **Always have something to say** |
| DM between two people | None. Tod never follows up on its own |

**Keep the convo going** isn't gone. It doesn't show in DMs with a shape. It also only adds messages right after a reply; it never starts a conversation later.

## How Come back later works

**Come back later** is the trigger that sends a message after a quiet spell. It's on by default.

- The shape checks in once, at a random time 6 to 12 hours after the last person's message.
- It doesn't check in again until someone posts.
- After you tell it to be quiet ("shut up", "leave me alone"), check-ins stop until a person talks to it again.

A bug stopped **Come back later** check-ins in Chat mode after a conversation wrapped up. It was fixed on November 8.

## Get a message at a set time

For a message at a time you choose, ask a shape in an Agent chat:

```text wrap
Message me every morning at 8 with my plan for the day.
```

It appears under **Settings** → **Tasks**. See [Tasks](/tasks).

If you text shapes.inc, only your texting chat texts you. See [When replies come by text](/texting#when-replies-come-by-text).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

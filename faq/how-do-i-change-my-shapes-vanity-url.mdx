---
title: "How do I change my Shape's vanity URL?"
description: "A Shape's username can't be changed right now. Duplicate the Shape to get a different URL."
---

You can’t change a Shape’s username right now, because the feature was causing issues. To get a different URL, duplicate the Shape: open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Advanced**. Under **Duplicate**, enter **Name of the copy** and select **Duplicate**. The name influences the new URL/username. Save or discard pending edits first; the copy uses the saved version.

The copy is yours and starts from the Shape's saved settings. Chats, memories, and stats stay with the original Shape.

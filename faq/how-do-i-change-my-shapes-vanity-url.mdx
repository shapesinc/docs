---
title: "How do I change my shape's vanity URL?"
description: "A shape's username can't be changed right now, but you can duplicate the shape to get a different URL."
---

You can’t change a shape’s username right now, because the feature was causing issues. To get a different URL, duplicate the shape:

1. Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select the shape.
2. Choose **Advanced**.
3. Under **Duplicate**, enter **Name of the copy**. The name influences the new URL/username.
4. Select **Duplicate**.

Save or discard pending edits first; the copy uses the saved version.

The copy is yours and starts from the shape's saved settings. Chats, memories, and stats stay with the original shape.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

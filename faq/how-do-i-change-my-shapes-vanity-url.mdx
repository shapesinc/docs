---
title: "How do I change my Shape's vanity URL?"
description: "Learn what to do if you want a different Shape URL."
---

Currently, you can’t directly change a Shape’s username, as this feature was causing issues. If having a custom URL is important, you can duplicate the Shape: open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose its **Settings** section. Under **Duplicate This Shape**, enter a **Nickname** and select **Create Shape**. The nickname influences the new URL/username.

The copy is yours and starts from the Shape's saved settings. Chats, memories, and stats stay with the original Shape.

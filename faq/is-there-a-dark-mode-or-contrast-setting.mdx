Yes, there is a dark mode. Open **Settings** → **Appearance**.

- **Mode:** choose **Auto**, **Light**, or **Dark**. **Auto** follows your device.
- **Light theme:** White, Slate, Fog, Oat, Sepia, Sage, Lavender, Clay, or Denim.
- **Dark theme:** Graphite, Ink, or Espresso.

You pick one light theme and one dark theme. **Auto** uses whichever matches your device at the time. Picking a theme from the other group while in **Light** or **Dark** switches the mode to match.

Themes are free on every plan and save to your account, so your other devices use them too.

On a computer, **Settings** is in the sidebar. On a phone, tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom, then **Appearance**.

## Brightness and contrast

shapes.inc has no brightness slider and no high-contrast theme. The closest options:

- **White is too bright:** pick a tinted light theme such as Oat, Sepia, or Fog, or switch **Mode** to **Dark**.
- **Brightness:** use your device's brightness control.
- **Text is hard to make out:** raise **Font size** in **Settings** → **General**. See [How do I make text bigger?](/faq/how-do-i-change-font-size)

## One chat is hard to read

A chat's background and bubble colors come from that chat's **Chat Appearance**, and they apply to everyone in it. Your theme does not change them. Chat owners set them in group chats. In a DM, anyone in the chat can.

Ask a chat owner to open the chat's **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** and lower **Background Opacity**, keep **Auto text contrast** on under **Bubble Colors**, or select **Reset appearance**. See [Customizing a chat's appearance](/chat-appearance).

For every display setting in one place, see [Accessibility](/accessibility).

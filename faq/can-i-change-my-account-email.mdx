No. shapes.inc has no setting to change the email address on your account. You can change how you sign in with that email.

## Change how you sign in

1. Open **Settings** and select your name or picture at the top. **Edit profile** opens.
2. Find **Sign-in**. It lists **Email**, **Google**, and **Apple**, and marks the one you're using as **current sign-in**.
3. Select **Connect** next to a method to add it, or **Disconnect** to remove one.

- A new sign-in method must use the same email address as your account. Otherwise you see "You can only link a login method that uses the same email address."
- **Connect** next to **Email** sets a password, so an account made with Google or Apple can also sign in with email.
- You can't disconnect the method you're signed in with. Sign in another way, or connect another method, first.

See [Sign-in methods and passwords](/make-or-recover-account#sign-in-methods-and-passwords).

## Your name in chats

To change the name shapes call you, edit your **Name** on **Edit profile**, or set up a profile under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Profiles**. See [How do I edit my profile?](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-profile)

**shapes.inc is ad-supported, so on the free plan you can see sponsored messages in Chat mode.** Each one is labelled **Sponsored**. Premium, any account-credit balance, Agent chats, and DMs don't show them.

## Where sponsored messages appear

A sponsored message appears in the conversation as a message from a shape in that chat. It has a short recommendation in the shape's voice and a product card marked **Sponsored**. The product belongs to an advertiser. Selecting the card opens the advertiser's page in a new tab.

Sponsored messages are chosen from the chat they appear in. The card says so under **Ad options**: "Shown based on this chat."

Not every message gets one, and a card whose offer has expired disappears.

## Who doesn't see them

- **Agent chats and DMs** don't show ads for anyone, on any plan.
- **Premium members** don't see ads anywhere, including chats shared with people on the free plan. What other members see doesn't change.
- **Accounts with credits** get the same: while your account has a credit balance, ads are hidden for you, with or without a plan. See [Pro benefits without a subscription](/credits#pro-benefits-without-a-subscription).

## Stop seeing ads

- **Everywhere:** [get Premium](/premium-subscriptions), or keep a credit balance on your account.
- **In one chat:** switch the chat to Agent. See [Chat, Agent, and Auto](/chat-and-agent-modes).
- **One card:** open **Ad options** (⋯) on the card and select **Hide this ad**. The card disappears for you.

The same menu has **Not relevant** and **Report ad**. Both remove the card for you and send that feedback.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

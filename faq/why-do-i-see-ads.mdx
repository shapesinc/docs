**shapes.inc is ad-supported, so you may see sponsored messages in some chats.** Each one is labelled **Sponsored**.

## What a sponsored message looks like

A sponsored message appears in the conversation as a message from a shape in that chat. It has a short recommendation in the shape's voice and a product card marked **Sponsored**. The product belongs to an advertiser. Selecting the card opens the advertiser's page in a new tab.

Not every message gets one, and a card whose offer has expired disappears.

## Hide or report an ad

Open **Ad options** (⋯) on the card:

- **Hide this ad** removes the card for you.
- **Not relevant** and **Report ad** remove the card for you and send that feedback.

## Remove ads

[Premium](/premium-subscriptions) removes ads for you.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

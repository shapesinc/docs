shapes.inc is made by [Circle Labs, Inc.](https://circlelabs.xyz), a company based in San Francisco.

Circle Labs was founded in 2021 by co-founders Anushk Mittal (CEO) and Noorie Dhingra (COO). See the [team](https://circlelabs.xyz/team/) and the [company's milestones](https://circlelabs.xyz/products/).

You can reach the company at hi@circlelabs.xyz. For help with the app, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

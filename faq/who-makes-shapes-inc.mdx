shapes.inc is made by [Circle Labs, Inc.](https://circlelabs.xyz), a company based in San Francisco. shapes.inc is the product; Circle Labs is the company behind it.

## Founders and team

Circle Labs was founded in 2021 by co-founders Anushk Mittal (CEO) and Noorie Dhingra (COO). See the full [leadership team](https://circlelabs.xyz/team/).

## History

- **2020–2021:** shapes started as AI friends on Discord.
- **2024–2025:** shapes on Discord reached over a million people a month, and the shapes.inc API let developers bring shapes into their own apps.
- **Summer 2025:** the shapes.inc app launched as a place to hang out with friends and AI.
- **October 2026:** shapes.inc moved to collaboration: shapes are assistants that do things with you and your people.

See the [milestones](https://circlelabs.xyz/products/) and [mission](https://circlelabs.xyz/mission/).

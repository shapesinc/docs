---
title: 'What does it mean if an engine is labelled "Unstable"?'
description: "Learn why an AI engine may be temporarily unavailable and how to switch to another one."
---

An engine labelled **Unstable** is temporarily unavailable and cannot be used at the moment.

This can happen for several reasons, including:

- The engine has been rate-limited by its provider
- The engine provider is experiencing an outage or downtime
- The engine provider has stopped supporting the engine

Choose another available engine to continue chatting. In a DM with a Shape or a chat you own, open **+** → **Engine** in the composer, type `/model`, or open the chat's **Settings** → **Engines**. See the [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) for help choosing.

You can try the unstable engine again later to see if it has become available.

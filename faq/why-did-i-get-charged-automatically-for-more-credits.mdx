---
title: "Why did I get charged automatically for more credits?"
description: "Understand auto top-up, Premium plan auto-upgrades, and other automatic credit charges."
---

Check **Settings** → **Premium** and your receipt to identify the charge. It may be a subscription renewal, an allowance upgrade you enabled, or a legacy account-credit top-up. Having a saved payment method does not mean every current account uses legacy auto top-up.

Legacy auto top-up can charge a saved payment method when account credits run out. Accounts that moved to subscription billing no longer use that legacy flow. See [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up) and the billing terms shown for your account.

Use the billing-management option on your Premium page. If it offers **Payment History**, review your saved payment methods there. Removing a payment method does not cancel an active subscription.

## If you have a monthly Premium plan

Each paid month adds your plan's credit allowance to your balance. If your Premium page shows **Auto-upgrade when credits run out** and it's turned on, running out moves you to the next allowance and bills the difference. Turn it off on the Premium page to be asked first. To cancel, use the management option shown for your billing provider: **Manage billing** where offered, or **Manage subscription** for a store-managed plan. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions).

---
title: "Why did I get charged automatically for more credits?"
description: "Understand auto top-up, Premium plan auto-upgrades, and other automatic credit charges."
---

This happens when auto top-up is enabled. Auto top-up is enabled by default when you have a saved payment method. When your account credits run low, shapes.inc refills your balance so your Shapes can keep running. See [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up).

Auto top-up is shown during checkout when you buy credits. It is also explained in the [Terms of Service](https://shapes.inc/terms).

If you do not want to use auto top-up, remove your saved payment method from **Payment History** on [shapes.inc/premium](https://shapes.inc/premium).

## If you have a monthly Premium plan

Each paid month adds your plan's credit allowance to your balance. If your Premium page shows **Auto-upgrade when credits run out** and it's turned on, running out moves you to the next allowance and bills the difference. Turn it off on the Premium page to be asked first. To cancel a plan, use **Manage billing**. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions)

Need help with a billing issue? Reach out to us anytime at [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc)

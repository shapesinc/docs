The charge is a Premium renewal, an allowance auto-upgrade you turned on, or the one-time move from retired auto top-up to the **$25 monthly Pro plan**. Check **Settings** → **Premium** and your receipt to see which one it is.

## Charged $25 but never subscribed

Auto top-up is retired, and its earlier top-up charge no longer runs. One case still charges a saved card. When all of these are true, your account is moved once to the $25 monthly Pro plan:

- Your account has a card saved from buying credits earlier.
- You have never had a Premium plan.
- Your account credits reach zero while you use them.

The card is charged $25 for the first month, the plan's credits are added to your balance, and you get an email that the subscription started. The plan then renews every month until you cancel it. It doesn't happen if you have credits left, have or had a Premium plan, have no saved card, or your last payment was declined. It applies to account credits, not credits on a chat or a shape.

### Stop future charges

1. Open **Settings** → **Premium** on the web.
2. Select **Manage billing**.
3. Cancel the subscription in the billing portal.

The move happens only once. After you cancel, your account isn't moved to a plan again.

These don't stop the renewal:

- **Auto upgrade** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**. It only chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use.
- **Auto-upgrade when credits run out** on the Premium page. It controls allowance upgrades, not the monthly renewal.
- Removing your card. An active subscription stays active.

**Don't use my credits**, in a chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**, keeps that chat from spending your account credits, so running out in that chat doesn't trigger the move. See [Auto top-up](/auto-top-up).

## If you have a monthly Premium plan

Each paid month adds your plan's credit allowance to your balance. If your Premium page shows **Auto-upgrade when credits run out** and it's turned on, running out moves you to the next allowance and bills the difference. Turn it off on the Premium page to be asked first. To cancel, use the management option shown for your billing provider: **Manage billing** where offered, or **Manage subscription** for a store-managed plan. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions).

On the web, **Manage billing** on your Premium page opens the billing portal with your invoices and saved payment methods. Removing a payment method does not cancel an active subscription.

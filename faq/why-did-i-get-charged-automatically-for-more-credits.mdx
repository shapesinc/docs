---
title: "Why did I get charged automatically for more credits?"
description: "Identify an automatic charge: a Premium renewal, an allowance auto-upgrade, or the one-time move from retired auto top-up to a monthly plan."
---

The charge can be a subscription renewal, an allowance upgrade you enabled, or the one-time move from retired auto top-up to a monthly plan. Check **Settings** → **Premium** and your receipt to identify it.

Auto top-up is retired, and its earlier top-up charge no longer runs. An account that has a card saved from buying credits earlier, has never had a Premium plan, and runs out of account credits is moved once to the lowest monthly Premium plan. The card is charged for the first month, and the plan renews monthly until you cancel. See [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up) and the billing terms shown for your account.

On the web, **Manage billing** on your Premium page opens the billing portal with your invoices and saved payment methods. Removing a payment method does not cancel an active subscription.

## If you have a monthly Premium plan

Each paid month adds your plan's credit allowance to your balance. If your Premium page shows **Auto-upgrade when credits run out** and it's turned on, running out moves you to the next allowance and bills the difference. Turn it off on the Premium page to be asked first. To cancel, use the management option shown for your billing provider: **Manage billing** where offered, or **Manage subscription** for a store-managed plan. See [What is shapes.inc Premium?](/premium-subscriptions).

For a billing issue, email [hi@shapes.inc](mailto:hi@shapes.inc).

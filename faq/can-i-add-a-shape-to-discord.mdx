---
title: "Can I add a shape to Discord?"
description: "No. You can't add a shape to a Discord server, and there's no bot token step."
keywords: ["Discord", "Discord server", "Discord bot", "bot token", "add shape to Discord"]
---

No. You can't add a shape to a Discord server, so there's no bot token to enter. Shapes live in shapes.inc, and you can also reach them from [Slack](/slack-and-hooks) and by [text](/texting). [Start a chat](/create-a-chat) and invite your friends instead.

  Need more help? Ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).
</Tip>

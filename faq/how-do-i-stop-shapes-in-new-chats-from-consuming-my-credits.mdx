---
title: "How do I stop Shapes in new chats from consuming my credits?"
description: "Start new chats with your account credits disabled."
---

To keep Shapes in new chats from using your account credits automatically, enable **Don’t use my credits in new chats**. New chats and channels will start with your account credits disabled, and you can change the setting for each chat later.

## Disable credits in new chats

1. Tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation. On larger screens, such as an iPad or computer, the same icon is in the upper-left corner of the sidebar.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-home-settings-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the Settings icon in the bottom navigation"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

2. Select **Notifications & privacy**.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-settings-notifications-privacy-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to Notifications and privacy in Settings"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

3. Under **Credits**, enable **Don’t use my credits in new chats**.

<img
  src="/images/shapes-faq-mobile-settings-credits-cursor.png"
  alt="Cursor pointing to the Don’t use my credits in new chats toggle"
  style={{ maxWidth: "390px", width: "100%", borderRadius: "1rem" }}
/>

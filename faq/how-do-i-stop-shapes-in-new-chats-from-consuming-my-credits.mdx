---
title: "How do I stop Shapes in new chats from consuming my credits?"
description: "Start new chats with your credits turned off, or turn them off for a single chat."
keywords: ["Don't use my credits", "credits", "new chats", "Profile & credits", "Personalization"]
---

To keep Shapes in new chats from using your credits automatically, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats**. It sets the default for chats you start, and you can still change the setting for each chat later.

## Turn off credits in new chats

1. Open **Settings**. On a phone, tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation. On a computer, select the **Settings** gear at the top of the sidebar.

2. Select **Personalization**.

3. Under **Credits**, turn on **Don't use my credits in new chats**.

## Turn off credits for one chat

1. Open the chat and select its name at the top to open the chat's **Settings**.

2. Select **Profile & credits**.

3. Under **Credits**, turn on **Don't use my credits**. When it's on, the chat won't spend your credits, and they won't upgrade replies there.

<Tip>
  **Auto upgrade**, also under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, only chooses which premium engine upgrades use. Turning it off doesn't stop chats from spending your credits. See [Shape Credits](/credits) for other ways to control spending.
</Tip>

---
title: "Where did the thinking panel go?"
description: "Understand reasoning effort, the internal monologue setting, and why a Shape's thinking text is no longer shown."
keywords: ["thinking panel", "thought process", "internal monologue", "Show reasoning", "reasoning effort"]
---

The current chat interface no longer displays the old thinking or internal monologue panel. A missing panel does not, by itself, mean the engine stopped using reasoning.

**Reasoning effort** controls how much effort a supported engine uses to produce its answer. Higher effort can take longer and use more credits. It does not turn the old thinking panel back on.

To change a chat's effort level, open its **Settings** → **Engines** → **Engine settings** → **Reasoning effort**. Only levels supported by the selected engine are available.

Shape owners may also see **Show Internal Monologue (experimental)** under **Settings** → **Your shapes** → choose the Shape → **AI Engine**. For supported engines, that toggle makes the Shape's **Reasoning effort** setting available. It does not restore the old panel in the current chat interface.

Use a reply's **⋯** → **Generation details** to check its engine, time, and credits. In Agent chats, **Show steps** shows actions such as searches and app use; it is not the old thinking panel. See the [AI Engine Guide](/aienginecheatsheet) and [Agent reply details](/chat-and-agent-modes#see-what-a-shape-did).

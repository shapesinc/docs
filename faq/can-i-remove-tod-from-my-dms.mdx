Every DM between two people includes Tod. You can't remove Tod from a DM or turn it off for one. Tod joins older DMs too, the next time one is opened or sent to.

Tod stays quiet while you two talk. It replies only when one of you addresses it by name or @mention:

```text wrap
tod, summarize our action items from the call
```

## What Tod does in a DM

- Answers from the DM's history, including transcripts of calls in it.
- Stays out of greetings and isn't triggered by being talked about.
- Drops a reply it started if the other person answers first.
- Never follows up on its own, so the DM gets no check-ins.
- Charges the reply to whoever asked.

The DM keeps its name. To keep Tod out of the conversation, don't address it.

See [DMs between people](/chat-and-agent-modes#dms-between-people).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

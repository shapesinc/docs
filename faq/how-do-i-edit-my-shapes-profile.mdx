---
title: "How do I edit my Shape's profile?"
description: "Edit a Shape's avatar, banner, name, and descriptions in Settings → Your shapes."
---

Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select the Shape. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**. Only the Shape's owners can edit it.

Select **Profile** in the editor:

- **Avatar and banner:** drop an image on either one, or tap its pencil. Images save right away.
- **Basics:** the Shape's name and **Description** (who it is and what it does).
- **Response style**, **Greeting**, **Personality**, **Conversation**, and **Custom messages:** the shape’s response style, first message, traits, and other behavior settings. See [the personality guide](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality).

Open **Share** to edit the **Description** shown on the Shape's shared page, or expand **Page customization** to edit its **HTML** and **CSS**, with **Use custom HTML and CSS** enabled. Select **View public page** to open the page.

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom to keep text changes, or **Reset** to undo them.

---
title: "How do I edit my Shape's profile?"
description: "Edit a Shape's avatar, banner, name, tagline, and description in Settings → My Shapes."
---

Open **Settings** → **My Shapes** and select the Shape. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**. Only the Shape's owners can edit it.

The editor opens on **Profile**. Use it to change the Shape's public profile:

- **Avatar and banner:** drop an image on either one, or tap its pencil. Images save right away.
- **Basics:** **Name**, **Tagline** (a one-liner shown under the name), and **Description** (what people see on the profile).
- **Discovery:** **Category** and tags.
- **Shape details:** optional extras for the profile page, such as featured prompts and typical phrases.

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom to keep text changes, or **Discard** to undo them. To check how the profile looks to other people, open **Manage** → **About** and use the profile link there.

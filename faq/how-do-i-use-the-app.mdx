---
title: "How do I use the app?"
description: "A short beginner intro to using shapes.inc: Home, new chats, Chat and Agent, and where to find settings and help."
---

shapes.inc is a multiplayer assistant for making decisions and getting things done together. Bring people and an AI assistant called a Shape into a shared chat, explain your goal, and ask it to help compare options, draft a plan, or work through a problem.

Start from the composer on Home (**Start here…**), or choose **New Chat** in the sidebar to select **Shapes** and **People**. On a phone, the bottom tabs are **Home**, **Chats**, **New**, and **Settings**. Find earlier conversations in the chat list or with **Search...**.

Chats can be **Chat**, for talking things through, or **Agent**, where Shapes can search the web, run code, and use apps you've connected. **Auto**, the default, picks for you. See [Chat and Agent](/chat-and-agent-modes).

Open **Settings** to edit your profile, set your preset and location in **Personalization**, connect apps, and change theme, app language, font size, and notification preferences. See [Navigation](/navigation) for where everything lives.

If you are new, start with the [Introduction](/introduction). If you used shapes.inc before October 2026, see [What's new](/whats-new).

<Tip>
  Need more help? Open **Settings** → **Support Chat**, or ask in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).
</Tip>

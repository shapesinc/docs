Start a chat, add people and a shape, and say what you're trying to do. shapes.inc is a group chat app. Shapes are group assistants: they compare options, draft plans, and work through problems with your group.

Start from the composer on Home (**Start here…**), or choose **New chat** in the sidebar to select **Shapes** and **People**. On a phone, the bottom tabs are **Home**, **Chats**, **New**, and **Settings**. Find earlier conversations in the chat list or with **Search...**.

In every chat, shapes talk things through with you and also search the web, run code, and use apps you've connected. When you're just chatting with a shape, it replies in its own voice. There's no mode to pick. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

Open **Settings** to edit your profile, set your preset and location in **Personalization**, connect apps, and change theme, app language, font size, and notification preferences. **Settings** → **Usage** shows today's free allowances and when they reset. See [Navigation](/navigation) for where everything lives.

## Other ways to use shapes.inc

- **Mac and Windows apps.** Download them from **Settings** → **Download the app**, or at [shapes.inc/downloads](https://shapes.inc/downloads). The desktop app can connect your computer so a shape works on it when you ask. See [Connect your computer](/connect-your-computer).
- **Texting.** Where texting is available, turn on **Text notifications** under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** to get replies by text and text back to keep a chat going. Text STOP to turn texts off.
- **Calls.** A shape can call your phone when you ask, and you can call the shapes.inc number you text. See [Phone calls](/phone-calls).

If you are new, start with the [Introduction](/introduction). If you used shapes.inc before October 2026, see [What's new](/whats-new).

To get help in the app, open **Settings** → **Support Chat**.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

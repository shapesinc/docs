---
title: "How do I use the app?"
description: "A short beginner intro to using Shapes, Inc."
---

Shapes, Inc. is a multiplayer assistant for making decisions and getting things done together. Bring people and an AI assistant called a Shape into a shared chat, explain your goal, and ask it to help compare options, draft a plan, or work through a problem.

Start from the **What are we doing?** prompt, or choose **New Chat** in the sidebar to select **AI** and **People**. Find earlier conversations in the chat list or with **Search...**. Open account **Settings** to edit your profile, theme, app language, font size, and notification preferences.

If you are new, start with the [Introduction](/introduction). It explains the main parts of the app and what to do first.

<Tip>
  Need more help? Ask in our [support chat](https://talk.shapes.inc/support).
</Tip>

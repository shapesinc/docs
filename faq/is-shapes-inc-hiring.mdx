Yes. [Circle Labs](/faq/who-makes-shapes-inc), the company behind shapes.inc, is hiring for design, engineering, and research roles in San Francisco.

See the current open roles on [circlelabs.xyz/careers](https://circlelabs.xyz/careers/).

## How to apply

Email [jobs@shapes.inc](mailto:jobs@shapes.inc) with:

- the role you're interested in
- the most impressive thing you've built or studied, and what you contributed
- links to relevant projects, a portfolio, or research

Don't see your role? Email anyway and tell the team what you'd bring.

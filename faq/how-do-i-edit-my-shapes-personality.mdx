---
title: "How do I edit my Shape's personality?"
description: "Edit a Shape's description, greeting, response style, and traits in the Personality section of Settings → My Shapes."
---

Open **Settings** → **My Shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Personality**. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**. Only the Shape's owners can edit it.

**Personality** has these parts:

- **Core:** **Description** (who the Shape is and what it does) and **Greeting** (the first message it sends to someone new).
- **Response style:** the length and format of every reply. Choose **Human**, **Short creative**, **Long creative**, **Intelligent**, **Balanced**, **Unrestricted**, or **Custom**. Editing the **Instructions** switches the style to **Custom**.
- **Traits:** personality, tone, age, birthday, and appearance.
- **Story & taste:** story, likes, and dislikes.
- **Conversation:** conversational goals and example exchanges.
- **Command replies:** what the Shape says after `/wack` and `/sleep`.

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom when you're done. If you leave without saving, you're asked whether to keep editing or discard your changes.

To change the Shape's name, avatar, or public description, use **Profile** instead. See [How do I edit my Shape's profile?](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile)

---
title: "How do I edit my Shape's personality?"
description: "Edit a Shape's description, initial message, response style, and traits in the Profile section of Settings → Your shapes."
---

Open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Profile**. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**. Only the Shape's owners can edit it.

Edit the **Description** under **Basic**. Open **Personality and behavior** for the other fields:

- **Response Style:** choose a built-in style, or choose **Custom** and write in **Custom Response Style**. Built-in instruction text is hidden while that style is selected.
- **initial message:** the first message the Shape sends to someone new.
- **Personality Traits**, **Tone**, **Age**, **Birthday**, and **Appearance**.
- **Story**, **Likes**, and **Dislikes**.
- **Conversational Goals** and **Conversational Examples**.
- **Custom Messages:** what the Shape says after `/wack` and `/sleep`.

Select **Save Changes** in the bar at the bottom when you're done. If you leave without saving, you're asked whether to keep editing or discard your changes.

The Shape's name and avatar are also in **Profile**. The description shown on its shared page is in **Share**. See [How do I edit my Shape's profile?](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile)

---
title: "How do I edit my Shape's personality?"
description: "Edit a Shape's description, initial message, response style, and traits in the Profile section of Settings → Your shapes."
---

Open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Profile**. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**. Only the Shape's owners can edit it.

In **Profile**, use these sections:

- **Response style:** choose a built-in style, or choose **Custom** and write in **Instructions**. Built-in instruction text is hidden while that style is selected.
- **Basics:** the name and description.
- **Greeting → First message:** the first message the shape sends to someone new.
- **Personality:** **Traits**, **Tone**, **Age**, **Birthday**, **Story**, **Likes**, **Dislikes**, and **Appearance**.
- **Conversation:** **Conversation goals** and **Example chats**.
- **Custom messages:** **Reset message** and **Sleep message**, used after `/wack` and `/sleep`.

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom when you're done. If you leave without saving, you're asked whether to keep editing or discard your changes.

The Shape's name and avatar are also in **Profile**. The description shown on its shared page is in **Share**. See [How do I edit my Shape's profile?](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile)

title: "How do I switch between Chat and Agent?"
description: "You don't need to anymore: Chat, Agent, and Auto modes are gone. Every chat can take actions, and when you're just chatting, a shape replies in its own voice."
keywords: ["Chat mode", "Agent mode", "Auto mode", "New chats setting", "mode missing", "where did Chat mode go", "just chatting"]
---

You don't need to. Chats no longer have **Chat**, **Agent**, or **Auto** modes, and there is nothing to pick. The mode choice in a chat's **AI settings** and **New chats** under **Settings** → **General** are gone.

- **Every chat can take actions.** Shapes search the web, run code, use your connected apps, and take other actions when your request needs them, with the same engines and credits Agent chats used.
- **Just chatting works as before.** When you're just chatting with a shape, it replies in its own voice, the way Chat mode did. Your next message can ask for help.

Each message is read on its own, so you don't switch anything back. If a shape answers the wrong way, say plainly what you want, for example "can you find us a train to Porto?".

See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes) for more.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

keywords: ["Chat mode", "Agent mode", "Auto mode", "New chats setting", "mode missing", "where did Chat mode go", "play a scene", "in character"]
---

You don't need to. Chats no longer have **Chat**, **Agent**, or **Auto** modes, and there is nothing to pick. The mode choice in a chat's **AI settings** and **New chats** under **Settings** → **General** are gone.

- **Every chat can take actions.** Shapes search the web, run code, use your connected apps, and take other actions when your request needs them, with the same engines and credits Agent chats used.
- **Scenes get in-character replies.** When you play a scene with a shape, it answers that message in character, the way Chat mode did. Your next message can go back to asking for help.

Each message is read on its own, so you don't switch anything back after a scene. If a shape answers the wrong way, say plainly what you want: "out of the scene: find us a train to Porto", or keep writing what your character says or does to stay in the scene.

See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes) for more.

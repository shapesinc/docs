title: "How do I switch between Chat and Agent?"
description: "The manual mode selector was removed. Shapes still chat in their own voice and use web search, code, and connected apps when your request needs them."
keywords: ["Chat mode", "Agent mode", "Auto mode", "New chats setting", "mode missing", "where did Chat mode go", "just chatting"]
---

You no longer need to switch: the manual mode selector was removed, and the capabilities remain. A shape uses your message and the conversation to decide how to reply.

- **Agent capabilities remain available.** Ask a shape to search the web, run code, or use your connected apps. Access, approvals, engine availability, and credits still apply.
- **You can still just chat.** The shape replies in its own voice. You can continue the conversation or ask for help without changing a setting.

The old **Chat**, **Agent**, and **Auto** choices in a chat's **AI settings**, and **New chats** under **Settings** → **General**, were removed in November 2026. This removed the manual controls, not the ability to chat or get things done. See [What's new](/whats-new).

If a shape misunderstands, correct it in your next message: "Keep chatting with me; I'm not asking you to search" or "Please search for a train to Porto." If it keeps happening, share the affected message in support so the team can investigate.

See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes) for more.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

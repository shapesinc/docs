You don't need to switch. Chat, Agent, and Auto were removed on November 23, 2026, and every chat now does both. A shape reads your message and the conversation to decide how to reply.

- **You can still just chat.** The shape replies in its own voice. You can keep the conversation going or ask for help without changing a setting.
- **Agent capabilities remain available.** Ask a shape to search the web, run code, or use your connected apps. Access, approvals, engine availability, and credits still apply.

## Why they were removed

The modes were a step on the way to what shapes.inc does now: it works out from each message whether you're just chatting or trying to get something done. Most people already used **Auto**, which ran on the same system. Now there's nothing to set before you talk, and one chat goes from talking to getting things done and back. See [Why the modes were removed](/chat-and-agent-modes#why).

The modes aren't coming back.

## What was removed

- The **Chat**, **Agent**, and **Auto** choices in a chat's **AI settings**.
- **New chats** under **Settings** → **General**, which set the mode for chats you started.
- Asking a shape to switch a chat's mode.

Your chats, shapes, and memories stay as they were. This was a November change, separate from the [October 2026 update](/october-2026-update). See [What's new](/whats-new).

## The old picker still shows

The page or app was opened before the update. Reload the page, or close and reopen the app. Choosing **Chat** in an old picker shows "Chats no longer have a Chat mode." and nothing changes.

## A shape misunderstands

Correct it in your next message: "Keep chatting with me; I'm not asking you to search" or "Please search for a train to Porto." If it keeps happening, open the ⋯ menu on that reply → **Report a problem** so the team sees the reply.

See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes) for more.

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://shapes.inc/help).

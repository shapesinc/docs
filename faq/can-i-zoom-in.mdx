It depends on where you use shapes.inc.

## On a computer

Browser zoom works. Press `Cmd` and `+` on a Mac or `Ctrl` and `+` on Windows to zoom in, `-` to zoom out, and `0` to reset.

## On a phone

Pinch-to-zoom is off for the app's screens. There is no setting in shapes.inc that turns it on. Use one of these:

- **Bigger text:** **Settings** → **General** → **Font size**, up to 160%. See [How do I make text bigger?](/faq/how-do-i-change-font-size)
- **iPhone and iPad:** turn on **Zoom** in the device's **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Zoom**, then double-tap with three fingers to magnify any screen.
- **Android:** turn on **Magnification** in the device's **Settings** → **Accessibility**. The name and place vary by phone maker.

## Photos

Tap a photo in a chat to open it in the image viewer. There you can pinch or double-tap to zoom, and drag to move around.

For every display setting in one place, see [Accessibility](/accessibility).

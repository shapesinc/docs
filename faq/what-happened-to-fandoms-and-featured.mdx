Fandoms, Featured, tag pages, and public shape browsing are gone and aren't coming back: shapes.inc is moving away from personalized entertainment and focusing on enabling collaboration.

We're building a place for you to spend time with your friends and use AI together. Chatting with shapes and making online friends are part of that, but they won't be everything.

## What still works

- **Direct shape links** still open a shape.
- **Pickers** show shapes you own and shapes you've messaged in the last 7 days.
- **An Agent chat** can find a shape for you by topic, name, or username. See [Search for Shapes](/shapes-search).
- **Sharing shapes with your friends** is still supported.
- **Your own shapes** give you complete control. See [How to make a Shape](/how-to-make-a-shape).

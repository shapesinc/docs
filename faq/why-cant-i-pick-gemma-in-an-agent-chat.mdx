Gemma 4 31B isn't on the shorter list of engines chats offer. It used to be available in Chat mode, but Chat, Agent, and Auto modes are gone and every chat now uses the same list. See [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

Chats do long, multi-step work with tools. The list keeps the engines that best understand loose instructions and finish long tasks at a sensible cost, based on benchmarks. See [Engines in chats](/aienginecheatsheet#agent-engines).

## If your chat was using it

A chat that was on Gemma 4 31B now replies on MiMo-V2.6-Flash, which is also free. For other free choices, see [which engines are free](/faq/why-do-i-see-fewer-engines-in-agent-chats).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

Yes. Gemma 4 31B is one of the engines that are good at just chatting, behind **Show more** at the bottom of the engines for work in the chat's engine picker (**+** → **Engine**, or **Settings** → **Engines**). Search for "Gemma" to find it right away.

It isn't one of the engines for work, which are the engines that best understand loose instructions and finish long, multi-step work with tools. So with Gemma 4 31B picked, it answers while you're just chatting with a shape, and when you ask for work, the reply runs on the shapes.inc default. See [Engines in chats](/aienginecheatsheet#agent-engines) and [How chats work](/chat-and-agent-modes).

For other free engines, see [which engines are free](/faq/why-do-i-see-fewer-engines-in-agent-chats).

For anything else, ask Tod in [Help Me](https://talk.shapes.inc/help).

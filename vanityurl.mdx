---
title: "Changing a Vanity URL"
description: "Changing your Shape's username."
---

Currently, you can’t directly change a Shape’s username, as this feature was causing issues. If having a custom URL is important, you can duplicate the Shape: open **Settings** → **Your shapes**, select the Shape, and choose **Advanced**. Under **Duplicate**, enter **Name of the copy** and select **Duplicate**. The name influences the new URL/username. Save or discard pending edits first; the copy uses the saved version.

The copy starts from the Shape's saved settings, so save or discard any open edits first. Chats, memories, and stats stay with the original Shape.

We are currently unsure if this feature will be returning to shapes.inc in the future.

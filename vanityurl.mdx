---
title: "Changing a Vanity URL"
description: "Changing your Shape's username."
---

Currently, you can’t directly change a Shape’s username, as this feature was causing issues. If having a custom URL is important, you can duplicate the Shape: open **Settings** → **My Shapes**, select the Shape, choose **Manage**, and use **Duplicate**. The name you enter in **Name for the copy** influences the new URL/username.

The copy starts from the Shape's saved settings, so save or discard any open edits first. Chats, memories, and stats stay with the original Shape.

We are currently unsure if this feature will be returning to shapes.inc in the future.

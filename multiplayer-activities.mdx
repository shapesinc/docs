A group decides faster when everyone can weigh in at once. Ask a shape for choices, a poll, a quiz, or a tracker inside the chat, or a shared page with a shortlist, checklist, game, or form. Each person takes part directly; the group can see votes, answers, scores, or progress as they change. Chat cards, inline widgets, and shared pages have different controls and access rules.

These activities are available in free and Premium chats. Use [Agent mode](/chat-and-agent-modes) for choice cards and custom widgets. A tap that asks the shape to continue can use [credits or allowances](/credits), just like the person's own request; saving a vote or tracker entry is a separate action.

## Choose the right activity

- **Choose between researched options:** use a choice card, with the shape's recommendation and the details that matter. One person can pick, or the group can vote before the person who asked settles it.
- **Collect opinions or availability:** use a poll. Votes do not automatically ask the shape to take the next step.
- **Answer questions with correct answers:** use a quiz card.
- **Keep a tracker, small editor, or interactive explanation in the chat:** ask for a custom widget with shared progress.
- **Compare or play with people outside the chat:** use a shared activity page and its separate share link.

## Ask for an activity

Describe what the group needs, who's taking part, and any rules. For example:

> **Vote on a restaurant:** Make a poll for Friday dinner with the three places we've mentioned. Show the price and distance for each, mark your recommendation, and show the votes live.

## Choose between options

In an Agent chat, give the shape the group's constraints and ask for options people can choose from:

> Compare three parks for our picnic using the travel time, step-free access, and facilities we need. Show the deciding facts, recommend one, and let everyone in this chat vote before I settle the choice.

A choice card has **2–6 options**. It can show a **pick** marking the shape's recommendation, short facts, or researched item details such as a photo, source, price, rating, and link. Some comparisons appear as a table. Check the sources and current details before deciding; showing an option does not establish that shapes.inc can buy or book it.

### Pick yourself or ask the group

By default, the card is for the person who asked. Everyone in the chat can see it, but only that person can choose. Select an option to settle the card and ask the shape to continue with that choice. **Pick it**, when shown, selects the recommendation.

To involve everyone, ask for a group vote in your request, or select **Ask the group** on your open choice card:

1. Each signed-in chat member selects one option. Select another to change your vote while the card is open.
2. Review the voter faces and count, such as **2 of 3 voted**. In a chat of up to **12 members**, the card can name who has not voted when **three or fewer** remain. Votes are visible to the chat.
3. The person who asked selects **Settle: …**, checking the option named on the button. A vote count alone does not settle the decision or start the shape's next reply.

After the choice is settled, the card shows the decision and who made it. The shape continues in that person's turn, with the group's tally when it was a vote. A settled card is not proof that the next reply, booking, purchase, or other action succeeded; check the resulting work separately.

### Change a decision

Select **Change** on a decided card to reopen it. For a personal choice, only the original requester can do this; for a group vote, any chat member can reopen it. Existing group votes remain, and people can change their votes before the original requester settles it again.

Reopening alone does not ask the shape to do more work. The next personal pick or settled group decision does. Changing the card does not undo an order, booking, or other action already completed.

## Answer a Yes/No offer

A shape may offer a next step before starting work nobody requested, such as checking several venues or investigating a code problem. This is a two-option offer, separate from a poll.

Any member of the chat can answer; the first answer settles it. **Yes** asks the shape to do the offered work as the person who tapped, using that person's applicable access, credits, or allowances. **No** closes the offer without starting another shape reply or charging for that reply. An answered offer has no **Change** control and cannot start the work a second time.

Read the offered action before answering. Other members can see the answer, but it does not give them the tapper's accounts or payment controls. Purchases, bookings, and other consequential actions still follow their usual controls and completion checks.

## Vote in a poll card

Use a poll for opinions, yes/no questions, or availability. For researched options with prices, deciding facts, or a recommendation, use a [choice card](#choose-between-options) instead.

1. Select an option to vote. If the poll allows multiple choices, select every option that works for you.
2. Select another option to change a single-choice vote, or select a chosen option again to remove your vote.
3. Check the live totals and voter faces. Votes are visible to other chat members; they are not anonymous.

You must be signed in and a current member of the chat to load the live results or vote. A poll has **2–10 fixed options**; voters cannot type in extra options. Ask the shape for a revised poll if the choices need to change.

The person who requested the poll can select **end poll**, or ask the shape to end it. You can also request an automatic closing time from **1 minute to 30 days** after creation. Once closed, the card keeps its results and no longer accepts votes.

For dinner plans, one person supplies the shortlist, the shape turns it into the poll, and friends vote directly. Ask the shape to summarize the result when everyone has had a chance to answer. A winning option does not reserve a table or commit anyone to paying.

## Study or play a quiz together

For a study group, one person uploads the agreed revision material and asks:

> Quiz us on these notes, with a round for each chapter. After we answer, explain the correct answers and show our scores so we can choose what to revise together.

The shape posts a quiz card in the chat. Each person selects an answer, sees the correct answer after answering, and moves through the questions. A saved answer cannot be changed. Answers and scores are part of the shared activity, not a private assessment. Ask the shape to show the scoreboard or end the quiz when the group is finished.

Native quizzes support up to **60 questions**, arranged into up to **8 sections**, with **2–6 answer choices** per question and one correct answer. A personality quiz with no right answers uses a shared page instead. If an answer fails to save, check the card's error and retry before counting it as submitted.

## Compare a shortlist together

Ask for a shared shortlist page when you want photos, facts, website or map links, and a vote alongside each option. The built-in shortlist supports **2–6 options** and can mark the shape's recommendation.

Select **Vote** on an option. Select **Voted ✓** to remove that vote, or choose another option to change it. A shortlist can also allow several choices. Votes persist when you reopen the same page; changing the option names, deciding facts, or single/multiple-choice setting starts a fresh vote.

If the page includes **Invite**, it shares the chat's invitation link so people can join the conversation under its joining rules. **Open chat** follows that same link. Sharing the activity page itself is a separate action, described below.

## Keep a checklist or collect responses

For a weekend trip, ask the shape to turn the group's agreed packing plan into a common list:

> Make a shared packing checklist for our camping trip. Start with the items we've agreed on; we will add anything missing and check off what we pack.

People can add, check off, and remove items on the same checklist. Saved progress survives reopening. Ask the shape to read the current list when you want a recap, and review the remaining unchecked items together before setting off.

A checklist starts with **1–24 items** and can hold up to **40 items** as people add to it. Keep each item within **120 characters**.

For a party, use an RSVP form when each person needs to give a separate answer. Each person saves their own response, and participants can see everyone's responses. Check the saved response on the page before assuming it was submitted. Use these shared forms for information you want the participants to see; [Saved details](/saved-details) and private booking forms serve a different purpose.

Shared forms support **1–8 fields**. Each field accepts text or offers **2–8 choices**.

Changing a checklist's initial items or a form's fields starts fresh progress or responses. Changing only the title or styling preserves them. Tell the group before changing the questions or starting a fresh list.

## Use a custom widget together

In an Agent chat, ask for a compact tracker, editor, chart, or interactive explanation inside the reply. For example:

> Make a shared picnic tracker here, with a row for each person to enter what they are bringing and a common list of what we still need. Keep our entries when we reopen the chat. Add a button to ask you for a recap.

The widget appears with the shape's reply. Signed-in chat members can use its controls, see saved changes, and return to the same state after reopening. It is part of the message; it is not a downloadable activity page or a separately published website. To involve people outside the chat, invite them under the chat's access rules or ask for a [shared activity page](#shared-activity-pages).

### Shared data and each person's entries

A widget can keep common data everyone changes and a separate entry each person changes themselves. The per-person entry is **not private**: the widget and the shape can use everyone's entries, and the group can see the activity. Use [private booking forms or Saved details](/saved-details) for personal information that should not appear in a shared activity.

Ask the shape that created the widget to change its layout or update its data. Its updates appear in the same widget for everyone; the widget editing tool is limited to widgets that shape created in that chat. Shapes can also read shared activity results when asked. Check the saved result after a change. A widget's data can be separate from a source spreadsheet, calendar, or app record; changing it does not automatically edit that original.

### Buttons that ask the shape to do something

Some controls only save an entry or update shared progress. Others, such as **Refresh prices** or a recap button, send a request to the shape. The widget must receive your interaction before sending that request; merely viewing it does not start work as you.

The chat shows who asked and the requested action beneath the widget, and the shape continues as that person's request in the same chat or thread. This can use their applicable credits or allowances. Requests through one widget are limited to **one per person every five seconds**. Saving state is separate from asking for another reply.

A widget button is not itself approval to buy, book, pay, or message someone. Those actions use their usual tools and controls. Check the actual returned result; a saved tap or changed tracker does not prove an outside action completed.

### Widget limits

Widgets cannot directly fetch websites, connect to outside accounts, load external scripts, or embed another website. A shape can fetch supported information with its tools and put it into the widget, or update it when asked. A chart showing prices is not an automatic price monitor; use [Tasks](/tasks) for an explicitly scheduled check.

Widgets keep up to **32 KB of shared data** and **4 KB per person**, with up to **64 per-person entries**. These are storage limits for a small interactive view, not a file store or a new chat membership limit.

  Custom widgets use the built-in `window.shapes` bridge. `shapes.state`, `shapes.people`, and `shapes.me` provide the saved state and participant information. `setState` changes shared data; `setMine` changes only the acting person's entry; `onState` receives updates. Per-person data remains shared with the chat.

  `sendPrompt` and `callTool` send an interaction to the shape as the person who tapped. Despite its name, `callTool` does not directly execute an outside service: it asks the shape to handle the named action with its normal access and controls. `open` opens a link after the person's interaction. This bridge does not install a third-party app or grant a connection.

  The creating shape uses `SHAPES_WIDGET` to show, read, or update its widget in this chat. Code is an HTML fragment or SVG of up to **40,000 characters**, with a title up to **80 characters**. Prompt text is limited to **500 characters**, and named-action arguments to **2 KB**. State objects support nesting up to **eight levels** and keys up to **64 characters**, without dots or a leading `$`. Embed fetched data instead of attempting network requests inside the widget.
</Accordion>

## Shared activity pages

The following open, download, share, and delete instructions apply to shared pages. Choice, offer, poll, and quiz cards and custom widgets keep their controls inside the chat; they do not use these separate public activity links.

If you ask for changes to a shared page, the shape posts a new version, and earlier versions start folded so the chat doesn't fill up with copies.

## Open, download, or fold it away

Use **More actions** (⋯) under a shared page, or the buttons along its top, to:

- **Open full view** for more space.
- **Download** a copy of the page.
- **Open in a new tab**.
- **Collapse** it. Folding it away doesn't reset anything people have done.

## Share it with people outside the chat

1. Select **Share** on the shared activity page.
2. Your device's share sheet opens. If your browser can't share, the link is copied instead.

Anyone with the link can join without signing in, so share it only with people you want to take part. Signed-in players may appear by their shapes.inc display name; people who join without signing in aren't shown by an account name.

You need to be signed in and a member of the chat to share an activity. If you had the activity open when someone shared it, reopen it to keep playing.

## Let the Shape read the results

Shapes can read an activity's live results: votes, settled choices, scores, form responses, checklist progress, and saved widget data. Name the activity if the chat has several. Ask in the chat:

> Which restaurant is winning? If it's a tie, suggest a tiebreaker we can all live with.

> Who hasn't filled in the RSVP form yet? Draft a friendly reminder for them.

## Delete an activity

To delete a shared activity page permanently, delete the shape message that contains it: open the message's ⋯ menu and select **Delete**. Chat owners can delete shape messages.

You have a few seconds to select **Undo**. After that, the activity and its share link stop working for everyone, including people who joined from the link, and it can't be restored. Deleting a whole chat also deletes its activities.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't choose or settle an option

Check that you are signed in, still belong to the chat, and the card is open. Only the person a personal choice is for can pick; only the original requester can settle a group vote. Another member can answer an open Yes/No offer. If a comparison layout lacks the control you need, ask the shape for a regular group choice card.

If saving fails, read the card's error and check its current state before retrying. If it shows a settled choice but no further reply, check the chat and any credit or access notice before asking again. The saved decision and the shape's follow-up are separate outcomes.

### A widget is unavailable or a change did not save

Check membership and sign-in, reopen the chat, and read the widget's error. An unavailable widget may show only its title. If another person changed it at the same time, check the current value before trying your edit again. A widget can also reach its saved-data or per-person-entry limit.

If a button asks you to wait, allow five seconds before requesting another shape reply through that widget. If the widget needs a fix, ask its creating shape in the conversation; do not keep repeating a tap whose outcome is uncertain. Choice cards and widgets are available in Agent mode, and a request for text only does not create them.

### I can't vote in a poll card

Check that you are signed in, belong to the chat, and the poll is still open. If saving a vote fails, the card shows an error; retry and check the selected option and totals before assuming it saved.

### "This activity is full"

Too many people are connected to it right now. Try again later.

### "This activity is unavailable"

It was deleted, or the link is no longer valid. Ask someone in the chat for a current link, or ask the Shape to build a new one.

### Your connection expired

Reopen the activity to continue. Changes that were already saved stay in place when you reopen it.

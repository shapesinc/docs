Start with a decision or project your group cares about. These guides help you bring the right people into a room, set up an assistant, and keep the work moving.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can a multiplayer assistant help with?" icon="shapes">
    Ask a Shape to help the group:

    - Summarize everyone's priorities and identify missing information.
    - Compare options and explain the tradeoffs.
    - Draft a plan, checklist, message, or explanation for review.
    - Work with images or voice where the engine supports them.
    - Research or take actions through configured [Shape Skills](/shapeskills).

    External services require the appropriate skill and access. Share the goal and constraints in the room so the assistant has something concrete to work from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Give the assistant context" icon="shapes">
    Different settings provide different kinds of context:

    1. **Profile:** help other people identify you.
    2. **Persona:** share your name and relevant preferences with Shapes. You can choose a default persona or apply one in a specific room.
    3. **Room instructions:** record the group's goal, constraints, and working style in [Custom Chat Instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
    4. **Memory:** [review and correct remembered information](/memory) when details change.
    5. **AI engine:** choose an engine suited to the task and check its capabilities and credit requirements.

    Shape owners can manage default behavior in the creator [dashboard](https://shapes.inc/dashboard). Personas are managed from your profile and can be applied in rooms.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Start with a decision or project your group cares about. These guides help you bring the right people into a chat, set up an assistant, and keep the work moving.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can a multiplayer assistant help with?" icon="shapes">
    Ask a Shape to help the group:

    - Summarize everyone's priorities and identify missing information.
    - Compare options and explain the tradeoffs.
    - Draft a plan, checklist, message, or explanation for review.
    - Work with images or voice where the engine supports them.
    - Set up a live poll or form so everyone can weigh in. See [multiplayer activities](/multiplayer-activities).
    - In [Agent chats](/chat-and-agent-modes), research or take actions through available [skills](/shapeskills) and [connected apps](/connected-apps).

    In Agent chats, built-in skills are included automatically. Apps need to be connected first, and external services may require adding and authenticating an MCP server. Share the goal and constraints in the chat so the assistant has something concrete to work from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Give the assistant context" icon="shapes">
    Different settings provide different kinds of context:

    1. **Profiles:** choose the name, photo, and details Shapes use for you, under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Profiles**.
    2. **Your preferences:** tell the assistant what matters to you in this task, including any constraints the group should consider. For standing preferences, use your preset in [Personalization](/personalization).
    3. **Chat instructions:** chat owners can use **More actions** → **Instructions** to record the group's goal, constraints, and working style. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
    4. **Memory:** [review and correct remembered information](/memory) when details change.
    5. **AI engine:** choose an engine suited to the task and check its capabilities and credit requirements.

    Shape owners can manage default behavior under **Settings** → **My Shapes**. See [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality) for the steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

**Shape Credits** are our platform's virtual currency, used to access premium AI Engines and other paid features. They aren't a 1:1 replacement for technical tokens — instead, they provide a **simplified, user-friendly way** to interact with powerful AI Engines without worrying about complex token math.

Each credit grants a **set amount of access** depending on the AI Engine you're using. Different AI Engines have different compute costs, and some premium AI Engines cost many times more to operate than others.

Premium engine replies are charged at **twice the engine's provider cost**. Free AI engines don't use credits. Actual usage depends on the engine selected and the configuration of your shape and chat. See the [Shape Credits Guide](/credits-guide) for what affects usage.

## **Ways to get Shape Credits**

- **Premium plans** add a monthly credit allowance to your balance. Separate one-time account credit packs are not currently available; see [buying credits without a subscription](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription).
- **Existing credits** remain usable without an active plan. Purchased Shape Credits **never expire**, and [cancelling Premium keeps credits already added to your account](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium).
- **Gift cards**, where offered, provide billing credit toward eligible purchases. They are not automatically converted into Shape Credits. See [Gift Cards](/giftcards).

## **How Credits Work**

### **Account Credits**

- **Your Account (all shapes)** – Use credits to chat with any shape in any chat
- With credits, replies can be upgraded to a premium engine (see **Control Where Your Credits Go** below)
- Legacy accounts may use [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up); current subscriptions follow their own billing terms

### **Chat Credits**

Chat credits provide a shared balance for eligible premium usage in that chat. For example, a group comparing trip options can use a sponsored balance for premium replies while everyone contributes their preferences and reviews the results. Availability still depends on the selected AI Engine, chat settings, and remaining credits.

Chat sponsorship is not offered in every purchase flow. See [How to Purchase Credits for a Chat](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat) to find the current options and confirm which chat a purchase funds.

### **Individual Shape Credits (if you have them)**

- If you bought credits for individual shapes before, you can still use them
- They work with those specific shapes just like before
- New individual shape credit purchases are no longer available.

## **What You Get Access To**

With Shape Credits, you get:

- Access to premium engines
- Access to premium image engines if enabled by the creator of the shape
- Spoken shape replies, such as voice calls and read-aloud replies
- Paid coding-agent runs and hosted computer/browser usage beyond the daily computer allowance

Free AI engines and the daily free image generations don't use credits. Generated voice also has a [daily fallback allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) when account credits cannot cover it; the same allowance covers voice messages, Read out loud, and spoken shape replies in calls.

## Coding agents, computers, and browsers

Free Claude Code and Codex access has three separate daily limits per user: **up to 2 coding starts shared between both agents**, **US\$1.00 of model-token cost**, and **US\$0.20 of hosted computer/browser cost**. Run Code, Browser, and Code Project operations that use the hosted computer share the computer allowance too.

Computer/browser use spends that daily allowance first, then eligible credits. Paid coding runs bill model tokens separately from computer use. Available credits determine paid access; an active Premium subscription is not required to spend an existing eligible balance.

Read [how free coding-agent limits work](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work) for reset times and why a run can be blocked even when free starts remain.

## Plan paid work with your group

Before asking for a longer comparison or a generated file, decide which AI Engine and paid capabilities you want to use. Everyone can contribute requirements and review the shared result, but a shared conversation does not split each charge equally between its members.

For eligible premium chat replies, an available chat balance is preferred. Otherwise, the available funding sources and the person's credit preferences determine what can be used. Each person controls **Don't use my credits** for their own account; the owner controls **Keep the chosen AI Engine** for the chat. Neither setting transfers credits between members.

After a reply, check **Generation details** for the engine and charge. The person requesting a regeneration pays for that regeneration, even when someone else asked the original question. Voice and hosted computer use have their own funding and allowance rules; the per-user coding limits above do not become a larger shared allowance just because more people join the chat.

## **Control Where Your Credits Go**

- **Auto upgrade:** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, this chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use: on means the best engine shapes.inc recommends. Turning it off doesn't stop upgrades from using your credits; use the **Don't use my credits** settings below for that.
- **Don't use my credits in new chats:** in the same place, this sets the default for chats you start. See [stopping new chats from using your credits](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits).
- **Don't use my credits** for one chat: open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. When it's on, that chat won't spend your credits or upgrade replies with them.
- **Keep the chosen AI Engine:** chat owners can find it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **General Settings**. It keeps shapes on their configured AI Engine even when premium credits are available.
- **Regenerating** a reply charges whoever requests the regeneration. See [Message Regeneration](/regeneration).
- **Reasoning effort:** higher effort can take longer and use more credits.

To see what a reply cost, open the ⋯ menu on the shape's message and select **Generation details**. If credits are being used unexpectedly, see [unexpected credit usage](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits).

## **When You Run Out**

Your messages are still saved, and shapes can switch to a free text engine so you can keep chatting. Premium engines and paid actions still need credits.

The number of credits used per message varies depending on the AI Engine and configuration, since some engines are significantly more expensive to run than others. Credit options and prices are shown on your Premium page.

**Shape Credits** are the virtual currency on shapes.inc. They pay for premium AI Engines and other paid features. Credits aren't a 1:1 replacement for technical tokens. They give you one balance to track instead of per-engine token math.

How far a credit goes depends on the AI Engine you're using. AI Engines have different compute costs, and some premium AI Engines cost many times more to run than others.

Premium engine replies are charged at **twice the engine's provider cost**. Free AI engines don't use credits. Actual usage depends on the engine selected and the configuration of your shape and chat. See the [Shape Credits guide](/credits-guide) for what affects usage.

## Ways to get Shape Credits

- **Premium plans** add a monthly credit allowance to your balance. [Web plans include the most credits](/premium-subscriptions#credits-per-plan). Separate one-time account credit packs are not currently available; see [buying credits without a subscription](/faq/can-i-buy-credits-without-a-subscription).
- **Existing credits** remain usable without an active plan. Purchased Shape Credits **never expire**, and [cancelling Premium keeps credits already added to your account](/faq/do-i-keep-my-credits-if-i-cancel-premium).
- **Gift cards** can't be purchased right now. A gift card redeemed earlier is billing credit toward eligible purchases. It is not converted into Shape Credits. See [what was retired](/october-2026-update#retired).

## Pro benefits without a subscription

A positive account-credit balance also gives you Pro benefits: premium AI engines, no ads, and longer recent-message context. This includes existing credits from earlier rewards or gifts. It does not start a subscription, add monthly credits, or create a payment. Without an active subscription, these benefits last while account credits remain. Credits funding only a chat or shape do not grant account-wide Pro benefits.

## How credits work

### Account credits

- Account credits pay for premium replies from any shape in any chat, unless **Don't use my credits** is on for that chat
- With credits, replies can be upgraded to a premium engine (see "Control where your credits go" below)
- Auto top-up is retired. See [Why did I get charged automatically for more credits?](/faq/why-did-i-get-charged-automatically-for-more-credits) for what happens when an account with a saved card runs out of credits; subscriptions follow their own billing terms

### Chat credits

Chat credits provide a shared balance for eligible premium usage in that chat. For example, a group comparing trip options can use a sponsored balance for premium replies while everyone contributes their preferences and reviews the results. Availability still depends on the selected AI Engine, chat settings, and remaining credits.

New chat sponsorship isn't available. Plans belong to your account, so you can't buy one for a single chat or shape. Credits already on a chat or shape still work.

To check a chat's balance, open **More actions** (⋯) in the chat header of a sponsored chat and select **View sponsor details**. The banner shows the credits left in that chat.

### Individual shape credits (if you have them)

- Credits you bought earlier for individual shapes still work.
- They apply to those specific shapes, as before.
- New individual shape credit purchases are no longer available.

## What you get access to

With Shape Credits, you get:

- Access to premium engines
- Access to premium image engines if enabled by the creator of the shape
- Generated voice, such as voice messages and Read out loud
- Calls with a shape in the app, paid from your account credits only. See [call credits](/voicecalls#credits).
- Paid coding-agent runs and hosted computer/browser usage beyond the daily computer allowance

Free AI engines and the daily free image generations don't use credits. The one-time trial-credit offer has been retired. Generated voice also has a [daily fallback allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) when account credits cannot cover it; the same allowance covers voice messages and Read out loud. Calls with a shape in the app don't use it: starting one sets aside 50 credits, you pay only for what the call used, and the rest goes back to your balance. When the day's free voice messages are used up and credits can't cover more, a shape's automatic voice replies arrive as text instead, with no out-of-credits card; voice you ask for still shows one.

## Coding agents, computers, and browsers

Free Claude Code and Codex access has three separate daily limits per user: **up to 2 coding starts shared between both agents**, **100 credits of model-token cost**, and **20 credits of hosted computer/browser cost**. Run Code, Browser, and Code Project operations that use the hosted computer share the computer allowance too.

Computer/browser use spends that daily allowance first, then eligible credits. Paid coding runs bill model tokens separately from computer use. Available credits determine paid access; an active Premium subscription is not required to spend an existing eligible balance.

Read [how free coding-agent limits work](/faq/how-do-free-coding-agent-limits-work) for reset times and why a run can be blocked even when free starts remain.

Coding tasks on [your own connected computer](/connect-your-computer#hand-coding-work-to-codex-or-claude-code) use your own signed-in Claude Code or Codex account by default, not shapes.inc credits. Credits are used only if you choose **Use shapes.inc credits** under **Local task account**, or if neither account is ready.

## See today's allowances

**Settings** → **Usage** shows the day's free allowances side by side: phone calls, voice messages, web research, computer, and coding runs, each with how much you've used, what's left, and when they reset (midnight UTC). The same card appears on your Premium page.

The cards in the chat show it too. A phone call paid from your free calling time shows "38 free credits" in its footer, with a line under it for what's left today; a call paid from your balance shows the credits it took. Under a shape's reply that searched the web, used a computer, or ran a coding agent, the activity line shows what those allowances have left.

Only the person whose allowance paid sees a "left today" line. Premium subscribers have no free calling time; their calls are charged from credits by the minute.

<span id="how-many-free-images-do-i-get" />

### Free images per day

Without credits, you get 10 free standard images a day, and the count resets at midnight UTC. Edits to an image count toward the same 10. If your credits can cover it, images use a premium image engine instead, and those don't count toward the 10.

## Plan paid work with your group

Before asking for a longer comparison or a generated file, decide which AI Engine and paid capabilities you want to use. Everyone can contribute requirements and review the shared result, but a shared conversation does not split each charge equally between its members.

For eligible premium chat replies, an available chat balance is preferred. Otherwise, the available funding sources and the person's credit preferences determine what can be used. Each person controls **Don't use my credits** for their own account; the owner controls **Keep the chosen AI Engine** for the chat. Neither setting transfers credits between members.

After a reply, check **Generation details** for the engine and charge. The person requesting a regeneration pays for that regeneration, even when someone else asked the original question. Voice and hosted computer use have their own funding and allowance rules; the per-user coding limits above do not become a larger shared allowance just because more people join the chat.

## Control where your credits go

- **Auto upgrade:** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, this chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use: on means the best engine shapes.inc recommends. Turning it off doesn't stop upgrades from using your credits; use the **Don't use my credits** settings below for that.
- **Don't use my credits in new chats:** in the same place, this sets the default for chats you start. See [stopping new chats from using your credits](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits).
- **Don't use my credits** for one chat: open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. When it's on, that chat won't spend your credits or upgrade replies with them.
- **Keep the chosen AI Engine:** chat owners can find it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI settings** → **General Settings**. It keeps shapes on their configured AI Engine even when premium credits are available.
- **Regenerating** a reply charges whoever requests the regeneration. See [Message regeneration](/regeneration).
- **Reasoning effort:** higher effort can take longer and use more credits.

To see what a reply cost, open the ⋯ menu on the shape's message and select **Generation details**. If credits are being used unexpectedly, see [unexpected credit usage](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits).

## When you run out

Your messages are still saved, and shapes can switch to a free text engine so you can keep chatting. A reply that finishes on the free engine looks like any other reply, with no card under it; **Generation details** shows why the engine switched. Premium engines and paid actions still need credits.

- **Agent chats keep working.** When you have fewer than 100 credits, Agent replies on the default engine start on the free Agent engine, **Formless v2.1**, and aren't charged. **Generation details** shows **Used the free Agent AI Engine**. If the chat has a premium engine picked and your credits can't cover it, your reply switches to Formless v2.1 too. If your credits run out partway through an Agent reply, it finishes on Formless v2.1, with no card if it completes; the steps before the switch stay charged. You can also pick Formless v2.1 yourself; see [Engines in Agent chats](/aienginecheatsheet#engines-in-agent-chats).
- **Stopped tasks resume after you add credits.** When a task stops because it needs credits, such as a coding run, image, research, or browser task, a card appears in the chat: **You're out of credits** when your balance is used up, or **More credits needed** when it's low but not empty. On a Premium plan, the card says when your credits refill and, when a bigger plan is available, offers **Upgrade plan** with the credits you get now and the new monthly price. After the payment goes through, the task resumes once by itself. If you choose **Not now** or cancel checkout, nothing is retried.
- **Scheduled tasks wait.** If a scheduled task keeps stopping for lack of credits, it pauses quietly and tries again every few hours until there are credits to run it.

Credits used per message vary with the AI Engine and configuration; some engines cost much more to run than others. Credit options and prices are shown on your Premium page.

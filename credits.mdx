**Shape Credits** are our platform's virtual currency, used to access premium AI engines and other paid features. They aren't a 1:1 replacement for technical tokens — instead, they provide a **simplified, user-friendly way** to interact with powerful models without worrying about complex token math.

Each credit grants a **set amount of access** depending on the model you're using. Different AI engines have different compute costs, and some premium models cost many times more to operate than others.

Premium engine replies are charged at **twice the engine's provider cost**. Free AI engines don't use credits. Actual usage depends on the engine selected and the configuration of your Shape and chat. See the [Shape Credits Guide](/credits-guide) for what affects usage.

## **Ways to get Shape Credits**

- **Premium plans** add a monthly credit allowance to your balance. See [Premium](/premium-subscriptions).
- **Credit purchases** give you pay-as-you-go access to premium engines, where your Premium page offers them. Purchased Shape Credits **never expire**.
- **Gift cards** can be redeemed for credit. See [Gift Cards](/giftcards).

## **How Credits Work**

### **Account Credits**

- **Your Account (all shapes)** – Use credits to chat with any shape in any chat
- With credits, replies can be upgraded to a premium engine (see **Control Where Your Credits Go** below)
- Works with [Auto Top-Up](/auto-top-up) so you never run out

### **Chat Credits**

- **Sponsor a Chat** – Give everyone in a chat premium access
- Perfect for sharing premium with friends in your favorite chats
- Everyone in that chat gets to use premium engines

See [How to Purchase Credits for a Chat](/how-to-purchase-credits-for-a-chat).

### **Individual Shape Credits (if you have them)**

- If you bought credits for individual shapes before, you can still use them
- They work with those specific shapes just like before
- **Note:** We're not selling individual shape credits anymore - just account and chat credits now

## **What You Get Access To**

With Shape Credits, you get:

- Access to premium engines
- Access to premium image engines if enabled by the creator of the shape
- Shape voice, such as voice calls and read-aloud replies

Free AI engines and the daily free image generations don't use credits.

## **Control Where Your Credits Go**

- **Auto upgrade:** under **Settings** → **Personalization** → **Credits**, this chooses which premium engine your credit upgrades use: on means the best engine shapes.inc recommends. Turning it off doesn't stop upgrades from using your credits; use the **Don't use my credits** settings below for that.
- **Don't use my credits in new chats:** in the same place, this sets the default for chats you start. See [stopping new chats from using your credits](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits).
- **Don't use my credits** for one chat: open the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**. When it's on, that chat won't spend your credits or upgrade replies with them.
- **Prevent Model Overrides:** chat owners can find it in the chat's **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. It keeps Shapes on their configured engine even when premium credits are available.
- **Regenerating** a reply charges whoever requests the regeneration. See [Message Regeneration](/regeneration).
- **Reasoning effort:** higher effort can take longer and use more credits.

To see what a reply cost, open the ⋯ menu on the Shape's message and select **Generation details**. If credits are being used unexpectedly, see [unexpected credit usage](/faq/why-is-a-free-engine-costing-credits).

## **When You Run Out**

Your messages are still saved, and Shapes can switch to a free text engine so you can keep chatting. Premium engines and paid actions still need credits.

The number of credits used per message varies depending on the AI model and configuration, since some engines are significantly more expensive to run than others. Credit options and prices are shown on your Premium page.

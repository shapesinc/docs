You can give your Shape audio replies by choosing a pre-made voice or cloning one from a short clip. Shapes use Fish Audio for voice generation.

## 1. Open Voice Settings

1. Open **Settings** → **Your shapes** and select your Shape. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**.
2. Choose **Voice Engine**.

Only the Shape's owners can change its voice.

## 2. Choose a Voice

Under **Voice**, pick one of two options:

- **Pre-made voices:** search the catalog, play a preview, and select a voice.
- **Your own voice:** upload a clip to clone a voice for the Shape.

For a cloned voice, use 10–30 seconds of clean, single-speaker audio. Audio and video files work, up to **4MB**. Uploading saves the voice sample immediately. Cloning can take a minute; until it finishes, the shape uses a default voice. If the clip fails, upload another clean sample. Selecting a pre-made voice replaces the uploaded voice when you save.

## 3. Choose When It Speaks

Under **How often**, choose **Never**, **Sometimes**, or **Often**, or adjust the **Voice replies** slider from 0% to 50%. Sometimes sets 25%; Often sets 50%. Picking a pre-made voice turns voice replies on at 10% if they were off.

Under **Engine**, **Latency** chooses between **Balanced** (starts speaking sooner) and **Normal** (takes a beat longer for a more polished read). **Send captions with voice messages** adds the spoken text under each voice message.

## 4. Fine-Tune Delivery

Open **Advanced** to adjust **Speed**, **Variation**, and **Range**. Change one setting at a time and listen to the result.

The separate **Voice instructions** section has **Instructions**, which the shape sees when replying with voice, and **Voice in History Instructions**, used when voice messages are in its recent context.

## 5. Save and Test

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom to keep frequency, delivery, and instruction changes. Use **Reset** to discard those unsaved edits. Leaving with unsaved edits offers **Keep editing** or **Discard**; this does not undo a voice sample already saved by uploading.

Send a few messages to hear the result, or use [Read out loud](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#3-read-out-loud). To remove an uploaded sample, select **Delete Voice Sample** and save the change.

<Note>
  Generated voice uses account credits, with a daily fallback allowance when those credits cannot cover it. See [voice credits and the daily allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance). If audio is unavailable, the shape's text reply can still arrive with a notice.
</Note>

## Tips

- Keep cloning clips short, clear, and free of background noise.
- Use a lower **Voice replies** setting for long chats.
- Turn on captions if you want text alongside the audio.
- Test after every major change.

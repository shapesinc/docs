You can give your Shape audio replies by choosing a pre-made voice or cloning one from a short clip. Shapes use Fish Audio for voice generation.

## 1. Open Voice Settings

1. Open **Settings** → **My Shapes** and select your Shape. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**.
2. Choose **Voice**.

Only the Shape's owners can change its voice.

## 2. Choose a Voice

Under **Voice**, pick one of two options:

- **Pre-made:** search the catalog, play a preview, and select a voice.
- **Your voice:** upload a clip to clone a voice for the Shape.

For a cloned voice, use 10–30 seconds of clean, single-speaker audio. Audio and video files work, up to **4MB**. Cloning can take a minute; until it finishes, the Shape uses a default voice.

## 3. Choose When It Speaks

Under **When to speak**:

- **Voice replies** sets how often the Shape answers with a voice message, from **Off** up to about half of its replies. Picking a pre-made voice turns voice replies on at a low setting if they were off.
- **Latency** chooses between **Balanced** (starts speaking sooner) and **Normal** (takes a beat longer for a more polished read).
- **Send captions with voice messages** adds the spoken text under each voice message.

## 4. Fine-Tune Delivery

Open **Advanced** to adjust **Speaking speed**, **Expressiveness**, and **Variety**, and to add **Voice reply instructions** (what the Shape sees when replying with voice) and **Voice history instructions**. The defaults work well for most Shapes, and small changes can make a big difference, so change one thing at a time.

## 5. Save and Test

Select **Save** in the bar at the bottom, then send a few messages to hear how your Shape sounds. If you leave without saving, you're asked whether to keep editing or discard your changes.

<Note>
  A Shape's voice uses credits. If there aren't enough credits for a voice reply, the Shape's text reply still arrives with a notice.
</Note>

## Tips

- Keep cloning clips short, clear, and free of background noise.
- Use a lower **Voice replies** setting for long chats.
- Turn on captions if you want text alongside the audio.
- Test after every major change.

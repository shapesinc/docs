Voice calls are an experimental feature in active development. You can use them to talk with a Shape, or with the people in a group chat, in real time.

## How It Works

1. Open the chat where you want to talk.
2. Start the call:
   - In a chat with one Shape and you, select **Start Call** in the chat header (a phone icon on smaller screens), or open **More actions** (**⋯**) and select **Call** followed by the Shape's name.
   - In a group chat, select **Start Call** in the chat header, or **Join Call** if a call is already going.
3. Allow microphone or camera access if your browser asks.
4. Start talking. Shapes and people in the call can respond in real time.
5. In a call with a Shape, select **Change voice** to switch the Shape's voice for this call only. It doesn't change the Shape's saved voice.
6. End or leave the call from the call controls when you are done.

Group calls can support microphone, camera, screen sharing, and call activities such as **Whiteboard** and **Zombie Tag** (see [Games](/games)), depending on the chat and your device.

Voice calls are live. If shapes.inc creates call messages or summaries, they will appear in the chat, but do not rely on every spoken exchange being saved as a full transcript.

## Requirements

- Voice calls work in supported chats and DMs.
- Some call features depend on the chat setup, device, browser, and whether Shapes are present.
- To choose who can ring you, open **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** and set **Who can call me** (**Everyone**, people you follow, or **Nobody**).

## Credits

- A Shape's voice in a call uses credits, like voice messages. If there aren't enough credits, the call shows a notice.
- If you use a Premium AI engine, it costs credits the same way it does in text chats; otherwise you can choose a free engine.

## Tips for the Best Experience

- Speaking **too slowly or too quickly** may confuse the Shape.
- **Long pauses** might be treated as the end of your message.
- Your Shape may take a moment to reply while processing what you said.

## Setting Your Shape’s Voice

1. Open **Settings** → **My Shapes** and select a Shape you own. You can also select the Shape's name or avatar in a chat and choose **Edit Shape**.
2. Choose **Voice**.
3. Adjust the voice settings and select **Save**. See [How to Give Your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).

If you don't configure a voice, your Shape will automatically use a default one.

During a call, you can also select **Change voice** to switch between preset voices on the fly. That choice only applies to the current call and never overwrites the Shape's saved voice.

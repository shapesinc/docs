Voice calls are an experimental feature in active development. There are two kinds:

- **A call with a shape:** you talk with the chat's shape in real time. The call is between you and the shape; when it ends, a card with its summary is posted in the chat.
- **A call with people:** the people in a chat talk together, with cameras and screen sharing if they want. Shapes don't speak in these calls, but they can catch up on them afterwards.

## Talk through a plan together

A call can help when a group's decision is easier to discuss aloud. For a weekend plan, start a call with the people in the chat: one person can explain the timing, another the travel constraints, and another what they would like to do. Afterwards, ask the shape in the chat "what did we decide on the call?" to get it written down, and correct anything it got wrong.

You can also call the shape on your own to think the options through out loud. Its summary, decisions, and to-dos land in the chat as a card everyone can read.

People join the live discussion from the chat's call controls. After the call, put the agreed plan back in the chat so people who missed it can review it and add changes. Do not rely on the call becoming a complete written record; the limits on call messages and summaries are below.

## How It Works

### Call a shape

1. Open a chat with one shape in it. It can be your chat with that shape or a group chat.
2. Open **More actions** (**⋯**) in the chat header and select **Call** followed by the shape's name. You can also open the menu on one of the shape's messages and select **Call**.
3. Allow microphone access if your browser asks.
4. The call screen shows **Connecting**, then **Listening** while the shape waits for you and **Speaking** while it talks. Talk any time to cut in.
5. Select **Mute** to pause your microphone, and **End** to hang up.

Calling a shape works in chats with exactly one shape. Only the person who starts the call is on it: other people in the chat can't hear or join it, and they see the card afterwards. You can have one call open per chat.

### Call the people in a chat

1. Open **More actions** (**⋯**) in the chat header and select **Start call**, or **Join call** if a call is already going. **View call** returns to a call you've joined.
2. Allow microphone or camera access if your browser asks.
3. End or leave the call from the call controls when you are done.

A call with people needs at least one other person in the chat. Starting one when you're the only person there shows "Voice calls require at least 2 human participants in the room".

Calls with people can use microphone, camera, and [screen sharing](#share-your-screen), depending on your device. The call toolbar no longer offers Games or reactions.

Shapes in the chat know who was on each call and for how long. After a call with two or more people, they can also read a transcript and short summary of what was said, so you can ask "what did we decide on the call?" later, including people who missed it. The transcript isn't posted as a message, and speakers are told apart by voice rather than by name, so check names and details in the answer.

To have a shape phone a business for you instead, see [Phone calls](/phone-calls).

## What a shape can do on a call

On a call, the shape talks as itself: its personality, examples, and instructions, the chat's instructions, and what you've set in **Personalization**. It knows the chat's recent messages and some of what it remembers about you and the chat, and it can look up more when you ask what it remembers. Your personal memories come up only in your private chat with it, and another member's never do.

What else it can do depends on the chat's mode:

- **In an Agent chat**, the shape can search the web and tell you the answer, set a reminder or scheduled task, and save a memory when you ask it to. For anything else it can do in the chat, like checking your email or calendar through your [connected apps](/connected-apps), it says it's checking, works on it in the chat, and tells you the answer. Its full reply is also posted in the chat. If the request takes a while, it keeps you posted and offers to leave the answer in the chat.
- **In a Chat chat**, the shape can save a memory when you ask. For anything else, it offers to pick it up in the chat after the call.

Paying, sending messages, inviting people, and other actions that need your approval aren't done by voice. If a request needs you to pick, fill in, approve, or sign in, the shape tells you which card in the chat to use, and carries on once you have. In a group chat, everyone can see what the shape posts.

## After the call

When a call with a shape ends, a **Call with** card for that shape appears in the chat for everyone in it. It shows who called, how long the call lasted, a summary, what was **Decided**, a **To do** list, and the cost. In a group chat, it says who paid. Only the person who made the call can open **Transcript**.

Cards for things the shape did during the call, such as a reminder, are posted in the chat in a message from the shape that starts "From the call with". Later replies in the chat can refer back to the call. A call that never connected posts nothing.

## Share your screen

Screen sharing is part of calls with people. Calls with a shape are voice only.

- **On a computer:** select **Share screen** in the call controls, and **Stop screen sharing** when you're done.
- **In the iPhone and Android apps:** coming in the next app update. **Share screen** then appears in the call controls on your phone. Your phone asks before it starts: on iPhone, start the broadcast; on Android, allow sharing your entire screen. Everything on your screen is shared, including other apps you switch to, until you stop. While you share, **Screen Shares** shows **You're sharing your screen** with a **Stop sharing** button. You can also stop from the red indicator in the status bar or Control Center on iPhone, or from the notification on Android. Leaving the call stops sharing.
- **On the call screen for a call you answered in the app**, use **Screen** on iPhone or **Share** on Android.
- **In a phone's web browser**, screen sharing isn't available. Use the app instead.

If **Share screen** doesn't appear in the app, your app doesn't have the update yet. If you close the sharing prompt without starting, you'll see "Screen sharing cancelled"; select **Share screen** again to retry.

With the same iPhone app update, the call screen for a call you answered has a minimize button at the top left. It shrinks the call to a green bar at the top of the app with the name and call timer, so you can keep using the app while you talk. Tap the bar to go back to the call. On Android, **Back** leaves the call screen without ending the call.

## Requirements

- Calls with a shape work in any chat with exactly one shape, except announcement chats. You need to be signed in.
- Calls with people work in chats and DMs with at least two people.
- Some call features depend on your device and browser.
- To choose who can ring you, open **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy** and set **Who can call me** (**Everyone**, people you follow, or **Nobody**).

## Credits

- **Calls with a shape use your account credits only.** The person who starts the call pays. These calls don't use the [daily voice allowance](/how-to-receive-voice-messages#credits-and-the-daily-allowance) or chat credits, and unlike [phone calls](/phone-calls), they have no free daily time.
- Starting a call sets aside 50 credits, and more is set aside as the call goes on. When it ends, you're charged only for what the call used, and the rest goes back to your balance.
- The call screen shows **Uses your credits** with the credits used so far. In a group chat, it says **You're paying for this call**.
- If you don't have enough credits, the call screen says "Calls with shapes use your credits. Add credits to start a call." and shows a card to get more. After you add credits, the call starts. See [Credits](/credits).
- If **Don't use my credits** is on for the chat (the chat's **Settings** → **Profile & credits**), a call with a shape can't be paid for in that chat.
- If your credits run out during a call, the shape finishes what it's saying, says goodbye, and the call ends.
- What a shape does during a call is charged the way it is in the chat: a web search like a search in an Agent chat, and a request it works on in the chat like the same request typed there. Reminders and memory saves don't cost extra.
- Calls between people don't use credits.

## If a call doesn't start or ends early

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Allow the microphone to call." | Allow microphone access for shapes.inc in your browser or device settings, then call again. |
| "Calls with shapes use your credits. Add credits to start a call." | Add credits from the card, and the call starts. |
| "Account credits are turned off for this chat, so a call can't be paid for." | Turn off **Don't use my credits** for the chat, or call from another chat. |
| "You're already on a call in this chat." | End your other call in this chat, then try again. |
| "The call couldn't connect. You weren't charged." | Try again. |
| "Calls with shapes aren't available right now." | Try again later. |
| "Call ended: not enough credits to keep going" | Add credits, then call again. |
| "Call ended: it reached the 30-minute limit" | Start a new call to keep talking. |
| "Call ended after a few quiet minutes" | A call hangs up after five minutes without talking. Call again when you're ready. |
| "Call ended because shapes.inc restarted. You're only charged for what you used" | Call again. |

## Tips for the Best Experience

- Speaking **too slowly or too quickly** may confuse the shape.
- **Long pauses** might be treated as the end of your message.
- Your shape may take a moment to reply while processing what you said.
- For something that takes a while, like checking several apps, ask the shape to leave the answer in the chat.

## Setting your shape’s voice

On the call screen, select **Change voice**. Choose the shape's own voice or one of seven call voices: **Marin**, **Cedar**, **Coral**, **Sage**, **Ash**, **Verse**, or **Alloy**. Play a sample before you pick.

- Your pick is yours alone: it applies to your calls with that shape and doesn't change the shape for anyone else.
- A pick made during a call applies from your next call.
- If nobody has picked one, the shape's own call voice is one chosen to fit the shape. It's the same voice the shape uses for [phone calls](/phone-calls).

The voice set in a shape's **Voice Engine** settings is for its voice messages and Read out loud, not calls. See [how to give your shape a voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).

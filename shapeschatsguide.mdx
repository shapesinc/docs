    ## Chats for Shared Work

    A chat holds the people, assistants, and context for a shared goal. Start by sharing what the group wants to decide or produce, then ask everyone to add their preferences and constraints.

    ### Create a Chat

    1. Select **New Chat** in the sidebar.
    2. Use **AI** and **People** to choose who to include. **Quick chat** is also available in this panel.
    3. Select **Continue** to open **Chat details**.
    4. Give the chat a name and select **Create Chat**.

    See [Create a Chat](/create-a-chat) for the full walkthrough.

    ### Adding Shapes

    Open **More actions** and choose the **Members** entry or **Invite** to manage who is in the conversation. The Members entry includes the current count, such as **Members (2)**.

    Choose **Add participants** → **AI** to find a Shape. See [adding Shapes to a chat](/how-to-talk-to-shapes) for help choosing an assistant. Give each Shape a distinct job, such as comparing options or reviewing a draft.

    ### Inviting Friends

    Use **More actions** → **Invite** to bring in the people whose information or agreement the group needs. Check who can access the chat before sharing personal information. See [group chat setup](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-group-chat) and [chat access](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join).

    <span id="activating-shapes" />

    ### Choose When Shapes Reply

    Open **More actions** → **Settings** → **AI Configurations**. Under **AI replies**, review **Auto-reply triggers**:

    - **Reply when mentioned**
    - **Keep the convo going**
    - **React to keywords**
    - **Always have something to say**
    - **Come back later**

    Choose triggers that suit the work. If the group wants to direct each contribution, start with **Reply when mentioned** and mention the Shape with `@`. For more guidance, see [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence).

    ### Chat Aesthetics

    Open **More actions** → **Settings** → **Chat Appearance** to make a project recognizable and the discussion comfortable to read. See [Chat Appearance](/chat-appearance).

    ### Skip Turn and Fast Forward

    Use [Skip turn and fast forward](/faq/how-do-i-use-fast-forward-or-skip-turn) when you want Shapes to continue without writing a new message. Review the replies and stop when the group has enough material to work with.

    ## Find Chat Settings

    **More actions** → **Settings** opens **Chat Menu**. These are the main sections:

    ### Participants

    Review who is in the chat. You can also reach the member list from **More actions** → **Members**. Invite the people needed for the task and check the list before sharing information.

    ### Pinned Messages

    Find the messages your group has pinned. Use them to revisit an agreed brief, a useful source, or the latest decision.

    ### Media & Files

    Find shared images and files so the group can return to source material while discussing it.

    ### Search

    Use **Search Messages** in Chat Menu, or **Search** in **More actions**, to find an earlier detail. **Mentions** is also available in **More actions**.

    ### Notifications

    Review notification settings for the chat. See [Notifications](/notifications) for help reducing interruptions while keeping track of the discussion.

    <span id="personal-ai-preferences" />

    ### Credits

    Check [Shape Credits](/credits) and the [account credit usage guide](/faq/how-do-i-stop-shapes-in-new-chats-from-consuming-my-credits) when planning premium usage. Review the settings and prices shown in the app before spending credits.

    ### AI Configurations

    - **Instructions:** describe the group's goal, constraints, and how the assistant should help. You can also open **Instructions** directly from **More actions**. See [chat instructions](/custominstructions-chats).
    - **AI replies:** choose **Auto-reply triggers** to guide when Shapes contribute.
    - **Skills:** built-in skills are included automatically and cannot be disabled.
    - **MCP servers:** use **Add MCP server** to connect an external service. Complete any authentication the service requires. See [Skills and MCP servers](/shapeskills).

    ### Chat Appearance

    Adjust the chat's presentation in **Chat Appearance**. See the [appearance guide](/chat-appearance) for its controls.

    ### Moderation

    Review who is in the chat and who can join. The [chat access guide](/faq/how-do-i-lock-a-chat-so-that-other-people-cant-join) explains access controls. If a control is unavailable, check your role and permissions before trying to change it.

    ## Shapes — Your AI Assistants

    A Shape is an AI assistant you can give a role in the group's work. It can help compare options, explain a topic, or draft something everyone can review. Capabilities depend on the selected engine, available skills, and connected services.

    ## Customizing Your Shapes

    Shape owners and co-owners manage default behavior from the Shape's page. Open its sidebar and look under **Shape Settings**.

    <span id="creator-dashboard" />
    <span id="for-everyone" />
    <span id="for-shape-owners" />

    ![Shape page sidebar with Shape Settings and User Shape Settings](/images/shapes-faq-shape-sidebar-light.png)

    - **Profile:** edit how the Shape introduces itself. See [Shape profile editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-profile).
    - **Personality:** give it a clear role and useful behavior. See [personality editing](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-shapes-personality).
    - **AI Engine:** review model choices and behavior settings. See [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet).
    - **Voice Engine:** choose how it sounds. See [voice setup](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice).
    - **Memory**, under **User Shape Settings:** review remembered information. See [memory](/memory).

    ### Stats

    Use **Stats** to review activity. Look at actual responses as well as usage when judging whether a Shape is helping the group.

    ### Personality

    Use **Personality** to explain what the Shape should contribute. A useful brief tells it to consider everyone's preferences, ask about missing constraints, and explain the tradeoffs behind a recommendation.

    <span id="shape-free-will" />

    ## AI replies

    Participation is configured per chat through **More actions** → **Settings** → **AI Configurations** → **AI replies**. Use **Auto-reply triggers** to guide when Shapes contribute. See [assistant participation](/designing-social-intelligence).

    ## Knowledge & Training

    **Knowledge** holds reference material for the Shape's role. **Training** provides examples of useful responses. Keep the material accurate and relevant; examples guide the assistant but do not guarantee an exact response. See [knowledge and training](/shapes101#knowledge).

    ## Image & Voice Engines

    ### Image Engine

    Review **Image Engine** on the Shape's page for the available image settings. When asking for an image, give the group a chance to agree on the brief and review the result. See [creating images together](/articles/best-ai-image-generators-2026).

    ### Voice Engine

    Use **Voice Engine** on the Shape's page for voice setup. Follow [Give Your Shape a Voice](/how-to-give-your-shape-a-voice) and [Receive Voice Messages](/how-to-receive-voice-messages) for the detailed steps.

    # AI Engine

    Choose an engine based on the group's task, required capabilities, and budget. Check [the engine guide](/aienginecheatsheet), [credits](/credits), and [presets](/presets) before adjusting the Shape's default behavior.

    ### Advanced Engine Parameters

    Available settings vary by engine. Change one setting at a time and compare responses to the same brief so you can understand its effect. Keep a copy of settings that work well.

    ## Memory Systems

    [Context windows](/contextwindows) limit how much information an engine can use at once. [Memory](/memory) can preserve useful information beyond recent messages, but it needs review when details change. Restate the current brief when an older constraint is missing.

    ## Settings & Ownership

    Use **Settings** on the Shape's page for management controls available to its owners. See the focused guides for [discoverability](/faq/how-do-i-make-a-shape-private) and [deleting a Shape](/faq/how-do-i-delete-a-shape).

    ### What You Can Customize

    Your profile helps people recognize you. Use a display name, picture, and description that make sense to the people you collaborate with.

    ### How to Customize

    Follow [Edit My Profile](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-profile) for the current steps and screenshots.

    ### Change Your Display Name or Username

    Use the [profile editing guide](/faq/how-do-i-edit-my-profile) to change how people identify you. Review the result on your profile after saving.

    ### Profile Picture & Banner

    Follow the image controls shown on your profile. Use an image that remains recognizable in a small thumbnail.

    ### Bio / Description

    Share information you want other people to know, such as what you work on or the kinds of projects you enjoy.

### How Friends Work

Follow the people you want to plan and work with on shapes.inc.

If you follow someone and they follow you back, you become friends.

Friends matter for privacy settings. With **Private account** on, people who aren't your friends must send an invite before they can DM you or add you to a chat or workspace. **Who can call me** can also be limited to people you follow. Both are under **Settings** → **Notifications & privacy**.

### Following Someone

To follow somebody:

1. Open a chat you share with them.
2. Select the person's picture or name to open their profile.
3. Select the heart icon (**Follow**).

You can also select **Follow** next to a person in the sidebar search results or in a chat's **Participants** list. After you've been chatting with someone, a banner at the top of the chat may suggest following them.

If they follow you back, they become your friend. Select the heart again to unfollow.

### Checking Followers

To see who follows you:

1. Select **Search...** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Before you type, select **Followers**.

Use **Following** to see people you follow, and **Friends** to see your mutual follows.
